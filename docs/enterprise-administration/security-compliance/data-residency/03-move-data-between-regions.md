Miro customers may require [data residency](../../canvas-25-admin-features/data-residency/02-data-residency-at-miro.md) outside their current region. Outside the European Union (EU), Miro offers data residency in Australia (AU) and the United States (US).

## Options for moving data between regions

The process for moving data residency is called cross-region migration. To migrate your organization to a new region, Miro offers the following paths:

- **Manual export and import**
  Miro Support provisions a new organization in the new region. Company Admin reconfigures all org and team settings and re-invites users. End users manually download their Miro boards from the old region and upload them into the new region. To learn more, see [Move data between regions – Manual export and import](../../canvas-25-admin-features/data-residency/06-move-data-between-regions-–-manual-export-and-import.md).
- **Automated data migration**
  Available as a paid engagement with Miro services. Automates the migration of the full organization, including all organization content, content hierarchy, users, and settings. To learn more, see [Moving data between regions – Automated migration](../../canvas-25-admin-features/data-residency/05-move-data-between-regions-–-automated-migration.md).

  > ✏️ Automated data migration is not currently available for [EKM enterprises](../../canvas-25-admin-features/encryption-key-management/01-encryption-key-management-overview.md).

To decide which migration path is best for you, reach out to your dedicated Miro contact person, or see [Contacting Miro Support](../../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/06-contacting-miro-support.md).

## Compare options for moving data between regions

The following table compares your automated and manual options for moving data between regions:

|  | Automated | Manual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | Paid engagement with Miro services. | No additional cost. The Company admin(s) and end users move their board content from one region to another. |
| Scope | The majority of the organization and its data is migrated, including the following data. For a comprehensive list of included data, see [the automated for admins article].   - Organization data - User profiles - Teams and Team settings - Team membership - Boards - Audit logs - Data classification - Content security settings | Admins must reconfigure organization settings, recreate Teams and apply Team settings, re-invite users and assign licenses, and re-install apps and integrations. End users must manually move their boards to the new region. |
| Duration | Typically <8 hours of downtime. The organization and its data is migrated to the new region. | Varies depending on how quickly the Company admin completes manual configuration, and end users move their boards. |
