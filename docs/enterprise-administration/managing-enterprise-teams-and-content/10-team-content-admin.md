The Team Content Admin role enables you to supervise all content for a given team. As Team Content Admin, you can manage access to Spaces, boards, and templates for a given team.

The Team Content Admin role is a team-level permission set. For organization-level permissions, see [Content Admin permissions](12-content-admin-permissions.md).

## Team Content Admin permissions

A Team content admin has the following team permissions:

- Manage all content including boards and Spaces
- Manage content metadata and access rights for all boards and Spaces
- Create custom templates
- Export board archive

To view all Team content admin permissions, and compare the permission set with other admin roles, see [Understand admin roles and their privileges](../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/02-understand-admin-roles-and-their-privileges.md).

## How to assign the Team Content Admin role

1. As Company admin or User admin, in Admin Console go to **Users** > **Admin roles**.
2. Click the **Team roles** tab.
3. Click anywhere on the **Team Content Admin** row.
   The **Team Content Admin** view opens.

   > 💡 Alternatively, from the **Team roles** tab, at the end of the **Team Content Admin** row, click the three-dots (**...**) menu.
4. Click **Assign role**.
   The **Assign Team Content Admin role** modal opens.
5. Select the team where you want to assign Team content admin(s).
6. Click **Next**.
   The **Select users** step lists each member of the team you have selected.
7. Search by user name or email, and select the team member(s) you want to assign. Max: 50 members.
8. Click **Assign**.
   You return to the **Users** tab in the **Team Content Admin** view. You can see all users that have the Team Content Admin role assigned.

## How to remove the Team Content Admin role

1. As Company admin or User admin, in Admin Console go to **Users** > **Admin roles** > **Team roles** tab.
2. Click **Team Content Admin**.
3. Under **Users**, for the Team Content Admin you want to to remove, at the end of the row click the three-dots (**...**).
4. Click **Remove role from user**.
