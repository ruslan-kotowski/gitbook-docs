Admin announcements let Company Admins post targeted, time-bound messages to people in their organization from the Admin console. Announcements are displayed as banners across boards and the dashboard for users who match the selected audience.

You can write a message, add a call-to-action (CTA) that links to any destination (such as a signup form, Typeform, or internal documentation), select the audience, and schedule when the announcement appears.

Use admin announcements to promote training, share product updates, support feature rollouts, or collect feedback from the right audience at the right time.

## Key capabilities

- **Create announcements with essential details**
  Add an internal name, text, link text, and link URL. A live preview shows how the announcement will appear.
- **Target the right audience**
  Reach the entire organization or a specific team. The editor shows an estimated audience size.
- **Schedule announcement visibility**
  Set start and end dates so announcements go live automatically and are removed after their scheduled window.
- **Prioritize important announcements**
  Mark announcements as priority to ensure they are displayed ahead of others.
- **Manage announcements across their lifecycle**
  View all announcements in one place with their status, audience, and schedule.
- **Edit announcements at any time**
  Update announcement content, audience, or schedule, including when announcements are live.
- **Reuse and control announcements**
  Duplicate announcements to reuse content, cancel scheduled announcements, or delete announcements when no longer needed.
- **Delegate using custom admin roles**
  Assign announcement management capabilities to specific roles to support governance and internal workflows.
- **Use templates for consistency**
  Create announcements faster using pre-populated content from the What’s new in Miro section.
