Priority announcements are displayed ahead of other announcements to ensure important messages are seen first.

## Mark an announcement as priority

When creating or editing an announcement, select the **Priority announcement** checkbox to mark it as high priority.

## How priority works

- Only one announcement is shown to users at a time.
- Priority announcements are shown before non-priority announcements.
- If multiple announcements are eligible, they are shown in order of creation.

## When to use priority announcements

- **Critical updates**
  Communicate urgent or important information that requires immediate visibility.
- **Time-sensitive announcements**
  Highlight announcements that are relevant for a limited time.
- **Organization-wide communications**
  Ensure important messages are shown with higher visibility across the organization.
