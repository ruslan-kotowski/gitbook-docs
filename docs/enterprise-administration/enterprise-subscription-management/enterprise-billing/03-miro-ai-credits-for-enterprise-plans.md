For knowledge workers looking to innovate faster, **Miro AI** enhances workflows and collaboration. It integrates AI capabilities into Miro's platform to automate tasks like insight clustering, diagramming, and content creation. Unlike other visual collaboration tools, **Miro AI** helps teams leverage AI across a wider variety of workflows and across their existing processes more efficiently.

Miro AI usage is based on a credit system, where most AI actions consume 1 credit per action. Some AI capabilities consume more per action.

For example, [Miro Prototypes](../../../using-miro/miroverse/prototyping/01-miro-prototypes-overview.md) consumes [5 credits per generated screen](../../../plans-billing/billing-and-payments/03-miro-ai-credits.md#miro-ai-credit-consumption).

Miro allocates a set number of AI credits to your account each month. For Enterprise plans, the balance resets on the first day of each calendar month. Unused AI credits do not carry over.

**More information:** See [Miro AI credits](../../../plans-billing/billing-and-payments/03-miro-ai-credits.md).

## Miro AI credit consumption

Every AI action consumes AI credit from your monthly allocation. For example, the [Miro Prototypes add-on](../../../using-miro/miroverse/prototyping/07-miro-prototypes-add-on.md) consumes five (5) AI credits per AI-generated screen, and any AI-generated image consumes three (3).

The following table shows the credit consumption for AI-generated output:

| AI output by feature | Credit(s) consumed |
| --- | --- |
| AI search | 0 credit |
| Catch-up | 1 credit |
| Diagrams | 5 credits |
| Doc | 2 credits |
| Image | 3 credits |
| Prototypes | 5 credits per screen |
| Remove background | 1 credit |
| Sidekicks | 2 credits |
| Slides | 5 credits per Slide |
| Sticky note | 1 credit |
| Tables | 5 credits |
| Text | 1 credit |

:::note
Sidekicks consume 2 AI credits each time you execute a prompt. Ensure your prompts are as detailed as possible to reduce the number of executions needed to achieve your result.
:::

:::note
If Miro AI is unable to generate output, and shows an error message, then no AI credit is consumed.
:::

:::note
Accessibility features, like generating image alt-text, do not consume AI credit.
:::

## AI Credits included for Enterprise users

For an Enterprise subscription, AI credit allocation varies based on license tier. In Admin Console, Company admins can monitor their organization's credit allocation and usage.

Go to **Admin Console** > **Billing** > **Subscription**.

The following table shows AI credit allocation per license tier as of March 2, 2026. [Credit consumption per feature](../../../plans-billing/billing-and-payments/03-miro-ai-credits.md) may change as models are optimized, or new models become available.

| License tier (Number of seats) | Monthly AI credits |
| --- | --- |
| 1-50 | 2,500 |
| 51-100 | 5,000 |
| 101-200 | 10,000 |
| 201-300 | 15,000 |
| 301-400 | 20,000 |
| 401-500 | 25,000 |
| 501-700 | 35,000 |
| 701-1000 | 50,000 |

:::note
AI credit allocation is subject to change.
:::

AI credits are pooled across the entire organization, and any credits used consume from that single pool of AI credits.

If your paid user count changes throughout the term, the number of allotted AI credits will adjust dynamically, with usage limits changing to fit the new number of team members on the next billing date.

Unused credits do not carry over to the next month.

## Which user roles can use AI Credits?

Any [member](../../user-management/11-user-access-levels-on-enterprise-plan.md#miro-roles) of your account can use AI credits when performing the actions listed above. Guests and Visitors cannot perform AI actions and therefore cannot use AI credits.

## What happens when my organization reaches the limit?

Enterprise plans have a soft limit on their AI Credit usage, which means you’ll be allowed to go over your limits. If you regularly exceed your monthly AI Credit allocation, you’ll need to purchase the Miro AI credit Add-On to get additional AI Credits.

## Miro AI credits add-On

While every Miro account gets an allotted number of AI credits each billing cycle, Miro AI credit Add-Ons are available for users who need additional AI credits.

To get the Miro AI credit Add-On, Enterprise Plan admins must contact their account team.

**More information:** See [Miro AI credit add-on](../../../using-miro/miro-ai/17-miro-ai-credits-add-on.md).

## How to check your AI credit balance

In Enterprise plans, the AI Credits section can be found on your Subscription settings page with how many credits you’ve used in the current month, and how many are remaining.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who can request a Miro AI-Add On?**

Only Organization admins or cardholders can request the [Miro AI Add-On](../../../using-miro/miro-ai/17-miro-ai-credits-add-on.md).

**Who can use your team’s AI credits?**

Currently, any member of your team who can access AI features can use AI credits. Only teams who have Miro AI enabled can access AI features.

**Does downgrading my Enterprise subscription also cancel the Miro AI Add-on?**

As long as you maintain a paid subscription (Business or Starter), you can continue to use the Miro AI Add-On (contact customer support once you’ve downgraded to get the Add-On). If you cancel your subscription or switch to a Free or Education plan, you’ll no longer have access to the Miro AI Add-On. Also by downgrading your plan; you will also decrease the number of AI credits allocated free of charge. Business plan has 50 credits per license per month, Starter has 25 credits per license per month and Free Plan has 10 credits for the team per month free of charge. Only Enterprise Plan has an allocation of 100 credits per license per month.
