Analyze and customize your subscription usage at scale with the help of software asset management. The integration allows getting the list of non-active users and deactivating them from an asset management app.

Miro has partnered with pioneers in this emerging category to help you better manage your SaaS offerings, including Miro investment. You can set up integration with the following services to optimize the management of licenses and ensure access is available to every team member:

- ServiceNow
- Productiv
- Zylo
