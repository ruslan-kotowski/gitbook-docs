The *Enterprise* *admin deployment guide* provides the basic knowledge and steps to configure Miro Enterprise for your organization.

As Company Admin, you make the initial specifications for how users collaborate, share content, extend the Miro platform, and ensure security.

This guide is divided into the following six sections:

[**1-Domain Control**](https://help.miro.com/hc/sections/21828935394066-1-Domain-control)

[**2-Admin roles, team structure, & billing**](https://help.miro.com/hc/sections/21155021427730-2-Admin-roles-team-structure-billing-groups)

[**3-User provisioning & security**](https://help.miro.com/hc/sections/21158476001042-3-User-provisioning-security)

[**4-Company & team permissions**](https://help.miro.com/hc/sections/21846205353874-4-Organization-team-settings)

[**6-Enterprise integrations**](https://help.miro.com/hc/sections/21852097306770-6-Enterprise-integrations)

#### Prerequisites

To onboard as a new Miro admin, we highly recommend that you visit our [Welcome Board.](https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVNO-oyr8=/?messenger=slack&utm_source=slack&utm_medium=messenger&utm_campaign=notifications&utm_content=unfurled_chat_link&utm_term=messenger_analytics) The Welcome Board defines technical concepts that are key to your success.

Before you start to configure Miro Enterprise, ensure that you do the following:

- The **Company Admin** role is assigned to you. The Company Admin has the highest permissions set.
- Understand the following key concepts from the Welcome Board:
  - Roles and licenses
  - Single sign-on (SSO)
  - Team structure
  - User Provisioning and Domain Control
- From the [Welcome Board](https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVNO-oyr8=/?messenger=slack&utm_source=slack&utm_medium=messenger&utm_campaign=notifications&utm_content=unfurled_chat_link&utm_term=messenger_analytics), access the interactive configuration checklists.
- If you would prefer to follow a physical document, review the [PDF printable configuration checklist.](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KQi4HF1HdYEJlqmVtRuKH4-_JQz_N5M2/view?usp=sharing)
- Know your Enterprise licensing model.
  > 💡 You can confirm your licensing model with Miro Support.
