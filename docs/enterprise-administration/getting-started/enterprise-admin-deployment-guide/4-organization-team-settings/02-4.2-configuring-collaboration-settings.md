Your collaboration settings define what users in your organization can share internally and externally, including who can invite new members, and the default for team privacy.

## How to specify collaboration settings

The following procedures explain how to specify collaboration settings for your organization.

#### Prerequisites

For each of the following procedures, go to **Admin console** > **Security** > **Sharing**.

### Automatically deactivate guests

A guest is a non-organization member who is invited to share and participate in board activity. You can configure Miro to automatically deactivate guests when they become inactive.

#### Procedure

To automatically deactivate guests, toggle **Automatically deactivate Guests** to the on position. Guests are now automatically deactivated after 30 days of inactivity.

### Allow domains for sharing

Prohibits users from sharing content outside of the domain(s) you specify.

#### Procedure

Follow these steps:

1. Under **Allowed domains**, for each domain you want to allow, repeat the following steps:
   1. In the box on the right, enter your allowed domain.
   2. Select **Add**.

      > 💡 Ensure that you add your own domain.
2. Toggle **Restrict to allowed domains** to the on position.
3. (Optional) Verify all users.
4. (Optional) To allow sharing with guests outside an allowed domain, tick **Allow sharing with guests outside these domains**...

**More information:** See [Sharing policy on Enterprise plan](../../../canvas-25-admin-features/data-security/07-sharing-policy-on-enterprise-plan.md).

### Configure sharing via public link

Enables users to share their boards outside the organization.

#### Procedure

Follow these steps:

1. Under **Content**, toggle **Allow public sharing** to the on position.
2. (Optional) To allow board owners to enable anyone to edit public boards, tick **Allow editing on publicly shared boards**.
3. (Optional) To make password protection mandatory for public boards, tick **Require passwords for publicly shared boards**.
4. (Optional) To set an expiration for links to public boards that are inactive, tick **Expire public sharing link for inactive boards**.

   > 💡 You can set a maximum of 999 days. Default: 30.

You have successfully configured sharing boards via public links.

### Specify who can invite new members

Enables all users, or only the roles you specify, to invite new members to your Enterprise subscription.

#### Procedure

To specify who can invite new members, under **Invitation**, select one of the following options:

- **Company Admins only**
  Only Company Admins can invite new members to your Enterprise subscription.
- **Company admins and Team Admins**
  Company Admins and Team Admins can invite new members to your Enterprise subscription.
- **Anyone in your organization**
  Anyone in your organization can invite new team members to your Enterprise subscription.

:::note
A new member to your Enterprise subscription gets the default license that you set when selecting your plan type.
:::

### Allow board Co-owner role for teams

Enables teams in your organization to assign the board Co-owner role to a team member.

#### Procedure

To allow the Co-owner role for teams, under **Roles and permissions**, toggle **Allow Co-owner role** to the on position. You have successfully enabled the Co-owner role.

:::note
Please note that the co-owner role needs to be enabled for each team.
:::

**More information:** See [Board Co-owner](../../../../using-miro/sharing-boards/06-co-owners-of-boards-and-spaces.md).

### Restrict organization template sharing

Prohibits users from sharing company templates with everyone in the organization.

#### Procedure

To prohibit sharing company templates with everyone, under **Roles & Permissions**, toggle **Restrict sharing of company templates** to the on position. You have successfully restricted company template sharing.

### Enable team privacy

Enables team privacy by default.

#### Procedure

To enable team privacy by default, under **Teams** toggle **Set teams to private** to the on position. You have successfully enabled team privacy.

:::note
Team admins can disable team privacy for their teams so that teams can be visible to the entire organization.
:::
