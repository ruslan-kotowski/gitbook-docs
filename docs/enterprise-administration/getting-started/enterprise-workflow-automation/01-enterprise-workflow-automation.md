Easily manage your Miro Enterprise plan at scale. Use integrations with low-code or no-code solutions like ServiceNow Automation Engine, Okta Workflows. More integrations coming soon.

### Why use workflow automation?

Workflow automation solutions allow you to automate repetitive and multistep admin tasks without needing the deep technical knowledge:

- End to end onboarding of users and seamless off-boarding of users
- Managing day-to-day collaboration lifecycle around creating teams and adding members
- Ensuring the sharing, discoverability and invitation settings at team and board level are in line with the company policy

### What workflow automation solutions are supported?

Miro currently has or is going to have integrations with the following services:

- [ServiceNow Automation Engine](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/application/dd64b2f9c370201021f5e81aa840ddb9/3.0.2)
