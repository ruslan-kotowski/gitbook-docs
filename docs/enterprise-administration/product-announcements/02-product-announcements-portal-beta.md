Track the status of upcoming releases with our Product announcements portal. Company Admins can effortlessly monitor the status of new and upcoming Miro features and plan accordingly for their organizations.

## How to access and use the portal

Accessing the portal is simple. [Sign in](https://miro.com/login/) to Miro and navigate to the [Product announcements portal](../../product-announcements) within our Help Center. We recommend bookmarking the portal for quick reference in the future.

New features and functionality from the Miro Product Roadmap will be announced on the portal two weeks before release. Quickly identify relevant updates by tailoring your viewing experience. You can filter announcements based on **sections** and **release status.**

**Sections**

- Canvas and collaboration
- Structure and automation
- Diagramming
- Product and service innovation
- Enterprise foundation
- Enterprise Guard

**Release status**

- Coming soon: The feature will be rolling out soon.
- Rolling out: The feature is gradually becoming available to Enterprise Plan users.
- Launched: The feature is available as either Public Beta or General Availability.

## Troubleshooting

If you’re having trouble accessing the portal, first ensure that you're logged in as a Company Admin in your Miro Enterprise Plan instance. If problems persist, [contact Miro Support](../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/06-contacting-miro-support.md#how-to-contact-miro-support). Access to the portal is currently limited to Company Admins.

If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your dedicated Miro account team. You can also use our [feedback form](https://coda.io/form/Feedback-form-Product-announcements-portal_dBUt3rLP4xW) to submit any suggestions or comments directly to the Product announcement portal team.
