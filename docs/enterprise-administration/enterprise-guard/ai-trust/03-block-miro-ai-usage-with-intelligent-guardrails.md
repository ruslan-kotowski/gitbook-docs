Use Intelligent Guardrails to block all AI-powered interactions in Miro when you need to protect sensitive or classified data. This setting disables AI across the workspace while allowing teams to continue working without AI assistance.

**What changes**

- All Miro AI features are disabled, including text generation, image recognition, and smart suggestions.
- Users cannot access, activate, or invoke any AI-powered tools.
- Existing AI-generated content remains on boards. Users can view, move, or manually edit it, but cannot use Miro AI to update it.
- Applies to everyone, including board owners and co-owners.

Learn more in [Intelligent Guardrails overview](../../canvas-25-admin-features/data-classification/01-intelligent-guardrails-overview.md). For setup guidance, see [Define guardrails](../../canvas-25-admin-features/data-classification/05-define-guardrails.md).
