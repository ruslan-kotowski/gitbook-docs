In most enterprises, data is growing exponentially in both volume and complexity. Proper management and governance of Miro boards is critical for multiple reasons, including:

- **Proactively adhering to industry-specific regulations or internal organization guidelines** that mandate the retention of certain content for a set period, which might require you to keep specific Miro boards for up to a certain number of days or months.
- **Minimizing risks in scenarios like legal disputes or security breaches** by securely and permanently deleting outdated Miro boards that are no longer obligatory to maintain.
- **Maintaining organizational efficiency** by ensuring team members have access only to relevant information.

Our Content Lifecycle Management feature allows you to configure and manage the following:

- [Trash settings](../../canvas-25-admin-features/content-lifecycle-management/04-trash-overview.md)
- [Retention policies](../../canvas-25-admin-features/content-lifecycle-management/05-retention-overview.md)
- [Disposition policies](../../canvas-25-admin-features/content-lifecycle-management/03-disposition-overview.md)

The **Content Lifecycle Management Overview** page provides a summary of all retention and disposition information at a glance. Additionally, you can also configure retention and disposition policies from the **Overview** page.
