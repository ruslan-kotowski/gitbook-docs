You can allow only admins, or both board owners and admins to restore deleted boards by performing the following steps:

1. Go to your [Miro settings](https://miro.com/app/settings).
2. Under **Enterprise Guard,** click **Content lifecycle**.
   ![trash_settings.png](images/21016968160146_trash_settings.png)
   *Figure 2: Content lifecycle > Configure who can restore boards*
3. Under **Deleted boards permissions**, in the list beside **Restore boards**, select the appropriate users type.

## Configure who can permanently delete boards

You can allow only admins, or both board owners and admins to permanently delete boards by performing the following steps:

1. Go to your [Miro settings](https://miro.com/app/settings).
2. Under **Enterprise Guard,** click **Content lifecycle**.
   ![trash_settings.png](images/21016968160146_trash_settings.png)
   *Figure 3: Content lifecycle > Configure who can permanently delete boards*
3. Under **Deleted boards permissions**, in the list beside **Delete permanently**, select the appropriate users type.
