**Are team members notified when a board is automatically and permanently deleted from the Trash?**

No. Team members are not notified when a board is automatically and permanently deleted from the Trash.

**Can I recover a permanently deleted board from the Trash?**

No. You cannot recover a permanently deleted board from the Trash.

**Can I avoid a board from being automatically and permanently deleted?**

This is not possible in the Beta release.

**Are there public APIs available to permanently delete boards from the Trash?**

Public APIs are not supported in the Beta release.
