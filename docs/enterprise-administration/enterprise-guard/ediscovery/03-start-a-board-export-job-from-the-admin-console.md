1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click Enterprise Guard, and then click **eDiscovery**.
3. Click the **Cases** tab.
   The list of cases appear.
4. Click the case that contains one or more legal holds you want to export.
   The list of legal holds belonging to the case appears.
5. You can start a board export in one of the following ways:
   - From the Legal hold list view: On the Legal hold list page, click the **Export Content** button at the top of the page, and then click the specific legal hold to export all related boards.
     A dialog box appears. Proceed to step 6.
   - From the legal hold details page: On the Legal hold list page, click the legal hold name to open it, and then click the Export button at the top of the page.
     A dialog box appears. Proceed to step 6.
6. In the **Export name** box, enter an export name for this board export job.
7. In the **Format** list, select the file format that you want to export the boards to. You can export to an SVG, PDF, or HTML file. By default, the format is set to SVG.
8. (Optional) To include a [content log](../../canvas-25-admin-features/ediscovery/06-content-logs-overview.md) for each board in the export job, tick **Include content logs**.
9. Click **Export**.
   The export job appears in the **Exports** tab of the case page. For information on monitoring export jobs and viewing the export status, [see this article](../../canvas-25-admin-features/ediscovery/07-monitor-board-export-jobs-and-view-status-from-the-admin-console.md).
