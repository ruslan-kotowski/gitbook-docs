1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click Enterprise Guard, and then click **eDiscovery**.
3. Click the **Cases** tab.
   The list of cases appear.
4. Open the case where the export was started.
5. Click the **Exports** tab.
6. Review the list of export jobs:
   - See job name, status, and number of boards included.
   - View real-time updates as boards are processed.
7. (Optional) Click **Filters** to filter export jobs by the following criteria:
   - **Export status**
     Filters by jobs that are in progress, cancelled, or finished.
   - **Created by**
     Filters by job owner.
8. Click an export job to view details:
   - Boards included in the export job.
   - Classification, owner, export state.
   - Export size and download availability. For more information on downloading exported boards, [see this article](../../canvas-25-admin-features/ediscovery/10-download-exported-boards.md).
9. (Optional) To cancel an export job, click the three-dots on the right (**...**), and select **Cancel export**.
