Sensitive Content Admins can now easily enable or disable specific labels when configuring Data Discovery scans, helping tailor results to your organization’s needs. For example, you can choose to focus only on GDPR labels. Once you save your scanning preferences, scans are automatically updated to reflect the data type you've enabled or disabled. This gives Admins greater control over scan outputs and helps reduce noise from irrelevant findings. By default, all data types/sensitivity labels are enabled.

To manage data type scanning, performing the following steps:

:::note
- To enable or disable labels for data discovery scanning, you must have the [Sensitive Content Admin role](../../enterprise-subscription-management/enterprise-guard-overview/03-understand-admin-roles-and-their-privileges.md). To request for the Sensitive Content Admin role, contact your Company Admin.
- You cannot disable labels used for data classification as disabling them might impact automatic classification and guardrails applied to content. Remove them from the classification criteria before disabling them.
:::

1. Go to your [Miro settings](https://miro.com/app/settings).
2. On the left pane, under **Enterprise Guard,** click **Data discovery**.
3. On the **Data discovery** page, click the **Configuration** tab.
4. If you want to enable or disable privacy-related data types/ labels, on the **Privacy-related** section, click the **Manage** button.
   If you want to enable or disable business-sensitive data types/labels, on the **Business-sensitive** section, click the **Manage** button.
   A side panel appears.
5. To disable a data types/sensitivity label: Click to clear the check box for the data type/label.
   To enable a data types/sensitivity label: Click to select the check box for the data type/label.
6. Click the **Apply changes** button.
   Updates can take up to 24 hours.
