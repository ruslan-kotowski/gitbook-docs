Business-sensitive data discovery empowers Sensitive Content admins to seamlessly identify, classify, and secure critical business data—without needing to know exactly what to search for. With  a single toggle click, Sensitive Content admins can leverage Miro's built-in machine learning labels to efficiently automate the identification and labeling of common categories of sensitive business information that belong to either of the following categories:

- Technical designs, including architectural and data flows, data models, security architecture.
- Customer information, including customer licensing, account renewals, usage metrics, account pricing, flexible licensing.
- Company strategy, including company OKRs, planning and goals, company objectives, annual roadmaps, key responsibilities.

## Key benefits

- **Ease of use:** Miro’s built-in labels are pre-configured and ready to use, allowing admins to effortlessly scan for business-sensitive information without requiring prior knowledge of specific keywords. This removes the need for extensive setup or customization, ensuring a simple and efficient experience for admins.
- **Scalability:** Designed for large-scale operations, Miro’s built-in labels ensure seamless identification and classification of sensitive content, even across extensive volumes of Miro boards. This automated approach ensures sensitive data remains securely managed, regardless of the scale.
