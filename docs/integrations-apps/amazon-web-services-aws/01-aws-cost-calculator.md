The AWS Cost Calculator app provides a straightforward way to estimate AWS service costs directly on your Miro boards. It's especially useful for project managers and IT professionals, streamlining cost estimations for efficient planning.

:::note
(Enterprise) AWS Cost Calculator is available only with Advanced licenses. For more information, see [Understanding Enterprise licenses](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/05-understanding-enterprise-licenses.md).
:::

## How to set up and use the AWS Cost Calculator

1. Install the [AWS Cost Calculator](https://miro.com/marketplace/aws-cost-calculator/) from the Miro Marketplace.
2. Open the Miro board where you want to calculate your AWS costs.
3. Click the **Tools, Media and Integrations** (**+**) in the [creation](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/05-toolbars.md#creation-toolbar) toolbar and search for ‘AWS Cost Calculator’.
4. Select **AWS Cost Calculator**. The AWS Cost Calculator panel will open on the left side of the board.
5. Click and drag to select all AWS icons in your diagram. The cost calculator will automatically populate with all of the AWS icons within your diagram.
6. For each AWS icons, click **Edit** to specify details like Region and Storage type, then click **Save service selection**.
7. After editing all icons, click **Calculate** to see your total AWS costs.
8. To better understand your costs, use the top tabs and dropdown menu to filter costs by time frame (**Hour**, **Month**, **Year**) and **Region**.

## Tips for accurate calculations

- **Be specific**: The more details you provide, the more accurate your cost estimate will be.
- **Review regularly**: AWS prices can change. Regularly check and update your estimates, and always confirm actual costs with AWS directly.

## Provide feedback on the AWS Cost Calculator

Your feedback is crucial for us to improve the AWS Cost Calculator and make it more useful for your needs.

1. Open the **AWS Cost Calculator** panel in your Miro board.
2. Click the feedback icon located in the bottom-right of the panel to rate your experience.
3. In the open text field, add any additional feedback, including which AWS icons you would like to see supported in future updates.
