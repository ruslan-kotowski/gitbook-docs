Get access to free high-quality images and photos and use them for any of your projects. Install the plugin and enjoy **Unsplash** images right on your board.

### Installing the app

You can install the plugin from [Marketpalce](../integrations-basics/04-how-to-install-apps.md) or right from your board: click the arrows on the creation toolbar, find Unsplash, and select **Add**.

:::warning
Non-admin users can't install the app if it's not allowed in the **Apps & Integrations** settings.
:::

![Unsplash_on_the_toolbar.jpg](images/21017429276562_Unsplash%20on%20the%20toolbar.jpg)
*Unsplash on the toolbar*

### Adding files

1. Click the **Unsplash**icon on the toolbar.
2. Choose an image from the suggested ones or use the search box to find something special.
3. Click an image or drag and drop it to the board.

![using_Unsplash_in_Miro.gif](images/21017429280018_using%20Unsplash%20in%20Miro.gif)
*Adding images with Unsplash*

You can rotate the image, change the size, update, download, crop, [lock](../../using-miro/working-on-the-board/17-locking-content-on-the-board.md) it, and go to the image source.

![editing_am_image_from_Unsplash.gif](images/21017416197010_editing%20am%20image%20from%20Unsplash.gif)
*Modifying an image added from Unsplash to Miro*

:::note
There are also many other ways you can add an image to your Miro board: upload from your device, Google Drive, IconFinder, or other sources. [Learn more](../../using-miro/working-on-the-board/07-ways-to-add-content.md).
:::
