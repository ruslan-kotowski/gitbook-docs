The Miro app for Webex enables teams to build, co-create, and connect in one seamless experience. The integration combines Miro’s infinite whiteboard with Webex for the most optimal in-meeting collaboration experience. This experience is available for all Webex users across Webex Messaging and Webex Meetings.

- **Miro for Webex Meetings:** Open new or existing Miro boards during Webex Meetings to co-create, brainstorm, and collaborate together with your team in real time
- **Miro for Webex Messaging:** Add live collaborative Miro boards as tabs in Webex team spaces to capture ideas together with your team without toggling between apps. Access existing boards or create a new one for you and your Webex team with the right access settings. Unregistered users or those not logged in can collaborate easily as visitors in Miro in the Webex tab

## Miro for Webex Meetings

### How to open Miro in your Webex Meeting

In your Webex Meeting on the Webex desktop app, click on the **Apps** button to see a list of available apps.

![apps_in_a_Webex_meeting.jpg](images/21016928785554_apps%20in%20a%20Webex%20meeting.jpg)
*Opening Apps in a Webex meeting*

Here, you will see the option to select Miro. Click on the Miro icon to add it to your meeting.

![Miro_among_apps.jpg](images/21016941750418_Miro%20among%20apps.jpg)
*Miro on the list of apps*

Now, you will be presented with the Sign-in window, through which you can access all of your existing Miro boards.

![Webex_Sign_in_to_Miro.jpg](images/21016941760530_Webex%20Sign%20in%20to%20Miro.jpg)
 *Prompt to sign in to Miro*

Once you sign in, you will be able to see the option to create a new, blank board or select from your existing boards.

![Miro_board_picker_inside_Webex.jpg](images/21016941752210_Miro%20board%20picker%20inside%20Webex.jpg)
*Miro board picker inside Webex*

Once you select the desired Miro board, you will have the option to select permissions for your team. You can choose from edit, comment, view, or private permissions, which will require others to sign in to Miro to access the board.

![setting_embed_sharing_settings.jpg](images/21016928792210_setting%20embed%20sharing%20settings.jpg)
*Setting up access settings for an embedded Miro board*

Now, your Miro board has been added to the meeting. Click on **Open together** to allow everyone else to see and access the board.

![open_together_button.jpg](images/21016941755154_open%20together%20button.jpg)
*Open together button will allow everyone on the meeting to see the board*

If you need more space to create, you can pop out Miro into a separate tab.

![Miro_board_in_a_separate_tab_in_a_Wenex_meeting.jpg](images/21016941756946_Miro%20board%20in%20a%20separate%20tab%20in%20a%20Wenex%20meeting.jpg)
*Miro board opened in a separate tab on the Webex meeting*

If you are done working in Miro, you can click on **Stop session** to go back to participant-only view.

![stop_session.jpg](images/21016928800146_stop%20session.jpg)
*Click **Stop session** to switch back to participant view*

### How to use the integration

**Admin controls**

Webex users will automatically have access to Miro in the Webex Meeting space without the need for pre-approval. *Access to Miro’s app can be turned off through the Webex admin controls.*

**Visitor experience**

If someone on your team does not have a Miro profile, they can still collaborate on the Miro board as a visitor. To do so, you can select edit, comment or viewing permissions before opening the board in your Webex Meeting.

**Collaboration experience**

- When a Miro board is added to an active Webex Meeting and the meeting organizer has clicked on **Open together**, all participants will have the ability to view and edit the Miro board
- If the meeting organizer clicks on **Stop session**, all participants will lose access to the board

**Collaboration with outside organizations**

- If your organization admin has enabled the Miro app and you are working in Webex with another organization that does not have apps enabled, the outside organization will still be able to see and participate in Miro
- If your organization admin does not have the Miro app enabled, the outside organization will not be able to access Miro in Webex

### How to disable Miro in Webex Meetings

By default, Webex users will have access to Miro as an Embedded App. If you don't want users to access Miro in Webex Meetings, you can disable it in the Admin panel. Keep in mind, disabling Embedded Apps will disable Miro including all other Embedded Apps for your organization.

1. From the customer view at [https://admin.webex.com](https://admin.webex.com/), go to **Apps**.
2. Under **Embedded Apps**, select the toggle button to disable all apps. This will remove Embedded Apps access for all apps (including Miro) and for all users of your organization.

## Miro for Webex Messaging

### Add Miro to Webex Messaging

Read more about Miro tab setting controls on the [Webex Help Center.](https://help.webex.com/n83vamf/Webex-Miro-Integration-for-Webex)

#### User instruction

1. In Webex, go to **Messaging** and choose a one-to-one or group space.
2. At the top of the space, click the **+Apps** tab. In the **Add an App** window, click **Miro**, and then **Continue**.
3. Sign in to Miro or create a new profile.
4. Select the board you want to add to the Webex Messaging space - or create a new board.
5. Select the permissions you want your team members to have.
6. Your Miro board is now part of the Webex Messaging space.

**Admin controls**

Webex users will automatically have access to the Webex App Hub in the Webex App to add Miro to their Webex team space. Access to Miro’s app can be turned off through the Webex admin controls.

### How to use the integration

**Sign-in experience**

Existing Miro users can leverage the full experience within Webex. After signing in and connecting Miro to Webex, team members can create a new board or choose an existing board.

Define the appropriate access settings for your Miro board within Webex. You can choose between four options: Anyone in Webex can edit/comment/view or Private (which means that the sharing settings will be as on the Miro side).

**Collaboration experience**

When a Miro board is added as a tab in your Webex team space and access settings are configured, all team members within the Webex space will be able to interact with the Miro board.

To collaborate as a registered user, team members can either sign in or create a Miro profile. Otherwise, members in your Webex space can collaborate as [a visitor](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/08-collaboration-with-visitors.md) on a board.

### How to disable Miro in Webex Messaging

By default, members can add tabs and apps with Miro boards. [Read more about Webex Tab controls](https://help.webex.com/n9yjwceb/Enable-or-Disable-Webex-Tabs). If you don't want users to access the web through the embedded browser in Webex Messaging, you can turn off the toggle in Control Hub.

1. From the customer view at [https://admin.webex.com](https://admin.webex.com/), go to **Apps**.
2. Under **Tabs**, turn off the toggle switch.

:::note
By turning off the toggle switch, you're only removing Apps from view. If you turn it on again later, all Apps and tabs that had already been added to spaces are restored.
:::

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use Miro in the Webex Meeting Breakout Sessions?**

This is not supported at the moment.

**Can I use the Webex integration on tablet or mobile?**

The Webex integration does not currently work on tablet or mobile.
