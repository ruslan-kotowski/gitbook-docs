Integration with Box allows you to use files from your Box account and add them to Miro boards for further discussion and collaboration. You can hold a discussion on the board, mark up feedback on top of your images and documents, mention people and iterate faster.

### Installing the plugin

To start using the plugin, you need to install the plugin on the team level and to connect your Box account to Miro. The plugin can be installed from the Marketplace or from a Miro board.

:::warning
Non-admin users can't install the app if it's not allowed in the **Apps & Integrations** settings.
:::

Go to [Miro Marketplace](https://miro.com/marketplace/box/?backUrl=%2Fmarketplace%2F) and click **Get App.** You'll be suggested to choose a team to install the app for. Select a team to finish the installation.

![install_Box_plugin.jpg](images/21019591350930_install%20Box%20plugin.jpg)
*Choosing a team for the Box plugin*

To install the app from a board:

1. In the Creation bar, select **Tools, Media and Integrations** (**+**).The **Tools, Media and Integrations** panel opens.
2. In the **Tools** tab, search for **Uploads**.
3. Within the **Uploads** panel, select **Box**.

![uploads-entry-point.png](images/21537454250514_uploads-entry-point.png)
*The Box icon on the toolbar*

Click **Get app** on the pop-up window and the plugin will be installed for the team.

To connect your Box account to Miro, choose **Box** on the **Upload** menu. If this is the first time you use Box with Miro you will see a pop-up menu asking you to authorize your Box account.

![authorize_box.jpg](images/21019591352338_authorize%20box.jpg)
*Box authentication window*

You can also connect or disconnect a Box account on your profile [Integrations page](https://miro.com/app/account/profile/integrations/).

![connect_Box.jpg](images/21019591356946_connect%20Box.jpg)
*Connecting Box to Miro*

### Adding files

To add files to the board, open the **Upload** menu on the toolbar, click **Box**, select documents or pictures from your Box account, and click **Choose**.

![adding_files_from_Box.jpg](images/21019535297298_adding%20files%20from%20Box.jpg)
*Adding files from Box to Miro boards*

To refresh your files on the board, click the **Update** button on a file.

![update_a_Box_file.jpg](images/21019535299218_update%20a%20Box%20file.jpg)
*Refreshing Box files imported to Miro boards*

### Disabling the plugin

If you'd like to uninstall the plugin for a team, go to the Team settings, find Box in the **Apps & Integrations** section, and click **Uninstall for team**.

![uninstall_Box.jpg](images/21019591362962_uninstall%20Box.jpg)
*Uninstalling Box in Team settings*

To disconnect your Box account go to the profile [Integrations page](https://miro.com/app/account/profile/integrations/) and click **Log Out**.

![disconnect_Box.jpg](images/21019591364498_disconnect%20Box.jpg)
*Disconnecting Box from Miro*
