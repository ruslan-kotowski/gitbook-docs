With IconFinder, you can create mockups, schemes, mind maps, and organize visual brainstorming sessions. **Iconfinder** icon set is used by millions of designers, developers, and other creative professionals all over the world to make designs and projects perfectly structured, visually appealing, and clear.

### Using icons

If you do not have the database on the toolbar, search for it in the **Apps** menu and click **Add** next to the icon set.

:::warning
Non-admin users can't install the apps if it's not allowed in the **Apps & Integrations** settings.
:::

![Iconfinder_on_the_toolbar.jpg](images/21016057485586_Iconfinder%20on%20the%20toolbar.jpg)
*Iconfinder on the toolbar*

To find an icon:

1. Open Iconfinder on the creation toolbar.
2. You will immediately see a variety of icons. Choose one of them or type in a search word to find the icon you need.
3. Click the icon or drag and drop it anywhere on the board. You can change its size and rotate it.

![using_Iconfinder_in_Miro.gif](images/21016057487378_using%20Iconfinder%20in%20Miro.gif)
*Adding Iconfinder icons*

:::warning
Please note, Premium Iconfinder icons are not available in Miro.
:::

:::tip
Other icon packages such as **AWS, Azure, Cisco** are available in the [Shape library](../../using-miro/essential-tools/11-shapes.md) for Business, Enterprise, and Education plan users.
:::

### Frequently asked questions

1. *Can I pay for Premium Iconfinder icons to make them available in Miro?*
   - No, this will not activate premium icons on the picker on the Miro side.
2. *Can I add a custom set of icons to the Iconfinder picker in Miro?*- No, this is not currently possible.
3. *Can I mirror an icon?*
   - No, but you can rotate it by dragging the arrow icon in the bottom-left corner.
4. *Can I add text to an icon?*
   - You can add text using the [text tool](../../using-miro/essential-tools/16-text.md), [select the icon and the text](../../using-miro/working-on-the-board/10-working-with-objects.md), and [group them](../../using-miro/working-on-the-board/08-structuring-board-content.md#grouping).
5. *Can I change the color of icons?*
   - You cannot change the color of icons from Iconfinder, but you can change the color of icons from the [Wireframes library](../../using-miro/miroverse/prototyping/02-prototyping-library.md).
