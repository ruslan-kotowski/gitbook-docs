Using physical Post-it® Notes is extremely helpful for brainstorming, ideation, or personal reminders. Digitize your work by using the integration between the Post-it® App and Miro. The integration will allow you to export Post-it® Notes created and captured via Post-it® to Miro boards.

To learn more about the Post-it® App, visit [this page](https://www.post-it.com/3M/en_US/post-it/ideas/app/faq/). Install the mobile app and easily create or capture your notes.

### How to export Post-it® Notes to Miro

:::note
Only text notes can be exported to Miro using the Post-it® App integration. Notes without text will be exported blank.
:::

1. After capturing your Post-it® Notes with the Post-it® App, select the Post-it®  board of notes that you would like to transfer to Miro and press **Export** in the bottom-right corner. Select **Miro board**.
2. You will see the list of your notes and you will be able to make edits before exporting the notes to Miro. Press **Next** to continue. The app will ask you to choose a team where the Miro board will be created. Select **Add a new team**. *![Post-it_and_Mirro.gif](images/21017318674706_Post-it%20and%20Mirro.gif)
   Exporting from Post-it® App to Miro*
3. You will be redirected to the in-app browser and will see the option to sign up for Miro. If you're already a Miro user, press **Sign in** at the top right corner of the screen and enter your credentials.
4. Once you sign up/sign in, you will be brought to the web window which will display all of your Miro teams. Choose a team where you wish to create a board with Post-it® Notes and press **Install & authorize**. You can only link teams that you're a [full member](../../getting-started/start-here/05-roles-in-miro.md) of.
   > ✏️ You can [export your notes to multiple Miro teams](#linking-and-unlinking-miro-teams).

   ![link_a_Miro_team.jpg](images/21017318675602_link%20a%20Miro%20team.jpg)
   *Linking a Miro team*
   If you're not allowed to install apps for a team, the **Install & authorize** button will be greyed out for you.
   If the Post-It app is restricted in an Enterprise team that you'd like to export notes to, you will be able to send an approval request to Company Admins.
   *![approval_request.jpg](images/21017348522898_approval%20request.jpg)
   Sending a request to install the app to Admins*
5. Once the Miro team has been linked, the **Export** button becomes available. Press **Export** to export your Post-It notes to Miro. Ta-dah! Note that this will create a new board in your team. At the moment, it's not possible to export stickies to an existing Miro board.
   You can now open the new board in Miro or share the board link with others.
   ![export_a_board.gif](images/21017318679698_export%20a%20board.gif)
   *Exporting a board to Miro*
   Pressing **Open board in Miro** will get you into the in-app browser. Note that Miro mobile browser version has *restricted functionality* (no editing, viewing only) - feel free to install Miro's [Mobile app](../../getting-started/apps-for-devices/08-mobile-app.md) to get access to the full feature set.
   *For the best experience,* we recommend working on large screen devices such as tablets or laptops, desktops, or office screens.

   Pressing the **Share link** will share the board link with people you've selected. Note that unregistered users will not be able to access the board unless it is [shared publicly](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-boards-via-a-public-link). Registered users who have access to the board via email invitation or [team](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md)/[project](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/16-projects.md)/[company](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-a-board-with-the-entire-company) access will be able to see the board. Check out [this guide](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md) to learn how you can share your Miro boards with other users.

   Note that Miro [Free plan](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md) has a limitation of three active boards: if the newly exported board exceeds this limit, the oldest board will get deactivated. You may need to upgrade your team to unlock unlimited boards. Students and educators can take advantage of our free [Education plan](../../plans-billing/miro-special-pricing/03-education-plan.md) that allows an unlimited number of active boards. Working for a Startup or NPO? Learn more about our complimentary plans: [Miro for Startups Program](../../plans-billing/miro-special-pricing/02-miro-startup-program.md), [NPO subscriptions](../../plans-billing/miro-special-pricing/04-miro-for-non-profits.md).

### Linking and unlinking Miro teams

To add another Miro team,  select **Add a new team** and choose a Miro team on the web pop-up.

![add_a_new_team.jpg](images/21017348517650_add%20a%20new%20team.jpg)
*The option to add a new Miro team to Post-it®*

To unlink a team, go to Post-it® App **settings** >  **Linked services**.

![linked_services.jpg](images/21017318678034_linked%20services.jpg)
*Linked services in Post-it® app settings*

Select  **Unlink** next to the team you'd like to disconnect from the app.

![unlink_a_team.jpg](images/21017318678418_unlink%20a%20team.jpg)
*The option to unlink a Miro team from the Post-it app*

If you need to change the linked *profile*, log out of Miro in your device web browser or clear the browser data. Once you try to link a new Miro team, you will be suggested to log in to Miro again.
