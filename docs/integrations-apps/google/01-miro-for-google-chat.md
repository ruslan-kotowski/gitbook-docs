Create and share Miro boards, get notifications, and more with Miro for Google Chat.

## Install and connect Miro for Google Chat

1. Go to Miro on the [Google Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/miro/1062019541050).
2. Click **Install**.
3. Select the Google account you want to connect to the add-on.
4. When the add-on is installed, you’ll see Miro in the Apps section of Google Chat and receive a welcome message from Miro.
5. Click **Connect**.
6. Select the **Miro team** you’d like to connect.
7. Once done, you’ll be redirected to Google Chat and receive a success message there.

## Commands in Google Chat

- /connect - Connect your Miro account to Google Workspace.
- /disconnect - Disconnect your Miro account from Google Workspace.
- /unsubscribe -  Keep your Miro account connected to Google Workspace, but stop receiving notifications in Google Chat.
- /subscribe - Start receiving notifications in Google Chat.
- /createboard - Create a new Miro board from within the Google Chat. You'll have options to name the board and add a description.
- /help - Get help for how to use the app.

## Miro Notifications in Google Chat

You can access a variety of Miro notifications within Google Chat.

- Comment added to a board or thread you follow.
- You're mentioned in a comment.
- You're invited to a board.
- A user requests access to your board.
- An access request is approved or expires.
- You're invited to a space.
- You're invited to a team.
- A user is requesting access to a team.
