Easily connect Miro to your favorite collaboration tools. Miro offers apps to help you work on the board and connect Miro to other tools.

## Apps overview

In Miro’s Marketplace, you can find several types of apps, either built by Miro or external developers, which are described below:

- Apps to help you save time & work effectively on the board
- Apps to help you connect Miro to other tools
- Apps to help you import content to the Miro board
- Apps for receiving Miro notifications

## Understanding app developers

The Miro Marketplace offers apps that are built by Miro as well as by third-party developers.

### Apps built by Miro

Apps marked as "**by Miro"** in Miro Marketplace have been built by Miro developers and are fully maintained by Miro.

You can get details about the information that the app is going to use from the app installation page.

![install_Asana_cards.jpg](images/21017653354386_install%20Asana%20cards.jpg)*Allowing Asana Cards to read and modify your information*

There are several Miro-built apps that are available for teams by default. They are built by (or on behalf of) Miro to extend Miro functionality. These apps are hosted on Miro’s infrastructure and don’t require any data exchange with third-party services:

- [Chat](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/360017572234-Chat)
- [Timer](../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/16-timer.md)
- [Voting](../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/19-voting.md)
- [Charts](../../using-miro/essential-tools/03-charts.md)
- [Wireframe Library](../../using-miro/miroverse/prototyping/02-prototyping-library.md)
- [Iconfinder](../more-integrations/09-iconfinder.md)
- [User story mapping](../../using-miro/advanced-tools/07-user-story-mapping.md)
- [Timeline Builder](../../using-miro/advanced-tools/06-timeline-builder.md)

### Apps built by 3rd party developers

Apps that are not marked as **by Miro** or **by Miro Labs** have been built by third-party developers. Your usage of any third-party app is subject to separate terms between you and the developer. You can access and review the third party’s Privacy Policy from the app’s page in the Miro Marketplace.

![privacy_policy.gif](images/21017682995986_privacy%20policy.gif)
*Accessing Privacy Policy of a third-party developer*

There are also marketplace apps and integrations that are published in the marketplace which have passed a special Miro review. It's important to note that Miro is not responsible for the performance of these types of apps.

## Integrations that you can embed

You can embed content from some third-party integrations directly onto your Miro board. To learn which integrations you can embed, and how, see [Embed integrations on a Miro board](03-embed-integrations-on-a-miro-board.md).
