Miro for Microsoft Teams allows users to receive real time notifications as well as collaborate on embedded Miro boards in Teams meetings, channels, chats, and calendar invites.

Miro also supports adaptive cards through link unfurling and search message extensions, giving users more context on shared boards and allows quick board access management - all without leaving their Microsoft Teams space.

- [Add a Miro board as a tab](04-add-miro-as-a-tab-in-microsoft-teams.md) in Microsoft Teams meetings, chats, channels and calendar events
- Receive Miro notifications in Microsoft Teams
- Share Miro links as adaptive cards through link unfurling and search message extensions

:::note
Your Admin must enable the Miro app within the Microsoft Teams settings and from the Miro app management settings. Unless Miro is approved, it won't be available for users. Visit the [Miro for Microsoft Teams (admin guide)](01-miro-for-microsoft-teams-admin-guide.md) to see how Teams and Miro Admins can enable the experience for their users.
:::

## Installing the app

1. Fnd Miro in [Microsoft AppSource](https://appsource.microsoft.com/product/Office365/WA104381906) or just follow the [direct link](https://teams.microsoft.com/dl/launcher/launcher.html?url=%2F_%23%2Fl%2Fapp%2F8216e453-3db5-48ee-a3d6-5122f505c8a3&type=app&deeplinkId=660e2c79-d07b-46b9-b56f-473431aa9dd9&directDl=true&msLaunch=true&enableMobilePage=true&suppressPrompt=true).
2. Click **Add** to install the app.

Once you’ve installed the Miro app, you’ll need to connect your account to begin adding boards to tabs and receiving notifications.
As part of the installation process, you can then go to the Miro chat where you can complete app configuration by connecting your account. You will be able to configure Miro notifications.

Learn more about adding Miro boards in Microsoft Teams.

## Setting up notifications in Microsoft Teams

With the help of the app, you can keep track of the following events:

- invitees sign up
- a board is shared with you
- there's a new comment or a reply to your comment on a board
- someone @mentions you in a comment or reply
- receive and approve board access requests

Notifications are received through the Miro bot chat. To set up the Miro bot chat:

1. Connect your account. Type in the **Connect** command and then click **Connect Miro to Microsoft Teams**.
2. In the new tab you'll need to authorize your Miro profile. Once you’ve successfully connected your account, you will see a greeting message from the bot indicating that everything is set up.
3. You will start to get notifications for new comments and mentions.
4. Sharing Miro board links in Microsoft Teams.

Using the Miro for Microsoft Teams app, you can share Miro board links as an adaptive card through:

- Link unfurling
- Search message extensions

Adaptive cards provide users with detailed context about the board, such as title, owner, and last updated. Users can also take actions such as managing board permissions and leveraging [Miro AI](../../../using-miro/miro-ai/03-create-with-ai.md) to summarize key takeaways in a doc.

Miro boards unfurl into an adaptive card automatically when a board link is pasted into the message compose box in Microsoft Teams channels when you have installed and connected the Miro app.

If you have installed the app but not yet connected your Miro account, you’ll see the option to sign in. If you have not yet installed the app, follow the steps [here.](04-add-miro-as-a-tab-in-microsoft-teams.md)

## Frequently asked questions

What should I do if I'm having trouble with the integration?

If you encounter difficulties with the integration, please attempt to reconnect your team via the Miro chat. This often resolves common connectivity issues.

What if reconnecting doesn't solve the problem?

If reconnecting through the Miro chat does not resolve the issue, please contact Miro Support for further assistance. They are equipped to handle a range of technical issues and can provide more specific guidance.
