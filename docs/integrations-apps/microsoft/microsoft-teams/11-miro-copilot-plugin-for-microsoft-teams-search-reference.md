## Miro Copilot plugin use cases and examples

Expand each use case to see an example.

**Roadmapping**

A product team quickly accesses and integrates feedback, design specs, and timelines from previous roadmaps stored in Miro and Office 365. The team can align on the most up-to-date plan without manually searching through disparate documents.

**Example prompt:** *Find timelines from previous product roadmaps on Copilot integration in Miro.*

**Scaled product planning**

During a planning session, a product manager easily retrieves relevant data from past planning sessions, market research reports, and customer feedback across multiple Miro boards, and Office 365 files, ensuring a comprehensive view for decision-making.

**Example prompt:** *Find data from past planning sessions, market research reports, and customer feedback in Miro and Office 365.*

**Research and insights synthesis**

A researcher gathers insights from various research sessions and surveys stored across Miro and Office 365, using Copilot to synthesize data quickly, enabling faster and more accurate conclusions.

**Agile team events**

In a sprint planning meeting, the team quickly accesses historical sprint reviews, backlog items, and user stories across their tools, facilitating efficient prioritization and sprint planning.

**Technical diagramming**

Engineers working on a system architecture diagram can pull in relevant technical specs, past diagrams, and documentation from Miro and Office 365, ensuring that they are working with the latest and most accurate information.

**Journey mapping**

A UX team creates a customer journey map, easily integrating insights from user interviews, previous journey maps, and customer feedback stored across Miro and Office 365, ensuring a holistic view of the customer experience.

**Cloud infrastructure optimization**

A cloud architect accesses cloud architecture diagrams, cost analysis reports, and performance metrics stored across different platforms, helping them quickly identify and implement optimisation opportunities.

**Process design**

A business analyst redesigning a process can swiftly pull in relevant process flows, stakeholder feedback, and compliance documents from Miro and Office 365, ensuring all considerations are factored into the new design.

**Example prompt:** *Find all process flows, stakeholder feedback, and compliance documents related to adding a sub-processor from Miro and Office 365.*

**Process and systems implementation**

During the implementation of a new system, the implementation team can access relevant project plans, training materials, and technical requirements across Miro and Office 365, ensuring a smooth and coordinated rollout.

**Strategy development**

A leadership team develops a new business strategy by accessing past strategic plans, market analyses, and financial data from their dispersed knowledge hubs, allowing for a well-informed and cohesive strategy.

**Goals and OKR management**

A manager tracks and updates OKRs by quickly accessing team performance metrics, progress reports, and feedback across Miro and Office 365, ensuring alignment and accountability.

**Organization design and planning**

HR and leadership teams access org charts, role descriptions, and employee feedback from various sources to design a new organizational structure that aligns with business objectives.

## Example Copilot prompts for searching Miro

The following examples show how a persona can prompt Microsoft Copilot to search Miro:

- **Product manager**
  *Show me the roadmap and feature specs for the 'Q3 Product Launch' project.*
- **UX researcher**
  *Find user feedback and design notes for the ‘User Experience Redesign’ study.*
- **Business Analyst**
  *Search for documents containing the keywords ‘compliance’ and ‘process flows’ for the ‘Onboarding Optimization’ initiative.*
- **Project Manager**
  *Find Miro boards with status updates and timelines for the ‘New CRM Implementation’ project.*
- **Marketing Manager**
  *Retrieve campaign plans and creative briefs for the ‘Holiday Marketing Blitz’ campaign.*
- **Engineer**
  *Show all technical specs and architecture diagrams modified in the past month for the ‘Cloud Migration’ project.*
