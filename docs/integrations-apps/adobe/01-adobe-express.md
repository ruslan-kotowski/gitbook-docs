The Adobe Express integration enables you to seamlessly work on images and videos within Adobe Express without leaving your Miro board. This allows for a more streamlined creative workflow, keeping all your assets and project discussions in one collaborative space.

Give your team two flexible ways to enhance visuals in Miro using Adobe Express:

- **Quick image editor**
  Make fast edits like cropping, object removal, image adjustments, and AI-powered tools without leaving the board. Most tools work without an Adobe login.
- **Full Adobe Express experience**
  Launch the full creative workspace inside Miro to access templates, brand kits, multi-page design, and video tools. Great for teams that need editable, on-brand content.

Both options keep assets connected to your board, making it easy to update visuals, stay aligned, and move work forward in one place.

## Key features

Discover the powerful capabilities of the Adobe Express integration in Miro:

- Play Adobe Express videos directly on your board for quick reviews and feedback.
- Access either a lightweight version for quick image edits or the full version of Adobe Express from the canvas.
- Edit images in a focused mode within Adobe Express, with changes syncing back instantly to Miro upon saving.
- Choose to update existing assets on your board or save new versions (when using the full version) to preserve previous work.
- Keep project timelines, brand guidelines, and creative assets consolidated in one shared space on your Miro board.

## Edit images with the light version of Adobe Express

To edit images already on your Miro board using Adobe Express, follow this procedure:

1. Select an existing image on your Miro canvas.
   The context menu for the image will appear.
2. Click the **Pencil** icon in the context menu.
3. The light version of Adobe Express will launch.
4. Once your edits are complete, click **Save**.
   A confirmation dialogue with image options will open.
5. Select the **File format** from the dropdown menu and click **Save** to instantly sync changes to your Miro board.

The same procedure applies when editing images that were originally added to your canvas from Adobe Express.

## Access the full Adobe Express integration

You can easily access the full Adobe Express integration directly from your Miro Creation toolbar. Follow these steps to get started:

1. Click the **Tools, Media and Integrations** (![Tools, Media and Integrations icon](images/24271064782354_icon-tools.svg)) icon on the Creation toolbar.
   The Tools, Media and Integrations panel will open.
2. In the panel, search for **Adobe Express**.
3. Click on the Adobe Express search result.
   The Adobe Express integration will open directly on your Miro canvas.
4. Make changes and choose from **Save as new** or **Save and replace**.
5. Once projects are added to the board they can be re-accessed through the **pencil icon** or the **Adobe Express icon** in the Context menu.

:::tip
You can tell whether an image on the canvas was originally created in Adobe Express when the Adobe Express icon appears in the context menu.
:::

## Edit videos with Adobe Express

You can also edit video assets originally from Adobe Express on your board using the Adobe Express integration. Here's how:

1. Select an existing Adobe Express video asset on your Miro canvas.
   The context menu for the video will appear.
2. Click the **Pencil** icon in the context menu.
3. Choose one of the available options to edit the video with Adobe Express.

## Collaborate on Adobe Express projects

Learn how to invite others to collaborate on your Adobe Express projects directly from the Miro integration:

1. Open the Adobe Express project from your Miro board.
2. Within the Adobe Express editing interface, click the **Invite** option.
3. A shareable link for your project will be generated.
4. Share this link with your collaborators.

   > ✏️ Only the person who initiated the collaborative session can save the changes back to the Miro board.

:::warning
When someone begins editing an Adobe Express asset on your Miro board, it automatically locks to prevent simultaneous changes. However, if a user edits the project directly in Adobe Express (outside of Miro) while another person is editing it through the Miro integration, the last person to save their changes will override any previous edits.
:::

## FAQ

**Do I need an Adobe Express account to use the feature?**

No account is required for basic image edits like cropping, resizing, or brightness and contrast adjustments. An Adobe Express login is only needed to access generative AI tools or the full Express experience.

**How will I know if an image or video is already linked to an Adobe Express project?**

When you select the image or video asset on your Miro board, you will see the Adobe Express logo in the context menu if it's linked.

**What will others without edit access see?**

Users who do not have edit access to the board will see your images and videos as they would any other asset. However, the option to edit these assets with Adobe Express will not be available to them.

**Why don't my Adobe Express asset titles appear in Miro?**

Titles given to images or videos within Adobe Express are not automatically transferred to Miro. When placed on the Miro board, they will appear as "Untitled asset", but you can rename them directly on the board.

**Why won't my video save to Miro from Adobe Express?**

To work correctly in Miro, videos must be under 30MB in size. If your video exceeds this limit, Miro will display an error message and will not save the video to your board. However, any edits you made to your video will still be saved in Adobe Express, even if the video is too large for Miro.

**Can I enable Adobe Express only for certain teams?**

Not right now. Adobe Express is pre-configured for all Miro users.

**How does the integration work?**

This integration operates as an iFrame embed. This means that the integration simply provides a visual component of the external website inside of Miro, without any form of data sharing between Miro and the external website. Authentication is handled by the website itself.
