Connect your Adobe® Creative Cloud™ account to Miro to upload files directly to your boards for discussion and collaborative feedback. You can comment on projects, mention colleagues, and iterate faster on your creative work within Miro.

## Install and connect the Adobe Creative Cloud app

To use Adobe Creative Cloud with Miro, you first need to install the app for your Miro team and then authorize your Adobe account.

:::warning
Non-admin users can't install the app if it's not allowed in your team's **Apps & Integrations** settings.
:::

**Install the app:**

You can install the app in the following ways:

- **From the Miro Marketplace:** Go to the [Miro Marketplace](../integrations-basics/04-how-to-install-apps.md), find Adobe Creative Cloud, and click **Connect**. Choose the team you want to install the app for and click **Install & authorize**.

_Selecting a team when installing the Adobe CC plugin from Marketplace_

- **From a Miro board:** On an open board, click **More tools** (the plus icon) on the Creation toolbar, then select **Adobe CC**. If the app isn't installed yet, you will be prompted to **Get app**. Follow the instructions to install it for your team.

**Connect your account:**

Once installed, or when you first attempt to use the integration, you need to connect your Adobe Creative Cloud account. Select **Adobe CC** from the **Upload** menu (accessible from the Creation toolbar by clicking **More tools** , then **Adobe CC**).

_Adobe CC icon on the creation toolbar_

If you are not already signed in to Adobe Creative Cloud in your browser, or if Miro is not yet authorized, a pop-up window will appear. Sign in with your Adobe ID and grant access to Miro.

_Signing in to Adobe to authorize Miro_

## Add and update Adobe Creative Cloud files on your board

After setting up the integration, you can easily add files from Adobe Creative Cloud to your Miro boards and keep them synchronized with the latest versions.

**Add files:**

To add files to your board, select **Adobe CC** from the **Upload** menu. In the Adobe Creative Cloud window that appears, browse and select the desired files, then click **Open**. The files will be added to your Miro board.

_Uploading files to Miro from Adobe Creative Cloud_

**Update files:**

To sync any changes made in Creative Cloud to a file already on your Miro board, select the file on the board. A context menu will appear; click the **Update** button to refresh the file with the latest version from Adobe Creative Cloud.

_Updating an Adobe CC file on a Miro board_

## Uninstall the Adobe Creative Cloud app

If you no longer need the integration, Company Admins or Team Admins can uninstall the Adobe Creative Cloud app from the team settings.

To do this, go to **Team settings** by clicking your team name in the top-left corner of your dashboard, then navigate to **Apps & Integrations**. Find the Adobe Creative Cloud app in the list of installed apps and click **Uninstall for team**.

_Uninstalling the Adobe CC plugin in Team settings_
