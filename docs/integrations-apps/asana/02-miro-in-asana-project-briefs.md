**Key Features**

- Embed Miro boards within Asana with the ability to give view, comment, or editing permissions to others
- Interact with content on the board, including adding comments, text, shapes, or using the drawing tool to leave feedback
- Easily zoom in and out to see as much of the board as you want

### How to install and authorize the Miro app in Asana

Authorize and install the Miro app from the [Asana Marketplace](https://app.asana.com/-/install_platform_ui_app?app_id=1201153108289813).

### How to embed Miro within Asana Project Briefs

Open the Miro board you’d like to embed. Click the share button on the top right. Click **Copy board link** in the dialog that appears.

![copy_board_link.jpg](images/21017606106898_copy%20board%20link.jpg)
*The option to copy the board link*

You can also copy the board link from the browser address bar or from your dashboard.

![copy_link_from_dashboard.jpg](images/21017606109586_copy%20link%20from%20dashboard.jpg)
*Copy link on the board menu*

In Asana, open the project where you’d like to embed your board. Go to the **Overview** tab.

In the **Key resources** section, click **Create project brief**. If there’s already a Project Brief, click anywhere on the title.

![create_project_brief.jpg](images/21017593125010_create%20project%20brief.jpg)
*The option to create a Project Brief in Asana*

Click the place in the Brief you’d like to embed. Paste in the URL and a preview of the board will expand below it. Click the preview to load the board.

![board_preview.jpg](images/21017606114962_board%20preview.jpg)
*Miro board preview in an Asana Project Brief*
