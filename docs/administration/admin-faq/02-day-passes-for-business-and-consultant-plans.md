We officially stopped support for the Occasional license on Consultant and Business plans on the 17th of December 2021.

All non-team users were automatically converted to [guests](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md), however, Admins and members with the Occasional license are now on Free Restricted license. It means that they retain their access to all boards they have access to but can only view/comment the content within the plan and cannot edit boards.

The Free Restricted role is temporary. Company Admins can transition Free Restricted members and Admins in Team settings or [**Company** settings on Consultant plan](../get-started-as-a-miro-admin/05-manage-business-plan.md#organization-level-settings)  > **Active users** in the following ways:

- **Upgrade to Full members (recommended)**
  - Members and Admins will retain edit access to boards
- Convert to [guests](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md)
  - Members and Admins will only retain access to boards explicitly shared with them via email
  - They might lose access to [team shared boards](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md), and can only access boards [explicitly shared with them](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email)
  - Admins will not be able to manage the plan and teams
- [Remove](../user-management/08-remove-users.md) (not recommended)
