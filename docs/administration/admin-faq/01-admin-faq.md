Below are the answers to popular questions on Admin rights.

1. *I want to be promoted to Admin. How can I do that?*
   - Reach out to the current team Admin and ask them to [change your role](../user-management/06-how-to-manage-admin-roles.md). If you don't know who the current Admin is, open **Admin console** > **Users** > **All users** to find out.
2. *How do I grant Admin rights to a teammate?*
   - Follow these steps: [How to manage Admin rights](../user-management/06-how-to-manage-admin-roles.md).
   > ⚠️ [Education plan](../../plans-billing/miro-special-pricing/03-education-plan.md) allows one Admin per team. Note that on [Education plan](../../plans-billing/miro-special-pricing/03-education-plan.md), you can only grant Admin rights to another user when [leaving the team](../../using-miro/managing-your-profile/06-how-to-leave-a-team.md).
3. *What's the difference between Billing Admin and Admin?*
   - Any user is made Billing Admin when they upgrade their team. That does not automatically give themAdmin rights. The Billing Admin is the cardholder and they have access to **Billing** settings (as long as the Billing admin is a member of the paid team) as they're the one paying for the subscription. They can buy or remove seats, cancel the subscription and re-activate it, request refunds. However, they *cannot* remove users, manage permissions, apps and don't have access to other settings if they don't have *Admin* rights.
   Billing Admins can request Admin rights: go to Team settings > **Active users**, click the three dots next to your name, and choose **Send request**.
4. *I don't want to take a paid seat on the team, but I want to be the payer and receive billing information.*
   - You can upgrade the team and [leave it](../../using-miro/managing-your-profile/06-how-to-leave-a-team.md) or ask the admin to [remove you](../user-management/08-remove-users.md). Payment receipts are sent to the [email address stated in the Billing section of your team settings](../../plans-billing/manage-your-subscription-and-plan/01-manage-your-subscription.md). A Team Admin can provide any address there thus you don't have to take a seat in this case. To change billing email open **Admin console** > **Billing** > **Overview** > **Billing actions** > **Change email**.
5. *I want to change the credit card attached to the subscription to a new one. How can I do that?*
   - This can be done by one of the Admins or by the Billing Admin if they follow [this guide.](../../plans-billing/manage-your-subscription-and-plan/01-manage-your-subscription.md)
6. *How to leave the team without the help of the Admin (they left the company, I can't contact them)?*
   - Follow the steps suggested in the article: [How to leave a team](../../using-miro/managing-your-profile/06-how-to-leave-a-team.md).
7. *I only see 5 boards, but in the team settings, it says that there are 8 of them. Why can't I, as an Admin, see and manage the rest of the boards?*
   - If you don't see some boards it means they have a private status and only their owners can see them. You can reach out to your team members and suggest they [share the boards with you.](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md)
   On Enterprise plan, it is possible to enable [Content Admin permissions](../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/12-content-admin-permissions.md) to manage all boards and projects in the Enterprise subscription.
8. *There are several users in the team who left the company. How can I remove them from the team and reduce the number of paid licenses?*
   - To do so [remove users](../user-management/08-remove-users.md) and reduce the team size following [this guide](../../plans-billing/billing-and-payments/04-miro-billing.md). To delete users on Enterprise plan, check out [this guide](../../enterprise-administration/user-management/10-remove-users-on-enterprise-plan.md).
9. *Users of my team invited new members and now I have to pay more. I don't want that.*
   - Follow the steps from the article [Accidentally added seats](../user-management/04-manage-extra-licenses.md).
10. *How can I cancel my subscription?*
    - Use this guide: [How to cancel your subscription](../../plans-billing/manage-your-subscription-and-plan/06-cancel-your-miro-subscription.md).
11. *I want new users with my corporate domain to join my team automatically.*
    - Check the article: [Manage team signup mode](../team-management/02-manage-team-signup-mode.md). Enterprise and Business plan users with [Single sign-on](../../enterprise-administration/security-integrations/single-sign-on-sso/09-single-sign-on-sso.md) enabled can also configure Just in Time provisioning.
    On Enterprise plan, it is also possible to capture corporate users via [Domain control](../../enterprise-administration/canvas-25-admin-features/domain-control/01-domain-control.md). Learn more about [user provisioning on Enterprise plan](../../enterprise-administration/user-management/13-user-provisioning-on-enterprise-plan.md).
12. *I want to delete my profile. What happens to my team after that?*
    - If you are the only member there, the team will be deleted together with your profile. If there are other members, the team will stay and your profile will simply be removed from it and then deleted. If you are the only Admin in the team, then after your profile's removal the first team member alphabetically will become the new Admin.
13. *Our Admin left the company and I want to get the Admin rights. What should I do?*
    - Follow the steps from this guide: [My Miro Admin left the company](../user-management/07-my-miro-admin-left-the-company.md).
14. *What if I see “The account must have at least one admin” error message when trying to remove former **Company Admin** (applicable for Enterprise, Business plans)?*
    - It's most likely that the user is the only Admin in one of the teams within the subscription. You may add yourself to these teams and grant Team Admin rights to yourself (so that in these teams there are 2 Team Admins). After that, you shall be able to remove the user from the Company.
    To do so open the **Admin console** > **Users** > **All users** > click on the number of teams of the respective user to learn which teams they are a member of.
    Click on the number of teams of your own profile and then the **plus +** icon > add yourself to the needed teams > **Save changes**.
    To grant yourself Team Admin rights open **Teams** section > choose the team where you need Admin rights > **Active users** > click 3-dots on the line with your name > choose **Grant team admin**.
15. *I'm a Team Admin of a team that is a part of Enterprise/Business subscription. What management options are available for me?*
    - Team Admins on Business and Enterprise plans can change team name and logo, remove team users, invite new team members if this is allowed by Company admins, grant and revoke Team Admin role, manage [sharing settings](../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/07-team-management-on-enterprise-plan.md), apps, and integrations. For more details, please take a look at [this article](../get-started-as-a-miro-admin/01-i-am-a-new-miro-admin.-where-to-start.md#team-admin-company-admin).
