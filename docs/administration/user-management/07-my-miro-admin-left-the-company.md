Learn how to regain control of your Miro team if your Miro Admin has left the company.

## Access the Miro Admin's profile

To regain access and admin rights to your Miro team, the Team Admin should have an active email address that you can use to change the password and log in.

Only Admins can promote other users to the Admin level. We recommend [recovering an Admin’s password](../../using-miro/managing-your-profile/05-how-to-change-your-password.md) and [promoting a team member to Team Admin](06-how-to-manage-admin-roles.md). This security procedure enables Miro to protect your Miro space against unauthorized access.

If you’re unable to access the Admin's email, please reach out to your Email Service Provider (ESP) or IT team for assistance. Miro is unable to help you regain access to the email on your behalf.

## How to promote a new user to Admin

Free, Starter Business Education

1. [Sign in](https://miro.com/login/) to Miro with the Admin's email and updated password.
2. Go to your Team members list by hovering over your team name and clicking the **Team members** icon.
   ![team_members_icon.png](images/25897598799506_team_members_icon.png) *Team members icon*
3. In the **All Users**page, click the three dots on the row of the user you want to promote to an Admin, and then click one of the following options: **Grant Team Admin****,** **Promote to Team Admin**, **Promote to Company Admin****.**

The new Admin will be able to administrate the team and [remove the previous Admin from the team](08-remove-users.md).

1. [Sign in](https://miro.com/login/) to Miro with the Admin's email and updated password.
2. Go to your Team settings by hovering over the team name and clicking the **Team** **settings** icon.
   ![biz_team_settings_icon.png](images/25899492999954_biz_team_settings_icon.png) *Team settings icon*
3. Click the team from which you want to remove a user.
4. On the side panel, click **Users**, and then click **All users**.
5. Click the three dots on the row of the user you want to promote to an Admin, and then click **Promote to Company Admin.**

The new Admin will be able to administrate the plan and [remove the previous Admin](08-remove-users.md).

The Education plan allows only one Admin per team. You can grant Admin rights to another user by leaving the team.

1. [Sign in](https://miro.com/login/) to Miro with the Admin's email and updated password.
2. Go to your Team settings by hovering over the team name and clicking the **Team** **settings** icon.
3. On the **Team profile** page, click **Leave team**.
   A dialog box appears.
4. Select a new Admin and leave the team.The new Admin will be able to administrate the team and [remove the previous Admin from the team](08-remove-users.md).

## I can’t access the Miro Admin's profile

If you can't gain access to the Miro Admin's profile, you can [create a new team](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/02-how-to-create-a-team-in-miro.md#creating-additional-teams-in-miro). You’ll then receive Admin permissions for a new team. Using this method, you can [invite new collaborators](01-invite-users.md) to this new team and [move the boards](../../using-miro/managing-boards/04-how-to-move-a-board.md) if necessary. Miro is not responsible for the migration of boards or users on your behalf.

If you’re still unable to access the Miro Admin's profile and can't create a new team, please [reach out to Miro Support](../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/06-contacting-miro-support.md) with details. We'll investigate the team and request additional confirmations and verifications to assist you.

We cannot guarantee recovery of the administrative access as we must abide by security and legal processes.   If you or any other requestor are unable to meet these criteria, we cannot proceed on your behalf.
