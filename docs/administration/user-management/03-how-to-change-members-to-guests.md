As Company admin, you can convert a Team Member to a Guest if you want to limit their access to your team boards and Spaces.

When you convert a team member to guest, you can re-assign their content to another admin. The guest retains edit access to boards they previously owned, but they are unable to create or own new content inside the team.

Guests can collaborate with you only on boards shared directly with them. They don’t have access to your team's shared content and can’t create new boards in your team. They’ll still be able to see boards shared with them explicitly over email.

**More information: See [Collaboration with Guests](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md).**

Business, Enterprise  Starter, Education

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Users**, and then click **All users**.
3. Under **Active users**, click the three dots next to a the user who you want to change to a guest user, and then click  **Change to Guest**.
   The **Change to guest** modal appears.
4. Click **Change**.

As soon as the user is converted to a Guest, they are removed from the subscription and will release a seat (license). Learn how to [remove extra seats](../../plans-billing/manage-your-subscription-and-plan/01-manage-your-subscription.md).

If the user owns any boards, Spaces, or templates, they will be reassigned to the corresponding Team Admins. The user will still have access to any boards they were invited to over email.

> **Who can do it:** Team Admins

On Starter Plan, if a user is a [trial member](01-invite-users.md#member-trials-on-starter-plan) you can convert them to a Guest in **Team settings > Users**.

Otherwise you cannot convert members to Guests on Free or Starter plans. Instead you can delete the user from the team and invite them again as a Guest.

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Users**, and then click **All users**.
3. Click the three dots next to a the user who you want to delete from the team, and then click  **Delete from team**.

:::note
When you remove a member, you also release a seat (license). If you don’t plan to use it again, you can always [reduce the number of seats in your plan](../../plans-billing/manage-your-subscription-and-plan/01-manage-your-subscription.md).
:::

> **💡** If you prefer to give a user editing access without inviting them to the team, you can [share the board for public editing](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/08-collaboration-with-visitors.md#how-to-enable-access-for-visitors).

4. Now you can invite the user to specific boards. Open the share settings, enter the user's email address, and select **Can view** or **Can comment** from the dropdown. They’ll appear as a Guest in your **Users** list.
