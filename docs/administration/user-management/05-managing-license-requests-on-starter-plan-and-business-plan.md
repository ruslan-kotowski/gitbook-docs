Review and manage all license and team access requests in one place, streamlining license usage and maintaining your workspace's security and organization.

## Access request settings

Within your **Access request** settings, you'll find an overview of all current [license and team access requests](#license-and-team-access-requests). Requests can be made by the individual needing access, or on behalf of someone else.

Access request details include:

- The name of the user the license request is for
- The name of the team the user is asking to join (Business Plan only)
- The name of the requester
- The date of the request
- A message from the requestor explaining the reason for the request

:::note
All requests will automatically expire and be removed after 30 days.
:::

## How to accept or deny license and team requests

1. Go to your Admin console.
2. Click **Users**.
3. Click **Access requests**.
4. The **Access requests** section will open. Here you can view any pending requests.
5. Under the **Actions** column, click the checkmark (**✓**) to accept, or the **x** to decline the request.
6. When you accept or decline the request, a modal will open with the request details, along with a message from the requester.
7. Click**Approve** to approve the request, or **Deny** to decline the request. Once you accept or deny a request, it will be removed from your access request overview.
8. If the license request is approved, the user will be added to the team with a full license. If you’ve reached your total allocated licenses, an extra license will be added to your subscription, and you will receive a [prorated invoice](../../plans-billing/billing-and-payments/07-understanding-your-invoice.md).

## License and team access requests

Certain actions or events in Miro will trigger license and team access requests automatically. The scenarios outlined below detail how these access requests can be initiated.

Once a request is approved, the user will be granted a Full license and will become a member within your subscription, and/or will also be granted team access.

| User type | Action | Scenario |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Existing Members (Full license)** | Access a new team | Members request access to a new team within the subscription. |
| Access to team for another user (within subscription) | Members request team access for another user within the same subscription but not in their team. |
| Access to team for another user (outside subscription) | Members invite new users outside your subscription to a team, usually through an [email or link](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#ways-to-share-miro-boards). |
| **Existing Guests** | Upgrade to Full license | [Guests](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md) request an upgrade from a Free to a Full license to be able to create and share boards. |
| Upgrade to Full license | [Guests](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md) request an upgrade from a Free to a Full license to be able to edit boards (Starter Plan only). |
| **New users (outside your Miro subscription)** | Join with same domain | New users can join the organization and/or specific teams when they [register or sign in with your domain](../team-management/02-manage-team-signup-mode.md). |
| Join without board permissions | New users can join the organization when they do not have access to a board shared with them. |
