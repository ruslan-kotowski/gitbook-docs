Easily invite people to your team and boards to start collaborating and creating together. Depending on [Invitation settings](02-invitation-settings.md), the option to invite new users may be available for Admins only or for all members.

:::note
Visit [this article](../../enterprise-administration/user-management/05-manage-user-invitations-on-enterprise-plan.md) to learn how to invite users on Enterprise plan.
:::

## Invite members

There are several ways you can add members to your team.

- From your dashboard. Click **Invite members** in the upper-right corner
  ![invite-members-dashboard.png](images/25007019083026_invite-members-dashboard.png)
  *The option to invite members from the dashboard*
- From the Admin console: Open the **All Users** tab. There you can see all team members and invited users.

  ![admin-invite-users.png](images/25007019084178_admin-invite-users.png)
  *The option to invite new members from Admin console*

  Click **Invite new members** in the upper right corner and enter the emails of your invitees. You can insert up to 500 emails in the invitation modal.

  ![invite_modal.jpg](images/21017416281746_invite%20modal.jpg)
  *Invitation modal*
- In this menu, you can alsocopy[**Team invite link**](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-boards-and-inviting-to-the-team-via-a-link) - everyone who follows the link will be able to join your team (available on [Free](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md), [Starter](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/08-starter-plan.md), [Education](../../plans-billing/miro-special-pricing/03-education-plan.md) plans). Admins can enable or disable the link in the [Invitation settings](02-invitation-settings.md)
- On Free plan, **every user** [**invited to a board via email**](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email) gets invited to the team as well and becomes a team member
- On Starter plan, **every editor** [**invited to a board via email**](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email)becomes a team member. You will see the following notification and will also have an option to invite the user with commenter access without adding to the team
  ![only_members_can_edit.jpg](images/21017429340690_only%20members%20can%20edit.jpg)
  *A notification that only members can edit boards on this type of plan*

Your invitee will receive an email invitation and will be able to join the work right away.

If the invitee is not registered with Miro, the invite will be active for 30 days. They will also receive an email notification on the 3d and 7th days if they don't accept it immediately. When clicking on the link in the notification email, they will be suggested to [sign up](../../getting-started/start-here/02-how-to-register-with-miro.md). If a non-registered invitee doesn't accept the invitation within 30 days, it expires and the user gets deleted from the **Active users** list.

Registered Miro users will be able to find your team on the left sidebar of their [dashboard](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md) as soon as you invite them.

If you accidentally sent an invite to a wrong email address or made a typo and now want to delete the extra invite, click the three dots next to the invited user and choose **Revoke invitation.**

### Member Trials on Starter plan

:::note
Only available for [Starter Plan](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/08-starter-plan.md).
:::

When inviting members to the team, instead of immediately adding them as a paying license, they will first be added as **Free** **members**.

The user's license remains free till the invited user performs any paid action within the team - opens a board/creates their own board/project.

Once the paid action is performed, their license is converted to trial for 7 days no license is consumed at this point.

The users can have full paid access for free to the team for 7 days — this enables collaboration without committing immediately to cost.

During the trial, new members can be promoted to Full members, or changed to guests in the **All users** section in Admin console.

Trial members become paid members if no action is taken by the end of the trial (7 days). If you don't want to add Trial members as **Full team members** after their trial finishes, you can [remove](08-remove-users.md) them from the team or convert them to guest users (which means that they will be able to view and comment on the board on which they were invited explicitly) at any time during the trial.

Please note that Member Trials are only available to new members a single time. Once a trial has been used, re-added members will occupy a paid license immediately.

If Starter plan is [upgraded to Business Plan](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/360011780620-How-to-Change-Your-Plan#h_8315f4f8-9f5b-4665-b271-e438aedaf289), all current Trial Members are converted to Full members at the moment of the upgrade.

The cost of the new license **is prorated**for the time remaining in your current subscription period (accurate up to a day), so your renewal dates for new licenses will always coincide with your existing licenses. If you have vacant licenses during your subscription period and add a new member - no charge is applied. To know more about the prorated system of charge, please check our article - [Billing and payments](../../plans-billing/billing-and-payments/04-miro-billing.md).

## Invite guests

You can share your boards with users via email without adding them to your team as members. Such users will be listed as guests in your Team settings.

:::warning
Note that guests are not available on [Free plan](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md).
:::

:::note
The option to invite guests may be restricted in [Invitation settings](02-invitation-settings.md) on Business plans.
:::

On Starter and Education plans, you can invite guests with view or comment access only.

On Business plan, you can invite guests as viewers, commenters, or editors to your boards.

Such users are listed as **guests** in your Admin console. Admins can convert them to members or delete them from the team/revoke invitation.

Guests cannot create their own boards in the team and cannot access [team shared boards](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md) and projects. If they request to join the team, the notification will be sent to Admins.

:::note
Learn how to convert members to guests on [this page](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md).
:::

## Invite visitors

You can also share your boards with visitors - such users do not get added to your team and can view/comment/edit public boards without being registered users. The option is available on all paid plans. Learn more: [Collaboration with visitors](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/08-collaboration-with-visitors.md).

:::note
See the difference between members, guests, and visitors on [this page](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md).
:::

## Frequently asked questions

1. *My invitee does not get an email with the invitation I sent. How can they access my team?*
   - Please ask the user to register with Miro or to log in if they have a Miro profile. The user will find your team on the left sidebar of their [dashboard](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md).
2. *I have been invited to a Miro team but I do not see it when I log in. How can I access the team?*
   - Try to find the team on the left sidebar of your [dashboard](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md) and switch to it. If there is no team, please make sure you are authorized in Miro under the same email address that has been invited to the team.
3. *There is no Invite members button on my dashboard. Why?*
   - Admins restricted the option to invite new members in the [Invitation settings](02-invitation-settings.md). Note that this option may be restricted for Team Admins on Enterprise plan.
4. *Can I invite users without them registering with Miro?*
   - Yes, you can [share your boards via public links](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-boards-via-a-public-link) with unregistered users free of charge.
5. *What to do if I accidentally invited new paid members?*
   - Please [follow these steps](../../plans-billing/billing-and-payments/04-miro-billing.md).
