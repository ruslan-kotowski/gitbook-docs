By removing a user Admins restrict their access to the team content and can take ownership of the user's content or delete it.

### How to delete users

To delete Members or [Guests](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md) from your team, go to **Admin console**. To access the console from your dashboard, click your profile avatar in the upper right and then the **Admin console** button.

In the **Users** > **All users** tab, click the **three dots** (**...**) icon next to a user and choose **Delete from team**.

:::note
If you do not see the option to delete a user, check out the steps [below](#frequently-asked-questions).
:::

![delete-from-team.png](images/25008730223122_delete-from-team.png)*Delete a team member*

If the user is the owner of some boards, [Spaces](../../using-miro/spaces/01-spaces.md), or [templates](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/04-templates.md) created in this team (shared or private), you will be given a choice whether to pass the ownership to one of the team members (on Free and Education plans—to one of the Admins) or delete them. Click the cross icon if you would like to change the owner-to-be. If you choose to delete the user and content, an Admin will still be able to [restore the boards](../../using-miro/managing-boards/08-how-to-restore-a-deleted-board.md) within 90 days.

:::note
On Business Plan, if you choose to transfer ownership to Team Admins, content gets automatically reassigned to one of the Admins.
:::

![delete_user_modal.jpg](images/21017515322002_delete%20user%20modal.jpg)*Changing board ownership, while removing a user*

On paid plans, you can also bulk delete users: select several users and choose **Delete from team** under **Bulk actions**.

![bulk-delete-users.png](images/25008759156370_bulk-delete-users.png)*Deleting several users in bulk*

The deleted user will lose all access to your team Spaces right away (without being notified). Please note that they will retain access to the team [boards that were shared via a public link](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-boards-via-a-public-link) - if the user saved the links to these specific boards.

After you've successfully removed Team Members, you'll see a notification at the top with the number of licenses now available. You can either invite new Team Members, or go to your billing settings to reduce the number of licenses in your team.

If you reduce your team size, the amount for unused time will go as a credit to the subscription balance. To know more about the prorated system of charge, please check our article - [Billing and payments](../../plans-billing/billing-and-payments/04-miro-billing.md).

### How to delete users on Business Plan

To remove a user and release a vacant license, open **Admin console** > **Users** > **All users** section and choose **Delete**in the user's menu. Define whether to transfer ownership of the user's content or to remove all the user's resources and click **Confirm**. If you choose to transfer the user's content, it will be reassigned to Admins of the teams where the content is located.

If you've removed users with a [Full license](../../enterprise-administration/user-management/11-user-access-levels-on-enterprise-plan.md#license-types), you'll see a notification at the top with the number of licenses now available. If you are not planning to invite Full members to take the vacant licenses, you can decrease your team size in your **Billing** settings > **change team size**.

:::note
If you see a notification **The team must have at least one Admin** when trying to delete a user, it means that the user is the only Admin in a team or teams within the subscription. To fix this, you may [invite yourself to these teams](01-invite-users.md) and [grant Team Admin rights to yourself](06-how-to-manage-admin-roles.md). Сlick on the number of teams of the respective user to learn which teams they are a member of.
:::

### Frequently asked questions

1. *I do not have the option to delete users. Why?*
   - Please note that the option to remove members is available for Admins only. If you do not see the option in settings, please ask the Admin to delete users. You can find the current Admin's email on the **Active users** list. You can also ask the user to [promote you to Admin](06-how-to-manage-admin-roles.md).
2. *Our Miro Admin has left the company. How can I remove them?*
   - Check the article: [My Miro Admin left the company](07-my-miro-admin-left-the-company.md).
3. *How can I remove empty licenses?*
   - If you have vacant licenses that you want to delete, follow [this guide](../../plans-billing/billing-and-payments/04-miro-billing.md).
