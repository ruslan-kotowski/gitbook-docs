The guide will be helpful for those who have just created a free Miro team and would like to learn more about the management options.

To open your Team settings, click your avatar in the top-right corner of the dashboard and then click **Admin console**.

Free plan Admins have access to the following sections of the Admin console:

Manage your team users in the **Users** tab. Invite new members by clicking the **Invite people** button in the top-right corner. Note that every user invited to a free team gets access to all the boards stored in the team. Click the three dots next to a user to [promote them to an Admin](../user-management/06-how-to-manage-admin-roles.md) or [delete them from the team](../user-management/08-remove-users.md).

![free-users.png](images/25005889311634_free-users.png)
*Managing users on Free plan*

**Security**. Free plan Admins can set up *two types of permissions* for their team: **Team signup mode** and **Invitation settings**. By [configuring the team signup mode](../team-management/02-manage-team-signup-mode.md) you can make your team discoverable for users with your domain. In the [**Invitation settings**](../user-management/02-invitation-settings.md), Admins define who can invite new users to the team and enable or disable [the team invite link](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-boards-and-inviting-to-the-team-via-a-link). You'll also find **Sharing settings** in this tab.

:::tip
[Upgrade your team](../../plans-billing/manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md) to configure board sharing and content permissions.
:::

![free-permissions.png](images/25005901477266_free-permissions.png)
*Permissions settings on Free plan*

By going to the **Apps** tab, you can install new apps for your team. Note that some integrations are not supported on Free plan. To uninstall an app, choose it on the list — this will open the app profile where you will find the button **Uninstall for team**.

**Team profile** tab is where you can [change the team name and logo](../team-management/04-update-team-name-or-logo.md). Under **Your plan details,** you can see the option to upgrade your plan by clicking **Change plan**. Below that you can find the total number of boards and the number of team members.
