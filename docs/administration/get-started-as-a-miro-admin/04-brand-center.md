Upload your brand fonts and colors so you and your team can easily create branded content. Ensure every board and presentation showcases your brand's unique personality, and impress stakeholders and clients with polished visuals.

## Managing your Brand center

Fonts and colors you add in the Brand center apply to your whole organization. This includes all teams and boards in your plan.

All [Visitors, Guests, and Team Members](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/10-visitors-guests-and-members.md) with edit access can apply your branded colors, fonts, and style to boards.

:::note
It is currently not possible to customize fonts and colors for individual teams.
:::

### Manage your Brand center settings

Control who can make changes to your Brand center. Admins can restrict access to the Brand center for all non-admin team members. To do this, turn on **Restrict Brand center management to admins** in the Brand center settings. For Enterprise Plan, this is already set as the default.

When this setting is turned on:

- **Business Plan, Enterprise Plan:** Only Company Admins can manage the Brand center.
- **Education Plan, Starter Plan:** Only Team Admins can manage the Brand center.

![Brand-center-admin-restriction-settings.png](images/21017683430930_Brand-center-admin-restriction-settings.png)*The option to restrict brand center management to admins turned on*

## Uploading brand colors and fonts

### How to upload your brand colors

**Guidelines for uploading colors**

- You can upload up to 24 colors
- Only HEX is currently supported
- For brand styles, you need to upload a minimum of 3 colors

Company Admins Team Admins, Team Members

1. Click on the profile picture in the top-right of your dashboard, and click **Brand center**; or open your **Company** settings, and under **Account**, click **Brand center**.
2. The Brand center panel will open.
3. Under **Colors** there are two sections: **Brand colors** and **Brand style**.
4. To add your **Brand colors**, click the plus (**+**) icon and enter the **HEX** color code and **Color name**.
5. Your **Brand style** is automatically created from the brand colors you add.
6. Customize the layout of your **Brand colors** and **Brand style**. Click **Reorder**, drag the colors into your preferred configuration, and click **Done**.
7. Click on a color to **Edit** or **Delete** it.

### How to upload your brand fonts

**Guidelines for uploading fonts**

- You can upload up to 10 fonts
- Supported font files include oft, ttf, woff, and woff2
- File extensions should be in lowercase, for example, brandfont.ttf
- The max font file size can be up to 20 MB
- You will need to upload the regular font file
- Only upload fonts you are licensed to use

Company Admins Team Admins, Team Members

1. Click on the profile picture in the top-right of your dashboard, and click **Brand center**; or open your **Company** settings, and under**Account**, click**Brand center**.
2. The Brand center panel will open.
3. Scroll down to **Fonts**.
4. Click **+ Add brand fonts**.
5. Click **Upload** to add the corresponding font file for each **Font style**. You will see a **Preview** of the font after it's uploaded.
6. Click **Save font**.
7. Next to the font name, hover over the pencil icon to **Edit**, or the trash icon to **Delete**.

### How to set a default font

Ensure a consistent look across your organization's content by selecting a default font. This setting applies to all boards and objects, including text, shapes, diagrams, and grids.

1. Click the three dots (**...**) next to the font you wish to set as default.
2. Toggle on **Set as default**.

## Applying your brand fonts and colors

After [adding your brand colors and fonts in the Brand center](#uploading-brand-colors-and-fonts), you and your team can start to apply them to boards.

### How to apply brand colors to an object or frame

1. Select a frame or object on the board.
2. The context menu will open.
3. Click on the **Color** selector.
4. Your **Brand colors** will appear at the top of the dropdown.
5. Select a color.

![Applying-brand-colors-on-a-board.gif](images/21017683427986_Applying-brand-colors-on-a-board.gif)
*Applying a brand color on the board*

### How to apply brand fonts to a text object

1. Double click any text object on the board.
2. The context menu will open.
3. Click on the **Font** selector.
4. Your **Brand fonts** will appear at the top of the dropdown.
5. Select a font.

:::warning
Font files are not included when exporting board backups. If you upload a board backup to an organization that does not support the brand font file, the text will default to Open Sans.
:::

![Adding-brand-fonts-to-the-board.gif](images/21017683428242_Adding-brand-fonts-to-the-board.gif)
*Applying a brand font on the board*

### How to apply your brand style to frames

1. Select a frame or multiple frames on the board.
2. The context menu will open.
3. Click on the **styles** icon.
4. The styles panel will open.
5. Select a **Brand style**.
6. The brand style will be applied to the frame and any objects within the frame.

:::tip
Try applying different versions of your brand style. Click your brand style color palette again to **Shuffle** the colors.
:::

![Applying-a-brand-style-to-frames.gif](images/21017653822098_Applying-a-brand-style-to-frames.gif)
*Applying a brand style to multiple frames*

### How to apply your brand style to diagram shapes

1. Go to the [creation toolbar](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/05-toolbars.md#creation-toolbar) on the left side of your board.
2. Click the **More apps** icon (**+**) and search for ‘Diagramming shapes’.
3. Launch the **Diagramming shapes** app, which will open the **Diagramming** panel.
4. Scroll down to find the shape pack you wish to use to build your diagram.
5. From the **Apply colors** dropdown, select a **Brand style**.
6. The selected brand style will be applied to the diagramming shapes.

![Apply-brand-colors-to-diagram-shapes.png](images/21017653825810_Apply-brand-colors-to-diagram-shapes.png)
*Applying a brand style to diagram shapes*

## Frequently asked questions

Are my brand fonts included when I export my boards?

When you export or copy boards that use brand fonts, the fonts are not exported with the board. If you move boards that use brand fonts to an organization that does not have the font loaded in their Brand center at the time of copying, the fonts will revert to Open Sans. For this reason, brand fonts appear as Open Sans in Miroverse templates.

Are brand fonts available to people outside of my organization?

Yes, once your Brand center is set up, anyone with editing rights on your team's shared boards can access and use the brand colors and fonts. This includes [Guests](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md), and [Visitors](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/08-collaboration-with-visitors.md) with edit access. This is great for workshops and meetings with people outside your team. They won't face any font issues, which often happens with tools that need specific fonts installed on your computer.
