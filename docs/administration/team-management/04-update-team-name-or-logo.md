To update the team name or logo:

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Team,** and then click **Team profile**.
3. (Optional) To update the team name, enter a new name in the **Team name** field.
4. (Optional) To update the team logo, click **Upload** and choose an image from your device.
   - **Supported file types**: .jpg, .jpeg, .bmp, .png, or .gif
   - **Maximum file size**:30 MB
5. (Optional) To remove the team logo, click **Remove**.
