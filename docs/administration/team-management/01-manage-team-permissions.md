To manage team permissions, from your Miro dashboard select your avatar in the top-right. Go to **Admin console** > **Security**. Then open the **Permissions** tab.

![managing-team-permissions.png](images/25007076612498_managing-team-permissions.png)

*Team permissions on Free, Starter, Business, Education plans*

From the **Permissions** you can manage the following team permissions:

- (Business plan) [**Team signup mode**](02-manage-team-signup-mode.md)
  Specify how new members can join a team in your organization. The setting must be enabled by a Company admin.
- (Business plan) [**Team invitation settings**](../user-management/02-invitation-settings.md)
  Specify who can invite new members to join a team. For example, you can prohibit non-admin members from inviting new members. The setting must be enabled by a Company admin.
- (Business plan) **Collaboration settings**
  Enable and disable [co-owner role](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/06-co-owners-of-boards-and-spaces.md).
- [**Sharing settings**](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/11-default-sharing-settings.md)
  Specify the default access and sharing permissions for boards and Spaces.
- [**Content security**](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md)
  Specify whether only team members have permission to copy board content.

:::note
Enterprise Plan Admins can specify more permissions for their teams. For more information, see [Team permissions on Enterprise plans](../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/10-team-permissions-on-enterprise-plan.md).
:::
