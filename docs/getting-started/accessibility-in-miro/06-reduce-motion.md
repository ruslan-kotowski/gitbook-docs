If you are impacted by motion when using Miro, you can stop or reduce animations and other movements on your Miro boards.

## Reduce motion within Miro

Turn on Reduce motion in your Miro settings to stop or reduce movements for the following Miro features and functionality:

- Presentation mode
- Navigating between frames
- Navigating with link to object
- Navigating widgets with hotkeys
- GIF autoplay

:::note
Enabling Reduce Motion in your Miro settings will reflect your system settings. You can enable or disable this setting at any time without changing your system settings.
:::

### Enable Reduce motion

1. In the Board bar, select the **vertical three dots** (![icon-main.svg](images/27695273339410_icon-main.svg)) icon.
   The Main menu opens.
2. Select **Accessibility**.
3. Toggle **Reduce motion** to the on position.
