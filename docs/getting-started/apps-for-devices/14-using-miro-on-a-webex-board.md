Enhance your whiteboarding activity by using Miro on a Webex board. Set up the Miro app and easily start collaborating, presenting your ideas, and creating next big things!

There are two ways of adding the Miro app on a Webex board: [directly from Control Hub](#adding-the-app-from-control-hub) or [within the UI Extensions editor](#adding-the-app-within-the-ui-extensions-editor).

### Adding the app from Control Hub

To add the app from the Control Hub, go to **Devices** and open the device (you need the device already registered to Control Hub, check [this article in Webex Help Center](https://help.webex.com/n3hi0z8/Activate-a-Cisco-Webex-Room-or-Desk-Device-or-Webex-Board) to learn how to activate a Webex Board or Desk Device).

![devices_on_Webex.jpg](images/21016044135954_devices%20on%20Webex.jpg)
*Opening the device on Webex Control Hub*

Scroll down and click **Web Apps**.

![Web_apps.jpg](images/21016057499538_Web%20apps.jpg)
*Getting to Web Apps on Webex Control Hub*

Choose to add a new web app. Enter the app name that will appear under the web app icon on the home screen of the Webex board. Type in URL (for example, miro.com/displays to have a launch screen and experience optimized for interactive displays). Add a custom icon by entering [this link](https://miro.com/static/images/awwapp-miro/miro_logo_rounded.png) in the **Icon URL**field (optional). Click **Add** and **Save** the results.

![Adding_the_Miro_web_app_from_Webex_Control_Hub.gif](images/21016057500818_Adding%20the%20Miro%20web%20app%20from%20Webex%20Control%20Hub.gif)
*Adding the Miro web app from Webex Control Hub*

The device will sync automatically and you will see the Miro icon on the home screen of your Webex board.

### Adding the app within the UI Extensions Editor

Open the web portal of your device and go to**UI Extensions Editor**.

![extensions_editor.jpg](images/21016057501458_extensions%20editor.jpg)
*Opening UI Extensions Editor*

Then you choose **New** on the panel of Extensions and click **Add** under Web App.

![Adding_a_new_Web_App_within_UI_Extensions_Editor_.gif](images/21016044138514_Adding%20a%20new%20Web%20App%20within%20UI%20Extensions%20Editor%20.gif)
*Adding a new Web App within* *UI Extensions Editor*

Enter the Id, Name, and URL on the left sidebar. Insert [this link](https://miro.com/static/images/awwapp-miro/miro_logo_rounded.png) in the **Web app icon URL** field to add a custom icon (optional). Choose **Always** to have the Miro App available while in a call. Click the arrow button on the top bar to finish.

![Adding_the_Miro_app_with_UI_Extensions_Editor_on_a_Webex_board.gif](images/21016044139154_Adding%20the%20Miro%20app%20with%20UI%20Extensions%20Editor%20on%20a%20Webex%20board.gif)
*Adding the Miro app with UI Extensions Editor on a Webex board*

:::note
Learn more about adding Web Apps on Webex Boards in [Webex Help Center](https://help.webex.com/h47w6/Add-Web-Apps-on-Webex-Boards-and-Desk-Pros).
:::

### Working in Miro on a Webex board

Once the Miro icon appears on the home screen of your Webex board, push it and log in to your Miro profile. Now you can use Miro with all the functionality of the app. Learn more about working on Miro boards by checking these Help Center sections: [Getting Started](..), [Using Miro](../../using-miro).

- To enjoy the best experience, we recommend using an ethernet cable over Wi-Fi
- Check your [navigation mode](../../using-miro/working-on-the-board/05-using-miro-with-a-mouse-trackpad-or-touchscreen.md) and change it to trackpad or mouse if need be
- Use the mini-map in the bottom-right corner of the board to easily navigate across the board

![navigation_bar.gif](images/21016057505042_navigation%20bar.gif)
*Mini-map of a Miro board*

Once you end the session and clean up, you will be automatically logged out of Miro.

### Frequently asked questions

1. *Can I use Miro* *on my Webex Desk Pro?*
   - Yes, Miro can be used on a Desk Pro via a browser.
