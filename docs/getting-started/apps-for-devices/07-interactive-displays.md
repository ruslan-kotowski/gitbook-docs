## About using Miro on an interactive display

Miro can be used on interactive displays in two ways:

- On the interactive display itself if it has a built-in PC
- By connecting a PC to the interactive display

### Recommended displays for Miro

We recommend considering the following interactive displays, tested by Miro to ensure compatibility and optimal performance.

Your company's needs and preferences are unique. Therefore it's important to test devices before making a purchase to ensure a perfect match. Learn more about [selecting the right display for collaboration](09-selecting-the-right-interactive-display-for-hybrid-collaboration.md).

**Displays with built-in PC:**

- Neat Board
- DTEN ONboard
- Vibe Board
- [Google Meet Series One Board](12-using-miro-with-google-meet-series-one-boards-beta.md)

**Displays with external PC connection:**

- Dell Interactive 4K Displays (55" to 86")
- Sharp (PN-L652B, PN-L752B; PN-L862B)
- NEC displays (WD551, M551/ M651 /M751/ M861 IGB)

**Recommended external PC setups:**

- Dell OptiPlex Micro PC with Intel Core i7 & 16GB RAM (Windows)
- Intel OPS/SDM with Core i7 & 16GB RAM

## Setting up Miro on interactive displays: app and browser options

On interactive displays, you can use the Miro app, or open Miro in the browser.

### Miro app

- [Download the Miro app for Windows-based displays](https://desktop.miro.com/platforms/win32/Miro.exe)
- [Download the Miro app for Vibe Board displays](https://vibe.how/en/articles/6880969-how-to-download-and-install-your-favorite-apps-in-vibe-app-store)
- Neat Board and DTEN ONboard: reach out to the customer success manager of your display provider to assist you with accessing the Miro app.

### Browsers

- Microsoft Edge browser (version 85 and above)
- Google Chrome browser (version 85 and above)

> **✏️** If you use an active Stylus when working in Miro, you are automatically switched to our [Pen](../../using-miro/essential-tools/10-pen.md) tool. This mode activates palm rejection and the ability to navigate with finger. To disable this mode (to start drawing with fingers), reload the board or stop using the digital pen for 10 minutes.

> **✏️** Miro only supports one touchpoint per display.

## Launching Miro on your interactive display

You have two options for launching a Miro board onto your interactive display: either through the display's Miro app or via the display browser.

Using the Miro app Using the display browser Windows displays

1. To start using Miro on the display, open the [Miro app](#miro-app). No sign-in needed.
2. You will have two options: **Send to this display** launches the Miro board from your phone, tablet, or laptop. You can also **Start a new board**.
3. Once the Miro board is open on the display, you're ready to start your session.
4. To end the session, log out by tapping the **Exit** icon on the interactive display, or click **Stop sharing** in the Miro web or mobile app.

1. To start using Miro on the display, open the [display browser](#browsers) and go to [miro.com/displays](https://miro.com/displays/).
2. You will have two options: **Send to this display** launches the Miro board from your phone, tablet, or laptop. You can also **Start a new board**. (If you prefer to directly send a Miro board to display, visit [miro.com/send-to-display](https://miro.com/send-to-display/).)
3. Once the Miro board is open on the display, you're ready to start your session.
4. To end the session, log out by tapping the **Exit** icon on the interactive display, or click **Stop sharing** in the Miro web or mobile app.

1. To start using Miro on a Windows display, open the Miro app.
2. On your physical or virtual keyboard, press the *alt* key. The context menu opens.
3. Select **Help** > **Public device**. The app changes to the interactive display experience.

:::tip
**Always remember to log out of the display to protect your profile data, and to allow the next user to sign in.**
:::

### Saving the board

If you create a new board, you can save it to access it later. To do this you'll need a Miro account.

1. In the top-left corner of the board, click **Save**
2. Enter your email and click **Send now**, or scan the QR code
3. Open the board from your email. If you scanned the QR code, the board will open automatically on your device
4. You'll be prompted to **Enter a new board name**, and **Choose a team** from the dropdown
5. Click **Save**

## Miro toolbar on interactive displays

When you work in Miro on an interactive display, the creation toolbar is placed at the bottom of the screen. By default, there will only be the most used tools: [sticky notes](../../using-miro/essential-tools/14-sticky-notes.md), [shapes](../../using-miro/essential-tools/11-shapes.md), [text](../../using-miro/essential-tools/16-text.md), [lines](../../using-miro/essential-tools/05-connection-lines.md), [pen](../../using-miro/essential-tools/10-pen.md), highlighter, and eraser. Other tools will be in the **Tools, Media and Integrations** (**+**) menu.

> **✏️** If you work in a browser on a 4K+ touch device and don't use [Quick sign-in](10-send-to-display.md) to log in to Miro, the toolbar is placed for you on the left side of the screen.

## Timeout settings for interactive displays

Administrators have the option to apply custom timeout settings for interactive displays in their organization. There are two settings that can be defined:

1. Idle time out period.
2. Whether to allow individual users to override the idle time out.

You can access these settings under **Security and compliance** > **Displays**.
