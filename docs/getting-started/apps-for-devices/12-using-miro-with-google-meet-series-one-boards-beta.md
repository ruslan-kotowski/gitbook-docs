Unlock the potential of dynamic collaboration through the Miro add-on on your Google Meet Series One Boards. This feature is perfect for activities like brainstorming, ideation, diagramming, and conducting retrospectives. The Miro add-on is available for both Board 65 and Desk 27 models.

:::warning
The Miro add-on is currently in open beta on Google Meet Series One Boards. Not all features are available yet.
:::

:::note
To activate the Miro add-on on your Google Meet Series One Boards, your IT admin must enable it via the Google Workspace admin panel.
:::

## How to use the Miro add-on

**During a Google Meet Call**

Use the Miro add-on during scheduled or impromptu Google Meet calls. This is ideal for collaborative sessions that benefit from real-time communication**.**

1. Initiate or join a Google Meet call through the Google Meet user interface.
2. Launch the Miro add-on during your call by clicking the **Start activity** button.

**Standalone mode**

Use the Miro add-on independently of Google Meet calls. It's perfect for times when you need to brainstorm, plan, or create without a live discussion.

1. From the Google Meet user interface, click the **Start a whiteboard** button.
2. Choose the Miro add-on to engage on a board without starting a full meeting.

:::note
