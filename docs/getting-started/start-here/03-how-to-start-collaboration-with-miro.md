[Miro](https://miro.com/index/) is the online collaborative workspace that enables distributed teams to work effectively together, from brainstorming with digital sticky notes to planning and managing agile workflows.

To kick things off, [create a new board](your-first-board/01-create-a-miro-board.md) and add a [frame](../../using-miro/essential-tools/07-frames.md) to it. Every board is saved automatically. Each board is endless, and frames act like pages. There is no need to start from scratch — just pick a pre-made [template](your-first-board/04-templates.md) from our growing library. You can easily navigate the board using frames, and [add different types of content](../../using-miro/working-on-the-board/07-ways-to-add-content.md).

[Invite](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md) co-workers and remote colleagues to hold real-time brainstorming and ideation sessions, or work at your own pace with people from different time zones. To share your board, click the **Share** button in the top right corner.

![share_menu.jpg](images/21019693362322_share%20menu.jpg)
*The option to share a Miro board*

Participate in [comment discussions](../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/asynchronous-tools/01-comments.md) and [get notified](../../using-miro/managing-your-profile/02-miro-notifications.md) of important comments and [mentions](../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/01-@mentioning-people-on-the-board.md) in the email, browser, or your corporate [Slack](../../integrations-apps/more-integrations/14-slack.md).
