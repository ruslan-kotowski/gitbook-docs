This is a test for the New MT workflow.

Learn how to create a Miro board. A Miro board is an online whiteboard that you can use to visualize your ideas and work on projects either individually or with a team.

When you [join Miro](02-how-to-register-with-miro.md) your first board is automatically created and is saved either in your own free team or in the team you’ve been invited to. You can find it under your dashboard, titled **My First Board**.

You can create as many new boards as you wish [from your dashboard](your-first-board/01-create-a-miro-board.md) or right [from your browser address bar](your-first-board/01-create-a-miro-board.md).

### Create a board from the dashboard

1. Open your dashboard
2. If you’re a member of several teams, [choose which team](miro-dashboard/04-profile-and-teams.md) you want to save your board to
3. To create a board in a particular project, open the [project](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/16-projects.md)
4. Click **+ New board**

Create content on the board

When you open your new board you’ll see [toolbars](your-first-board/05-toolbars.md) on the top, left and bottom-right. The blank area is your board interface. To start creating content, select a tool from the creation toolbar on the left.

You can create, import, move, edit, and delete objects on your board - all the changes that you make on the board are saved in real-time.

Find detailed instructions about the [tools](../../using-miro/tools) you can use to create content and watch helpful videos about [getting started with Miro](03-how-to-start-collaboration-with-miro.md).
