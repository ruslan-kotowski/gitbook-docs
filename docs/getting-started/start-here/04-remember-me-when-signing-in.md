When signing in to Miro, you can opt to have your email address and authentication method remembered for faster future sign ins. Your last three sign in profiles can be remembered in this way.

1. When signing in, select the **Remember Me** checkbox after entering your email address. ![remember-me-checkbox.png](images/20716822838290_remember-me-checkbox.png)*Select Remember me when signing in*
2. When signing back in to Miro, you'll see your three most recently remembered sign in profiles saved. If you choose to remember an additional profile after three have been added, the oldest profile will be forgotten.
3. Click on the preferred profile to complete the sign in process.![remember-me.gif](images/20716818238994_remember-me.gif)*Signing in to Miro using a remembered profile*

To forget a remembered sign in profile:

1. Click the **three dots** (![icon-main.svg](images/26092786370706_icon-main.svg)) icon next to the profile.
2. Select **Forget this method**.![forget-this-account.gif](images/26092786370962_forget-this-account.gif)*Forgetting a remembered sign in method*
