The [Dashboard](https://miro.com/app/dashboard/) is your main workspace in Miro. You use it to navigate between your teams, [Spaces](../../../using-miro/spaces/01-spaces.md), and boards. From the dashboard, you have quick access to your profile settings, [team settings](../../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/01-i-am-a-new-miro-admin.-where-to-start.md), projects, any boards shared with you within a selected team, and all boards you have starred across your entire profile. The dashboard is also where you can [create a new board](../your-first-board/01-create-a-miro-board.md), search through your existing boards, upload [board backups](../../../using-miro/import-and-export/export/05-how-to-save-board-backup.md), and organize your boards.

## Understand your dashboard layout

Your dashboard provides a centralized view of your teams and boards, allowing for efficient navigation and management. You can see an overview of available teams and how boards are displayed.

![A list of multiple teams accessible from the Miro dashboard.](images/21264478709778_dashboard-teams-list.png)
*This user has several teams on the dashboard*

From here, you can access key areas such as your profile, team settings, [Spaces](../../../using-miro/spaces/01-spaces.md), boards shared with you, and your ★ starred boards. You can also perform actions like creating new boards, searching, uploading backups, and sorting your boards to fit your workflow.

## Navigate between your teams

You can easily switch between different Miro teams you are a member of or have been invited to. To see your teams, click your current team's name in the upper-left corner of the dashboard. A dropdown menu will appear, allowing you to select and switch to another team by clicking its name. You can also pin frequently used teams for quick access and reorder them in the list by dragging and dropping. If your organization has [team discovery enabled](../../../administration/team-management/02-manage-team-signup-mode.md) or an [Enterprise plan](../../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/11-manage-team-privacy-and-discovery-on-enterprise-plan.md), you may also find options to join or request access to other teams.

## Manage your profile settings

You can update your personal information and manage account-specific settings directly from the dashboard. To access your profile settings, click your name and avatar in the bottom-left corner of the dashboard, then click **Settings** to open your **Profile details**.

![User avatar and name in the bottom-left corner of the dashboard, with the Settings option highlighted.](images/21264485205522_dashboard-profile.png)
*Opening the profile settings*

In your profile settings, you can edit your name, company, and role. You can also [change your email](../../../using-miro/managing-your-profile/04-how-to-change-your-email.md), update your [password](../../../using-miro/managing-your-profile/05-how-to-change-your-password.md), or [delete your profile](../../../using-miro/managing-your-profile/07-how-to-delete-your-profile.md).

## Organize and locate your boards

The dashboard offers several ways to organize and find your boards efficiently. You can filter boards by ownership (**owned by anyone**, **owned by you**, or **not owned by you**), sort them alphabetically or by date (**created**, **last opened**, **last modified**), and switch between a **grid view** or **list view**. The **Recent** section in the top-left corner shows boards you recently opened from any team. For quick access to important boards, you can **star** them, and they will appear in the dedicated **Starred boards** view on your dashboard.

:::note
Password-protected boards are only shown in the **Recent** and **Starred** lists as long as they are visible to you. Once the session expires, the boards are removed from these lists until you open the board again from a direct link and re-enter the board password.
:::

## Use the board menu for quick actions

You can perform various actions on your boards directly from the dashboard using the board menu. To open the menu for a specific board, click the three dots (**...**) on the board card.

![Board card on the dashboard with the three-dot menu icon highlighted, showing options like Share, Rename, Move.](images/21264478713874_dashboard-board-menu.png)
*Board menu*

From this menu, you can [share](../../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md) boards, rename them, [move boards to different projects or teams](../../../using-miro/managing-boards/04-how-to-move-a-board.md), [duplicate (copy)](../../../using-miro/managing-boards/03-how-to-duplicate-a-board.md) them, [delete](../../../using-miro/managing-boards/07-how-to-delete-a-board.md) boards, change board thumbnails, open board details, download board [backups](../../../using-miro/import-and-export/export/05-how-to-save-board-backup.md), and copy board links.

## Find content with search

You can quickly find a specific board by using the search function. Click **Search** at the top of the dashboard page or press **Ctrl + E** (for Windows) or **Cmd + E** (for Mac) and start typing the board's name. The search results will show all boards shared with you in the current team. If you are on an [Enterprise plan](../../../plans-billing/miro-plans/04-enterprise-plan.md), you can perform a full cross-team search, which includes board titles, descriptions, and text from widgets, comments, and sticky notes across all teams under your Enterprise subscription. You can also install the [Chrome search extension for Miro](https://miro.com/marketplace/chrome-search/?backUrl=%2Fmarketplace%2F) to search for Miro boards directly from your browser's address bar. For more details, see [Search in Miro](03-how-to-search-in-miro.md).

## Explore templates and learning resources

Miro provides resources to help you get started and find inspiration. You can explore the Miroverse community templates gallery by clicking the **Explore templates** button in the upper-right section of the dashboard. This gives you access to both official Miro templates and templates created by the Miro community.

![Dashboard view with the 'Explore templates' button highlighted in the upper-right.](images/21264478712978_dashboard-miroverse.png)

For additional help, the Learning Center offers guides, use-case specific content, and links to support, webinars, and the Miro Academy. Access the Learning Center by clicking your profile avatar in the bottom-left corner of the dashboard and then clicking the **Learning Center** button. When you use the search function within the Learning Center, relevant Help Center articles will also be surfaced.

## Detailed dashboard functions

This section provides more specific details about some of the dashboard's functionalities.

### Sorting and filtering options

To help you manage your boards, the dashboard offers several sorting and filtering options:

- Filter by board ownership: **owned by anyone** in the team, **owned by you**, or **not owned by you**.
- Arrange boards in **alphabetical order**, or by the date they were **created**, **last opened**, or **last modified**.
- Switch between **grid view** or **list view** for board display.

### Accessing deleted boards (Trash)

If you are on a Starter, Business, Enterprise, or Education plan, you can restore boards that have been deleted within the last 90 days from the Trash bin. To access the Trash, click your name and avatar in the bottom-left corner of the dashboard.

:::warning
The **Trash** icon is not available on the [Free plan](../../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md). Free plan users can recover a deleted board by [opening the board link](../../../using-miro/managing-boards/08-how-to-restore-a-deleted-board.md#restoring-a-board-using-the-board-link) if they have it.
:::

## FAQ

**Can I merge two profiles attached to two different emails?**

Yes, you can do that by [moving your boards from one profile to another](../../../using-miro/managing-boards/04-how-to-move-a-board.md).

**I cannot load my Miro dashboard. What can I do?**

Please try the troubleshooting steps from [this article](../../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/04-board-performance-and-loading-issues.md).

**How can I join another team?**

You can ask a team member or Admin to [invite you to the team](../../../administration/user-management/01-invite-users.md). Additionally, if team discovery is enabled for [users in your domain](../../../administration/team-management/02-manage-team-signup-mode.md) or for [users in the Enterprise plan you are part of](../../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/11-manage-team-privacy-and-discovery-on-enterprise-plan.md), you should be able to join the team by clicking the plus icon on the left sidebar of your dashboard.
