When getting started with Miro and registering your profile, you can create your first Miro team or join someone's team. As your product usage grows, you may want to create additional teams to be able to separate work on different projects.

## Creating your first team in Miro

When registering a Miro profile, you may either *create* a new team or *join* a team where you have been invited or provisioned.

If you submit a *corporate email address* when registering, you might find out your company already has a team in Miro which is set to be [discoverable for users with your domain](../../../administration/team-management/02-manage-team-signup-mode.md). In this case, you can see the options to **Request a license, Join a team** or **Create a new team** to start whiteboarding.

![Join_other_teams.jpg](images/21017005313938_Join%20other%20teams.jpg)*Creating a new profile as a corporate user*

By choosing the **Request** license option, you will send the request to the Admins of the team. As it might take some time for the Team Admin to process your request, Miro will suggest you create *your own free team* to start working right away.

If you choose to **Join** an existing team, you will be redirected to the team's dashboard right away.

:::note
You can join teams discoverable for your domain later on your dashboard - [learn more](../../../administration/team-management/02-manage-team-signup-mode.md).
:::

You can also choose to create your own free team by clicking **Create a new team**.

### Creating a new team when registering a profile

After clicking **Create a new team**, you will be suggested to fill out details:

![set_up_your_team_in_Miro.jpg](images/21017005314834_set%20up%20your%20team%20in%20Miro.jpg)
*Setting up a new corporate team*

You can invite your teammates when setting up the team or do this later.

![invite_team_members.jpg](images/21017005315858_invite%20team%20members.jpg)
*The option to invite users when registering a team*

Once the team is ready, you can start creating boards, invite users to collaborate and [upgrade](../../../plans-billing/manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md) to a paid plan according to your needs.

## Creating additional teams in Miro

You can see all your teams on the [left sidebar of your dashboard](04-profile-and-teams.md). Creating new teams for new projects makes the most sense when you need to invite different people to work on them, and want to be able to manage content and access to it in the most efficient way possible.

There are three main options available for users seeking multi-team functionality:

- Purchasing multiple *paid* teams as needed. Click the plus icon on the left sidebar to purchase a new team:
  ![create_a_new_paid_team.gif](images/21016967781138_create%20a%20new%20paid%20team.gif)
  *Creating a new paid team*
- Purchasing a Business Plan. The plan allows creating multiple teams within one subscription (without paying additionally for each new team). Learn [here](../../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/05-manage-business-plan.md#how-to-create-a-new-team) how to create a new team on Business plan.
- Purchasing [Enterprise Plan](../../../plans-billing/miro-plans/04-enterprise-plan.md) if you work in an organization with advanced security and user management requirements, where different teams will be using Miro for different projects. Read how to [create a new team on Enterprise plan](../../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/09-create-a-new-team-on-enterprise-plan.md).

## Frequently asked questions

**I am a member of someone's Miro team but I want to create my own one. How can I do this?**

- You can purchase a new team by clicking the plus icon on the left sidebar of your dashboard. For some users, however, this option may be restricted by corporate Admins via [Domain control](../../../enterprise-administration/canvas-25-admin-features/domain-control/01-domain-control.md). Note that creating a *new* *free team via the plus button* is only possible if you're not a member of any other Miro team (if you're a [guest](../../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md) in someone's team or if you've [left](../../../using-miro/managing-your-profile/06-how-to-leave-a-team.md)/[deleted](../../../administration/team-management/06-delete-and-restore-teams.md) all of your teams).

**How do I join other teams in my organization after I have set up my own team?**

- You may ask your colleagues to [invite you to their teams](../../../administration/user-management/01-invite-users.md) or join [teams discoverable for you](../../../administration/team-management/02-manage-team-signup-mode.md) (if there are any) when clicking the plus icon on the left sidebar of your dashboard.

**Will a member of another paid team take a paid seat in mine?**

- Yes, when inviting a new member to your paid team, you need to purchase a license for them. On Starter plan, you can invite [Trial members](../../../administration/user-management/01-invite-users.md#member-trials-on-starter-plan).
