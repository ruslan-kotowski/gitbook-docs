[Miro](https://miro.com/index/) is the online workspace for innovation that enables distributed teams of any size to dream, design, and build the future together.

With Miro, you can take advantage of a full set of collaboration capabilities, make cross-functional teamwork effortless, and organize [meetings and workshops](../feature-intros/03-miro-for-workshops-meetings.md): use [video chat](../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/18-video-chat.md), [presentation](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/360017731073-Presentation-Mode), [sharing](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md), and [many other features](../../using-miro).

Empower your design, development, and engineering teams to align and innovate in a platform that makes it all possible in real-time. Create concepts, [map user stories](../../using-miro/advanced-tools/07-user-story-mapping.md) or customer journeys, or conduct roadmap planning easily, enabling you to focus on delivering the right products for your customers.
