Create and share custom templates to standardize processes and avoid duplicating work.

## Creating a custom template

From a whole board  From any part of a board  From scratch

1. Select the vertical three dots in the Board bar.
   The **Main** menu opens.
2. Select **Board** > **Export** > **Save board as template**.
   The **Save as a template** modal opens.
3. Complete the on-screen instructions.
4. Select **Save template**.

:::warning
Content in [notes](../../../using-miro/essential-tools/17-visual-notes.md) is not saved as a part of a custom template.
:::

1. [Select a group of objects](../../../using-miro/working-on-the-board/10-working-with-objects.md) that you’d like saved as a template. Make sure they are unlocked. If you want to use locked objects, unlock them first and save the template, then relock them in edit mode.
2. Click the three dots on the context menu.
3. Choose **Save as template**, optionally update your template details, and then click **Save**.

![save_objects_as_template_.jpg](images/4810085542802_save%20objects%20as%20template%20.jpg)*Save as template option in the object's context menu*

1. Click the **Templates** icon on the Creation toolbar.
2. Navigate to the **CUSTOM TEMPLATES** section on the template picker.
3. Click on the option that suits your needs based on your plan (Company, Team, or Personal).
   > ✏️ The options you see will depend on your plan. Starter Plan will see "Personal" and "Team" options under Custom Templates.
   > "Company", "Team", and "Personal" options will be available on Business, Enterprise, and Education plans.
4. Click **Create new template**. A new board will open where you can create a new template from scratch.

![custom_templates_create_new_template.gif](images/14706321586834_custom_templates_create_new_template.gif)
*Creating a new template from scratch*

Your template will be named **New template** by default.

To update your template details (name, description, sharing status, preview) do the following:

1. Select the name of the template in the upper-left corner.
2. Make your changes, and then click **Save changes** to update.![customizing_template_info.gif](images/9624752195218_customizing_template_info.gif)*Saving a new template*

:::note
Custom templates are saved to the library of the team to which your current board belongs. If you save your custom template in one team, you won't be able to access it in another team unless you create it there separately or the template is shared with the whole Company (on Enterprise Plan and Business Plan).
:::

## Using and managing your templates

### Mix and match templates together

You can add as many templates to your board as you want. Feel free to mix templates to best suit your project’s needs.

### See a preview, get details, and use a custom template

When viewing custom templates, the template creator's name and profile picture is shown below the template card. Hover over a template to see the options to use it or see the template preview.

![custom_templates_preview.png](images/9625843845010_custom_templates_preview.png)*Custom templates on the template picker*

Preview helps to easily identify the right template before adding it to a board. The preview shows the template name, description, and a small example of the template. You can also edit and delete your template here.

:::note
If you want to change the board preview area, edit your template and [reset the start view area](../../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#start-view).
:::

![custom_template_preview.png](images/11167923149074_custom_template_preview.png)*Custom template preview*

### Edit and delete your template

> **Set up by:** Template owners, Company Admins with [Content Admin permissions](../../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/12-content-admin-permissions.md) (on Enterprise plan)

:::warning
Content Admins can delete templates and edit board details. If they want to edit the contents of a template they would need to add themselves to the board through the sharing settings.
:::

You can easily modify or remove templates that you created. To edit or delete a template:

1. Navigate within the template gallery to the template you want to edit or delete.
2. Hover over the template you want to affect, and click the three dots on the context menu. The following options will appear:
   - Edit
   - Delete![custom_templates_edit_template.png](images/14706321594770_custom_templates_edit_template.png)*Edit and Delete buttons in the template picker*
3. To edit the template details, click the **Edit** option.
4. Click the template’s name in the top-left corner. You can change the name, description, preview area, and sharing options. Click **Save changes** when you’re finished.
5. To delete a template, click the **Delete** option. This will immediately delete your template. If you want to undo this delete, a message will appear at the bottom of your board with an option to Undo the deletion.

#### **Collaborative template editing**

> Who can do it: Board Owners and Editors, anyone with sharing permissions

Editing custom templates can now be done collaboratively, eliminating the need for version control and back-and-forth emails. Through sharing and collaboration tools, teams can work seamlessly to create templates for their groups or for the whole company.

To enable sharing and collaboration, do the following:

1. Click the **Tempates** icon from the creation toolbar.
2. Select the template you want to edit, click **…** in the top-right corner of the template and choose **Edit**.
3. When the template loads, it will have its own unique URL. You can now click the **Share** button and choose who you want to invite to collaborate with you.
4. You and your collaborators will now see the “Show collaborators’ cursors” and “Comment” toolbar buttons, and everyone invited will be able to work together on the template.
5. When you’re finished editing, click **Save and exit** in the top-left corner of your board to save your changes.![custom_templates_sharing_collaboration.gif](images/14706316557330_custom_templates_sharing_collaboration.gif)
   *Enabling collaboration on custom templates*

### Accessing Company and Team templates

For easier navigation, both Company and Team templates can now be found under a single, personalized company name in the Custom Templates section. This will display the name of the company as found in your Company settings, followed by the word 'templates' (for instance, **Mycompany templates**).

When you access this area, you’ll see two tabs at the top of of right-hand panel: **Company** and **Team**. Any templates you created under or shared with your team will be found in the Team tab. Any templates shared with the company will be found under the Compay tab.![custom_templates_company_and_team_tabs.png](images/14706316559762_custom_templates_company_and_team_tabs.png)
*Navigating custom templates with Company and Team tabs*

## Sharing a custom template

### Access levels for templates

Keep templates private or share them with your team or company to avoid duplicating efforts.

There are three access levels for custom templates: *Personal*, *Team*, and *Company*.

- **Personal** templates are visible to their creator only
- **Shared with the team** templates are available to every team member
- **Shared with the company** templates are shared across all teams within the subscription

:::note
On Business Plan, all team members can publish **company templates** without prior admin approval. This setting is applied by default.
:::

You can update your access levels from the Templates picker by entering edit mode:

1. Click the **Templates** button from the Creation toolbar.
2. Choose the access level your template lives in (Personal, Team, Company).
3. Click the **three dots** in the upper right corner of your template and choose **Edit**.
4. Click the name of the template in the upper-left corner of the board, change your access level, and then click **Save changes** to update.

### Sharing links  for templates

Sharing links helps you direct others to quickly find the right template, extends the reach of recommended resources, and helps you to create your own private [Miroverse](../../../using-miro/miroverse/01-what-is-miroverse.md).

Navigate to the template you want to share and copy the sharing link in two ways:

- Click the three dots on the context menu and select **Copy link**
- Click preview to see the template details and select **Copy link** **to share** in the top right

![copy_template_link.png](images/11168231423890_copy_template_link.png)*The option to copy a template link on the template preview*

When opening the link, your team member will land on the template preview page. Note that you can only copy links to Team and Company templates.

:::note
Visibility notes indicate who can view certain custom templates. These are indicated by clicking the three dots on a template card (visible to), and in the template preview (visible only to). For example, if you create a template only for the developer team, when someone previews the template, they will see “Visible only to Developer team”.
:::

## Frequently asked questions

Can I share my custom templates with other users?

Yes. The fastest way to do this is to use the [Copy link to share option](#sharing-links-for-templates) when previewing a template. If you are on Business Plan, you can share your template with your team or with your organization via the template picker. Simply let them know what the template is called and it will be discoverable in Team templates and via the search bar.
If you're on Enterprise Plan, sharing will depend on [settings chosen by your Company Admin](../../../enterprise-administration/canvas-25-admin-features/data-security/07-sharing-policy-on-enterprise-plan.md).

Can I share my board as a template via a link?

Yes, with the correct Board content settings, now anyone can make a copy of your board with only a link.

1. Open the board **Share** menu > **Sharing settings** > **Permissions.**
2. Allow anyone with board access to copy the board content (note that the menu is available on paid plans only; the option is enabled and non-configurable on boards on [Free plan](../../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md)).
3. Share the board for [public viewing](../../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-boards-via-a-public-link).
4. After sharing a board link, registered Miro users can now go to the board where the option to duplicate the board can be found.

Can I import another user's template to my Miro?

Yes. Simply select and copy the template and paste it on your own board or duplicate the template board to your own team and save it as a template. Note that the option to copy the content and board may be restricted by the board owner.
You can also read more about [how to turn off the ability to copy or export templates](../../../using-miro/sharing-boards/14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md).

How can I delete a template?

1. Open the template picker and find your template in the CUSTOM TEMPLATES area.
2. Click the three dots on your template and choose **Delete**. Note that only Template owners and Company Admins with Content Admin permissions on Enterprise plan can delete templates.

My subscription expired. Will I lose my custom templates?

When your paid subscription expires, the custom templates won't be available until you renew the subscription. Templates that have already been inserted into boards will stay intact.
