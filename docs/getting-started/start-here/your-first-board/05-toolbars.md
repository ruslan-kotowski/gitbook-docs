### Board toolbar main menu

The main menu is available by clicking on the **three dots** (**...**) icon. The Main menu gives you the option to:

- Access **Board**, **Edit**, **View**, **Preferences** and **Accessibility** settings.
- Send to interactive display.

![board-menu.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/28438518338322)
*Board settings / Main menu*

From the **Board** submenu, you can:

- **Make a copy** of [the board](../../../using-miro/managing-boards/03-how-to-duplicate-a-board.md).
- **Export** the board.
- M[ove the board to another team](../../../using-miro/managing-boards/04-how-to-move-a-board.md) or [space](../../../using-miro/spaces/01-spaces.md).
- **Delete** the board.
- Change the board’s **Background color**.
- Start presenting the board.
- View the **Board history**.
- View the board **Details**.

 You’ll also see submenus for Edit, View, and Preferences.

The **Edit** submenu allows you to:

- Undo.
- Redo.
- Commands (which opens a Command modal).
- Find (which can also be accessed via CMD+f).

The **View** submenu allows you to control:

- **Grid**, which opens an additional menu where you can select the type of background grid you want (none, line grid, or dot grid), and if you want objects on the Board to snap to the grid.
- Collaborator cursors toggle.
- Comments on board toggle.
- Scroll bars toggle.
- Object dimensions toggle.
- Undo/Redo controls toggle (this places the Undo and Redo buttons on the bottom left of the board).
- Entering full screen.

The **Preferences** submenu allows you to control:

- Mouse or trackpad interactivity.
- Align objects toggle.
- Follow all threads toggle.
- Profile settings (which opens in a new tab or window).

The **Accessibility** submenu allows you to:

- Turn on or off the Precise selection toggle.
- Turn on or off the Reduce motion toggle.
- Launch the Accessibility checker.

## Creation toolbar

Tools for navigation and editing are available on the **Creation** toolbar on the left side of the board. Click on the **Tools, Media and Integrations** (**+**) icon to see more available tools. To select a tool, just click on the icon or [use a shortcut](../../../using-miro/working-on-the-board/06-shortcuts-and-hotkeys.md) to activate it. Learn more about Miro tools in [this section](../../../using-miro/tools).

![](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21537436261650)
*Creation toolbar*

Note that the tools are available for board owner, co-owners, and [editors](../../../using-miro/sharing-boards/01-board-access-rights.md) but are locked for [viewers](../../../using-miro/sharing-boards/01-board-access-rights.md) and [commenters](../../../using-miro/sharing-boards/01-board-access-rights.md). Commenters can only leave [comments](../../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/asynchronous-tools/01-comments.md) while viewers cannot make any changes to the board.

All tools (except [pen, eraser, lasso](../../../using-miro/essential-tools/10-pen.md), and [comments](../../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/asynchronous-tools/01-comments.md)) are **one-time**. This means that once you use the tool, it will be switched back to **Select**.

### Customizing the toolbar

You can change the tools order and add or remove the ones you don't need. Click and drag tools to change their order on the toolbar or right-click to **Unpin** the tool.

Please note, your tools order and available tools will be saved under your profile only; you cannot change the creation toolbar of other users on the board. Toolbar customization isn't supported on touchscreen devices.

### Drag and drop

To add text, comments, frames, shapes, or stickies to the board, simply drag and drop the icon from the creation toolbar to the board. (Alternatively, you can click the icon and then click on the board to place the item there.)

![](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21537436267794)

*Drag and drop functionality*

**Select/hand tools**

**Select** / **Hand** cannot be removed from the creation toolbar. Click on the pointer to enable **Select** tool and click again to switch back to the **Hand** tool (or use **V** and **H** hotkeys). You can also move around with the right mouse button or by sliding two fingers on a trackpad. [Learn more about navigation in Miro](../../../using-miro/working-on-the-board/05-using-miro-with-a-mouse,-trackpad,-or-touchscreen.md).

## Issues with toolbars

**Toolbars disappear or won’t load**

If some toolbars are not available for you:

1. Make sure that you have editor/commenter access to the board. You can check your access level  by hovering over your avatar in the top right corner of the board.
   ![toolbars-board-permissions.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21537452328850)
*Checking your access rights on the board*
2. If you still experience the issue while working in Miro on desktop, try [these troubleshooting steps](../../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/07-how-to-troubleshoot-and-report-a-bug.md).
3. If the issue perists on **iPad**, please be aware that this may be caused by iPad RAM  limitations. Try the following:

- close all unnecessary background applications before using Miro - this should better the performance
- work on boards that are smaller in size - this should lighten the load of the app on the system
- switch to another device (laptop or computer) with better RAM and use the browser version of Miro

**Tools are hidden on the toolbar**

1. Make sure that you have editor access to the board. You can check your access level by hovering over your avatar in the top right corner of the board.
   ![toolbars-board-permissions.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21537452328850)
*Checking your access rights on the board*
2. Check your browser zoom level - for the best experience, we recommend setting it to 100%. Otherwise, tools may be hidden for you.
   *![browser_zoom.jpg](images/21016134308370_browser%20zoom.jpg)**Browser zoom*
3. Make sure tools are not hidden on your creation/collaboration toolbars.

**Tools are locked/greyed out on the toolbar**

1. Make sure that you have editor access to the board. You can check your access level by hovering over your avatar in the top right corner of the board.
   ![toolbars-board-permissions.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21537452328850)
*Checking your access rights on the board*

If you still experience the issue while working in Miro on desktop, try [these troubleshooting steps](../../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/07-how-to-troubleshoot-and-report-a-bug.md).

## Frequently asked questions

Can I change the size of the toolbar?

If you are working in a browser, you can change the size of board toolbars by changing the browser zoom level.

Can I move toolbars?

This is not currently possible.

Can I hide toolbars?

Toolbars are hidden when you start the [presentation mode](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/360017731073-Presentation-mode). While you’re working on the board, you cannot hide toolbars but you can minimize the creation toolbar by hiding tools.
![removing_tools.gif](images/21016134305170_removing%20tools.gif)*Hiding tools on the main toolbar*
To hide the context menu of objects that appear when you select them, hold down **Shift**.
