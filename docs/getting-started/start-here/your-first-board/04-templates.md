From a spark of inspiration to a quick start for any project, Miro offers a variety of **t****emplates** to help you boost your creativity and productivity.

From Design Thinking exercises, Agile rituals, frameworks for decision making, our [template library](https://miro.com/templates/) has more than 300 custom-crafted templates designed for everyday use cases that'll have you collaborating in a matter of seconds.

Miro also provides templates for Spaces, called Blueprints. You can select a pre-structured Space with boards and formats that best fits your workflow. To learn more about Blueprints, see [Blueprints](#blueprints).

:::

## Finding and using templates to get started

Browse the template gallery to get started with a pre-made framework or combine different templates to keep your project moving forward.

Get into collaboration mode faster by [creating your own template](#creating-a-custom-template), or using a [template](02-custom-templates.md) made by Miro that fits your needs. Templates live in one of four areas:

- [Your dashboard](#from-your-dashboard) is your jumping-off point for starting something fresh
- [The template picker](#from-the-template-picker-templates-and-blueprints). Your custom templates will be available here along with select Miro-made and Community-made Miroverse templates
- The Miro website. You can find all of the Miro-made templates on [miro.com/templates](https://miro.com/templates/)
- [Miroverse](../../../using-miro/miroverse/01-what-is-miroverse.md). You can find all of the Community-made templates on [miro.com/miroverse](https://miro.com/miroverse/)

### From your dashboard

To add a template board from the dashboard, select a template or click **Explore templates** to open the template picker. Once you choose a template, a new board will be created.

### From the template picker – templates and Blueprints

To add a template to your existing board, or select a Blueprint for your team, choose the **Templates** (![icon-templates.svg](images/24123863574546_icon-templates.svg)) iconon the toolbar. Select a template or Blueprint from the template picker.

Template picker allows you to easily browse Miro's **300****+ templates**, including the best-in-class templates made by Miroverse creators. Use search to easily find a template that best meets your needs. The search also applies to all [custom templates](02-custom-templates.md).

![template-picker.png](images/24123863575058_template-picker.png)
*The template picker*

Browse top use cases using categories, topic tags, and featured templates. Learn all about a template with template authors, interactive previews, and educational resources.

Decide if you’d like to start with pre-filled example content (available on some templates) or a blank template.

![pre-filled-template.png](images/24123869107474_pre-filled-template.png)
*The options to use a pre-filled or blank template on the template preview*

If you prefer to start a board from scratch, deselect **Show when creating a new board**, and the template picker will no longer show up every time a new board is created. You can always open the template picker again from the toolbar.

![new-board-template.png](images/24123863576210_new-board-template.png)
*The option to show the template picker every time you create a board*

### Blueprints

The template picker also enables you to select a [Blueprint](../../../using-miro/spaces/04-blueprints-beta.md).

Blueprints are templates for Spaces. You can select a Space Blueprint to pre-structure your Space with boards and formats that suit your workflow and team needs. The boards and formats come pre-populated with everything you need to start your flow.

To access Blueprints from your Miro dashboard, on the right select **Explore templates**. The template picker opens, then select **Blueprints**.

:::tip
You can also access the template picker and Blueprints from a Miro board. Select **Templates** (![icon-templates.svg](images/24123863574546_icon-templates.svg)) from the creation bar. The template picker opens.
:::

:::note
Blueprints are not available on Free plans. Users on a Free plan can see the Blueprints feature, and the option to upgrade their plan.
:::

### Mix and match templates

You can add as many templates to your board as you need. Combine frameworks and activities from multiple templates to seamlessly continue collaborating on the same board. Simply navigate to the template picker from your chosen board and select any pre-made template or [one of your own](02-custom-templates.md).

When the template is added, the elements are automatically ungrouped. You can individually click on an object in order to move or edit it. If you'd like to move more than one object at once, you can [group](../../../using-miro/working-on-the-board/08-structuring-board-content.md) them or [create a frame around them](../../../using-miro/essential-tools/07-frames.md). You can copy template elements and paste them if you need to add more similar items. Feel free to [select all elements of a template](../../../using-miro/working-on-the-board/10-working-with-objects.md) to move it or delete it if you don't need it on your board.

:::tip
Create consistency and match your own unique style by updating the [colors](../../../using-miro/working-on-the-board/14-colors.md) and/or [fonts](../../../using-miro/essential-tools/06-fonts.md).
:::

## Clear Pre-filled Content

If a template's objects are pre-filled with content, you can clear the content of multiple widgets (such as sticky notes, shapes, and cards) simultaneously. This feature allows you to remove all text, tags, emojis, authors, assignees, and due dates from the selected widgets, reverting them to their blank state while retaining their styling (colors, sizes, etc.).

To clear content:

1. Select the widgets you want to clear.
2. Right-click to open the context menu.
3. Choose "Clear content" from the menu.
4. Alternatively, use the shortcut Cmd + Delete (Mac) or Ctrl + Delete (Windows).

## Working with Intelligent templates

Intelligent templates are designed to make workflows more efficient and keep teams engaged during activities like sprint planning, retrospectives, and roadmapping. Intelligent templates include AI, interactive tools, and integrations into a single streamlined experience.

Intelligent templates include functions like:

- Generating a Doc summarizing takeaways from a meeting.
- Generating next steps in a retrospective.
- Adding team members to cards with the People widget.
- Generating insights from user research.
- Getting feedback on ideas.

You can add Intelligent templates to new or existing boards in the same ways you can access regular templates: through your dashboard or the template picker. There’s a section dedicated to Intelligent templates, or you can find them mixed in within the template categories. Look for the **Intelligent** banner in the top left corner of the template thumbnail.

## Sharing links for templates

Get team members up to speed on how you'd recommend using Miro in seconds, not hours. Sharing links help you direct others to quickly find the right template and extend the reach of recommended resources.

Navigate to the template you want to share and you can copy the sharing link in two ways:

- Click the three dots on the template thumbnail and select **Copy link**.
- Click **Preview** to see the template details and select **Copy link to share** in the top right.

## Creating a custom template

If you constantly rebuild or repeat the same activities, or any time you see people copy-pasting a lot, you've found yourself a great candidate for a custom template. Standardize processes and avoid wasting time duplicating efforts by creating custom templates visible to you and your team. [Learn more about creating, sharing, and editing custom templates](02-custom-templates.md).

:::note
Starter plans will see "Personal" and "Team" options under Custom Templates.
"Company", "Team", and "Personal" options are available on Starter, Business, Enterprise, and Education plans.
:::

## FAQ

**Can I import a template from another service to Miro?**

No, but feel free to use any template from the Miro template library or create your custom templates.

**Can I access the template picker from the Mobile app?**

No, this is not currently possible.

**Are Miro templates free?**

Yes, templates from the library and picker are free, while custom templates are supported on paid and Education plans only. Templates that include smart diagramming shape packs are only available on Business, Enterprise, and Education plans.

**Can I create a template board so that other users can copy it to their teams?**

Yes, enable board copying in the board settings and share the board with other users. Registered Miro users will be able to duplicate the board to their own teams.
If custom templates are available in your team, you can create your own template and share it with your team. Learn more here.
