Miro uses role-based access control to manage what a user can see or do in the product. User roles are defined for boards, [Spaces](../../using-miro/spaces/01-spaces.md), teams, and companies. Some user roles may require a specific plan or a paid license.

## Roles for collaboration

**Visitors** are one-time Miro users who are not part of a team or organization. [Visitors](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/08-collaboration-with-visitors.md) are perfect for quick sharing, instant collaboration or a one-off brainstorm session. Visitors can only access public boards shared with them via a link. Visitors don't have to be registered with Miro and can access public boards without a Miro account. Any plan can invite visitors.

**Guests** are occasional Miro users but are also not part of a team or organization. [Guests](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md) are perfect for infrequent collaboration on a private board(s). You can give access to a registered user and revoke their access at any moment. This option is only available on paid plans.

**Members** are full team participants for ongoing collaboration. Members can access all boards shared with them in a team. They have full access to the plan features and can create their own boards and content.

Learn more about the [features available for visitors, guests, and members](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/10-visitors-guests-and-members.md).

## Roles on boards and Spaces

### Roles on boards

Roles on boards can be received directly by invitation or indirectly by [default sharing settings](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/11-default-sharing-settings.md) or [sharing a board on the team level](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md). To configure your board access level, open the board's Share dialog and choose from the roles listed below.

- **Editor**

Editors can use various board tools and settings depending on whether they’re a visitor, guest, or member.  Editors can copy the board content if this is allowed in the [board content settings](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md).

### Roles in Spaces

> **Available for:** Starter, Business, Enterprise, and Education plans

Roles in [Spaces](../../using-miro/spaces/01-spaces.md) are used to grant users specific access to multiple (both existing and newly created) boards in the space. Space-level access and participants are set by the space creator and can then be changed by any space editor for each specific participant.

Roles in Spaces can be received directly by invitation or indirectly by [default sharing settings](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/11-default-sharing-settings.md) or sharing the space on the team level. You can [configure access to your space](21040490154898) at any time.

- **Owner** of a space can edit or delete the space, add/remove boards from the space, make the space visible/invisible to the entire team and define specific users' access. Ownership can be transferred to another user.
- **Co-owners** can be invited to a space, and change access rights of the team and other users in the space but cannot delete a space.
- **Editors** can edit boards in the space and define specific users' access.
- **Commenters** can comment on all boards in the space.
- **Viewers** can view all boards in the space.

Note that space access and board access complement each other and work at the same time:

- If a user already has a role on a specific board, the role with *maximum access* takes effect
- When their membership in a space is revoked, the user loses space-incurred access rights and retains only the roles on boards received directly

## Roles in teams

- **Team Admins** are members with extended rights to manage users, access rights settings, and other team properties. They don’t receive any special level of access to other users' content. If there are a few teams within a subscription, each of them has its Admin. There can be multiple Admins per team
- **Members** are users who were invited to a team and can access all [team-shared](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md) boards and [Spaces](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/16-projects.md) as well as create their own boards. On paid plans, members always occupy a license in your team
- [**Guests**](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md) are users who were invited directly to boards via email, but not to the team itself. They can access the boards but can't create their own ones. Guests are available on paid and Education plans, and guests with edit access are only available on Business plans and on select Enterprise plans
- On the subscription level, [**Billing Admin**](../../administration/admin-faq/01-admin-faq.md) is also recognized. This status is given to a person who starts the subscription having attached their card

To see the list of your team users, [open Team settings](../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/01-i-am-a-new-miro-admin.-where-to-start.md) and switch to **Active users**.

## Company level

> **Available for:** Business Plan, Enterprise Plan

A company can have several teams under its umbrella, please see below the roles at a company level, compared to a team level.

- **Company Admins** are members with extended rights to manage users, Team Admins, security, access rights settings, and other properties of the Company and teams within it. They don’t receive any special level of access to other users' content. There can be multiple Company Admins
- **User Admins** can create and manage users and teams and manage user licenses. This allows the Company Admin to delegate responsibilities without assigning unnecessary permissions.
- **Content Admins** can manage and organize content without needing full admin privileges. This role enables precise control over content access, editing, and sharing, ensuring efficient content management while maintaining data security. For Spaces and sections, the Content Admin can change metadata, move and delete boards inside a Space or section, and move boards to another Space or section. The same privileges apply to private boards, Spaces, and sections. It's an essential role for companies aiming to delegate content-related tasks securely and effectively.
- **Security Admins** can view and edit security management-related settings, such as audit logs, authentication settings, collaboration settings, and managed domains.
- **Members** are those who were provisioned/invited to at least one of the Company's teams. They can access all the [team-shared](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md) or [company-shared](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-a-board-with-the-entire-company) boards and [Spaces](../../using-miro/spaces/01-spaces.md#roles-in-spaces) as well as create their own boards
- **Guests**, previously known as non-team users, are users who are a part of your plan but are not a member of any of the teams. Thus they do not have any access to the boards shared with the team/Company but only to the boards that were specifically shared with them via a direct email invitation

  - When adding a guest, these users get a Free license and can be converted to Full members to see & create team boards and access the full functionality of your Miro paid plan.

:::tip
Learn more about [admin roles and their privileges](../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/02-understand-admin-roles-and-their-privileges.md) in the Enterprise Plan.
:::

## Assigning admin roles

### How to assign a User Admin

> **Available for:** Enterprise Plan

1. Navigate to your [settings](https://miro.com/app/settings/user-profile/).
2. Under **User Management**, click on **Admin roles**.
3. Next to the **User Admin** role, click the three dots (**…**) and select **Assign role** from the dropdown menu.
4. Select the user or users you wish to grant User Admin rights to. You can select up to 50 users.
5. Click the **Assign** button to confirm your selection.
6. To view all users who have been assigned the User Admin role, click the three dots (**…**) again, and select **View users**.

   ![User-Admin-settings.png](images/21016941324434_User-Admin-settings.png)
*Assigning a User Admin role*

To view the permissions assigned to User Admins, click on the **User Admin** bar, switch to the **Privileges** tab, and scroll down to to see all **User & Team Management** permissions.

![User-Admin-permissions.png](images/21016941324562_User-Admin-permissions.png)
*Viewing User Admin permissions*

### How to assign a Content Admin

> **Available for:** Enterprise Plan

1. Navigate to your [settings](https://miro.com/app/settings/user-profile/).
2. Under **User Management**, click on **Admin roles**.
3. Next to the **Content Admin** role, click the three dots (**…**) and select **Assign role** from the dropdown menu.
4. Select the user or users you wish to grant Content Admin rights to. You can select up to 50 users.
5. Click the **Assign** button to confirm your selection.
6. To view all users who have been assigned the Content Admin role, click the three dots (**…**) again, and select **View users**.

![Content-Admin-settings.png](images/21016928361490_Content-Admin-settings.png)
*Assigning a Content Admin role*

To view the permissions assigned to Content Admins, click on the **Content Admin** bar, switch to the **Privileges** tab, and scroll down to see all **Content** permissions.

![Content-Admin-permissions.png](images/21016928361874_Content-Admin-permissions.png)

## Frequently asked questions

**How can I convert a member to a guest?**

On Business, and Enterprise plans you can [convert a member to a guest](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md) in **Company settings > Active users**. On [Team plan](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/08-starter-plan.md), you can only convert a trial member to a guest in **Team settings > Active users**.

**Where can I see what has been changed on my board by a contributor?**

You can check the [board history](../../using-miro/managing-boards/11-board-history-activity.md). If a user is not registered and editing your board while being a visitor, you will not see individual names attached to their activity.

**What access level will my invitees get?**

If you invite users as members of your team, they will be able to see all [team-shared](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md) boards and [Spaces](../../using-miro/spaces/01-spaces.md#roles-in-spaces). If you invite them to a particular board without adding to the team, they will only access this board.

**How can I add another Admin to my team?**

Follow [these steps](../../administration/user-management/06-how-to-manage-admin-roles.md). Note that only Admins can promote other users to the Admin role. Please note that on Education plan, only one Company or Team Admin is allowed.
