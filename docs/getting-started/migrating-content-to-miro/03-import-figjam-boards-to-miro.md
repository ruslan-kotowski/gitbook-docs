You can import your content from FigJam into Miro to consolidate your work into a single, versatile solution. Miro offers a wide range of features, including over a hundred apps and integrations, hundreds of pre-made templates, and unique collaboration tools like [Talktrack](../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/asynchronous-tools/02-talktrack-board-recordings.md) and [Smart Meetings](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/8530534418066-Miro-Smart-Meetings-Updated-BETA-), to enhance your workflow.

:::warning
Editing of imported content is one-way only. Changes made to the content in Miro will not be reflected back in FigJam. The imported content will be a static representation (e.g., image or PDF).
:::

## Export your FigJam board

To begin, you need to export your board content from FigJam. FigJam allows you to export your boards in various formats.

1. Open the FigJam board that you wish to export.
2. Click the FigJam menu icon (typically in the top-left corner).
3. Navigate to **File > Export As**.
4. In the **Export** dialog box, choose your desired export format.
   Options include **PNG**, **JPG**,
   **PDF**, or **CSV** (this option exports sticky
   notes only).
5. Select a **Background** option for your export: Transparent,
   Solid, or Grid.
6. Under **Export area**, choose whether to export the
   **Entire board** or only a specific **Selection**
   of content.
7. Click the **Export** button.
8. Choose a location on your device to save the exported file.
   ![figjam_export_options.png](images/21017005680402_figjam_export_options.png)
   *The FigJam Export as menu and export options*

## Import your FigJam content into Miro

Once you have exported your FigJam board as an image (PNG, JPG) or PDF, you can import it into a Miro board.

1. Navigate to the Miro board where you wish to import the FigJam content. You
   can use an existing Miro board or create a new one.
2. You have two primary ways to upload your file:
   1. **Drag and drop:** Simply drag the exported image or
      PDF file from your computer directly onto your Miro board.
   2. **Upload tool:** On the Creation toolbar (usually on
      the left side of your Miro board), click on
      **Tools, Media and Integrations** (the **+**
      icon). Search for "upload" and then click on the **Upload**
      tool. Choose **My device**, and then navigate to and
      select your exported FigJam file.
3. The file will be uploaded and will appear on your Miro board.
   ![upload_files.gif](images/21016968116754_upload_files.gif)
   *Adding the Upload tool to the Creation toolbar in Miro and uploading a file*

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I bring in content to Miro from other visual collaboration platforms?

Yes! Miro supports importing content from various other tools, including Mural and Lucidchart. The methods may vary depending on the platform.

When should I recreate my FigJam content in Miro versus uploading a static image or PDF?

Consider the following:

- **For projects that are still in progress** and require further iteration, we recommend importing the FigJam content as an image or PDF for reference, and then recreating the specific areas that need additional editing and changes using Miro's native tools. This allows you to fully leverage Miro's features for ongoing work.
- **For projects that are completed** but may need to be referenced in the future, importing the FigJam export as an image or PDF is a good approach. The content will then reside in Miro as the new source of record.

What if I have hundreds of FigJam boards and do not want to migrate them all manually on my own?

If you have a large number of boards to migrate, please contact your Customer Success Manager (if applicable) or [Miro Support](../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/06-contacting-miro-support.md) for information on potential assistance and alternative options.
