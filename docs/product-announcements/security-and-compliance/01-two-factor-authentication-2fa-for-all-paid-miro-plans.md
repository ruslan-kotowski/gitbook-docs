Having seen how valuable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) is for our Enterprise customers, we’re now bringing it to all paid Miro plans: Starter, Education, and Business. Admins can now [enforce 2FA for their org](../../enterprise-administration/security-integrations/two-factor-authentication-2fa/01-enterprise-two-factor-authentication-2fa-–-admin-guide.md) users, ensuring even more secure authentication. End users get the benefit of [using Trusted Devices](../../enterprise-administration/security-integrations/two-factor-authentication-2fa/02-enterprise-two-factor-authentication-2fa-–-user-guide.md) to streamline their sign-in flow, while maintaining security.
