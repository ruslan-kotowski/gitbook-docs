Improve your company’s data management, admin experience and user support, with this new workflow to manage deleted teams. We’ve added a “Deactivated’ tab to the [Team management settings](../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/07-team-management-on-enterprise-plan.md#team-management-settings), where teams can be listed, restored or permanently deleted.
