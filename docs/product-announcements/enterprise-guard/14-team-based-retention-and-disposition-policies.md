Enterprise Guard admins can now scope retention and disposition policies to specific teams in order to tailor content lifecycle management to their specific requirements. Setting retention policies for specific teams helps protect valuable information from being deleted. Setting disposition policies for specific teams helps reduce clutter and maintain the overall quality and relevance of information within each team’s domain.
