Miro AI admin controls enable Enterprise Company Admins to decide which AI capabilities are available in their organization and manage who can use them. Admins can also view the models that power each AI feature. With the Enterprise Guard add-on, Miro AI controls extend to the feature level within each capability category, which helps prioritize features based on organizational needs and security requirements. In addition to the complete Miro AI capability category, Admins can also enable, restrict, or remove specific Miro AI features. For example, within the Images category, you can enable Create images with AI and disable Remove background. Use these controls to roll out AI safely and meet security requirements while driving adoption of AI capabilities.
