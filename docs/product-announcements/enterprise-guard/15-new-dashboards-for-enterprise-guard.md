We are excited to introduce five new dashboards for Enterprise Guard customers. These dashboards provide a comprehensive view of current and historical metrics across various domains: Data Discovery, Data Classification, Content Lifecycle, and eDiscovery. Additionally, an overview dashboard will offer a high-level summary of metrics from all domains. These dashboards will serve as the new entry point into the Content Explorer, which will no longer be a separate menu item.
