Admins now have two options for rolling out Intelligent Guardrails in their organization:

- Default Mode: Guardrails will not affect active sharing options on the board, ensuring minimal disruption to ongoing collaboration.
- Strict Mode: When the "Apply guardrails in Strict Mode" toggle is turned on, guardrails will override all existing sharing options. This may result in some users losing board access immediately.
