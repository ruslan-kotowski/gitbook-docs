We’re excited to announce that board export from the Admin console is now generally available. Enterprise Guard admins can now export boards directly from the Admin console, giving organizations a streamlined way to meet compliance, governance, and legal requirements. This release provides admins with direct access to export boards without relying on extra tools, while offering greater control over governance workflows and simplifying compliance and legal readiness.

Additionally, we’re also releasing bulk download for exported boards. In addition to downloading boards individually, admins can now download all exported boards in a single archive. This update makes it easier to handle large exports, organize board packages, and supports archives larger than 50 GB by automatically splitting them into multiple files.
