Miro is deepening its partnership with Microsoft to launch an integration with Microsoft Purview Data Security Posture Management (DSPM) for AI from the Microsoft Purview portal. Built for organizations using Azure as their identity provider, this feature sends AI prompts and responses from Miro directly to the Purview AI hub. This gives security and compliance teams a single, trusted platform to monitor and control generative AI usage, reducing overhead, mitigating risks like data leaks and misuse, and reinforcing Miro’s enterprise-grade AI governance.
