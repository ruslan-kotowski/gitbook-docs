Teams can now create and collaborate on any type of diagram using the Draw.io/Diagramming.net app, bringing technical documents and people together in one workspace.

- Access to thousands of shapes including Archimate’s Enterprise Architecture, Electrical Circuits, Floor plans and many more
- Import and export Visio (vsdx.), Draw.io/Diagramming.net, and Lucidchart diagrams into Miro to start collaborating
- Apply layers to your diagram to separate different components of a diagram, such as shapes, lines, or text, into distinct layers
