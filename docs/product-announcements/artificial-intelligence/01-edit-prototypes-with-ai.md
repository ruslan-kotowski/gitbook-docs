The Prototypes add-on now includes Edit With AI, which enables structured and controlled iteration on live prototypes. Instead of regenerating a new artifact from scratch, teams can now apply improvements to a prototype that is already applied to the canvas.

For prototypes, Edit with AI removes the friction between creation and refinement.
