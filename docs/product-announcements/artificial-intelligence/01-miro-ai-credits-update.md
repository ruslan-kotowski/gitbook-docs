Starting October 1st 2025, Miro AI credits will renew on the 1st of each calendar month for all organizations, regardless of contract start date.

This change makes credit management more predictable and consistent.

If you have a scheduled renewal before October 1st 2025, you will receive those Miro AI credits as planned, plus your regular allocation on October 1st.

No action is required on your part.
