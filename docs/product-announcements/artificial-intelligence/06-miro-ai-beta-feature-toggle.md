There is now a toggle on the "Feature access" page in settings to enable Miro AI beta features. Beta features - like AI sidekicks and Miro comment summaries - are controlled with this toggle. Future Miro AI beta features will also be controlled by this setting. If Miro AI is set to “No one can use” in settings, the beta toggle will be hidden.
