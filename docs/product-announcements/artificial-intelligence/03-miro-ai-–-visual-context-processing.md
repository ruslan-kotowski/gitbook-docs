When writing your Miro AI prompts, you can now select more widgets on the board as context. **Create with AI** now understands visual context with enhanced accuracy.

**Create with AI** can now process selected context, like images, diagrams, and visual layouts on your board, not just text. On your Miro board, select visual and textual content and click **Create with AI**.

This powerful enhancement enables Miro AI to analyze visual relationships between elements, and deliver more relevant responses to your requests.

For example, use **Create with AI** to expand on an existing diagram, summarize only a specific color of Sticky notes, or create a prototype based on visual examples.
