Based on your prompt, Miro AI now adds relevant [Activities](../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/02-miro-engage-activities-(beta).md) to your [Slides](../../using-miro/formats/13-miro-slides.md) deck automatically when you generate your deck using [Sidekicks](../../using-miro/miro-ai/06-sidekicks-overview.md) or [Flows](../../using-miro/miro-ai/04-flows-overview.md). For example, icebreakers, polls, and word clouds. You can also use Miro AI to enrich your existing deck with Activities based on presentation content.
