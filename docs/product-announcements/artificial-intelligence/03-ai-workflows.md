Starting in February, AI Workflows will transition from public beta to a paid add-on. The add-on unlocks access to Flows and Sidekicks — powerful tools for building AI-powered workflows and generating structured content directly on the canvas.

Sidekicks are Miro's conversational AI that can read your canvas, leverage knowledge sources, create artifacts, and iterate in open ended exploration.

Flows help teams build, share, and run structured AI automations in Miro's canvas. Once purchased, AI Workflows unlocks org-wide access.

The public beta for customers on Free, Starter, and Business plans is ending, and AI actions will begin consuming credits. To learn more about Miro AI Workflows, reach out to sales.
