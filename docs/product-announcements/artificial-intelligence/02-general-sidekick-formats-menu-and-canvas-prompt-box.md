Starting this week, we're gradually rolling out an updated creation toolbar + blank canvas experience that gives you quick access to Miro's formats and a general Sidekick—available on all plans.

You can chat with your general Sidekick to work on problems, summarize board content, and create formats in the canvas.

Talk to Sidekicks to visualize your ideas as diagrams, slides, tables, docs, and more. When you start from a blank canvas, you can start conversations with your Sidekick or start with a suggested prompt.
