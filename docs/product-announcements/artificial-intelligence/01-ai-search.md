AI Search enables you to describe what you want to find in Miro without knowing exact keywords. For example, you can search 'Boards I worked on last week with Steve', or 'I want help roadmapping with my team'. AI Search provides answers with context, and suggests relevant templates. To learn more, see [AI Search](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/33909611360658).
