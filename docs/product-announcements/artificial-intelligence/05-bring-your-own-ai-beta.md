Miro Admins on Enterprise plans can connect the AI model used by your organization to power Miro AI functionality. In the Miro Admin Console, securely share your API key to access your instance of OpenAI or OpenAI through Azure (more AI providers will be coming soon).

With this capability, you can:

1. Leverage your company specific AI
2. Control data paths for functionality that uses your AI model
3. Securely input and store your API key
