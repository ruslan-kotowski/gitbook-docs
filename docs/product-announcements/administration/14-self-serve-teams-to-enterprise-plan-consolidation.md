As a Company Admin you can now bring all teams created from your domains into your Enterprise plan. This ensures enhanced security, collaboration, and streamlined management by unifying all teams in one place. Additionally, you can also audit domains to identify and consolidate users and teams that are part of your managed domain but currently outside your subscription. For more information, [see the Team Consolidation documentation](../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/06-self-serve-teams-to-enterprise-plan-consolidation.md).
