To make it easier to choose the right capabilities for different teams and workflows, Miro is introducing **Standard** and **Advanced** Enterprise licenses.

### **Full is becoming Standard**

If you’re currently on our legacy Enterprise licensing model with Full licenses, you’ll continue using the platform as you do today. AWS Cost Calculator and Jira Cards will only be available with the new Advanced license as part of the migration to the new licensing model. Your account manager will work with you to plan and support the transition, helping ensure it’s as smooth as possible.

### **Core Miro, plus purpose-built workflows for Product Acceleration**

Both **Standard** and **Advanced** include the core Miro experience. This includes the flexible canvas, collaboration, templates, and the foundational capabilities that teams love.

In addition, the Product Acceleration solution helps teams go deeper in specific, high-value workflows, including:

- **Technical Design and Specs**: reduce engineering rework, create clear technical documentation, and generate reliable code in AI tools.
- **Goals, Roadmaps, and Delivery**: connect strategy to execution, surface blockers early, and manage cross-team dependencies.

The table below provides a summary of what’s included in **Enterprise Standard** vs **Enterprise Advanced** for these workflows.

| Enterprise Standard License | Enterprise Advanced License *Recommended for Product, Design, Engineering, IT* |
| --- | --- |
| **Technical Design and Specs** Reduce engineering rework with clear technical specifications that keep teams aligned.  Powered by:   - Basic MCP functionality, like code explanation - 3600+ shapes, layers, PlantUML, Draw.io, etc. - Code block widget | **Advanced Technical Design and Specs** Speed up development and decision making by connecting Miro and your AI tools to build the right thing with better context.  Powered by:   - Advanced Miro MCP capabilities, like summarizing full boards and diagram creation, with integration to all major code-gen tools (GitHub, Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code, and others). - AWS infrastructure visualization and cost calculation |
| **Goals, Roadmaps, and Delivery** Keep product development on track with company goals, timelines, and defined blockers before they derail delivery. Powered by:   - TreeView in tables for clear visual hierarchies - Advanced bi-directional integrations with DevOps systems, such as Jira, Azure, Linear, and others |
