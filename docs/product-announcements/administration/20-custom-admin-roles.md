To better meet the diverse needs of our customers, Miro now offers customizable admin roles. This new feature allows you to define specific scopes and permissions for each admin role, ensuring that each administrator has precisely the access they need to perform their duties effectively. With customizable admin roles, you can tailor Miro’s admin functions for content, users, and security.

Choose the correct functions to align with your organization’s unique requirements, enhancing both security and efficiency.
