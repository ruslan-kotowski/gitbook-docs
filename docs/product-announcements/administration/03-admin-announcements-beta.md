Admin announcements let you post targeted, time‑bound messages to people in your organization from the Admin console. You can write a message, add a call‑to‑action (CTA) that links anywhere (such as signup form, Typeform, Confluence/SharePoint article), choose the audience, and schedule when it appears.

You can use admin announcements to promote training or feature rollouts, and to collect feedback from the right audience at the right time. Choose whether to reach the whole organization or a specific team and drive action with a CTA. Schedule start and end dates so messages go live automatically or remain scheduled until their window.
