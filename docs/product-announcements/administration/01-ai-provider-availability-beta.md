Company Admins on the Enterprise plan with the AI Workflows add-on can now control which AI providers are available across Miro AI features.

This capability allows organizations to manage how AI is used across workflows, Sidekicks, and content generation by enabling only approved AI providers and restricting others based on internal policies. This includes AI-powered capabilities such as Docs, Images, Tables, and AI workflows.

Admins can configure AI provider availability at the capability level, allowing for more granular control across different AI features. Within these constraints, users can select from the available providers and models in supported capabilities.

This enables organizations to:

- Align AI usage with security, compliance, and governance requirements.
- Standardize AI behavior and outputs across teams.
- Test new AI providers before enabling them broadly.

For more information, see the [documentation on AI provider availability](https://help.miro.com/hc/en-us/articles/34182831271570).
