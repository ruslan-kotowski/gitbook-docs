The Miro Admin Console is getting a new look and navigation to enhance efficiency and deliver Miro at scale. The changes include:

- **Streamlined navigation**: Expand and collapse the left navigation to focus on essential tools.
- **Integrated team management**: Teams view is now built into the main UI for easier management.
- **Centralized multi-team actions**: Multi-team organization actions - like updating name or users - are centralized to save time.
- See your Miro plan and admin role directly in the main UI.

These changes are just the beginning of a long and exciting list of UI enhancements we are bringing to Miro Admins, ensuring you can manage your environments more effectively and unlock the full potential of your teams. Learn more at our [Help Center](../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/01-miro's-new-admin-console.md).
