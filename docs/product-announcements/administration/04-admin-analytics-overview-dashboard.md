The Overview dashboard provides insights that enable you to track adoption and understand your organization's activity through the following metrics:

- **Boards:** View board totals, see how many boards are active in terms of usage, and follow historical trends.
- **Users:** Track how many users are actively engaging with Miro over time. You can also track based on a specific user role, such as members, company admins, guests, or team guests.
- **Teams:** Monitor team counts and check activity levels.
- **Licenses:** Track different types of allocated licenses and see how this changes over time.
- **Templates:** Discover which templates are being used most across your organization.
