To achieve team management at scale, Miro is enhancing the teams list in Admin Console with advanced filtering options.

For example, you can filter your teams list by **Team discovery** settings, to find all teams that are hidden. You can also locate teams by their **Team invitation** setting, to find which teams have **All team members** specified.
