Users can directly access Microsoft PowerPoint, Word, and Excel without leaving Miro, enabling them to refer to content, give and receive feedback from stakeholders, and cross reference information that’s normally spread across various tools. With security at the core, users will be required to log in to 3rd party tools, ensuring that access controls apply to the embedded content in Miro.
