We’ve released five new ticketing integrations—Asana, ClickUp, Linear, Rally, and Trello—to **P****ublic Beta**.

#### Availability

- **Enterprise**: All five apps are pre-installed and ready to use.
- **Business**: Find the apps in the Marketplace and request Beta access to install.

#### Looking ahead

When these integrations reach general availability (GA), **Asana** will replace the legacy **Asana Cards** integration and **Rally** will replace the **CA Agile** app. No additional action will be required from Enterprise customers.
