As part of our ongoing effort to enhance the Miro experience, we’re refreshing the canvas design with updates that improve accessibility, flexibility, and visual appeal. These changes include an expanded color palette and redesigned board thumbnail icons, offering users greater customization and a more vibrant interface.

**Miro Colors Update**
We’re introducing an updated set of default colors and palettes to enhance accessibility and usability. With more shades for each color (for shapes, sticky notes, text and other editable board elements), users can enjoy increased flexibility to pair colors that complement each other beautifully, adding polish and professionalism to their content.

**Board Thumbnail Icons Update**
The board thumbnails in the dashboard are getting a fresh new look! Our redesigned icons bring a more dynamic and invigorating aesthetic, making it easier and more enjoyable for users to navigate and represent their boards.
