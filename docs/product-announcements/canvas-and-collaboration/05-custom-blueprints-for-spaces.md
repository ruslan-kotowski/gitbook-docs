You can now create Spaces templates and save them to your organization, team, or personal library. Transform your Spaces into reusable Blueprints for anyone in your organization to adopt.

Custom Blueprints for Spaces enables you to tailor Miro features and capabilities for end-to-end innovation and team collaboration.

Users can find Custom Blueprints in the template gallery.
