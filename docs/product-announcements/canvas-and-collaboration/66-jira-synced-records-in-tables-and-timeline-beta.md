Users can drag and drop Jira cards directly into Tables or Timelines to create Jira-synced records. In this first version, we will sync the title, description, estimation, start and end date fields. As a V2, we will add and sync the status and assignee fields. This will allow you to seamlessly create cross-team plans, mixing your engineer’s work in Jira with that of design, UXR, and marketing. Or use it to mix work from different Jira projects to show cross-team roadmaps, ensuring that your plan always reflects the latest information.
