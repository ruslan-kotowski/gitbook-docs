Formats and Focus modes let your team seamlessly switch between free-form brainstorming and structured Docs, Tables, Diagrams, and Slides. Start with a blank canvas or a structured Format, and use Focus mode to minimize distractions and stay in the flow. Set a Format as the default view to make sure everyone opening the board lands where you want them to.
