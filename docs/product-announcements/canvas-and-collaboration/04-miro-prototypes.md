Starting October 6th, Miro Prototypes will begin transitioning to a paid add-on for Enterprise plans. The add-on unlocks advanced features such as AI creation, screenshot-to-mockup, full-screen preview, and format containers. Once purchased, Miro Prototypes unlocks org-wide access and AI actions consume AI Credits. When teams request access to Miro Prototypes, company admins will also receive an email, making it easier to see where there’s interest across your org. To learn more about Miro Prototypes, reach out to sales.
