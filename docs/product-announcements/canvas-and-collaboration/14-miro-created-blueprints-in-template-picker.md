You can now access Miro-built Blueprints, end-to-end pre-configured guides with all the steps, tools, and best practices to execute your work. Blueprints enable fast creation of a new Space with all the boards, formats, tools, and examples you need to complete your innovation workflow. Blueprints are not available on Free plans; users on these plans will see Blueprints on the dashboard and in the template library, with prompts to upgrade their plan to gain access.
