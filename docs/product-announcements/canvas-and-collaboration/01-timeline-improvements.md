Timeline is a Format for interactive planning and roadmapping. Miro has added several enhancements, like resizeable records column, date ranges in years, not only quarters and months, and jump-to-record, which enables you to navigate quickly to records that are out of view.

For all features and enhancements, see [Timeline](../../using-miro/formats/15-timeline.md).
