Miro will soon upgrade the Create with AI panel. This version enables you to iterate on results without restarting, and experiment in a private stage so you can perfect AI-generated content before sharing to the board. You can also create AI-generated Docs, sticky notes, Diagrams and images on your board. Features like sticky capture and clustering are also added to the upgraded Create with AI panel to make these features more easily accessible.
