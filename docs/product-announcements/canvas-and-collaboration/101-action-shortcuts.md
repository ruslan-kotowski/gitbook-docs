**Action Shortcuts** allow users to place pre-defined actions on a board so that those actions can be taken with the click of a button during a meeting or presentation.

Action Shortcuts are available in certain templates used by facilitators for meetings like retrospectives, planning meetings, design sprints, reviews, and ideations or brainstorms. Action Shortcuts are available to instantly trigger 5 features: start a **timer**, turn on **private mode**, show **Dependencies**, run **Estimations**, and play a **Talktrack**.

![ActionShortcuts-2.png](images/19528227785874_ActionShortcuts-2.png)

*Action Shortcuts allow certain actions to be triggered with a single click*

Placing these Action Shortcuts on specific parts of your board can help you prepare the board for your meetings so they run smoother and are easier to manage. It also helps you keep track of which actions you should take at different times during your meeting or presentation.

You can also create Action Shortcuts directly from the app in your own boards. To add the functionality, select the type of action you want to add, set the parameters the action should follow, if any, and click on the **Add to Board** button.
