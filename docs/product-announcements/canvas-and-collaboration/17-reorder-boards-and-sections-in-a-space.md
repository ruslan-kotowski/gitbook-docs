Users can now easily drag-and-drop boards and sections to reorder them, allowing for more effective Space setup. This feature is available on all paid plans that include Spaces. Note that the feature is not available on mobile devices. Only users with edit or higher permissions to a Space can move boards to sections, and reorder boards and sections inside a Space.
