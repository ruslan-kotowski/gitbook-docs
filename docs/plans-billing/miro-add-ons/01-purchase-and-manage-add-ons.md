Add-ons unlock features and functionalities that enhance the Miro platform. For example, the Miro Prototypes add-on enables you to use AI and advanced prototype features.

Miro currently offers the following add-ons:

- [**Miro AI credits add-on**](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/28898524631058)
  Increases your monthly allocation of Miro AI credits.
- [**Miro Prototypes add-on**](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/28913505056914)
  Generates AI prototypes from screenshots, diagrams, docs, images, or selected brainstorm objects, and includes additional AI credits.
- (Enterprise) [**Enterprise Guard**](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/15699815402514)
  Enhances security and governance to help organizations identify and manage sensitive content inside their subscription.
- (Enterprise)[**AI Workflows**](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/29722516406546)
  Includes Sidekicks, specialized AI agents that work alongside your team on the canvas, and Flows, visual AI workflows that transform your canvas content into deliverables like prototypes, documents, or diagrams.

## Purchase Add-ons

As Billing admin or Company admin, you can purchase Miro add-ons for your organization anytime during your billing period.

:::note
For Enterprise subscriptions, Company admins must contact their account team.
:::

Starter and Business plan Billing admin or Company admins can follow these steps:

1. From your Home dashboard, in the top-right click **Upgrade**.
2. A modal opens that shows pricing for plans and add-ons.
3. Scroll down to review add-ons.
4. For the add-on you want to purchase, click **Add to subscription**.
5. If applicable, select the amount of credits you want to include with your add-on.

   > ✏️ The Miro Prototypes add-on may include additional AI credits, [depending on your plan](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/19756209116178).
6. To complete your purchase, follow the on-screen instructions.
   You are billed using the payment method set up for your organization.

## Add-on billing and renewals

Add-ons are billed with your existing plan subscription and linked to your organization. You can purchase an add-on anytime during your billing period, and you pay a prorated amount for time remaining before your next renewal. Your add-ons renew automatically with your plan subscription in one invoice.

- For monthly plans, the add-on renews each month at the monthly rate.
- For yearly plans, the add-on renews annually.

:::tip
Yearly add-on pricing includes a 20% discount.
:::

## AI credit usage

The AI credit limit for your organization includes plan and add-on credits. Your AI credits apply to the organization and can be consumed for any AI actions in Miro by any member with a full license.

For monthly and yearly plans, AI credits reset monthly.

**More information:** See [Miro AI credits](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/19756209116178).

## Cancel Add-ons

You can cancel add-ons any time. After cancelation, add-ons remain active on your account until the end of your current billing period.

Follow these steps:

1. From your Miro dashboard, click your profile avatar in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Admin console | Settings**.
3. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
4. In the **Billing actions** section, for the add-on you want to cancel click **Cancel add-on**.
5. A dialog box opens.
6. Click **Continue to cancel**.
   The **Billing** view now shows the date when the add-on is removed at the end of your current billing period. When the add-on is removed, the AI credit limit for your organization reduces to the limit corresponding with your plan allocation.

:::note
You can reactivate the add-on anytime before renewal.
:::

## Plans eligible for add-ons

Add-ons are available for paid monthly or yearly subscriptions, namely Starter, Business, and Enterprise plans.

If you are on a Free plan, learn how to [upgrade your plan](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/360017730273-Upgrade-your-plan).

If your plan has expired, then you must [reactivate your subscription](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/26757556067986-Reactivate-your-subscription).

If you are currently on a Business plan free trial, wait until the trial ends then [purchase your add-ons](#purchase-add-ons).

:::note
For Enterprise subscriptions, Company admins must contact their account team.
:::

## FAQ

**How to add more credits to the current plan (increase the credit balance)?**

When your account runs out of AI credit, a modal opens in Miro that shows your options for additional AI credits, including the paid [Miro AI add-on](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/28898524631058). You can upgrade to unlock more AI credits or request the Miro AI Add-on to get more credits through the Support Contact form. Enterprise plan Admins must contact their account team to get the [Miro AI add-on](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/28898524631058).

**Can I activate add-ons on monthly basis if my plan is currently on yearly?**

No, the add-on billing period must be the same as your underlying plan subscription. To activate add-ons on monthly basis, you have to [downgrade to a monthly plan](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/4408891948946).
