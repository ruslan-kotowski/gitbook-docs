As Company admin, you can set the licensing configuration for your organization. Your licensing configuration specifies whether anyone in your organization can invite new members to their team.

:::note
License configuration is a setting available on Business plans. To learn about setting the sharing policy for an Enterprise plan, see [Sharing policy on Enterprise Plan](../../enterprise-administration/canvas-25-admin-features/data-security/07-sharing-policy-on-enterprise-plan.md).
:::

Licensing configuration is an organization level setting. If enabled, then any team can optionally allow all its members to add licenses.

A license is added to your subscription when a user outside your organization accepts an invitation from an existing team member.

In Admin Console, when you enable your licensing configuration, a banner appears at the top of the **Billing** and **Active users** sections, and below the **Team invitation settings** in Team permissions.

For Business plans, licensing configuration is enabled by default. To check your licensing configuration, see [Set licensing configuration](#set-licensing-configuration).

:::tip
In team settings, Company admins can specify whether all members or only admins can add licenses for a given team.
:::

## Set licensing configuration

### Business plan

1. From your Miro dashboard, click your avatar in the top-right, then select **Admin Console**.
2. Go to **Billing**.
3. Under **Licensing configuration**, toggle **Anyone can expand the team** to the on position. To disable, toggle to the off position.

## What next?

With the licensing configuration enabled for your organization, each team in your subscription can decide whether to allow all members to add new licenses viz newly invited members.

:::note
Newly created teams have **All team members** in Team permissions enabled by default.
:::

When a member shares a board with an invitee, the member sees the following message informing them that adding a new member incurs an increase in licensing and cost.

**More information:** See [Sharing boards](../../using-miro/sharing-boards).
