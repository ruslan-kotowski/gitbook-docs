Learn where to find your billing settings, how to manage payment methods, update invoice and billing information, change your team size or subscription type or upgrade your plan. You can also cancel or reactivate a subscription, and request a refund.

:::note
If your subscription is set to cancel, then you must reactivate your subscription to update your billing settings. You can cancel your subscription again after you make your changes.
:::

## Billing settings

Within your billing settings you can:

- Access billing information and update your billing details
- Edit your billing email address
- View invoices
- View your current plan details
- Upgrade your plan
- Review and change your payment method
- Adjust your team size by adding or removing licenses
- Manage your license configuration
- Cancel your subscription
- Reactivate your subscription

### Who can edit or update your Billing settings

The below users have access to Billing settings and can manage your subscription:

- Team Admins on Starter Plan
- Company Admins on Business Plan
- Billing Admins

### Admins versus Billing Admins

A user is made Billing Admin when they upgrade their team. That does not automatically give them Admin rights. The Billing Admin is the cardholder and pays for the subscription. They have access to Billing settings as long as they're a member of the team.

The Billing Admin can add or remove licenses, cancel and reactivate the subscription, as well as request refunds. However, unlike a Team Admin, they can't remove users, manage permissions or apps, and they don't have access to other settings.

**Billing Admins can request Admin rights**: go to Team settings > Active users, click the three dots next to your name, and choose Send request.

### Where to find your Billing settings

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The Billing Overview page appears.

## Update your credit card information

You can update your credit card information anytime within your Miro billing settings.

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The Billing Overview page appears.
3. Click the **Payment methods**.
4. Click **Change payment method**.
5. Enter the new card details, and then click **Update**.

## Change your invoice and billing details

You can change your billing details that appear on future invoices and receipts.

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The Billing Overview page appears.
3. Click **Billing information**.
4. Update your billing details.
5. Scroll down and click **Save settings**.

## Change your billing email

All invoices and receipts are sent to the email address specified in the Overview of your Billing settings.

:::note
Owners of the billing email are not necessarily Billing admins. The Billing admin attaches their card to the account and initiates the subscription. If the Billing admin is also a team member, they can access Billing settings. The owner of the billing email, if they are not also a Billing admin, cannot access or manage Billing settings, even if they are a team member.
:::

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The Billing Overview page appears.
3. Scroll down to **Billing actions**.
4. Click **Change email**.
5. Enter a new email address.
6. Click **Save**.

## Switch between annual and monthly subscriptions

You can easily change from an annual to a monthly subscription or vise versa. Note that the switch to monthly will not be immediate. The switch to monthly will be scheduled at the end of your current yearly billing cycle (at time of renewal) so that you get the maximum value from your current purchase.

:::note
Switch from annual to monthly is only available for credit card payments, not invoicing, and takes effect at next renewal.
:::

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The Billing Overview page appears.
3. Under the **Plan details** section, click **Switch to monthly**(or **Switch to annual**).
   You’ll see the exact details of your subscription and price.
4. Click **Change to monthly** (or **Change to annual**) to confirm.

## Change your team size (add or remove licenses)

You can add or remove licenses in your subscription. However your team size (the number of licenses allocated in your plan) can't be less than the current number of members in your team. For example, if your team has 17 members and you have 21 licenses, you can remove a max of 4 licenses.

To remove licenses, you may need to [remove team members](../../administration/user-management/08-remove-users.md) first in the **Active users** tab under **User and Team Management** (this option is available for Admins only).

You may also increase your team size by directly inviting members to your board as Team members in the board sharing settings. As soon as new members join your team, the number of paid licenses increases. Admins can customize the [invitation settings](../../administration/user-management/02-invitation-settings.md) to prevent [accidentally added licenses](../../administration/user-management/04-manage-extra-licenses.md).

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The Billing Overview page appears.
3. Under the **Plan details** section, click **Change team size**.
4. Select the number of licenses.
5. Click **Confirm**.

## Manage your license configuration

Easily manage new license requests and help your team grow. Once license configuration is enabled, users are automatically added as team members when invited to boards. Anyone with [invitation rights](../../administration/user-management/02-invitation-settings.md) can invite new members. You'll be charged for the additional licenses in your next billing cycle. This setting is turned off by default.

Business Plan Starter Plan

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The Billing Overview page appears.
3. Scroll down to **Licensing configuration**, and then click to **t**oggle on **Anyone can expand the team.**

On Starter Plan, licensing configuration is enabled for everyone by default, but Company Admins can control who can use it by adjusting their team invitation settings.

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Security**, and then click **Permissions**.
3. Under the **Team invitation settings** section, select who you want to give permission to invite others and configure licenses. You can additionally include the team invite link so others can join the team.

## Cancel your subscription

You can cancel your subscription anytime.

After you cancel the subscription, you can use the paid features until the end of the paid period. When it's over, your boards are *not* deleted. Instead, [all boards are locked in view-only mode](07-expired-subscription-reference.md) until you reactivate your subscription or downgrade to a free plan. Learn more about [canceling your subscription](06-cancel-your-miro-subscription.md).

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The Billing Overview page appears.
3. Under the **Billing actions** section,click **Cancel subscription**.

> *✏️* You can’t change or update your team size, billing or plan type if your subscription is canceled.

### Canceling your subscription with invoicing enabled

To cancel your subscription:

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The Billing Overview page appears.
3. Click **Cancel subscription**. If you cancel your subscription before paying your first invoice, the team will be downgraded to the [Free plan](../miro-plans/09-free-plan.md) immediately.

If you already paid the invoice, you will have access to all paid features until the end of the paid period, but you won't be able to add new licenses to your team. In case you added new licenses during the current quarter, you'll receive an additional invoice for the outstanding payment.

## Reactivate your subscription

In case you cancel your subscription but would like to use it again later, you can reactivate it before the expiration date.

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The Billing Overview page appears.
3. Under the **Plan details** section, click **Reactivate subscription**

## Upgrade or downgrade your plan

To can upgrade or downgrade your Miro plan at any time:

1. Click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The Billing Overview page appears.
3. Click **Change plan**.

For more details on how to upgrade a plan or switch plan types, visit [Upgrading your plan](03-upgrade-your-plan.md).

To downgrade your plan, visit [Downgrading your plan](04-downgrade-your-plan.md).

## Update your payment method

Starter and Business plan users can [change from credit card to invoice payments](../billing-and-payments/05-payment-options-invoicing-and-automated-payments.md) anytime in the **Billing overview** under **Plan details**.

To switch from invoicing to credit card payments, you will need to [contact Miro Support](../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/06-contacting-miro-support.md).

## Request a refund

If you think that you were incorrectly charged, [contact Miro Support](../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/06-contacting-miro-support.md) and include the transaction details. Please note that some special offers are non-refundable.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I remove my card from the settings?**

Yes, you can replace your card with another one or cancel your subscription if you'd like to stop further charges.

**Can I transfer my subscription to another person?**

Yes, invite the person to the team, [promote them to the Admin](../../administration/user-management/06-how-to-manage-admin-roles.md), and set their email as the billing contact in Billing settings. The user will be able to manage the plan and subscription and will receive billing-related notifications.

**Can I transfer my subscription to another team?**

No, but you can move your boards to the upgraded team. You can also invite other users to the team so that they can move their boards.
