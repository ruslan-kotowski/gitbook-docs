Miro plans provide flexibility, allowing you to adjust your subscription by upgrading or downgrading at any time to better align with your current requirements.

Downgrading your plan means moving to a subscription tier with a different set of features and a lower cost, which can be beneficial if your team's needs have changed or if you require fewer advanced functionalities.

This article explains how to downgrade your plan. To learn about the implications of downgrading your plan, see [Downgrade your plan reference](05-downgrade-your-plan-reference.md).

:::note
Only Admins or Billing Admins can initiate a plan downgrade. The process involves canceling your current subscription and then selecting a lower-tier plan once the current subscription period expires.
:::

## Key implications of downgrading

Understanding the implications of a downgrade is important for a smooth transition.

- **Feature Access**
  Downgrading will change the features available to you and your team. For example, access to custom templates or the number of active boards may be reduced.
- **Data Retention**
  While Miro aims to preserve your data, some content might become view-only or locked depending on the plan you downgrade to (e.g., private boards on a Free Plan).
- **Billing Adjustments**
  Your billing will be adjusted according to the new plan's rate. Any outstanding payments for additional licenses may be charged upon cancellation of the higher-tier plan.
- **Team Structure**
  If downgrading involves plans with different team structures (e.g., from a multi-team Business Plan), teams might be merged.

**More information:** See [Downgrade your plan reference](05-downgrade-your-plan-reference.md).

## Downgrade your Miro plan

### Starter to Free plan

1. Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The **Billing Overview** page appears.
3. Under the **Billing actions** section, click **Cancel subscription**.
   A dialog box appears.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. Follow the on-screen instructions to specify your reason for downgrading, and that you are staying with Miro and just switching plans.
6. Click **Cancel subscription**.
7. On the date when your current Starter plan expires, you are prompted to choose your new plan. Under **Free plan**, click **Downgrade**.
   > 💡 See the expiration date for your current plan under **Plan details** in your billing settings.

### Business to Starter plan

1. Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The **Billing Overview** page appears.
3. Under the **Billing actions** section, click **Cancel subscription**.
   A dialog box appears.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. Follow the on-screen instructions, and review the presented details about downgrading your plan.
6. At the bottom of the dialog box, tick the checkboxes to agree to Miro's Terms of Service, and agree to downgrade your plan at the end of your billing cycle.
   > 💡 See the expiration date for your current plan under **Plan details** in your billing settings.

   > ⚠️ If you have multiple teams, they will be merged into one Free plan. All Members, Guests, and boards from all teams will be merged, and all Guests will be converted to Members. Private boards will become locked.
7. Click **Continue**.
8. Select the reason why you want to downgrade.
9. Click **Downgrade**.
   A dialog appears to confirm that your plan downgrade is scheduled.

:::tip
If you wish to cancel your downgrade, before your billing cycle ends go to **Admin console** > **Billing** > **Cancel downgrade**.
:::

### Business to Free plan

1. Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner, and then click **Admin console**.
2. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The **Billing Overview** page appears.
3. Under the **Billing actions** section, click **Cancel subscription**.
   A dialog box appears.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. Follow the on-screen instructions to specify your reason for downgrading, and that you are staying with Miro and just switching plans.
6. Click **Cancel subscription**.
7. On the date when your current Business plan expires, you are prompted to choose your new plan. Under **Free plan**, click **Downgrade**.
   > 💡 See the expiration date for your current plan under **Plan details** in your billing settings.
8. Review the presented details about managing access before downgrading, and make any necessary changes to your boards, team members, and guests.
   > ⚠️ If you have multiple teams, they will be merged into one Free plan. All Members, Guests, and boards from all teams will be merged, and all Guests will be converted to Members. Private boards will become locked.
9. Click **Downgrade**.

Frequently asked questions

**Why am I still being charged after downgrading?**

Upon canceling, you may receive an additional [prorated invoice](../billing-and-payments/04-miro-billing.md#monthly-and-yearly-billing) for extra seats you’ve added during the billing period.
