Miro plans are flexible, which means you can upgrade or [downgrade](04-downgrade-your-plan.md) at any time.

## How upgrades work

You can upgrade Teams within Miro. A **Team** is a collection of boards and [projects](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/16-projects.md). Your Miro plan is connected to your team.

You can be a member of [multiple teams](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md#navigate-between-your-teams) by creating your own, or through invitation to teams owned by other Miro users*.*

You can view and navigate between all your current teams in the panel on the left side of your [dashboard](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md#navigate-between-your-teams).

## Billing changes after upgrading your plan

As soon as you complete the purchase, your team will be upgraded and you’ll receive a payment receipt to your email address and within your billing settings.

- After upgrading, the person who purchased the subscription is promoted to Billing Admin. The Billing Admin is the cardholder who pays for the subscription. They have access to Billing settings as long as they're a member of the team. Billing Admins can add or remove licenses, cancel and reactivate the subscription, as well as request refunds.
- If you upgrade a paid team, for example Starter to Business, a new billing period will begin and you’ll receive a new invoice. The [prorated amount](../billing-and-payments/04-miro-billing.md#general-billing-information) for any unused time on your previous plan will be deducted from your new invoice.
- If you upgrade with [invoicing](../billing-and-payments/05-payment-options-invoicing-and-automated-payments.md), the invoice will be sent within an hour to your billing email. The invoice will include your new billing due date. It should be paid within **30 days.** Remember to add your company invoice details during the upgrade. Invoices are finalized *within 1 hour* and you'll be able to download and pay the invoice either in your Miro billing settings or in the billing email.

## How to upgrade your plan

Upgrade to unlock advanced features. Your existing team boards are not affected.

:::note
You can only add up to 100 licenses in the upgrade flow. To add more than 100 licenses, after upgrading go to Settings > Billing > Overview > Change team size.
:::

Free to Starter Free to Business Starter to Business Enterprise

To upgrade from Free Plan to Starter Plan:

1. Choose a [team to upgrade](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/04-profile-and-teams.md#upgrading-a-team).
2. In the top-right of the team dashboard, click **Upgrade**.
   A modal opens with a table comparing plan options.
3. Under **Starter Plan**, click **Upgrade**.
4. Select the number of **Licenses**. If you require fewer licenses than in your current plan, first you will need to [remove extra users from your team](../../administration/user-management/08-remove-users.md).
5. Choose your **Payment method** (credit card or invoice) and **Billing period** (yearly or monthly)**:**

   [**Invoice payments**](../billing-and-payments/05-payment-options-invoicing-and-automated-payments.md) are only available for Starter plans on a yearly subscription with 10+ licenses. If you select **Invoice**, your **Billing period** will automatically change to **Yearly**. Adjust the number of **Licenses** to a minimum of 10 to continue. Enter your billing email, as well as any tax and company information you want reflected on your invoices. Click **Purchase**.
    **[Credit card payments](../billing-and-payments/05-payment-options-invoicing-and-automated-payments.md)** are available for monthly and yearly plans. Under **Payment method** select **Credit card**. Enter your credit card information, as well as any tax and company information you want reflected on your invoices. Click **Purchase**.

## Request an Education plan

Staff members and students of educational institutions can [apply for our free Education plan](https://miro.com/education-whiteboard/). Once your application is approved, you’ll receive an email notification.

**If you're a student** and the Team Admin for a team of 10 members (or less), the team will be converted to an Education plan.

**If you're a teacher** and the Team Admin for a team of 100 members (or less), the team will be converted to an Education plan.

If you do not fit the above criteria, a new Education team will be created in your profile. You can find your Education plan team on the left sidebar of your [dashboard](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md#navigate-between-your-teams). You’ll be able to [move your boards](../../using-miro/managing-boards/04-how-to-move-a-board.md) to the new Education Plan team.

## Frequently asked questions

**I purchased a subscription but my plan is still free. Why?**

Try to switch to your paid team on the left sidebar of your [dashboard](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md#navigate-between-your-teams).

**Why don't I have the Upgrade button on my dashboard?**

The team you're trying to upgrade is already on a paid plan. Follow the instructions for [How to upgrade](#how-to-upgrade-your-plan) Starter to Business, or request an Enterprise plan.
