You can reactivate your subscription at any time before or after it expires.

1. Ensure you have switched to the team associated with the subscription you wish to reactivate.
2. From your Miro dashboard, click your profile avatar in the top-right corner.
3. Select **Admin console**.
4. On the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
   The **Billing Overview** page appears.
   > 💡 The **Billing Overview** also shows the expiration date for your current plan if it has been cancelled.
5. In the **Billing actions** section, click **Reactivate subscription**.
   A dialog box appears and confirms that your subscription has been reactivated.
