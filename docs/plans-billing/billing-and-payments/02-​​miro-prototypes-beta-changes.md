Miro Prototypes is a [paid add-on](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/28897507989138) for Starter, Business, and Enterprise plans.

This article explains what's changing for Miro Prototypes, when, and how it impacts your team.

:::note
During the beta, these capabilities were called Miro Prototyping. It is now called Miro Prototypes.
:::

## What's changing?

- **Enterprise (October 2025)**
  The beta program ends. To enable Miro Prototypes for your organization, as Company admin, or Billing admin, do one of the following:
  - Contact your Miro sales representative.
  - Complete [this form](https://miro.com/prototyping/contact/sales/).
- **Starter and Business (August 2025)**
  To continue to use Miro Prototypes features, as Company admin, or Billing admin, you must purchase the add-on.
- **Free and Education (August 2025)**
  To be eligible to purchase the Miro Prototypes add-on, admins must upgrade to a paid plan.

:::note
Existing prototypes remain editable across all plans. Only advanced features, like AI tools, connector lines, and preview mode, are disabled if you do not purchase the Miro Prototypes add-on.
:::

## Pricing overview

The Miro Prototypes add-on includes a base price and flexible AI credit bundles. This lets you choose the amount of credits to match your team's needs.

The add-on is billed together with your plan and renews automatically unless you cancel it.

Here’s how billing and credits work:

- Added to Starter, Business, or Enterprise subscriptions
- Your chosen bundle increases your monthly AI credit limit
- Credits reset each month
- Credits are shared across all Miro AI features, including Prototypes

If you cancel your Starter or Business subscription, your Prototypes add-on will also be canceled. For Enterprise, billing and renewals follow your contract terms.

Enterprise Company admins can [reach out to their Miro sales rep](https://miro.com/prototyping/contact/sales/) to discuss custom pricing.

**More information:** See [Manage and purchase add-ons](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/28897507989138).

:::note
The core Prototyping (Wireframing) component library remains free for all users.
:::

## Getting the add-on

  [Contact your Miro sales representative](https://miro.com/prototyping/contact/sales/) to purchase and enable. Miro Prototypes features will continue working until the beta program ends, after which they will require the add-on.

## FAQ

**Will I lose access to my prototypes if I don't buy the add-on?**

No, you’ll still be able to edit device frames and content. Only advanced features, like AI tools, connector lines, and preview mode will be disabled if the add-on isn’t active.

**Can I try the add-on before I buy it?**

Yes. On a Starter or Business plan, you can start a 7-day free trial from your Billing settings. Enterprise customers can request a trial through their Miro sales rep.

**Who can purchase and manage the add-on?**

For Starter and Business plans, Company admins and Billing admins can purchase in their Billing settings. For Enterprise, Company admins can purchase via their Miro sales rep. Enterprise Company admins can also monitor the add-on in Billing settings in Admin Console.

**Do guests and viewers have access to Miro Prototypes features?**

[Guests](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/360021415119) can see and interact with prototypes, but only full-license members can create and edit with AI.

**How do AI credits work with the Miro Prototypes add-on?**

The add-on includes flexible AI credit bundles. Credits are shared across all Miro AI features, including Prototypes. To learn more about Miro AI credits, see [Miro AI credits](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/19756209116178).
