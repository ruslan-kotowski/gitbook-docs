Miro has introduced the Business + [AI Workflows](../../using-miro/miro-ai/02-miro-ai-workflows-overview.md) plan, which includes [Flows and Sidekicks, and several advanced features](#what-does-the-new-business-plan-include), that help teams coordinate, visualize, and automate their work.

To access [AI Workflows](../../using-miro/miro-ai/02-miro-ai-workflows-overview.md) and advanced features on a Business plan, customers on the legacy Business plan must [upgrade](#how-to-upgrade) to the new Business plan.

:::tip
Trial all Business + AI Workflows features free for 14 days. Users on selected Free, Starter, and legacy Business plans can enroll in the free trial. To learn more, see [Business + AI Workflows plan 14-day free trial](07-business-plan-14-day-trial.md).
:::

**More information:** See [Impact on existing Business plan customers](#impact-on-existing-business-plan-customers).

:::note
First-time subscribers to a Business plan will only be able to purchase the Business + AI Workflows plan. The legacy Business plan will be sunset.
:::

## What does the new Business plan include?

The Business + AI Workflows plan includes all the legacy Business features, plus [AI Workflows](../../using-miro/miro-ai/02-miro-ai-workflows-overview.md) and the following advanced features:

- AI Workflows
  - [Sidekicks](../../using-miro/miro-ai/08-ai-sidekicks.md)
    Specialized AI agents that work alongside your team on every canvas
  - [Flows](../../using-miro/miro-ai/04-flows-overview.md)
    Visual AI workflows that transform your content into deliverables like prototypes, documents, or diagrams
- [Knowledge](../../using-miro/miro-ai/09-knowledge.md) integrations
- 50 AI credits per member per month.
  To learn more about Miro AI credits and how to apply additional credits, see [Miro AI credits](../billing-and-payments/03-miro-ai-credits.md).
- Model content protocol (MCP) server
- Records nesting and TreeView visualization for Tables
- Bi-directional ticketing system integrations
- Approval and status workflows

## Impact on existing Business plan customers

Miro is replacing the legacy Business plan with the Business + AI Workflows plan. Meanwhile, upgrading to the Business + [AI Workflows](../../using-miro/miro-ai/02-miro-ai-workflows-overview.md) plan is voluntary.

Before you upgrade, you may be eligible to [enroll in a 14-day free trial](07-business-plan-14-day-trial.md) to experience the full range of features designed to enhance business performance, including AI Workflows.

Customers now on a legacy Business plan can continue using their Business subscription without disruption. However, legacy Business plan customers can access only the default Sidekick, and have a maximum of 5 Flow runs per member. The runs count does not reset.

Miro will sunset the legacy Business plan in approximately 1 year. Existing customers who do not upgrade will receive advance notice before any changes occur.

## How to upgrade

Company admins and Billing admins can upgrade anytime. In Admin Console, go to **Billing** > **Subscription**.

:::note
If you have a coupon applied to your existing Business plan, then your coupon is retained when you upgrade to the new Business + AI Workflows plan.
:::
