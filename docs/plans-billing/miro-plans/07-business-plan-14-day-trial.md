Experience the [Business + AI Workflows](01-introducing-the-business-+-ai-workflows-plan.md) plan with a 14-day free trial. Users on selected Free, Starter, and [legacy Business](06-business-plan.md) plans can explore the full range of features designed to enhance business performance, including AI Workflows.

The Business + AI Workflows plan includes [all features on the legacy Business](02-plans-and-features-available.md) plan, with several advanced features, including specialized AI agents and Miro's [MCP server](../../integrations-apps/miro-mcp-server-beta/01-miro-mcp-server-overview.md).

This article describes the Business + AI Workflows free trial for [Free](#business-trial-for-free-plans), [Starter](#business-trial-for-starter-plans), and [legacy Business](#business-ai-workflows-trial-for-legacy-business-plans) plans.

## Business trial for Free plans

As a user on a Free plan, you are eligible to trial the full range of Business and advanced AI features free for 14 days. When you enroll for the trial, you provide credit card information at checkout.

Unless you cancel during the 14-day period, your trial automatically converts to a paid Business subscription.

### Add team Members and Guests during the trial

During the trial, you can add new team Members and Guests with edit access at no cost. After the trial period, additional members and guests join your Business subscription.

### Cancel the trial

You can cancel the Business trial anytime during the 14-day period and you will not be charged. Your account will expire and your account enters [a view-only mode](../manage-your-subscription-and-plan/07-expired-subscription-reference.md).

:::tip
Upgrade to a [Starter](08-starter-plan.md) plan to continue using [some advanced features](02-plans-and-features-available.md). Members you added during the trial transition with you to your Starter subscription.
:::

To cancel and revert to your Free plan after the trial period expires, follow these steps:

1. In the top-right click your profile avatar, then click **Profile settings** | **Settings**.
2. Go to **Billing**.
3. In the **Licensing configuration** section, click **Cancel trial**.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. Select **Cancel trial**, then click **Cancel subscription**.
6. Select a reason for cancelling the Business plan trial.
7. Click **Cancel trial**.
   A message shows confirming that you have cancelled your Business trial. You can continue to use Business features until the trial period expires.

After cancelling the trial, your account will expire and your account enters a view-only mode. To reactivate your account after the trial ends, choose a paid plan or revert to your Free plan in the pricing page when you log into Miro after the trial ends.

:::note
If you do not cancel during the trial period, then your subscription transitions automatically to the Business plan with charges reflected on your next invoice.
:::

## Business trial for Starter plans

As Company admin or Billing admin, you can enroll your team in a 14-day Business trial, with no price change for your Starter subscription. Each team member can trial the full range of Business plus advanced AI features free for 14 days.

Unless you cancel during the 14-day period, your trial converts automatically to a paid Business subscription.

:::note
Unused time on your Starter plan is prorated on your first Business + AI invoice.
:::

### Add team Members and Guests during the trial

During the trial, you can add team Members and Guests to your Business plan at no cost. When the trial ends, new Members and Guests automatically join your Business subscription.

:::note
If you cancel the trial, new members transition to your Starter subscription and license fees apply. You can [monitor and manage](#manage-licenses-and-billing-during-the-trial) your licenses at any time.
:::

:::note
You can add free Guests on a Starter plan. However, only on a Business plan can Guests optionally have edit privileges. If you cancel your Business trial, Guests with edit privileges revert to Comment or View after the trial period ends.
:::

### Manage licenses and billing during the trial

- **Change payment method**
  During the trial, you can optionally update your payment details, including credit card information, and default payment method.
- **Starter plan renewal**
  If your Starter plan renews during the trial, you are charged only for the Starter Plan, with prorated adjustments if you continue with a Business plan after the trial expires.
- **Control costs**
  If you decide to cancel, add or remove team members before the trial expires to control future costs.

### Cancel the trial

You can cancel anytime during the 14-day trial. If you cancel, then you return to your Starter plan after the trial period expires, with no changes or additional charges to your Starter subscription.

To learn how to cancel, see [Cancel the Business trial for Starter and legacy Business](#cancel-the-trial-for-starter-and-legacy-business-plans).

### Role changes when returning from Business to Starter

After the trial expires, Business Company admins become Starter Team admins. Likewise, Business Team admins become Starter team members.

:::note
Role changes impact access to settings and privileges.
:::

:::tip
If you decide to cancel, ensure you review and update admin roles before the trial period expires.
:::

## Business + AI Workflows trial for legacy Business plans

As Company admin or Billing admin, you can enroll your team in a 14-day Business + AI Workflows trial, with no price change for your legacy Business subscription. Each team member can trial the full range of Business plus advanced AI features free for 14 days.

The Business + AI Workflows plan includes all the legacy Business features, plus [AI Workflows](../../using-miro/miro-ai/02-miro-ai-workflows-overview.md) and the following advanced features:

- AI Workflows
  - [Sidekicks](../../using-miro/miro-ai/08-ai-sidekicks.md)
    Specialized AI agents that work alongside your team on every canvas
  - [Flows](../../using-miro/miro-ai/04-flows-overview.md)
    Visual AI workflows that transform your content into deliverables like prototypes, documents, or diagrams
- [Knowledge](../../using-miro/miro-ai/09-knowledge.md) integrations
- 50 AI credits per member per month.
  To learn more about Miro AI credits and how to apply additional credits, see [Miro AI credits](../billing-and-payments/03-miro-ai-credits.md).
- Model content protocol (MCP) server
- Records nesting and TreeView visualization for Tables
- Bi-directional ticketing system integrations
- Approval and status workflows

After the trial period, your plan transitions to a Business + AI Workflows subscription.

:::note
Unused time on your legacy Business plan is prorated on your next Business invoice.
:::

### Adding team Members and Guests during the trial

Add new team Members and Guests at no cost during your Business + AI Workflows Plan trial. Once the trial ends, they'll seamlessly join your Business + AI Workflows Plan. If you cancel the trial, they'll move to your legacy Business plan, where additional license fees apply. Additional license fees do not apply for Guests.

To monitor future costs, you can [monitor and manage](#manage-licenses-and-billing-during-the-trial) licenses at any point during the trial.

### Cancel the trial

You can cancel anytime during the 14-day trial. If you cancel, then you return to your legacy Business plan after the trial period expires, with no changes or additional charges to your legacy Business subscription.

To learn how to cancel, see [Cancel the Business + AI Workflows trial for Starter and legacy Business](#cancel-the-trial-for-starter-and-legacy-business-plans).

:::note
As of March 2026, Miro expects to sunset the legacy Business plan in approximately 1 year. For more information about impact on existing legacy Business customers, see [Introducing the Business + AI Workflows plan](01-introducing-the-business-+-ai-workflows-plan.md).
:::

### Managing licenses and billing during the trial

- **Change payment method**
  During the trial, you can optionally update your payment details, including credit card information, and default payment method.
- **Legacy Business plan renewal**
  If your legacy Business plan renews during the trial, you are charged only for the legacy Business Plan, with prorated adjustments if you continue with a Business + AI Workflows plan after the trial expires.
- **Control costs**
  If you decide to cancel, add or remove team members before the trial expires to control future costs.

## Cancel the trial for Starter and legacy Business plans

As Company admin or Billing admin, you can cancel the free trial at any time.

If you cancel, you and your team can continue to use the Business + AI Workflows features until the trial period expires, then you return to your Starter or legacy Business plan with no change or charge to your subscription.

Follow these steps:

1. In the top-right click your profile avatar, and then click **Admin Console**.
2. Go to **Billing** > **Subscription**.
3. In the **Plan** section, click **Cancel trial**.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. Agree to the Terms of Service.
6. Click **Downgrade**.
7. Select a reason for canceling the Business Plan trial.
8. Click Submit.
   A message shows confirming that you have cancelled your Business + AI Workflows trial.

:::note
If you do not cancel during the trial period, then your subscription transitions automatically to the Business + AI Workflows plan with charges reflected on your next invoice.
:::
