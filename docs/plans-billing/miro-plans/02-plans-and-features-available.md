### Frequently asked questions

1. *How can I upgrade my team?*
   - Take a look at [this page](../manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md) for instructions and details.
2. *I purchased a subscription but I still see free features only. Why?*
   - Please switch to your paid team on the left sidebar of your [dashboard](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md).
3. *How many members can I invite to my team?*
   - You can invite any number of team members and increase your team size. For large teams, we recommend choosing [Enterprise plan](04-enterprise-plan.md) as it provides advanced user management options.
