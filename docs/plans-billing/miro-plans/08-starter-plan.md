The Starter plan helps to organize collaboration in your team and gives access to the essential, advanced features.

## Starter plan features

- [Access rights management](../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/06-manage-starter-and-education-plan.md) and other administrative capabilities
- Advanced [Attention management](../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/04-attention-management.md)
- [Blueprints](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/04-templates.md#blueprints) for Spaces
- Board backup and import
- [Board version history](../../using-miro/managing-boards/12-board-history-versions.md)
- [Custom templates](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/04-templates.md)
- [Guests](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md) with view/comment access
- High-quality resolution [export](../../using-miro/import-and-export/export/03-how-to-export-your-board.md) without a watermark
- [Jira Cards](../../integrations-apps/atlassian/03-jira-cards.md), [Jira Add-on](../../integrations-apps/atlassian/02-miro-for-jira-cloud.md), [Confluence Plugin](../../integrations-apps/atlassian/01-miro-for-confluence.md), [Asana Cards](../../integrations-apps/asana/01-asana-cards-legacy.md)
- [Member trials](../../administration/user-management/01-invite-users.md#member-trials-on-starter-plan)
- [Password protection for public boards](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/13-password-protection-for-public-boards.md)
- [Private boards](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/15-make-a-miro-board-private.md)
- [Spaces](../../using-miro/spaces/01-spaces.md)
- [Timer](../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/16-timer.md)
- Unlimited editable boards
- [Video Calls](../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/17-video-calls-beta.md)
- [Visitors](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/08-collaboration-with-visitors.md) via a publicly shareable link with no login required
- [Voting](../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/19-voting.md)

If you and your colleagues need to work together on the same projects this plan would be the way for you. All members of your team are able to create and edit team boards. You can also invite unlimited [guests](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md) with view or comment access and [visitors](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/08-collaboration-with-visitors.md) via a [shareable link](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-boards-via-a-public-link).

:::note
Starter plans include limited Miro AI functionality. To unlock the full scope of Miro AI Workflows, including Flows and Sidekicks, upgrade to a Business plan. For more information, see [Starter plan vs Business plan](#starter-plan-vs-business-plan).
:::

You can [upgrade](../manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md) to the Starter planif you're already a Miro user. If not, you can purchase at [Miro pricing](https://miro.com/pricing/).

:::tip
Select the team to upgrade from the [team switcher](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/04-profile-and-teams.md) at the top-left of your Miro dashboard.
:::

## Starter plan vs other Miro plans

### Starter plan vs Free plan

When you upgrade from a free version to Starter plan, you and your team members immediately get access to advanced functionality. Check out our [comparison of Free and Starter plans](09-free-plan.md#free-plan-vs-starter-plan).

### Starter plan vs Business plan

:::tip
Experience the benefits of our Business Plan with a [14-day trial](07-business-plan-14-day-trial.md).
:::

If you need extra security, consider upgrading to [Business plan](06-business-plan.md). Take advantage of all Starter Plan features as well as [Single Sign-On](../../enterprise-administration/security-integrations/single-sign-on-sso/09-single-sign-on-sso.md), the option to invite [guests who can edit,](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md) and multiple teams.

To unlock the full scope of AI Workflows, upgrade to a Business plan.

Miro AI Workflows include [Sidekicks](../../using-miro/miro-ai/08-ai-sidekicks.md) and [Flows](../../using-miro/miro-ai/04-flows-overview.md), AI collaborators that help you go from draft to done in minutes. A Starter plan includes 5 Flow runs per team member, and limited Sidekicks functionality.

## Frequently asked questions

1. *When I upgrade, will my team members get access to paid features?*
   - Yes, all members of the upgraded Miro team will be able to use advanced features in this team. If you need to purchase fewer licenses, [remove extra users from the team](../../administration/user-management/08-remove-users.md).
2. *How can I check whether my organization already has a plan with Miro?*
   - Your organization's teams may be discoverable for users with corporate domains. [Learn more](../../administration/team-management/02-manage-team-signup-mode.md).
3. *Where can I see prices?*
   - Discover [Miro pricing page](https://miro.com/pricing/).
