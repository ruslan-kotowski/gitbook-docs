Miro is a hub for your team, where you can visualize, share and collect ideas. You can use Miro **Free plan** as long as you want, with as many people as you need!

When registering a profile, you can create your own free team or join existing teams where you've been invited to or which are [discoverable for you](../../administration/team-management/02-manage-team-signup-mode.md).

If you're already a member of someone's team but would like to create your own team, you can

- [leave the teams](../../using-miro/managing-your-profile/06-how-to-leave-a-team.md) you're a member of. Once there're no teams left in your profile, you will be suggested to create your own free team
- [create a new team](../manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md) by clicking the plus icon on the left sidebar of your dashboard. Note that creating a new free team is only possible if you're not a member of any other Miro team

Learn more about team and user management on Free plan in [this article](../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/07-manage-free-plan.md).

:::note
Free plans include limited Miro AI functionality. To unlock the full potential of Miro AI Workflows, including Flows and Sidekicks, upgrade to a Business plan. For more information, see [AI Workflows on Free plan](#ai-workflows-on-free-plan).
:::

## Unlimited team members

We believe in teamwork, and want to provide the best experience for teams to collaborate without any boundaries or limits. All invited users are automatically added to your team and get access to all boards (**no** [**private boards**](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/12-public-boards-vs.-private-boards.md)are allowed on Free plan).

All team members can:

- create their own boards
- view, comment, or edit all active boards (depending on the decision of the board owner)
- [upgrade the team](../manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md)
- use all features of the Free plan
- invite other members if [this is allowed in Team settings](../../administration/user-management/02-invitation-settings.md)

:::warning
Every user that you [invite to your boards via email](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email) on Free plan gets added to your team.
:::

If you're a Team Admin, you can [remove users from your team](../../administration/user-management/08-remove-users.md) at any moment. To learn more about team management, check out [this article](../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/06-manage-starter-and-education-plan.md).

## Board access on Free plan

Please note that if you invite other users to collaborate on your Free plan, you are granting access for them to view **all** of the boards on your [dashboard](https://miro.com/app/dashboard/). All boards created in a free team get automatically shared with the whole team. [Board privacy](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/15-make-a-miro-board-private.md) within a team is only supported on paid plans.

## Unlimited boards

The team members can create as many boards as they want, all of them will be saved in your team. There is no need to share your boards - they all are visible and available to all the team members.

Please note, that only**3 most recently created boards**in your team are available for editing and collaboration with your team. The rest of the boards in the team are inactive and are locked in a view-only mode. To edit inactive boards you can delete unnecessary active boards (or ask other members to delete their boards), [create copies of inactive ones](../../using-miro/managing-boards/03-how-to-duplicate-a-board.md), or [upgrade your team](../manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md).

:::note
If your team includes 3 boards only but they're still locked, check out [this article](../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/15-the-board-is-locked.md).
:::

## AI Workflows on Free plan

Miro AI Workflows include [Sidekicks](../../using-miro/miro-ai/08-ai-sidekicks.md) and [Flows](../../using-miro/miro-ai/04-flows-overview.md). Sidekicks are your specialized AI collaborators on every Miro board. Flows enable you to automate tasks in multi-step workflows that instantly generate Formats, like Tables, Mindmaps, and images.

A Free plan has limited access to Sidekicks, and includes 5 Flow runs per team member.

To unlock the full scope of AI Workflows, upgrade to a Business plan.

## Upgrade your Free plan

[Upgrade](../manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md) your free team to any of our [paid plans](https://miro.com/pricing/) to get access to advanced Miro features. If you're a small team that would like to get access to more cool collaboration features, choose our [Starter plan](08-starter-plan.md).

:::note
Free and Starter plans include limited Miro AI Workflows functionality. Start a [14-day Business trial](07-business-plan-14-day-trial.md) to experience the full scope of AI Workflows, including Flows and Sidekicks.
:::

### Downgrading to Free plan

After [downgrading to Free plan](../manage-your-subscription-and-plan/04-downgrade-your-plan.md) all private boards become inactive. They won't affect the limit of the active boards available. If a private board is within 3 board limitation you can [share it with the team](../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/15-the-board-is-locked.md) to enable editing, or [upgrade](../manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md).

### Frequently asked questions

**Can I be alone on my free team?**

- Yes. Unless you [invite new users to the team](../../administration/user-management/01-invite-users.md) created by you or to [your boards](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email), only you have access to your content. You can see the list of your team members and remove them in [Team settings](../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/06-manage-starter-and-education-plan.md) > **Active users**.

**Can I decide which of the boards will be active in my free team?**

- No, those are always the 3 most recently created boards. You can delete unnecessary ones that belong to you (or ask other team members to delete their boards), [duplicate inactive boards](../../using-miro/managing-boards/03-how-to-duplicate-a-board.md), or [upgrade your team](../manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md).

**Can I change my board to view-only for my team on Free plan?**

- Yes, you can. Though all boards in a free team can be accessed by all team members, you can downgrade team access to viewing only: open the board's **Share** dialog, choose **Can view** for team access to the board. Note that if you're not the board owner you should be [explicitly invited to the board](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email) as an editor - otherwise, your access will be limited as well.
