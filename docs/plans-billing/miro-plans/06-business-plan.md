Miro's Business plan is the best choice for teams that need extra security and flexibility on the user management side of their organization.

For new customers, a Business plan also includes [AI Workflows](../../using-miro/miro-ai/02-miro-ai-workflows-overview.md) that unlocks Sidekicks and Flows.

:::note
Miro's new [Business + AI Workflows](01-introducing-the-business-+-ai-workflows-plan.md) plan is replacing the legacy Business plan. For existing customers, complete access to some AI features is only available when you upgrade to the new Business plan. To learn more about impact on existing customers, see [Introducing the Business + AI Workflows plan](01-introducing-the-business-+-ai-workflows-plan.md).
:::

Experience the benefits of our Business Plan with a [14-day trial](07-business-plan-14-day-trial.md).

### Why choose Business Plan?

The Business Plan is ideal for when you need to scale collaboration beyond your team securely - using trusted technologies like SSO and a secure Guest role for collaboration with external collaborators.

| **Feature** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| [Miro AI Workflows](../../using-miro/miro-ai/02-miro-ai-workflows-overview.md) | Work with Sidekicks, specialized AI collaborators with expertise for any task, and automate tasks with Flows, visual AI workflows that generate output in any Format, like Tables, Docs, and images. You can also create and share Flows and custom Sidekicks. |
| [SAML-based Single Sign-On](../../enterprise-administration/security-integrations/single-sign-on-sso/09-single-sign-on-(sso).md) | The Business plan allows you to provide your team members with access to Miro through an identity provider of your choice. |
| [Guests](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md) | Invite your clients or partners outside your team to collaborate safely on certain boards without the need to invite them as team members. |
| [Paying by invoice](../billing-and-payments/05-payment-options-invoicing-and-automated-payments.md) | With invoicing, you can fully manage your subscription via Billing settings and pay your invoice with an ACH credit transfer, wire transfer, or credit card.  Requires a minimum of 5 members with yearly billing only. |
| Multiple teams | A team in Miro is a collection of boards and Spaces created by users that are part of this team. Company Admins on Business plan can create [multiple teams within their subscription](../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/05-manage-business-plan.md#how-to-create-a-new-team) - this is extremely helpful when working with clients on different projects or for organizing work with different teams or functional units. You can create separate teams for separate projects or multiple teams to share a larger project. Learn more about [how to configure multiple teams](../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/05-manage-business-plan.md#teams). |

To subscribe to the **Business Plan**, feel free to visit our [pricing page](https://miro.com/pricing/) or [upgrade your Free Plan or Starter Plan](../manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md).

For a custom arrangement (teams of 30+ users, with advanced [Enterprise Plan](04-enterprise-plan.md) features), reach out to [sales@miro.com](mailto:sales@miro.com).
