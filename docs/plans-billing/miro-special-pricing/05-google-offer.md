Miro is offering 3 months of the [Starter plan](../miro-plans/08-starter-plan.md) to all Google Workspace admins for free. In this guide, we explain how you can take advantage of the offer and answer the most popular questions.

### Offer details

- 3 months free of the Starter plan, no limit on seats
- No credit card required to redeem
- The offer is not valid for existing paid users

### How to activate the licenses

You will receive an email about the offer from Miro. Click **Start 3 months for free** to activate the trial.

:::warning
Note that the offer cannot be activated on a mobile device.
:::

Review Miro Terms and Privacy Policy. Check the boxes if you agree to the Terms and would like to receive news and updates about Miro. Click **Activate offer**.

![offer_activation.jpg](images/21019694513938_offer%20activation.jpg)
*Activating the offer*

Choose a Google account to authorize in Miro.

![choose_a_Google_account.jpg](images/21019694516242_choose%20a%20Google%20account.jpg)
*Choosing a Google account*

If you’re new to Miro, you will be suggested to set up your first team and invite team members.

If you have a free team, the team will be immediately upgraded to [Starter plan](../miro-plans/08-starter-plan.md). If you manage several free teams, we will create a new Starter plan team for you.

If you already have a subscription with Miro, this offer is not valid for you. You will see the corresponding notification.

### What happens when the trial ends

During your trial period, we will show you the notification specifying the number of days left. You will be able to purchase a subscription by clicking the button **Get Starter Plan**.

You will be suggested to purchase the number of seats that is equal to your current team size. If you need to get fewer, please [remove extra users from your team](../../administration/user-management/08-remove-users.md) beforehand.

- If you purchase the **Starter** **plan**, the trial period will continue and your card will be charged at the end of the trial. If you choose to upgrade with [self-serve invoicing](../billing-and-payments/05-payment-options-invoicing-and-automated-payments.md), at the end of the trial you will receive an invoice
- If you purchase **another plan** ([Consultant](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/360017571794-Consultant-Plan) or [Business](../miro-plans/06-business-plan.md)), the trial will end and your card will be charged (or you will [receive an invoice](../billing-and-payments/05-payment-options-invoicing-and-automated-payments.md)) immediately

If you do not purchase a subscription by the end of the trial, your team will expire. All the team boards will be paused in a view-only mode until you re-activate the subscription or downgrade to [free](../miro-plans/09-free-plan.md). Please rest assured that your content will stay safe. Learn more: [What happens when a subscription expires](../manage-your-subscription-and-plan/07-expired-subscription-reference.md).

### Frequently asked questions

1. *What if I used to have a subscription but downgraded to Free plan before activating the trial?*
   - You will be eligible for the trial and will start with blank payment details.
2. *What if I decide to upgrade to [Enterprise plan](../miro-plans/04-enterprise-plan.md)?*
   - Please [get in touch with our Sales team](https://miro.com/enterprise/) to discuss the upgrade.
3. *Where can I find the description of Starter plan?*
   - Take a look at these articles: [Starter plan](../miro-plans/08-starter-plan.md), [Manage Starter and Education plans](../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/06-manage-starter-and-education-plan.md).
4. *Can I add new members during the trial period?*- Yes, you can invite as many members as you wish. Note that if you upgrade, you will need to purchase a seat for every member of your team.
