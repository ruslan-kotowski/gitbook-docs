[Miro Professional Network](https://miro.com/professional-network/) is a free program for consultants, agencies and freelancers who use Miro in their client-facing work. By joining this program, you’ll gain access to exclusive resources and events, a community of like-minded professionals, and much more.

In this article, you’ll learn how to join and unlock the benefits of our Miro Professional Network.

Note that there are two tiers in this program: Miro Professionals and Miro Experts. For information on Miro Experts visit [this page](07-miro-experts.md).

> We are closing new applications for now. Stay tuned for a new, revamped Miro Professional Network!

### Benefits

When you join Miro Professional Network, you receive multiple benefits that will help in your work with clients:

- Referral benefits
- Exclusive Miro Academy consultant course
- Miro Professional Network newsletter
- Priority support
- Members badge

* If you are on the Free Miro plan you are  eligible to get a free upgrade to the Consultant plan:

- For teams up to 5 members, you can receive 5 (five)  free annual [Consultant plan](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/360017571794) seats. If you want to expand your team, you would need to pay for all seats
- For teams with 6 or more members, we offer a free 3-month trial with unlimited team members on our Consultant plan

If your team is already on a paid Miro plan, this benefit does not apply to you.

### Eligibility criteria

To become a member of the Miro Professional Network, your company must work with external clients and align with the “Professional Service Provider” definition:

*Firms with knowledge workers that provide their expertise as a service to other segments and can produce products as long as it is not for themselves (e.g. digital tools).*

### Requirements for renewal

The membership in Miro Professional Network is free of charge. After you have been accepted to the program, you’ll start receiving your benefits. At year end, to renew your annual benefits, one of the following requirements must be met during one year after application approval:

- Have used Miro to work with a minimum of 5 clients
  or
- Refer a client to Miro where the client purchases a minimum of 50 [Enterprise plan](../miro-plans/04-enterprise-plan.md) seats



### Referral program

To refer your clients and get a monetary benefit, you need to [activate your referral partner status](https://miro.com/professional/referral-partner/apply/) first. In case you have any questions, please reach out to channel@miro.com.

Only leads that meet our minimum license requirements based on customer segment will be eligible for the referral partner program including:

- >50 Enterprise licenses for companies with more than 500 employees and
- >10 Enterprise or Business licenses for companies with fewer than 500 employees

Once a client is approved and a deal has been closed with leads that have been shared, you can get 10% of Annual ARR at the time of purchase, with a max payout of 50,000 USD. These terms are subject to change at Miro discretion at any time.



### Frequently asked questions

1. *Our company has been approved for the 5-seat free* [Consultant plan](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/360017571794) *package. How can I access the subscription?* - Miro has either upgraded your existing Free plan team or created a new upgraded team. On the left side of your dashboard, you should see all your teams as square icons. If you have multiple teams, check if another square is your upgraded team. In case you have trouble finding your team, please contact [Miro Support.](../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/06-contacting-miro-support.md)
2. *We want to invite one more teammate(s) to our free 5-seat team. Can we buy one more seat?*- Yes, you can. In this case, you would need to start paying for all 6 seats. To convert your subscription, please reach out to the [Miro Support](https://help.miro.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) team for assistance.
3. *My company has been approved for a free trial. How can I activate it?* - You can activate your trial on the checkout page in the Miro application. To do so:

1. Log in using the email address you’ve indicated in the application.
2. Click **Upgrade** on the dashboard.
3. Choose [Consultant plan](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/360017571794), under the tab **Working with clients.**
4. On the checkout page, you will see a message about the 3-month trial credit. Do not proceed if you don’t see it.
5. Choose the number of seats.
6. Enter credit card details.
7. Click **Purchase.**

4. *Can I transfer the ability to activate the trial to my teammate?*- No, the trial is tied to the email address that you’ve indicated in the application. It cannot be transferred to any other user and can be activated only once.
5. *How many seats are available on the trial?*- You can invite an unlimited number of users on trial. Please note that once the trial ends, Miro will start charging your credit card.
6. *How can I request promo codes for my clients?* - Please request the coupons via [this form](https://miro-survey.typeform.com/to/aILyOcjE). Coupon requests can only be made by a member of Miro Professional Network and will be sent to your email to share with your clients.
7. *How can I submit a ticket to the Support team?*- Please check this article: [How to contact Miro Support](../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/06-contacting-miro-support.md).
8. *I am using Miro right now and paying for the Consultant plan (or* [Team plan](../miro-plans/08-starter-plan.md) *or* [Business plan](../miro-plans/06-business-plan.md)*). Can I still enter the program, and what will I receive?**-* You may become a member of the program if you're paying for a Consultant or Business plan. You may still apply with the Team plan, but in order to get the benefits, we will ask you to upgrade to a Consultant or Business plan. If your application is approved, you will receive all the benefits, except for the free upgrade to the Consultant plan, which is only available to Miro users on the Free plan.
9. *For how long are these program benefits valid?* - Program benefits are valid for 1 year after your acceptance to the program. After the one year mark, you will be up for a renewal if you adhere to the requirements for renewal. If you wish to share ideas/feedback to improve this program, you can reach out at professional@miro.com.
10. *Can I switch from Consultant plan to another Miro plan?*- Yes, please [get in touch with Miro Support](../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/06-contacting-miro-support.md) for assistance. Please note that in this case, you will lose access to the program benefits.
