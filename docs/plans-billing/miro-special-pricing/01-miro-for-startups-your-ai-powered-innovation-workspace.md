## Miro Blueprints: Launch your project faster

Skip the blank canvas. Blueprints are pre-populated, expert-designed Spaces that centralize your work, organize boards, docs, tasks, and updates into streamlined, repeatable workflows.

For startups focused on rapid iteration and product-market fit, these Blueprints are most valuable:

- [**Goal Setting (OKR)**](https://miro.com/app/dashboard/?tpTemplate=94febc6a-d7b8-4a1d-abb1-22e1f75c9a1d&isCustom=false&templateType=space&share_link_id=66473953319)
  Set clear, measurable goals and track your progress to maintain focus and team alignment (critical in early stages).
- [**Roadmap Planning**](https://miro.com/app/dashboard/?tpTemplate=adf986ec-96c5-4365-9a2c-8f223a48c059&isCustom=false&templateType=space&share_link_id=965456095121)
  Prioritize features, manage sprints, and create briefs to streamline product development and ensure you build what matters.
- [**AI Initiative Planning**](https://miro.com/app/dashboard/?tpTemplate=e547ff15-ecca-4e37-bbe4-b37475418e37&isCustom=false&templateType=space&share_link_id=65915400729)
  Structure and align your strategy for integrating AI into your product or operations.

## Integrate with your stack

Miro works seamlessly with your most-used tools, keeping your team connected and your single source of truth current.

The following list shows the top integrations for startups:

- [**Slack**](https://miro.com/integrations/slack/)
  Bring your team together by receiving Miro board updates and comments directly in your Slack channels.
- [**Jira and Asana**](https://miro.com/integrations/atlassian/)
  Turn ideas and discovery work on your Miro board into actionable tickets in your project management tools without leaving the canvas.
- [**Google Workspace**](https://miro.com/integrations/google-workspace/)
  Embed Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides directly into your board for context-rich planning sessions and presentations.
- [**Over 250+ integrations**](https://miro.com/integrations/)
  Explore the Miro Marketplace to connect with hundreds of other essential apps.
