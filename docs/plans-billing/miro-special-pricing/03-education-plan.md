Bring interactivity and power of visual collaboration to your classroom and study! Miro provides the free Educational plan to**staff members and students of educational institutions**.

## Institutions that qualify

Qualified educational institutions include all accredited public or private schools, including:

- Middle schools and high schools
- Junior colleges
- Colleges, universities, and technical schools
- MBA schools
- Online schools accredited by the local Department of Education and awarding an academic degree.

## Organizations that do not qualify

The following types of organizations **are not** qualified educational institutions:

- Non-accredited educational institutions
- Training programs, vocational schools, retraining programs
- Churches, hospitals, health care systems, and public libraries
- Research laboratories

If your organization does not qualify, check if you qualify for [Non-profits program](https://miro.com/npo/) or [Startup program](https://miro.com/startups/).

## How to apply for an Education team

To apply for the Education plan, fill out the [application form](https://miro.com/education) on our website. There you will need to provide a link to a document supporting your student/employment status (a school ID would do, or a web page from your school's website where your position is stated).

It is also necessary to include your **educational email address**: an email that ends in **.edu* or **.k12*, or belongs to the domain of your public educational institution. If you don’t have an educational email address, provide proof of your affiliation with an educational institution.

It may take us **up to 10 days** to respond to your application. When your Education team is created you will receive an invitation email with the login information from Miro (if you are not registered with Miro) or a notification saying that your Education team is now available on [your dashboard](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md) (if you already have a profile with Miro). Please note that you may be a member of several teams and need to switch to the Education team on the left sidebar.

## Education plan limitations

- The plan is valid forever for **educators**. 100 members in a team
- The plan is valid for two years for **students**. 10 members in a team
- One Education team per email (multiple teams per domain)
- One Team Admin
- No [Video chat](../../using-miro/facilitation-tools/18-video-chat.md) / [Rally](../../integrations-apps/more-integrations/13-ca-agile-central-rally.md) / [Azure Cards](../../integrations-apps/microsoft/03-azure-cards.md) / [Asana Cards](../../integrations-apps/asana/01-asana-cards-legacy.md)
- No email support

## Frequently asked questions

*1. How can I apply for the Education plan?*
- If you are a teacher/staff member/student of an educational establishment, please fill in the application form [here](https://miro.com/education/) providing any documentation that proves your employment/student status.

*2.* *Is Education plan free?*
- Yes, it is free.

*3. My company is a non-profit organization but not an educational institution. Are we eligible for the Education plan?*
- No, but we have a special offer for NPO as well, please check [this article](04-miro-for-non-profits.md) for more information.

*4. Is Education plan available for educational institutions outside the US?*
- Yes, educational institutions accredited in the country of residence by an authorized governmental agency are eligible. Find the list of institutions that qualify [here](#institutions-that-qualify).

*5. Why are Education teams for students valid for 2 years only?*
- Sometimes Education teams are created for graduate students who may need just 1 or 2 years of Miro usage. You can re-apply for the license once it’s over by providing us with proof that you are still a student and we will prolong your plan for another 2 years.

*6. I was granted the Education license but my team is still **free** and I have to **upgrade**.*
- You're probably a member of multiple teams. Please switch to the Education team on the left sidebar of your [dashboard](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md).

*7. How many members can I* [*invite to my Education team*](../../administration/user-management/01-invite-users.md)*?*
- An Education team is set to 100 members for *educators*. If you're a *student*, your team is set to 10 members. If you would like to invite more users, feel free to [share boards publicly](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-boards-via-a-public-link) with unlimited visitors or invite any number of viewers and commenters [to individual boards via email](../../using-miro/sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email).

*8. Can I create multiple teams within my Education plan?*
- No, [multi-team functionality](../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/07-team-management-on-enterprise-plan.md) is not supported on Education plan. You can use [Spaces](../../using-miro/spaces/01-spaces.md) to organize your boards or consider [Business](../miro-plans/06-business-plan.md) or [Enterprise](../miro-plans/04-enterprise-plan.md) plans that support the multi-team feature.
Accredited educational institutions are provided with a discount for Enterprise plan, [contact Sales](https://miro.com/contact/sales/) for more details.

*9. Can I move my boards from my other teams to my new Education team?*
- Yes, please find the instructions [here](../../using-miro/managing-boards/04-how-to-move-a-board.md).

*10. I have a Miro profile registered under my personal email and would like the Education team to be created in this profile. What do I do?*
- Change your profile email to your educational email following [this guide](../../using-miro/managing-your-profile/04-how-to-change-your-email.md) and apply for Education plan under your educational email.

*11. I have a Miro profile registered under my personal email while Education plan has been created for my educational email. How can I merge my two profiles?*
- You can move your boards from one profile to another using [this guide](../../using-miro/managing-boards/04-how-to-move-a-board.md).

*12. Can I use Miro on mobile?*
- Yes, to learn more about Miro Mobile App visit [this page](../../getting-started/apps-for-devices/08-mobile-app.md).

*13. My Education team expired and I'm not eligible for Education plan anymore. What do I do?*
- You can downgrade your team to [Free plan](../miro-plans/09-free-plan.md). After downgrading to free, you can also [upgrade your team to any other Miro plan](../manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md).

*14. Can I upgrade my Education plan to* [*another Miro plan*](../miro-plans/02-plans-and-features-available.md)*?*
- Yes, please [get in touch with Miro Support team](../../using-miro/tools/troubleshooting/06-contacting-miro-support.md).
