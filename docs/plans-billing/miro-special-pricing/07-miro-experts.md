Miro Experts are top level consultants, agencies and freelancers that use Miro to deliver their services. Miro Experts are listed in the Experts directory. Every Miro Expert has an [Expert Miroverse profile page](../../using-miro/miroverse/03-miroverse-profiles.md#miroverse-profile-pages-for-miro-experts) allowing our user base to find and contact them to help with their business needs.

:::warning
Miro Experts is currently under construction and not accepting new member applications. Stay tuned for a new, revamped Miro Experts program. If you're a current Miro Expert and have questions regarding the program, please reach out to us directly at experts@miro.com.
:::

### What benefits do I get as a Miro Expert?

- Miro Experts Courses promotion to Miro’s  user base
- Opportunity to host a Miro Expert Hour webinar for Miro’s user base
- Opportunity to be showcased in our annual *Miro* *Distributed* conference to showcase your expertise
- Opportunity to be featured in Miro content
- Listing in our partner directory and a dedicated landing page on miro.com, through which you can land new leads
- Access to co-marketing program
- Official Miro Expert badge

### What do I need to do to remain a Miro Expert?

At the end of a full year of being a Miro Expert, the following requirements must be met to renew your membership:

- Participate in one event organized by Miro as a facilitator or speaker
- Organize one public event, either in collaboration with Miro or individually, for any audience During this event, Miro should be used as the main tool for collaboration
- One of the below:

- Have used Miro to work with a minimum of 15 clients; or
- Refer a client to Miro where the client purchases a minimum of 150 [Enterprise plan](../miro-plans/04-enterprise-plan.md) seats

### Referral program

To refer your clients and get a monetary benefit, you need to [activate your referral partner status](https://miro.com/professional/referral-partner/apply/) first. In case you have any questions, please reach out to channel@miro.com.

Only leads that meet our minimum license requirements based on customer segment will be eligible for the referral partner program including:

- >50 Enterprise licenses for companies with more than 500 employees and
- >10 Enterprise or Business licenses for companies with fewer than 500 employees

Other plans such as Starter  licenses do not apply.

Once a client is approved and a deal has been closed with leads that have been shared, you can get 10% of the Annual ARR at the time of purchase, with a max payout of 50,000 USD.
