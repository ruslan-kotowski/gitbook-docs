The Miro Consultant Community is a free program for consultants, coaches, facilitators, or freelancers using Miro in their client-facing work. By joining this community, you’ll gain access to a global network of peers from all stages of the Miro journey with whom you can connect to find the answers, resources, and advice needed to grow and scale your client base.

In this article, you’ll learn about the Miro Consultant Community and how to apply to join.

## Program details

When you join the Miro Consultant Community, you gain access to multiple resources and benefits that will help you in your work with clients:

- Access to a private digital community channel where you can connect with other program members and with teams at Miro
- The ability to network, learn from, and share your expertise with peers in your industry
- Guaranteed approval to become a [Miro Affiliate](https://miro.com/affiliates/join-our-program/) partner
- Invitations to participate, facilitate, and speak at Miro Community events
- Opportunities to share your feedback about Miro product offerings, submit feature requests, and help shape the future of Miro
- Access to the latest news and updates about Miro
- Opportunities to be featured in Miro marketing materials
- Exclusive swag & a digital badge that you can show off to your networks

See [Miro Consultant Terms & Conditions](https://go2.miro.com/rs/228-GPV-835/images/Miro_Consultant_Community_Terms_Conditions-16Jun2023.pdf) for additional program details.

## Eligibility criteria

To become a member of the Miro Consultant Community, you must meet the following membership requirements:

- You use Miro in your work with clients and align with our Professional Service Provider definition: *A knowledge worker or someone who works at a firm with knowledge workers that provides their expertise as a service to external clients.*

## How to apply to become a member

To apply to join the Miro Consultant Community, complete the [membership application](https://miro.com/consultant-community/). We will review your application and send you a response within 10 business days. If you haven’t heard from us after this time, please check your spam folder or reach out to [consultantcommunity@miro.com](mailto:consultantcommunity@miro.com).

:::note
During the Miro Consultant Community application review process, Miro reserves the right to ask for additional resources and information or reject the application at our discretion.
:::

## Frequently asked questions

How much time do I need to commit to participating in the Miro Consultant Community

There is no participation requirement for members of the Miro Consultant Community! We want this community to be a valuable resource for you to utilize as frequently as you’d like to or are able to.

For how long is my membership in the Miro Consultant Community valid?

There is no expiration date on membership in the Miro Consultant Community.

How do I invite someone to join the Miro Consultant Community?

Please invite others from your network who you think would benefit from being a member of the Miro Consultant Community! You can simply share the application with anyone you’d like to invite: [miro.com/consultant-community/apply](https://miro.com/consultant-community/)

Who should I contact if I have any other questions about the Miro Consultant Community?

Reach out to our team anytime by emailing [consultantcommunity@miro.com](mailto:consultantcommunity@miro.com)
