If your board is locked in view-only mode even though you have not hit the limit of three active boards on the Free plan, this article will help you understand why it happened and how you can unlock the board to continue working on it. The issue may arise when [you downgrade to Free plan](#when-you-downgrade-to-free-plan) or [you move a private board to a free team](#when-you-move-a-private-board-to-a-free-team).

:::note
Please note that a board may be locked for you in a view-only mode because you have been granted [view-only access](../../sharing-boards/01-board-access-rights.md) to it.
:::

:::tip
Learn more about locking content on a board on [this page](../../working-on-the-board/17-locking-content-on-the-board.md).
:::

## When you downgrade to Free plan

As soon as you [downgrade your team and change the subscription type to Free plan](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/360011780620-How-to-Change-Your-Plan#h_0d9704b0-5e4c-4ca5-99c7-e6b95dfb46c2), your boards might get locked in a view-only mode for two reasons:

**The board limit was exceeded.** [Free plan](../../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md) allows creating an unlimited number of boards but *only the 3 most recently created ones are active*. Note that this limit is applicable to the whole team (not to every team member individually). To see *all* team boards, make sure to adjust [filters on the dashboard](../../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md#sorting-and-filtering-options).

![inactive board.jpg](images/21017348491666_inactive%20board.jpg)
*Inactive board*

**The board becomes *inactive* because it was created as *private* when on the paid plan.** Since board privacy is not supported on the Free plan, the board gets locked and available for the Board owner and explicitly invited users for viewing only. When hovering over the board you'll see the message: *Free plan users can collaborate on 3 active team boards. Private boards are locked.*

![inactive board.jpg](images/21017318650514_inactive%20board.jpg)
*Inactive board on the dashboard*

To start working on the inactive board again, you should [share it](../../sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md) with your team.

![team access to board on Free plan.jpg](images/21017318652050_team%20access%20to%20board%20on%20Free%20plan.jpg)
*Sharing a private board with the team*

## When you move a private board to a free team

Once [a private board is moved to a free team](../../managing-boards/04-how-to-move-a-board.md), it gets locked in a view-only mode unless [shared with the team](../../sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md).

## FAQ

**I am on paid plan but my board is still locked because the team has reached the three boards limit. What do I do?**

Please be sure to switch to your paid team on the left sidebar of your [dashboard](../../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md) if you are a member of several teams.

**I have shared a board with the team but it is still locked in a view-only mode. Why?**

Please make sure that the Free team's limit of three boards has not been hit. Please note that it is 3 boards per team (not 3 boards per user). Besides, check that you are authorized in Miro under the correct email and that you have been granted editing access to the board under that email.

**Can I decide which of the boards will be active in my free team?**

No, those are always the *3 most recently created boards.*You can delete unnecessary ones that belong to you, [duplicate inactive boards](../../managing-boards/03-how-to-duplicate-a-board.md), or [upgrade your team](../../../plans-billing/manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md).

**I changed team access to a board to view-only and now I am unable to edit it. How can I retain editing access?**

Please reach out to the Board owner and ask them to share the board with you or [request access from the board](../../sharing-boards/01-board-access-rights.md#how-to-request-access-to-a-board).

**I left/was deleted from a team and now cannot access a board that belonged to me. What do I do?**

You may follow the board link and [request access from the current Owner](../../sharing-boards/01-board-access-rights.md#how-to-request-access-to-a-board). If the board was deleted, you can ask the Team Admin to [restore it](../../managing-boards/08-how-to-restore-a-deleted-board.md) within 90 days if the board was *not* deleted together with the team.

**I used Miro without being registered, but after signing up I lost access to the board. What can I do?**

There are two ways you can work in Miro without being registered: in [Web whiteboard](../../special-features/10-web-whiteboard.md) or as a [visitor](../../sharing-boards/08-collaboration-with-visitors.md) on someone's board.

If you work in [Web whiteboard](../../special-features/10-web-whiteboard.md), please keep in mind that [Web whiteboard](../../special-features/10-web-whiteboard.md) boards are available for 24 hours. If you want to continue using them afterward, log in or sign up for Miro to save your work. Otherwise, the board will be permanently deleted after 7 days.

If you work as a visitor on someone's board, you should be able to access the board via the same link if it still exists and is being shared publicly. If your access has been restricted, you may either [request it](../../sharing-boards/01-board-access-rights.md#how-to-request-access-to-a-board) or reach out to the board owner.

**What do I do if I lost access to my board?**

Please try to find the board link in Miro notifications or in your browser history. If the board has been deleted, you can restore it within 30 days if you are still the board owner. If you were removed from the board, you can [request access](../../sharing-boards/01-board-access-rights.md#how-to-request-access-to-a-board).

If you are struggling with getting access to your locked board, [reach out to Miro Support](../../tools/troubleshooting/06-contacting-miro-support.md).
