Sometimes users who connect Miro with Google (by logging in with their Google account or using Google integrations) receive the following error message:

![Google_authorization_error.jpg](images/21019535033234_Google%20authorization%20error.jpg)
*An error message during Google authorization*

In this case, the best solution is to remove all Google accounts associated with Miro and set it up again. Here's a step-by-step instruction on how to do it:

1. Go to your **Google account.**

*![Google_account.jpg](images/21019591077906_Google%20account.jpg)**Getting to Google account settings*

2. Open the **S****ecurity**tab.

![security_tab.jpg](images/21019591083282_security%20tab.jpg)
*Google account Security settings*

3. Go to the **Third-party apps****with account access**section and click **Manage third-party access.**

![third-party_apps.jpg](images/21019535043730_third-party%20apps.jpg)
*The option to manage third-party access in Google settings*

4. Find Miro on the list. Click **Remove Access**.

![remove_Miro_access.jpg](images/21019535046674_remove%20Miro%20access.jpg)
*The option to remove Miro access to Google*

As an additional step, once you log in, please remove all Google integrations in the [Integrations](https://realtimeboard.com/app/account/profile/integrations/)section of your Miro **Profile settings**.

![log_out_from_Google_accounts.jpg](images/21019591096978_log%20out%20from%20Google%20accounts.jpg)
*Log out from your Google accounts*

Having gone through the suggested steps, feel free to re-associate your Miro profile with your Google account.

We understand that the proposed procedure might seem quite effort- and time-consuming, but the reward will be getting your Google Drive integration back to work.

:::note
If you cannot log in to your Miro profile via Google, please try to log in using standard credentials (login and password). If you do not remember the password, you can restore it using [this guide](../../managing-your-profile/05-how-to-change-your-password.md).
:::
