### How to contact Miro Support

> **Available for:** Starter, Business, Enterprise plans

You can submit a support request for any of the following topics:

- Miro Product
- Billing and subscription
- Login and registration
- Security and legal
- Special offers and programs
- Miro for developers

#### Prerequisites

Ensure that you are signed into a paid team. You can only submit a request to Miro Support as a member of a paid team.

#### Procedure

Follow these steps:

1. From the Miro dashboard, in the top-right select your avatar.
   Your profile menu opens.
2. Select **Learning Center**.
   The **Learning Center** modal opens.
3. Select **Contact support**.
4. Select a topic for your request.
5. Follow the on-screen prompts to choose subtopics.
   The support request form opens.
6. (Optional) Above the request form, see the suggested related articles.
7. Complete the support request form.
8. Select **Submit**.

   You have successfully submitted a request to Miro Support.

![support-contact.png](images/21537436630418_support-contact.png)
*Submitting a Support request*

To submit a request in the [Mobile app](../../../getting-started/apps-for-devices/08-mobile-app.md), press the team icon in the top-left corner > press **Help**. Be sure to switch to a paid team if you're a member of multiple teams.

You’ll be required to log in to your Miro profile before submitting a Support request (if you’re not logged in yet).

:::warning
We can share profile/subscription-related details with the profile/subscription owners only.
:::

:::note
If you cannot sign in, use [this form](https://miro.com/contact/recover/) to submit a Support request.
:::
