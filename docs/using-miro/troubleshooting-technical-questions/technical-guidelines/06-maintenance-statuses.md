We perform maintenance to ensure the uptime and the service consistency of Miro. In order to provide the best performance and security for our users, we have several types of maintenance.

:::tip
Go to [Miro Status Page](https://status.miro.com/) to check the app availability.
:::

## Maintenance types

### Maintenance windows

A maintenance window is a period of time scheduled in advance, during which we perform preventive maintenance that could cause disruption of service. These regular windows take place **once per quarter** **on** **Saturday at 04:30 AM UTC** and last for **1-3 hours**.

If there's a need to implement an **urgent** **critical infrastructure upgrade**, we can also perform maintenance without awaiting the planned maintenance window to make sure our servers are operating correctly and are able to avoid any breakdown or downtime. It is scheduled for the **nearest Saturday at 04:30 AM UTC**.

:::warning
During the maintenance, Miro is unavailable. The users are notified via the in-app message on the [dashboard](https://miro.com/app/dashboard/) a week before the maintenance.
:::

### Incident

An incident is a **critical issue** that is fixed by our team **immediately**. Some or all of Miro users may be affected by incidents. Miro remains online and is not affected by this maintenance, but some processes can be terminated. We try to do our best to restore service as soon as possible. We appreciate your patience and understanding. If you have any questions, please [contact Miro Support Team](https://help.miro.com/hc/requests/new?referer=help-center-article).

:::tip
You can check the [Miro Status Page](https://status.miro.com/) at any time or subscribe to updates to receive information about upcoming scheduled maintenance and incidents.
:::

:::tip
If you experience performance or loading issues with Miro, but all systems are shown as operational on the [Status Page](https://status.miro.com/), please try these [troubleshooting steps](../../tools/troubleshooting/07-how-to-troubleshoot-and-report-a-bug.md).
:::
