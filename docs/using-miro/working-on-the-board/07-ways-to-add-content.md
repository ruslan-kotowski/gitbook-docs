Import files, media, icons and map all the visual information on one board!

### What can be added to the board?

- Documents
- Spreadsheets
- Images
- Presentations
- Web-pages and links with preview
- [Icons](../essential-tools/08-images-and-icons.md)
- You can also [embed videos and other media files](../../integrations-apps/integrations-basics/03-embed-integrations-on-a-miro-board.md)

### How can you quickly add content?

- Simply drag and drop files from your desktop to a board. Please see the [list of supported file formats](../troubleshooting-technical-questions/technical-guidelines/03-supported-file-formats.md)
- Add texts and images from clipboard (use **Ctrl + C** and **Ctrl + V** *for Windows*, **Cmd + C** and **Cmd + V** *for Mac*)
- [Upload from your device](../import-and-export/import/05-uploading-files-to-boards.md) (you can upload xls, doc, ppt, svg, jpg, and other formats, see the full list [in this article](../troubleshooting-technical-questions/technical-guidelines/03-supported-file-formats.md))
- Upload via URL
- Upload documents, spreadsheets, or images from [Google Drive](../../integrations-apps/google/05-google-drive.md), [Dropbox](../../integrations-apps/more-integrations/06-dropbox.md), [Box](../../integrations-apps/more-integrations/05-box-legacy.md), [Adobe XD](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/360016621780), or [OneDrive](../../integrations-apps/microsoft/06-onedrive.md)

  > ✏️ To get access to the options listed above, click the **Upload**button on the collaboration toolbar
- Use icons and images from [Fontawesome](../essential-tools/08-images-and-icons.md), [Unsplash](../essential-tools/08-images-and-icons.md), and [Google Image](../../integrations-apps/google/06-google-images.md)
- Embed video, audio, and other media using iFrame code. [Learn more](../../integrations-apps/integrations-basics/03-embed-integrations-on-a-miro-board.md)
  ![embed_iframe_in_Miro.jpg](images/21020253797266_embed%20iframe%20in%20Miro.jpg)
  *Use iFrame codes to embed media into Miro boards*
- Add content from spreadsheets (use **Ctrl+C** and **Ctrl+V** *for Windows*, **Cmd+C** and **Cmd+V** *for Mac*) as Miro [tables](../advanced-tools/05-grid.md#importing-from-microsoft-excel-or-google-spreadsheets) or [sticky notes](../essential-tools/14-sticky-notes.md#pasting-from-a-spreadsheet-google-sheets-excel-etc)
  ![cells_as_sticky_notes_in_Miro.gif](images/21020264789266_cells%20as%20sticky%20notes%20in%20Miro.gif)
  *Pasting sticky notes from a spreadsheet*

## FAQ

**How can I import an image to my board?**

You can [upload it from your device](../import-and-export/import/05-uploading-files-to-boards.md) via the **Upload** menu, or paste it from the clipboard (use **Ctrl + C** and **Ctrl + V** *for Windows*, **Cmd + C** and **Cmd + V** *for Mac*), or simply drag from your device to the board.

**What is the best way to organize my content on the board?**

Check this article for more information: [Structuring board content](08-structuring-board-content.md).

**Can I upload a video to my board from my device?**

No, but you can [embed the video](../../integrations-apps/integrations-basics/03-embed-integrations-on-a-miro-board.md): upload the video to a video-sharing platform (*YouTube*, *Vimeo*, etc.) and simply paste the video link on a board using the shortcuts **Ctrl + C** and **Ctrl + V** *for Windows*, **Cmd + C** and **Cmd + V** *for Mac.*

**How can I change images on my board?**

You can move, [replace](21-work-smarter-not-harder.md#replace-images), change size, [crop](21-work-smarter-not-harder.md#crop-images), and [rotate](21-work-smarter-not-harder.md) uploaded images.

**Can I add wireframes and shapes to my board?**

Yes, learn more here: [Prototype library](../miroverse/prototyping/02-prototyping-library.md), [Shapes](../essential-tools/11-shapes.md). The list of other Miro tools can be found on [this page](../tools).

**Can I import data from a spreadsheet into a Miro board?**

Yes, spreadsheets can be imported as view-only files, or to create a Table or Grid. For more information: [Tables](../formats/14-tables.md) or [Grid](../advanced-tools/05-grid.md).

**I cannot upload content to my board. What do I do?**

Please make sure that the content size and format are supported - check out [this guide](../troubleshooting-technical-questions/technical-guidelines/03-supported-file-formats.md).
