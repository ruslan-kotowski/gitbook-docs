You can work in Miro with a **mouse**, **trackpad**, or **touchscreen**.

If you use a mouse:

- To move around the board, press the right mouse button and drag
- To zoom, scroll the mouse wheel
- To create the selection field, [switch to select tool](#switching-between-the-pan-and-select-tool), click and drag the canvas

If you use the trackpad:

- Slide two fingers to move around the board
- Pinch to zoom
- To select objects, [switch to select tool](#switching-between-the-pan-and-select-tool), press and drag the canvas

If you use the touchscreen:

- Drag the canvas to move around
- Pinch to zoom
- Long press and drag to select objects

To view the tips, click the **Main menu** hamburger icon > **Preferences** > **Mouse or Trackpad** > **Learn more**.

**Auto-switch** is set by default, but you can change it to **Mouse** or **Trackpad**. The navigation model is set for every user individually and applies to all tabs in the current browser.

- Select **Mouse** if you work with the Apple magic mouse. If you choose **Mouse** and use the trackpad, sliding with two fingers will zoom you
- Select **Trackpad** if you use the trackpad in combination with a mouse or graphic tablet. Trackpad mode is most convenient when you are used to scrolling down with a wheel and zooming with mouse wheel + **Ctrl** (*for Windows*)/**Cmd** (*for Mac*)

![new-mouse-trackpad.png](images/28438517225618_new-mouse-trackpad.png)
*Switching between mouse and trackpad in navigation controls*

You can also open the controls by right-clicking anywhere on the board and choosing **Mouse or trackpad.**

Touch events are automatically distinguished from mouse or trackpad events, so you do not need to set a specific navigation mode for your touchscreen device.

### Switching between the pan and select tool

To pan around your board, please make sure to switch to the hand mode. To select an object, please activate the select mode. You can switch between the two modes by enabling/disabling the select tool on the toolbar (see below) or using [the hotkeys](21-work-smarter-not-harder.md#switching-between-selectpan-modes) **V** and **H.**

![select_and_hand_mode.gif](images/4604597173522_select%20and%20hand%20mode.gif)*Switching between the pan and select tool*

The select tool is not available for viewers/commenters that are not authorized in Miro or are not allowed to copy the board objects in the [board content settings](../sharing-boards/14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md).

If you use a mouse, you can also move around the board

- while holding the right mouse button
- with the left mouse button while holding the Space bar
- with the left mouse button while holding the mouse wheel (available for some mice only)

### Navigation on the board

Find the navigation controls in the bottom-right corner of your board. The controls allow you to change the zoom level, make the board content fit to the screen, open and navigate around the minimap (which can also be shown or hidden via the **M** hotkey), enter and leave the full-screen mode.

## Frequently asked questions

**I don’t see the touchscreen mode in the navigation settings. Can I use Miro on touchscreen?**

Yes, you can use Miro on all touchscreen devices. Miro automatically detects touch events and distinguishes them from mouse/trackpad events. Touchscreen always works as described above regardless of mouse or trackpad selected in the settings.

**Is Miro compatible with graphic tablets?**

Miro can be used with graphic tablets but we don't provide a special navigation mode for them. A graphic tablet pen is accepted as a mouse and works on the basic level without supporting specific features.

**My trackpad is frozen, which is preventing me from zooming in or out of Miro boards. How do I fix this?**

For Mac, click the Apple menu in the top-left corner of the screen > **System Settings** > **Trackpad** > **Scroll & Zoom** and then toggle **Zoom in or out** off and on again. For Windows, open the menu in the bottom-left of the screen > **Settings** > **Devices** > **Touchpad** > toggle **Touchpad** off and on again.

**When I try to pan around the board, I move the board objects instead. How do I fix this?**

Click the right mouse button and drag to move around the board.
