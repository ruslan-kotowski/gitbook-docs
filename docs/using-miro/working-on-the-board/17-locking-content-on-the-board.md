Prevent objects from being accidentally moved, edited, or deleted by locking them on your Miro board. Editors who were invited to a board [via email](../sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email) or have access because they are part of a [project](../sharing-boards/16-projects.md) or [team](../sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md) can lock and unlock objects.

For extra protection, board owners and co-owners on paid or Education plans can use Protected Lock. This feature ensures that only the board owner or co-owner who locked an item can unlock it, preventing other editors from accidentally moving or deleting important content.

:::warning
Visitors are not able to lock or unlock objects on the board. Content within [tables](../advanced-tools/05-grid.md), [Kanban boards](../advanced-tools/02-columns-formerly-kanban.md), [mind maps](../advanced-tools/03-mind-map.md), [user story maps](../advanced-tools/07-user-story-mapping.md), and [swimlanes](../formats/diagramming/01-miro-for-mapping-diagramming.md#organize-diagrams-with-swimlanes) also cannot be locked.
:::

## Lock and unlock objects

You can lock and unlock single or multiple objects on your board.

To lock an object, select it and click the **Lock** icon on the context menu. Alternatively, use the shortcut **Ctrl + Shift + L** (*for Windows*) or **Cmd + Shift + L** (*for Mac*). On mobile, the Lock option can be accessed through the vertical three dots (![icon-main.svg](images/27775755698450_icon-main.svg)) menu after tapping on an object.

![The Lock option on an object's context menu.](images/21016057891346_lock%20in%20Miro.jpg)
*The Lock option on the context menu*

To lock multiple objects, select the relevant objects, then click the **Lock** icon in the context menu or use the keyboard shortcut.

To unlock an object, long-press the **Lock** icon on the context menu. You can also use the same shortcut again, or right-click the object and choose **Unlock**.

:::note
If an object has a [protected lock](#use-protected-lock) enabled, only the board owner or co-owner who locked it can unlock it.
:::

![Long-pressing the lock icon to unlock an object.](images/21016057894418_long%20press%20to%20unlock.jpg)
*Long-press the icon to unlock an object*

### Unlock all objects

To unlock all objects on the board at once, right-click a blank area of the board and select **Unlock all**. You can also click the three dots on any locked object's context menu and choose the same option.

:::note
[Protected lock](#use-protected-lock) items won't be unlocked unless you are the board owner or co-owner who enabled the lock.
:::

![The option to unlock all objects on the board.](images/21016044523410_unlock%20all.jpg)
*Unlocking all elements on the board*

## Use protected lock

> **Set up by:** board owners, [board co-owners](../sharing-boards/06-co-owners-of-boards-and-spaces.md)

Use protected lock to prevent other collaborators from unlocking important board content.

To enable protected lock, first lock an item as usual. Then, click the shield icon that appears on the context menu. Once the shield is active, other people on the board will not be able to unlock the object. You can disable the Protected Lock at any time by unlocking the item yourself.

![A user enabling the Protected Lock feature on a locked object.](images/21016044524434_protected%20lock.gif)
*Enabling Protected Lock on an object*

### What collaborators see

Objects with a protected lock appear differently to other collaborators on the board. For other users, the option to unlock the object is unavailable, and a message shows that the item is protected by the board owner.

![A widget showing that an object is protected by the board owner.](images/21016057899794_protected%20locked%20for%20users.jpg)
