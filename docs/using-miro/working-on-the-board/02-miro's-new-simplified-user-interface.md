Miro is enhancing the board user interface to be more inclusive and intuitive.

<iframe allowfullscreen="" frameborder="0" height="315" src="//fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/uqbg7sog7l" width="560"></iframe>

This article describes updated entry points for collaboration and creation tools, board options and controls, and access to helpful resources.

## Create with AI & Creation bar

To access Miro AI features, select **Create with AI** above the Creation bar.

![Create with AI button above the Creation bar.](images/21064617157010_create-with-ai.png)*Create with AI above the Creation bar*

The Creation bar enables you to access the essential creation and editing tools.

By default, the following tools are pinned to the creation bar:

- **Select**Selects objects on the board
- **Templates**Opens the templates modal
- **Sticky notes**Opens the sticky notes menu
- **Text**Enables text mode
- **Shapes and lines**Opens the shapes-and-lines menu
- **Create freehand drawings**Opens the freehand drawing menu
- **Frame**Opens the frame menu
- **Stickers, Emoji and GIFs**Opens a library of stickers, emoji, and GIFs
- **Comment**Enables comment mode
- Tools, Media and Integrations
  Opens the **Tools** and **Marketplace** libraries

:::note
Shapes and Lines have been combined into a unified tool.
:::

## Canvas controls bar

In the lower right corner of the canvas, the canvas control bar includes the following options:

- **Frames and layers**Enables you to organize, present, and specify settings for each frame or layer on your board
- **Zoom and navigation**Adjusts zoom, and enables you to pin a map of content on your board
- **Help and resources**Opens a menu that includes access to a feedback form, the Miro Help Center, and additional learning resources

:::note
To pin or unpin the map, use fit-to-screen, or enter full screen mode, you must now click the zoom percentage to access a secondary menu.
:::

:::note
Activities have been moved under Frames and layers.
:::

## Collaboration bar

The Collaboration bar appears in the upper-right corner of the board, and includes options and tools that help facilitate and drive engagement.

### Facilitation tools

The facilitation tools menu includes the following tools:

- **Reactions**
  Provides a set of Emoji that enable collaborators to express their feelings
  > ✏️ The Reactions menu no longer includes **Raise hand**, and board owners and co-owners can no longer enable or disable reactions for all users from the **Reactions** menu.
- **Timer**Sets a time limit for timed activities
- **Voting**Enables collaborators to vote on a decision, and collates results
- **Private mode**Hides content on sticky notes until disabled. You can also make collaborators anonymous on the board
- **Note**Write and pin a **Visual Note** to the canvas

:::note
The following features are now available in the **Tools, Media and Integrations** menu: Estimations, Dependencies, Video Calls, Chat, and Breakout Frames.

Talktrack is now available in the **Present** menu.
:::

### Activity

We’ve combined board comments, Talktrack recordings, and team notifications (previously Feed) into a unified experience. Now you can get caught up in one place.

Select the bell icon to see board activity, including comments on the board and Talktrack recordings. To view notifications from any board in the current team, switch to the **Team** tab.

### People bar

The **People** bar enables you to interact with collaborators on the board.

When collaborators are present on the board, click the dropdown **People** bar to expand the collaborator list, and select one of the following options:

- Select a collaborator to follow, or bring them to you
- Search for a collaborator
- Bring everyone on the board to you
- Enable or disable cursors

### Profile menu

Select your avatar to open the profile menu, which shows your name and role. You can also access **Profile settings**.

:::note
To use **Bring everyone to me** and **Bring to me**, use the collaborator list in the **People** bar.
:::

### Present

The **Present** menu enables you to present frames on your board as slides. You can also create a Talktrack recording.

:::note
**Present the entire board** and **present activities** have been removed.
:::

### Share

The **Share** menu enables you to copy a link to your board, invite collaborators to your board, embed your board, and save or publish your board as a template.

You can also access **Sharing settings**.

## Board bar

The Board bar enables you to change the board name with one click and an updated selection of board thumbnails are available to choose from. If you have the appropriate privileges, you can also change the board classification.

The Board bar includes the **Main menu**, which you can access by selecting the vertical three-dots.

![menu.png](images/28438519349650_menu.png) *Access the Main menu from the Board bar*

:::note
Export, Board Search, and Starring the board are now included in the **Main menu**.
:::

The following list shows the full list of options in the **Main menu**:

- **Board**
  - Make a copy
  - Export, save as template, or embed
  - Move to another Space
  - Star the board
  - Delete
  - Change background color
  - Specify the start view for newly invited members
  - See board history
  - See board details
- **Edit**
  - Undo/redo
  - Access **Command palette**
  - Find board content
- **View**
  - Specify grid options, including disable grid
  - Enter full-screen mode
  - Show or hide collaborator cursors, comments, scroll bars, object dimensions, and controls for redo and undo.
- **Preferences**
  - Enable mouse or trackpad control
  - Specify whether to align objects on the board
  - Enable **Follow all threads**
  - Access your profile settings
- **Accessibility**
  - Enable **Precise selection**
  - Enable **Reduce motion**
  - Check accessibility
