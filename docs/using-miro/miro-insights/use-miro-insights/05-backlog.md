Backlog is your main workspace in Miro Insights where you manage all your features and issues. This central hub provides comprehensive tools for organizing, prioritizing, and tracking your product development work.

Your backlog contains two main types of items:

- **Features**: New capabilities or enhancements you're planning to build.
- **Issues**: Bugs, problems, or technical debt that needs to be addressed.

Each item includes details like priority level, associated companies, feedback counts, dollar value impact, and current status.

## Organizing your backlog

You can customize how you view and manage your backlog using fields, grouping, filtering, and views.

### Adding custom fields

Customize your backlog to match your workflow by adding fields that matter to your team. This helps you capture the specific information you need for decision-making.

### Grouping items

Organize your backlog by grouping items by different criteria:

- **Initiative**: Group by product area or strategic objective.
- **Priority**: Organize by urgency or importance levels.
- **Status**: Group by development stage (idea, research, in progress, shipped).
- **Companies**: See which features are requested by specific customers.

### Filtering and searching

Narrow down your view to focus on what matters:

- Use the **Filter** button to show specific subsets of your backlog.
- **Search within views** to quickly find specific items.
- Combine multiple filters for precise control over what you see.

## Working with views

Views save your preferred combinations of grouping and filtering settings.

### Managing views

- **Default view**: Your standard backlog perspective that others see.
- **Create new views**: Save custom combinations of grouping and filtering with a descriptive name.
- **Reorder views**: Drag views to change their order in the interface.
- **Shared visibility**: View changes affect what all team members see.

### Effective view strategies

To get the most out of views, consider these strategies:

- Create views for different team focuses (engineering backlog, customer requests, high-priority items).
- Use descriptive names that explain the view's purpose.
- Regularly review and update views as your needs change.

## Bulk operations

To manage your backlog efficiently, you can perform actions on multiple items at once.

### Multi-select actions

Select multiple items to perform batch operations:

- **Merge items**: Combine duplicate or related features/issues.
- **Delete items**: Remove obsolete or invalid entries.
- **Bulk status updates**: Change status across multiple items simultaneously.

:::tip
To ensure accuracy and avoid mistakes:

- Use filtering first to isolate the items you want to modify.
- Double-check your selection before performing irreversible actions like delete.
- Merge similar items to reduce clutter and focus discussions.
:::

## AI-powered features

Enable AI suggestions to get intelligent recommendations throughout your backlog:

- **Prioritization suggestions**: AI highlights items that might need attention based on customer feedback and business impact.
- **Categorization help**: Automatic suggestions for grouping and tagging items.
- **Relationship detection**: AI identifies connections between related features and issues.

:::tip
To make the most of AI features:

- Keep your data current for better AI recommendations
- Review AI suggestions before accepting them
- Use AI insights as a starting point for deeper analysis
:::

## Adding new items

1. Click **New** to add items to your backlog
2. Choose between **Feature** or **Issue** types
3. Fill in the essential details:
   - Title and description
   - Priority level
   - Associated companies or customers
   - Estimated impact or effort

To ensure clarity and usefulness:

- Write clear, specific titles that explain the value or problem.
- Include context about why the item is important.
- Link related customer feedback when available.
- Set appropriate priority levels based on your criteria.

## Import and export

You can bring in existing work from other tools or share your backlog data with stakeholders.

### Importing data

Bring in existing work from other tools:

- **CSV files**: Import structured data from spreadsheets
- **JIRA integration**: Use JQL queries or search to import existing tickets
- **Other integrations**: Connect with your existing product management tools

### Exporting data

Share backlog information with stakeholders:

- **Export as CSV**: Create spreadsheets for analysis or reporting
- **Share views**: Provide filtered perspectives for different audiences
- **Generate reports**: Use exported data for executive summaries or team updates

## Data accuracy

For the best insights and AI suggestions, ensure your backlog data is up to date. The AI recommendations are based on your current data, so regular updates will improve the quality of insights and suggestions you receive.

## Access from canvas

The backlog is also available through the Insights side panel on your Miro boards. This lets you browse, filter, and search your backlog items, then select specific items to add directly to your canvas as cards. Perfect for roadmap planning sessions and prioritization meetings where you want to visualize your actual product data.
