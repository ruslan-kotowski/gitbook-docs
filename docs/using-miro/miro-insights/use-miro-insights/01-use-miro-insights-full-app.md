Miro Insights is an AI-assisted product management tool that helps you analyze customer feedback, prioritize features, and make data-driven product decisions. The platform integrates with your existing tech stack and provides intelligent suggestions highlighted in magenta throughout the interface.

## How to access Miro Insights

You can mainly access Miro Insights in two ways:

- From any Miro board.
- From Miro dashboard, for full web app experience.

### From a Miro board

Access Miro Insights directly from any Miro board as a convenient side panel. This quick-access method allows you to select items and add them directly to your board as cards without leaving your workspace.

Learn more about Use Miro Insights on the canvas [using Insights on the canvas](../../tools/use-miro-insights/02-use-miro-insights-on-the-canvas.md).

### From Miro dashboard

Access the complete Miro Insights web app through the dedicated application interface. This provides full functionality across all sections with comprehensive data views, advanced filtering, and complete workflow management.

## Main sections

To access the Miro Insights full app, navigate to the app from your [Miro dashboard](https://miro.com/app/dashboard/).

Miro Insights is organized into six main sections, accessible from the left sidebar. Feel free to explore each area to discover the full capabilities of the platform.

### Ask AI

Ask questions about your backlog, features, and customer feedback. The AI analyzes your data to provide insights for prioritization and decision making. Perfect for getting quick answers and exploring your data conversationally.

[Learn more about using Ask AI](../../tools/use-miro-insights/03-ask-ai.md).

### Automations

Set up automated workflows to streamline your product management processes. Create rules that automatically categorize feedback, update feature status, or send notifications based on specific triggers.

[Learn more about using Automations](../../tools/use-miro-insights/04-automations.md).

### Backlog

The main workspace where you manage all your features and issues. View, organize, and prioritize your product backlog with advanced filtering, grouping, and bulk actions.

[Learn more about using Backlog](../../tools/use-miro-insights/05-backlog.md).

### Roadmap

Plan and visualize your product roadmap with timeline views. Track feature progress and communicate plans with stakeholders using interactive roadmap visualizations.

[Learn more about using Roadmap](../../tools/use-miro-insights/06-roadmap.md).

### Feedback

Analyze customer feedback with visual charts and insights. Track feedback trends, identify patterns, and understand what customers are saying about your product.

[Learn more about using Feedback](../../tools/use-miro-insights/07-feedback.md).

### Companies

Manage company information and track which organizations are requesting specific features. View key metrics like ARR impact and connect directly to your CRM systems.

[Learn more about managing Companies](../../tools/use-miro-insights/08-companies.md).
