The Ask AI feature lets you ask questions about your backlog, features, and customer feedback using natural language. The AI analyzes your data and provides insights to help with prioritization and decision-making.

## How it works

Ask AI is powered by your backlog data, so ensure your information is up to date for the best insights. The AI can help you understand patterns, prioritize features, and make data-driven product decisions.

## Asking questions

1. Navigate to the **Ask AI** section from the left sidebar.
2. Type your question in the text field. For example: "What are the top requested features?"
3. Press **Enter**.
4. Review the AI-generated response and supporting data.

## Understanding results

When you receive AI responses:

- **Company and feature names** in results are clickable links that show more details.
- **Side panel context** displays the full content of the item.
- **External links** in a side panel let you jump to original records in your connected systems.
- **AI suggestions** are highlighted in magenta throughout the interface.

## Follow-up questions

You can ask follow-up questions to dive deeper into specific areas. This conversational approach helps you explore your data from multiple angles:

**Example conversation flow**:

1. Initial question: "What are the top requested features?"
2. Follow-up: "Which of these features have the highest ARR impact?"
3. Further exploration: "Which companies are asking for the highest ARR feature?"

## Question examples

Here are some effective question types to get you started:

### Feature prioritization

- "What are the most requested features?"
- "Which features have the highest customer value?"
- "What features are enterprise customers asking for?"

### Customer insights

- "Which companies provide the most feedback?"
- "What are customers saying about [specific feature]?"
- "Which high-value customers are requesting new features?"

### Market analysis

- "What trends do you see in recent feedback?"
- "Are there any common pain points across customers?"
- "Which features could impact our largest deals?"

## Tips for better results

- **Be specific**: Instead of "Tell me about feedback," try "What are the main complaints about our mobile app?"
- **Include context**: Mention timeframes, customer segments, or feature areas when relevant.
- **Ask follow-ups**: Build on previous answers to get more detailed insights.
- **Check data recency**: Ensure your backlog is current for the most accurate AI recommendations.

## Sharing results

You can share the results with your team by copying a chat link. This allows for easy collaboration and discussion around the findings.

To share a result:

1. Select **Share** at the top right of the screen.

:::note
Sharing a link requires the recipient to have access to the same Miro Insights organization.
:::
