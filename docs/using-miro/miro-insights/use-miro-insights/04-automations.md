Automations help you streamline your product management workflows by automatically performing actions based on specific triggers. Set up rules to categorize feedback, update feature status, send notifications, and generate reports without manual intervention.

Automations save time and ensure consistency by:

- **Automatically categorizing** incoming feedback and feature requests.
- **Generating regular reports** and summaries for stakeholders.
- **Updating statuses** based on predefined criteria.
- **Sending notifications** when important events occur.
- **Analyzing trends** and providing insights at scheduled intervals.

## Creating a new automation

1. Navigate to **Automations** from the left sidebar.
2. Select **Create automation**.
3. Configure your automation:
   - **Title**: Give your automation a descriptive name.
   - **Prompt**: Define what the automation should do. For example, "List the top 5 most frequently reported issues".
   - **Destinations**: Choose where results should be sent. Currently, you can send results to **Slack**.
   - **Schedule**: Set when the automation runs (manually or scheduled).
   - **Tag**: Add tags to organize automations.
4. **Save Draft** to review later, or **Create Automation** to activate immediately.

### Scheduling options

Choose manual execution when you need specific insights. Perfect for ad-hoc analysis and one-time reports.

Choose regular schedules:

- **Daily**: For critical metrics and urgent issue monitoring.
- **Weekly**: For regular team updates and progress reports.
- **Monthly**: For broader trend analysis and strategic reviews.

## Managing automations

After creating automations, you can manage them from the main Automations page.

### View automation details

Each automation entry displays key information at a glance:

- **Title** of the automation.
- **Prompt** showing the specific task or query.
- **Destinations** where results are delivered.
- **When** indicating how often it runs.
- **Status** showing if it's "Active", "Paused", or "Draft".
- **Owner** indicating who created it.
- **Created** date for tracking.
- **Tags** for organization.
- **Last run** showing the most recent execution time.

### Control automation execution

You have several options to manage each automation. To manage an automation:

1. Click the automation **Title** to open its details.
2. Use the following controls:
   - **Trigger now**: Run an automation immediately regardless of schedule.
   - **Pause Automation**: Temporarily stop an automation.
   - **Activate Automation**: Reactivate a paused automation.
   - **Edit**: Modify automation settings and configuration.

### Delete automations

To remove an automation:

1. Click the automation **Title** to open its details.
2. Select the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
3. Choose **Delete** and confirm the action.

## Automation types

Automations can serve various purposes depending on your needs. Here are some common examples.

### Reporting automations

Generate regular summaries and insights:

- "List the top 5 most frequently reported issues or bugs"
- "Identify the top 5 most requested features ranked by frequency"
- "List all active product initiatives, ordered by status"

### Analysis automations

Provide ongoing data analysis:

- "List the top 10 companies with the highest volume of feedback"
- "Analyze feedback for customers describing challenges"
- "Summarize all customer interviews and feedback from the past 60 days"

### Monitoring automations

Track important metrics and changes:

- "Detect churn risk based on customer feedback patterns"
- "Monitor competitive gap analysis for frequently mentioned features"
- "Track initiative progress and highlight any blockers"
