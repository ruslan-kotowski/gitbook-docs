You can move a Miro board from one team to another, or transfer your Miro board to another profile.

:::warning
Enterprise plan Company Admins can restrict the option to move boards to and from a team for all non-admin users and board owners.
:::

## Common scenarios

Here are several common scenarios that involve moving boards:

- You have two Miro profiles (email addresses associated with Miro) and you want to move boards from one profile to another.
- You have upgraded from a Free plan to a paid plan and want to move boards to the paid plan.
- You want to move boards between two paid teams.

## Moving boards between teams

The following procedures explain how to move boards between teams.

**Prerequisites**

To move a board between teams, you must:

- own the board
- be a member of both teams

### How to move a board directly within the board

**Procedure**

Follow these steps to move a board between teams from within the board itself:

1. Open your board and click the **Main menu** icon to the right of your board name (top-left corner).
   The Main menu opens.
2. Navigate to **Board** > **Move to** > **Other team**.
   A Move board to team dialog opens.
3. Select the team you'd like to move the board to from the dropdown and click **Move**.

   You have successfully moved the board to a different team.

### How to move a board using the dashboard

**Procedure**

Follow these steps to move a board between teams from your dashboard:

1. Go to your dashboard to see all boards in a team.
2. Hover over the card of the board you want to move.
   The **three dots** (**...**) icon will appear in the upper right corner of the card.
   > ✏️ If your dashboard is in list view, the three dots icon will be visible on the right side of the board line item.
3. Click the **three dots** (**...**) icon.
   A menu will open.
4. Click on **Move to team**.
   A popup will open.
5. Select a new team from the dropdown.
6. Click **Move**.

   You have successfully moved the board to a different team.
