## 2023

### January 9

- **Over 90 new community templates added to Miroverse:** Our endlessly creative community shared over [90 new templates in Miroverse](https://miro.com/miroverse/newly-added/) so you can build the right foundations with Prod MBA’s [Compelling Product Offer](https://miro.com/miroverse/the-compelling-product-offer/), kick of the new year with Klaas Hermans's [Stronger, Better, Faster: Self-Growth Plan](https://miro.com/miroverse/stronger-better-faster-selfgrowth-plan/), or realign the team with Mode team’s [Mission Based Values Workshop](https://miro.com/miroverse/mission-based-values-workshop/).

## 2022

### December 19

- **Value Stream Mapping Shape Pack:** Teams can easily create and collaborate on [value stream maps](../formats/diagramming/01-miro-for-mapping-diagramming.md#available-shape-packs) in real-time, allowing for more efficient and effective continuous improvement efforts.
- **Bugs and other updates**
  - **Templates:** It’s now easier to find the templates you’ve been looking for with improvements to template search functionality.

### December 12

- **Reduce Motion Setting:** Turn on [Reduce Motion](../../getting-started/accessibility-in-miro/06-reduce-motion.md) in your Miro settings to stop or reduce movements for selected Miro features and functionality.

### December 5

- **Miro Connector for Okta workflows:** Enterprise admins can automate repetitive user and team management tasks with [Miro Connector for Okta Workflows](../../enterprise-administration/enterprise-subscription-management/enterprise-workflow-automation/02-miro-connector-for-okta-workflows.md).
- **Spinny Picker Wheel app for Miro:** Make brainstorms or planning sessions easy with the new [Spinny Picker Wheel app](https://miro.com/marketplace/spinny-picker-wheel/). Simply input your options and spin the wheel - it will generate a random choice for you automatically.
- **Bugs and other updates**
  - **Web Clipper Extension:** We’ve fixed an issue where full pages would not show up on the board when using the Web Clipper Extension.
  - **Performance improvements:** You will notice significant performance improvements when moving widgets on large boards.

### November 28

- **Bulk Conversion of Miro Cards to Jira Cards:** [Convert up to 50 cards into Jira cards](../../integrations-apps/atlassian/03-jira-cards.md) in one go by simply selecting the Miro card, clicking on the Jira icon, and hitting ‘Convert.’
- **Stay in the flow by adding Miroverse templates to existing boards:** Choose between [adding a template to an existing board or starting a new board](../miroverse/01-what-is-miroverse.md) from template pages.

### November 21

- **Write code in Miro and ship better-quality software:** Start collaborating on code in Miro — [Code Block](../essential-tools/04-code-block.md) includes 14 popular programming languages, copy & paste from and to Miro, and automatic syntax highlighting.
- **Bugs and other updates**

  - Miroverse search now available on mobile devices: You can now search Miroverse content across all template names, descriptions, and profiles from your mobile device.

November 7

- **Find & replace with the** [**Super Search**](https://miro.com/marketplace/super-search/) **[app](https://miro.com/marketplace/super-search/):** Ever needed to find content within the board? Now you can easily find & replace text on cards, sticky notes, and more with the Super Search app.
- **Color palettes now available in [PowerPack](https://miro.com/marketplace/powerpack/):** With the PowerPack app you can now create your own color palettes & share them with the team. Discover 50 pre-made color combination palettes and gradients to save you time on the board.
