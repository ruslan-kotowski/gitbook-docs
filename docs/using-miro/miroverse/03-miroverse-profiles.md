Showcase your expertise and take credit for your work. Profile pages ensure that anyone can learn about you and see every board you’ve published. From here you can also find all your submitted templates, including saved drafts and submission status updates.

## Creating a Miroverse profile

1. Go to [Miroverse](https://miro.com/miroverse/).
2. Click **Login** in the top-right corner. Log in with your Miro username and password.
3. Once you're logged in, click the avatar in the top-right corner to open the dropdown, and select **Create profile**.
4. Update your **Profile name** and **Username**, and click **Create Profile.**

![create_profile.jpg](images/21020253872402_create%20profile.jpg)*The option to create a Miroverse profile*

![Creating_a_Miroverse_profile.jpg](images/21020264872850_Creating%20a%20Miroverse%20profile.jpg)
*Adding Miroverse profile details*

You can then access and edit your Miroverse profile page. To open your Miroverse profile, click the profile picture or avatar in the top-right corner.
 ![Miroverse-profile-page.png](images/21020253875858_Miroverse-profile-page.png)
*Miroverse profile page*

:::note
Miroverse profile information is not connected to your Miro profile. Any updates made to your public Miroverse profile will not be reflected in your Miro user profile and vice versa.
:::

:::note
Your profile will not be discoverable in Miroverse via search until a template is published to your Miroverse profile.
:::

## Miroverse settings

To get to your Miroverse settings, click on your profile picture or avatar in the top-right corner, and click **Settings**.

Within your Miroverse settings you can [edit your profile details](#edit-your-profile), and manage your notification preferences.

![Miroverse-settings.png](images/21020264880658_Miroverse-settings.png)*Opening Miroverse profile settings*

### Edit your profile

You can update the following details of your Miroverse profile:

- **Profile image**: Your profile image will be displayed alongside your name, whenever it appears. *JPG or PNG. Max size: 5MB*
- **Profile name**: Your name will appear on your public profile page, and next to your boards. It represents who your boards will be attributed to
- **Job title**: E.g. Product Manager or Visual Collaboration Platform. This information will be displayed alongside your name
- **Company name:**This information will be displayed alongside your job title
- **Description**: This is where you can put information about yourself
- **Social links**: These links will be displayed on your public profile page

![Edit-Miroverse-Profile-Settings.png](images/21020264882450_Edit-Miroverse-Profile-Settings.png)
*Editing a Miroverse profile*

### Manage your Miroverse notifications

Select the email notifications you wish to receive. You can choose to receive updates on **Template feedback**, as well as **Thread notifications**.

![Miroverse-notification-settings.png](images/21020264878866_Miroverse-notification-settings.png)*Miroverse notification settings*

## Displaying Miro Academy Badges

When you complete courses at [Miro Academy](https://academy.miro.com/), you earn badges. These badges are showcased on your Miroverse profile.

![miroverse_academy_badges.png](images/21020253874322_miroverse_academy_badges.png)*Miro Academy badges in Miroverse*

## Miroverse profile pages for Miro Experts

Showcase your bespoke services alongside your best-in-class templates with a unique profile designed exclusively for Miro Experts.

Miro Expert profile pages display key information about your company including expertise, size, languages you support, as well as your social media links, templates and likes.

Users can reach out to you directly through the **Get in touch** button on your profile.

:::note
When viewing your own profile you'll see the **Submit** button in place of the **Get in touch** button. To preview how others will see your profile either log out or open your profile in incognito.
:::

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I delete my Miroverse profile?**

To delete your Miroverse profile, email us your request at miroverse@miro.com. Deleting your Miroverse profile will not delete your Miro profile or existing boards.

**Can I transfer ownership of my Miroverse profile?**

Yes, the owner of a Miroverse profile can transfer primary ownership to another registered Miro user. Please email miroverse@miro.com to request the transfer, and include the name and email of the new profile owner.
