[Miroverse](https://miro.com/miroverse/) is a community templates gallery that showcases useful and innovative board templates created and shared by the Miro community. You can use its search and browse functions to explore a wide variety of workflows, projects, and frameworks. When you find a template you like, you can make a copy to your own team and get started right away.

:::tip
Visit the [Miroverse Creator Design Toolkit](https://miro.com/miroverse/miroverse-creator-design-toolkit/) for more Miroverse publishing tips and resources, plus access to the Creator Community and events.
:::

:::tip
Learn more about how to [publish your own board](06-how-to-get-published-on-miroverse.md).
:::

:::warning
Miroverse is unavailable for customers enrolled in US and AU Data Residency.
:::

## Miroverse vs. the template Library

Both Miroverse and the template library allow you to make copies of existing board templates to your Miro team, helping you start new projects without building a board from scratch. However, the templates offered in each gallery have distinct origins and purposes.

### The template library

- Built by the Miro team.
- Suitable for a variety of general use cases.
- Based on industry best practices and customer feedback.

### Miroverse

- Built by Miro users.
- A showcase of both personal and professional use cases.
- Highly specific to their individual ways of working.
- A window into how real companies and industry leaders use Miro.
