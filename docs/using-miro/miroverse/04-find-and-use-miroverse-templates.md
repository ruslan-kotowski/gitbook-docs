Miroverse is a community template library where you can discover and use boards created by other Miro users. Find templates for various collections, roles, and use cases to kickstart your work.

You can access [Miroverse](https://miro.com/miroverse/) directly or from your Miro dashboard:

1. On your dashboard, click **+ Create new**.
2. Select **Explore Miroverse**.

The main page features a collection of popular templates, a search bar, and a list of categories to help you find the perfect board for your needs.

## Find a template

This section describes how to locate templates in Miroverse by Browse categories or using the search function.

### Browse by category

On the Miroverse homepage, you can browse templates by category.

1. Click a category at the top of the page to see all templates included in that category.
2. From a category page, you can sort templates by **Recently added**, **Most used**, **Views**, and **Likes**.

### Search for a template

You can use the search bar to find specific templates.

1. Type your search terms into the search box. Your query will be matched against template titles, profile names, or company names. Matching terms are highlighted in the results.
2. To return to the Miroverse homepage from a search or category page, click **Miro | Miroverse** next to the search bar.

:::tip
To share search results, copy the URL generated in your browser's address bar.
:::

## Use a template

Once you find a template you like, you can preview it and add it to a new or existing board.

1. Click on a template's card to open its page and preview the content.
2. On the template page, you have two options:
   - To create a new board from the template, click **Use template**.
   - To add the template to a board you've already created, click the dropdown menu next to **Use template** and select the board.

:::note
If you do not have a Miro profile, you will be prompted to sign up for free first. If you have a profile but are not signed in, you must sign in to use a template.
:::

:::tip
For quick access to the templates you use regularly, save the board as a [custom template](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/02-custom-templates.md).
:::
