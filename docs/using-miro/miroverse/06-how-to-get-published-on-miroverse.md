[Miroverse](https://miro.com/miroverse/) is a community library of templates created by Miro users. A template is a Miro board with a pre-made framework that can be used for repeatable activities. By sharing your unique workflows, projects, and frameworks in Miroverse, you can inspire others and help them get started faster.

## How to submit your template

To publish your board in [Miroverse](https://miro.com/miroverse/):

1. Navigate to the [Miroverse](https://miro.com/miroverse/) site.
2. Log in with your registered Miro profile.
3. Click **Submit a template** in the top-right corner of any Miroverse page to open the submission form.
4. If you don't already have a Miroverse profile, you'll need to create one to submit a template. Add your photo, role, company name, and a short bio.
5. Click **Save and continue**.
6. Choose the board you'd like to submit as a template by using the dropdown to **Select a** **board**. Only the boards that you own will appear in the dropdown list.
7. Click **Continue**.

:::note
If you're on a Starter, Business, Enterprise, or Education plan, remember to [allow anyone with board access to copy the board content](../sharing-boards/14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md).
:::

8. Provide your **Template title**. Your template’s public-facing name. Choose a clear, straightforward title that clearly describes its purpose.
9. Choose up to three relevant **Categories** for your template based on its use cases.
10. Select the main **Role** or function your board benefits (Engineering, Product, etc.). If you don’t see an exact match, choose the one that fits most closely.
11. Write a **Description** for your template. Include sections such as about your template, how to use it, and who it's for, plus any tips or best practices. Use the formatting toolbar in the description field to add section headers (H1, H2), bullets, numbered lists, or other formatting that makes your content easier to read and navigate. For more, visit [How to write a Miroverse template description](10-tips-for-writing-a-miroverse-template-description.md).
12. Add a **Cover image**. Upload a screenshot (max size 1080x480px) of the most compelling section(s) of your template.
13. (Optional) Add a **Video walkthrough**. Add a [Talktrack](../facilitation-tools/asynchronous-tools/02-talktrack-board-recordings.md) to visually explain and demonstrate how to use your template with audio and video. You can also add a YouTube, Loom, or Wistia link.![Step_2__Describe_the_template.jpg](images/21019734910866_Step%202_%20Describe%20the%20template.jpg)*Adding details about the template*
14. (Optional) Click **Preview** to view your template before submitting it for review.
15. Click **Submit** once the form is complete.

The Miroverse submissions team will review your template, which typically takes up to two business days. The team will either publish your template if it meets the [publishing criteria and guidelines](08-miroverse-publishing-checklist-and-guidelines.md), or send you feedback to get it publish-ready. You will receive updates via the email address associated with your Miroverse profile.

:::note
If you have any questions about the process, contact the Miroverse team at [miroverse@miro.com](mailto:miroverse@miro.com), or reply to any of the submission status emails directly.
:::

## Submitting Format Templates

In addition to a board template with multiple elements, you can also submit [Format](../formats/02-formats-focus-modes.md) Templates with a single Doc, Table, Timeline, Diagram, or Slides. These focused, singular tools are perfect for teams to use as building blocks for larger frameworks, or on their own.

### Create and submit your Format:

1. Build a Format in Miro.
   1. Open your board and click the **Tools, Media, and Integrations icon** (**+**) in the toolbar.
   2. Search for and select the Format you want to use: Doc, Table, Timeline, Diagram, or Slides.
   3. Click anywhere on the canvas to add the Format.
   4. Customize the Format to create your template.
2. Set Focus mode as the default view.
   1. Click the **vertical three dots** (![icon-main.svg](images/28020430370962_icon-main.svg)) icon in the context menu next to your Format.
   2. Select **Set as default view**.![](images/28020475735954_9bddcb20-38ba-4a25-b33c-f7d59a42609f.png)
      *Set as default view*
3. Use the [template submission form](https://miro.com/miroverse/submission/) to share your Format with the community.

## How to edit, preview, or delete your drafts and templates

All changes to your submission are automatically saved as you go. To leave the submission form if you’re not ready to submit, simply click **Exit**, or the **Miroverse logo** in the top left corner. The template will appear as a draft on your profile page and will be visible only to you. You can access your submission anytime from your Miroverse profile drafts.

If you need to [edit](05-how-to-edit-your-templates-on-miroverse.md), **preview**, or **delete a draft**:

1. Navigate to your Miroverse profile page.
2. Click the **three dots** (**...**) icon in the top-right corner of the template box.
3. Select the action you’d like to take (**Edit**, **Preview**, or **Delete**).

:::note
If you have any questions about the process you can reach the submissions team by replying to any of the submission status emails.
:::

### Frequently asked questions

**How do I adjust the preview for my template?**

Set the [start view](../sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#start-view) for your board before uploading it to the submission form. To adjust the preview after its been published, you must upload a new board with the adjusted start view set through edit mode.

**How do I add a Talktrack to my template?**

Record a [Talktrack](../facilitation-tools/asynchronous-tools/02-talktrack-board-recordings.md) on the board you’d like to submit. Your Talktracks will be automatically included with your template, and your template will have a special tag indicating that it includes a Talktrack.

**Can I delete the board after I submit it to Miroverse?**

To ensure boards remain available for all users after publication, we create a copy of your board when you hit submit. You can delete your version once submitted to Miroverse, but we recommend deleting only after successfully being published in case edits need to be made.

**How can I delete my template from Miroverse?**

To remove your published template from Miroverse, please reach out to the Miroverse team at [miroverse@miro.com](mailto:miroverse@miro.com). Deleting a template doesn't affect copies already made of the board. Anyone who created a copy of your template before it was removed can continue to access and edit their copy.

**What happened to my template comments?**

When a template is submitted to Miroverse, any associated comments will be removed in order to provide a fresh experience. The comments will not be removed from the template's board, only from the template itself.

**How should I approach accessibility when creating my template?**

Miro aims to create an inclusive experience for all users. Miroverse, our community-driven space, hosts user-generated templates. Creators retain ownership and are responsible for their content, ensuring compliance with laws and audience needs. While accessibility isn't required, we encourage [inclusive design](../../getting-started/accessibility-in-miro/04-how-to-make-your-miro-boards-more-accessible.md) for broader usability.

By submitting to Miroverse, creators agree that:

- Their content is public and not regularly monitored by Miro.
- Miro isn’t responsible for the accessibility or accuracy of submissions.

Learn more about how we approach accessibility in our core product [here](https://miro.com/accessibility/), and explore a [detailed overview of our accessibility features](../../getting-started/accessibility-in-miro/01-overview-of-miro-accessibility.md) in our Help Center.
