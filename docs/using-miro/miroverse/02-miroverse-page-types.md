Miroverse consists of two main page types to help you find what you need: template description pages and user profile pages.

### Template description pages

Each template in Miroverse has its own description page where you can find detailed information about the board and its creator. Some templates also include a short video introduction that provides a personal tour and extra context to help you get started.

From a template description page, you can also see how the community is engaging with a template. You can check the number of likes, views, and copies a template has, and also review feedback from other users.

### Miroverse profile pages

Profile pages allow you to learn more about template creators and see every board they’ve published. From a creator's profile page, you can view their bio, profile picture, social media links, and all of their published templates.

## Use and engage with Miroverse content

You can use templates from Miroverse and engage with the community by providing feedback and liking your favorite templates.

### Use a template

When you find a template you want to use, click **Use template** to get started.

If you're logged into your Miro account, the new board will be automatically saved in the **All Boards** section under your last active team.

:::tip
You can easily [move the created template between teams](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/05-toolbars.md) or to a specific Space. To start collaborating, [invite users to the board](../sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#how-to-invite-to-a-board) or [enable public access to the board via a link](../sharing-boards/08-collaboration-with-visitors.md).
:::

**More information:** See [Find and use Miroverse templates](04-find-and-use-miroverse-templates.md).

### Like templates and give feedback

Anyone logged into their Miro profile can add likes and feedback to a template in Miroverse. Liking a template is a way to show your appreciation and save your favorite Miroverse templates for later.

- To show your support, click the **♡** icon on a template card or on the template's description page.
- To remove a like, click the **♡** icon again.
- To give feedback or leave a comment on a template, click the message icon.

### Find liked templates and creator profiles

There are two main ways to find the templates you have liked:

- In the top-right corner of any Miroverse page, click your profile picture and select **Likes**.
- Select the **Likes** tab from any public Miroverse profile page.

To learn more about a Miroverse creator and see all the templates they’ve created, click their name on any template page or template card. You can view any creator’s liked templates from their Miroverse profile. You can also share your own liked templates from your Miroverse profile. If you don't have a Miroverse profile, your liked templates are saved to a private gallery that only you can access.
