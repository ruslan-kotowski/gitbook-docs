Miroverse allows users to share feedback and exchange insights on templates, helping to ensure quality and create collaborative learning opportunities. By leaving constructive comments, you can help creators improve their templates and contribute to the community.

:::note
To leave template feedback, you need to be logged into [Miroverse](01-what-is-miroverse.md).
:::

:::warning
All comments on Miroverse templates are moderated. Please follow our [Miroverse feedback guidelines](#miroverse-feedback-guidelines).
:::

## How to share feedback and comments

You can share your thoughts on a template directly from your Miroverse profile or from the template's page. You can also engage with the community by posting and replying to comments.

### Give feedback from your profile

1. Click your user profile picture in the top-right corner.
2. Click **Template feedback**.
3. Under the **Template feedback** tab, you’ll see all the templates you’ve used.
4. Click **Give feedback**, or **Skip** to remove a template from your list.
5. Under **What did you like about this template?** select the applicable reasons.
6. You can also **Leave a public** [**comment**](#how-to-manage-comments) (up to 1,000 characters). Remember to follow our [Miroverse feedback guidelines](#miroverse-feedback-guidelines).
7. Click **Share feedback** to submit.

![Giving-template-feedback-from-profile.gif](images/21016044663058_Giving-template-feedback-from-profile.gif)
*Leaving feedback on a template from the Miroverse profile page*

### Give feedback from a template page

1. Navigate to the template page you want to give feedback on.
2. Click the **Like** (heart) icon to save the template.
3. Click the **Template feedback** icon.
4. Under **What did you like about this template?** select the applicable reasons.
5. You can also **Leave a public** [**comment**](#how-to-manage-comments) (up to 1,000 characters). Remember to follow our Miroverse feedback guidelines.
6. Click **Share feedback** to submit.

![Leaving-template-feedback-from-template-page.gif](images/21016044664082_Leaving-template-feedback-from-template-page.gif)
*Leaving feedback on a template from a template page*

### How to manage comments

All comments you leave as part of your feedback automatically appear in the **Comments** section on the template page. You can also go directly to a template page to share your thoughts, engage in discussions, and manage your comments.

To leave a comment:

1. Start typing in the **Leave a comment** field.
2. Click **Post**.

![Leaving-a-comment-on-a-Miroverse-template.png](images/21016044661778_Leaving-a-comment-on-a-template.png)
*Leaving a comment on a Miroverse template*

To join the conversation, you can **reply** to other comments.

![Replying-to-comment-miroverse-template.png](images/21016044667026_Replying-to-comment-miroverse-template.png)
*Replying to a comment*

You can sort comments by **Newest first** or **Oldest first**.

![Sorting-Miroverse-template-comments.png](images/21016044667538_Sorting-Miroverse-template-comments.png)
*Sorting comments*

To **Edit** or **Delete** your comments after submission, click the **three dots** (**...**) menu next to your comment.

![Deleting-a-comment-on-a-miroverse-template.png](images/21016044666386_Deleting-a-comment-on-a-miroverse-template.png)
*The option to edit or delete a comment*

:::note
You can turn on notifications for template comments and replies in your [Miroverse settings](03-miroverse-profiles.md#manage-your-miroverse-notifications).
:::

## Miroverse feedback guidelines

Thank you for contributing to our vibrant community. To maintain a positive and collaborative environment in Miroverse, please adhere to the following guidelines when leaving comments. Your active participation enhances the experience for all members, and we eagerly anticipate your continued contributions.

### **Things to do:**

- **Express your gratitude and appreciation** for template creators. Building templates is a labor of love, and your positive comments go a long way in acknowledging their hard work and dedication.
- **Provide constructive feedback**: Specific and well-detailed feedback empowers creators to make great templates. For instance, instead of stating, "I don't understand how to use your template," you could say, "It would be helpful if you included more detailed instructions for steps one and two of your workshop. Great work!"
- **Offer positive encouragement**: Highlight what creators are doing well. Positive feedback is just as important as constructive feedback. Be specific in your praise, as it assists creators in understanding what aspects of their templates resonate with others.
- **Interact with others**: Jump into conversations to engage and network with other commenters on a post! Plus up ideas, add context, or provide tips and resources to help others be more successful using Miroverse’s vast library of templates. Everyone is welcome!

### **Things to avoid:**

- **Refrain from promoting or selling**: Comments should focus on engaging with the template and the community, not on promotion.
- **Avoid excessive self-promotion**: Linking to or promoting irrelevant content or social media profiles without adding value to the conversation is not appropriate. Such behavior may result in the removal of your comment.
- **Steer clear of spammy behavior**: Keep comments relevant to Miroverse, the specific template, or the ongoing discussion. Avoid posting the same comment repeatedly or introducing unrelated content.
- **Do not provoke negativity**: Comments with the intent of provoking or inciting negative interactions are not allowed. Repeated violations may lead to content removal and restrictions on commenting.
- **No hate speech or discrimination**: Comments targeting individuals or groups based on their race, religion, gender, or other characteristics are unacceptable. Such comments will be promptly removed, and commenting privileges may be revoked.
- **Do not be disrespectful**: Avoid personal attacks, insults, or name-calling directed at other commenters or individuals mentioned in the discussion. Comments of this nature will be removed, and the ability to comment might be restricted.
