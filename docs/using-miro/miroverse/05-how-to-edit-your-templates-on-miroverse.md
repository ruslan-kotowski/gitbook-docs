## Enter editing mode

You can enter editing mode in one of two ways:

1. Navigate to your template and select **Edit** from the top right of the page, just above the preview.

   ![Entering the template editing mode](images/19597178689938_template-1.png)

   *Entering the template editing mode*
2. Alternatively, enter editing mode for a published template directly from your Miroverse profile.

   ![Entering the template editing mode from your Miroverse profile](images/19597178692498_template-2.png)

   *Entering the template editing mode from your Miroverse profile*

## Update template contents

From the template editing form, you can change the board used for the template and update its details.

![Template editing form](images/19597178694034_template-3.png)

*Template editing form*

### Change the template board

When you submit a template to Miroverse, a copy of your original board is created which becomes the live version. To change the board content in your template:

1. First, edit your board in Miro.
2. Once you've made the necessary edits, return to your template on Miroverse and click **Edit**.
3. Choose **Change board** at the top of the form.
4. Select your updated board.

### Change template details

From this editing form, you can also change your template’s:

- Name
- Categories
- Description
- Video links
- Cover image

## Publish your changes

When you've finished updating your template, you must publish the changes to make them available in Miroverse.

1. Once you’re happy with the edits, preview them.
2. Select **Publish** to push them live.

After you click **Publish**, you’ll receive a confirmation via email and your updates will be reflected on your template within minutes.
