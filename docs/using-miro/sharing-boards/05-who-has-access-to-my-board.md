Miro users can share their boards with collaborators and restrict access at any moment.

### How to check your board sharing settings

To see who has access to your board, open the board **Share** dialog. You can do that from your dashboard or from within the board.

![share_from_the_dashboard.jpeg](images/21017528905618_share%20from%20the%20dashboard.jpeg)
*Opening the board Share dialog from the dashboard*

![share_menu.jpg](images/21017515925138_share%20menu.jpg)
*The Share button in the top-right corner of the board*

A board can be shared on different levels:

- [Team](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md) (anyone at the team can view/comment/edit)
- [Company](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-a-board-with-the-entire-company) (every member can access the board via link, available on [Enterprise plan](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/04-enterprise-plan.md) only)
- [Public](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-boards-via-a-public-link) (anyone with the board link can view/comment/edit as a [visitor](08-collaboration-with-visitors.md))
- [Spaces](../spaces/01-spaces.md) (every space participant can view/comment/edit depending on their role in the space)
- Board (specific users invited to the board [via email](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email))

Click **Sharing settings** to see the emails of users that were invited to the board via email. In the example below, every member of **My Team** can [comment](01-board-access-rights.md) the board while **Jane** can [edit](01-board-access-rights.md).

![who_has_access_to_the_board.gif](images/21017515917458_who%20has%20access%20to%20the%20board.gif)
*Sharing settings show emails of users invited to the board*

A board may be shared on different levels at the same time. The level with **higher access** applies to a user. Please see examples:

- If a team member was invited to a board via email as a **commenter** *and* anyone at the team **can edit** the board, the team level access applies to the user and they **can edit** the board
- If a board is shared publicly with edit access (Anyone with the link **can edit**), every user on the board can change its content

:::tip
[Learn how to create a private board](15-make-a-miro-board-private.md).
:::

### Who can change the board sharing settings

Board owners, [co-owners](06-co-owners-of-boards-and-spaces.md), and editors who are members of the team where the board is stored can share and restrict access to the board. Board owners and [co-owners](06-co-owners-of-boards-and-spaces.md) on paid plans can [configure sharing permissions](02-who-can-share-a-miro-board.md) and withdraw the editors’ option to change board access and to invite other users.

### Board access on Free plan

Please note that boards stored in [free teams](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md) can be accessed by all team members. To create private boards, please upgrade your team.
