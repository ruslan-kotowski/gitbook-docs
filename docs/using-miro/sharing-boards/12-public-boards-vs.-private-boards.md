Miro allows setting different levels of access for your boards. Read the article to learn how private and public boards are different from each other.

## What is a private board?

> **Which plans:** Starter, Business, Enterprise, Education

Private boards are accessible by the board owner only and are not shared with anyone else. Depending on [default sharing settings](11-default-sharing-settings.md), all created boards on a paid plan can be set as private until [shared](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md) by the owner or shared with the team automatically. Learn [here](15-make-a-miro-board-private.md) how you can create a private board in a paid or Education team.

On a [Free plan](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md), *all boards created will be accessible to the team* without being explicitly shared. The **Share** window for Free plan boards lets users know it is available to the whole team by default. You can see your team members in [Team settings](../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/01-i-am-a-new-miro-admin.-where-to-start.md) on the **Active users** tab.

## What is a public board?

> **Which plans:** Free, Starter, Business, Enterprise, Education

A public board is a board accessible by anyone who has the board link. To set your board public:

1. Click the **Share** button in the upper right corner of your board.
2. Choose **Can view/comment/edit** next to **Anyone with the link**.
3. Copy the board link to share with other users. They will then be able to view/comment/edit the board according to the permissions set. Note that the board will become available for unregistered users as well.

:::note
Apart from sharing boards publicly, Miro allows you to invite collaborators via email or enable team/Company/Spaces access. Learn more in the article [Sharing boards and inviting collaborators](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md).
:::

## Set a shared board to private

> **Available for:** Starter, Business, Enterprise, and Education plans

To make your board private, make sure to stop sharing on [different access levels](01-board-access-rights.md): with the team, Company (if you're on Enterprise plan), publicly, with a space, and with particular users by unselecting them in **Sharing settings** on the **Share** dialog. You will have to remove each user until it only shows you (the owner).

If you're **not** the board owner and have access to the board via [team](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md)/[space](../spaces/01-spaces.md#how-to-move-boards-between-spaces), please make sure to [invite your own email](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email) to the board. Otherwise, you will **lose** access to the board as soon as you remove team/space access.

Note that on [Free plan](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md) you cannot stop sharing a board with the team.

**More information:** See [Make a Miro board private](15-make-a-miro-board-private.md).

## FAQ

Why did my boards become locked after downgrading to the Free plan?

The Free plan doesn't allow private boards. Please share the board with your team to unlock it. Learn more in the article [The board is locked](../tools/troubleshooting/15-the-board-is-locked.md).

I have seen the option to upgrade to make my board private. Is my board public by default?

On the Free Plan, it is shared with the entire team by default. It only relates to your boards being "shared with the team" within your Free team.

Can my public boards be found online?

Miro strives to keep public boards from being indexed by search engines like Google or Bing. However, anyone with the link can access these boards, and the link might be shared beyond your intended audience. Depending on your Miro plan, you can enhance security by [setting a password for your public board](13-password-protection-for-public-boards.md).

I am a member of a free team and of a paid team. Can I move my boards from the free team to the paid one to make them private?

Yes, you can [move your boards](../managing-boards/04-how-to-move-a-board.md).

Can my public boards be duplicated?

Yes, if this is allowed in your [board content settings](14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md).

When I share a board publicly, do I need to pay for users who will access the board via the board link?

No, visitors will be able to access the board for free. Learn how you can [share your boards publicly](08-collaboration-with-visitors.md).
