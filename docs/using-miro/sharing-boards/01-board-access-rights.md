Users can have three different levels of access to Miro boards: viewing, commenting and editing. Learn more about access levels and how to manage them for users.

### Setting up access rights

You can [share a Miro board](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md):

- with specific users via email
- with space members
- with the whole team or company
- publicly with anyone who has the board link

Whenever you share a board, you can specify access rights for your invitees by choosing **Can edit**, **Can comment,** or **Can view**.

1. Hover over the board on your dashboard.
2. Click the three dots in the top right corner.
3. Click **Share.**
4. Type the email address of the user you want to invite or choose another option on the Share window (e.g., Anyone at the team, Anyone with the link, etc.).
5. The default access level is **Can edit**. Click **Can edit** to change the access level to **Can comment** or **Can view.**

### Board access rights

Owners, co-owners, editors, commenters, and viewers have different access rights on the board.

Board owner Board co-owner Edit Comment View

Each board has a board owner. There can only be **one** board owner per board. The board owner can:

- [move the board between teams](../managing-boards/04-how-to-move-a-board.md)
- download [board backups](../import-and-export/export/05-how-to-save-board-backup.md) (on paid plans)
- [add co-owners](06-co-owners-of-boards-and-spaces.md) (Business Plan and Enterprise Plan)
- [set board password](13-password-protection-for-public-boards.md) (on paid plans)
- [hide frames](../essential-tools/07-frames.md), [use protected lock](../working-on-the-board/21-work-smarter-not-harder.md) (on paid plans)
- [configure board content settings](14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md) (on paid plans)
- configure [collaboration tools settings](../facilitation-tools/06-collaboration-tools.md)
- configure [who can share the board](02-who-can-share-a-miro-board.md)
- [transfer board ownership to another collaborator](../managing-boards/05-how-to-transfer-board-ownership.md)
- [delete the board](../managing-boards/07-how-to-delete-a-board.md)

> **Relevant for**: Business Plan, Enterprise Plan

On Business and Enterprise plans, users can also add board co-owners. Board co-owners can:

- manage [board content settings](14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md) and [collaboration tool settings](../facilitation-tools/06-collaboration-tools.md)
- [h](../facilitation-tools/06-collaboration-tools.md)[ide and reveal frames, a](../essential-tools/07-frames.md)[dd protected lock](../working-on-the-board/17-locking-content-on-the-board.md#use-protected-lock)
- [add board password](13-password-protection-for-public-boards.md)
- [download a board backup](../import-and-export/export/05-how-to-save-board-backup.md)
- add other co-owners
- change the board cover image and rename the board
- [share the board](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md)
- configure [advanced board sharing permissions](02-who-can-share-a-miro-board.md)

Learn more about [board co-owners](06-co-owners-of-boards-and-spaces.md).

Editors can make changes to board content and delete or upload new files. Editors can use various board tools and settings depending on whether they’re a visitor, guest, or member.

Learn more about the [difference between visitors, guests, and members](04-sharing-with-users-outside-your-team.md).

Editors can copy the board content if this is allowed in the [board content settings](14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md).

**Sharing with editor rights**

When an editor is invited to a board as a member of the team (they have a paid seat/license), they can access and change the sharing settings.

Board owners and [co-owners](06-co-owners-of-boards-and-spaces.md) on paid plans can [restrict this option for board editors](02-who-can-share-a-miro-board.md).

Commenters can view the board and leave [comments](../facilitation-tools/asynchronous-tools/01-comments.md) on it. Registered Miro users who are invited to a board as a commenter can join [voting](../facilitation-tools/19-voting.md) sessions and see the running [timer](../facilitation-tools/16-timer.md). Commenters can copy the board content if this is allowed in the [board content settings](14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md).

Commenters can’t edit the board and share it.
![commenter_s view.png](images/21016033196434_commenter_s%20view.png)
*Commenter's view of the board*

Viewers can't make any changes to the board, they can only see its content. Their toolbox only displays the zoom and map options, and the toolbar is unavailable. Registered viewers can join [voting](../facilitation-tools/19-voting.md) sessions and see the running [timer](../facilitation-tools/16-timer.md). Registered viewers can also copy the board content if this is allowed in the [board content settings](14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md). If there are [frames](../essential-tools/07-frames.md) on the board, viewers can switch between them in the board overview, located in the bottom-left corner of the board.

### How to request access to a board

If you follow a board link and see this message, it means the board isn’t shared with you yet.

![board is not shared.png](images/21016019782162_board%20is%20not%20shared.png)
*Requesting access to the board*

To view, comment, or edit a board, click **Request Access**. This will send an email message to the board owner. They will be able to give you access and define your access rights.

If you only have viewing or commenting rights, you can request editing access by clicking the **Comment only** button in the top right corner and selecting **Request editor rights**. Please note that this feature is not supported on the [Education plan](../../plans-billing/miro-special-pricing/03-education-plan.md).

:::tip
If you can’t access a board even though you’ve been granted access or you created the board, visit this guide: [I can’t access or edit a Miro board](../tools/troubleshooting/08-i-can't-access-or-edit-a-miro-board.md).
:::

### Changing access rights

Change an individual user’s access Change team or company accessChange Spaces access Change public access

1. Click on the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the board.
2. Open **Sharing settings.** You will see the list of people who have access to the board via email.
3. Specify access rights for a user by clicking on the drop-down list.
4. Here you can also remove collaborators by clicking **Remove**.  The board owner will also see the option to give another user board ownership.
5. Confirm the change by clicking **Done**.
   > ⚠️ The user may **retain their previous level of access** if the board is shared with the whole team, a space, the company, or publicly. See [who has access to your board.](05-who-has-access-to-my-board.md)


   ![change_access_rights_greybg.gif](images/21016033213586_change_access_rights_greybg.gif)
   *Changing access rights of an editor to **Can comment***

1. Click the **Share** button.
2. If you're **not** the board owner and have access to the board via team/company access, please make sure to [invite your own email](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email) to the board prior to the next step. Otherwise, you will **lose** access to the board as soon as you remove team/company access.
3. Find **Anyone with team** or **Anyone with the link at Company** on the **Share** window.
4. Choose a different access type on the drop-down menu or remove access by selecting **No access**.
   > ⚠️ Users may **retain their previous level of access** if the board is shared via email, with a space, or publicly. See [who has access to your board.](05-who-has-access-to-my-board.md)

   ![removing_team_access_to_a_board_greybg.gif](images/21016033198994_removing_team_access_to_a_board_greybg.gif)
   *Removing team access to a board*

You can either remove a board from a space or change the access level of space members.

To remove a board from a space:

1. Click the **Share** button.
2. If you're **not** the board owner and have access to the board via Spaces access, please make sure to [invite your own email](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email) to the board prior to the next step. Otherwise, you will **lose** access to the board as soon as you remove space access.
3. Click the space name at the bottom of the Share window.
4. Select **None**.

:::warning
Users may **retain their previous level of access** if the board is shared with the whole team, via email, with the company, or publicly. See [who has access to your board.](05-who-has-access-to-my-board.md)
:::

To change the access level of Spaces members.

2. Click the space name to open its settings.

### Frequently asked questions

I removed a user from my board but that user is still present on the board. How do I stop the user's access?

The user who is not invited directly can still access your board if it is shared [via other means](01-board-access-rights.md) ([team](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md)/[space](../spaces/01-spaces.md)/[Company](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-a-board-with-the-entire-company)/[public](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-boards-via-a-public-link) access). Please check the board sharing settings and [remove the access](#changing-access-rights).

How do I check who is the board owner?

The board owner's name is visible on the [board information card](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/01-create-a-miro-board.md). Note that your access level may not be enough to see this information. However, if you received an invitation to the board, you can also see the inviter's name in the email notification.

I created a board but I can’t access it now. How do I restore access?

There could be multiple reasons for this. Learn about potential causes: [I can’t access or a edit a Miro board](../tools/troubleshooting/08-i-can't-access-or-edit-a-miro-board.md).

How do I transfer my board out of a team?

You can [move your board to another team](../managing-boards/04-how-to-move-a-board.md) if you're a member of several teams. If you're not, you can [create your own team](../../plans-billing/manage-your-subscription-and-plan/03-upgrade-your-plan.md).

Can I transfer my board to a user who is not a member of my team?

No, you can only assign board ownership to your team members. As a workaround, the user can [create a copy of your board](../managing-boards/03-how-to-duplicate-a-board.md) in their own team (please make sure that this is allowed in [the board content settings](14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md)). The person should be a registered Miro user and a member of another team.
