Looking for a quicker way to share all newly created team boards or Spaces with a specific audience? Admins can customize the default sharing settings for boards and Spaces you and your team create, and share them from the very start with a particular level of access rights.

### How to configure default sharing settings

As an admin, feel free to adjust new boards' status to your workflow by going to your **Team Settings** > **Permissions** > **Sharing settings** section.

Note that sharing settings of individual boards and Spaces can be changed by their owners, co-owners, and editors. Users can share boards with team members and people outside the team. To learn more about sharing options, visit this article: [Sharing boards and inviting collaborators](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md).

![sharing_settings.png](images/21016941369618_sharing_settings.png)*Sharing settings*

### Board sharing settings

By default, all newly created boards *on paid and Education plans* are set as private and can only be visible to their owners. Choose from the drop-down menu whether you want all new team boards:

- to remain private
- to become accessible to any team member with [viewer, commenter, or editor rights](01-board-access-rights.md)
- to become accessible to any [Enterprise plan](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/04-enterprise-plan.md) member, regardless of teams they participate in, with viewer or commenter rights

:::warning
All boards on [*Free plan*](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md) are shared with the team by default and cannot be private.
:::

:::warning
Company shared boards (on Enterprise plan) do not appear on a dashboard unless users participate in the same team where the board is located. At the same time, all members of your Enterprise plan can open such boards by a link, find such boards and open them from search results.
:::

:::note
Team admins on the Starter, Business, Enterprise, and Education plans can [configure default board content settings](14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md).
:::

### Invite to board and edit sharing settings

The default setting for who can invite users to a board and edit the board's sharing settings. Board owners and co-owners can change this setting.

:::note
This setting applies only to newly created board. This setting does not change invite to board and sharing settings for existing boards.
:::

Click to select the appropriate users who can invite users to a board and edit the board's sharing settings by default.

### Spaces and sections sharing settings

Choose from one of the two options:

- you can either keep new Team Spaces *private*and not accessible to anyone but their owner or
- make them *viewable*for all members of your team so that the space owner doesn't need to add users from the team manually

After setting the general sharing settings for all newly created boards or Spaces, you will still be able to [customize the sharing settings](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#how-to-invite-to-a-board) on particular boards' and Spaces' menus.

Content admins have content management privileges. For Spaces and sections, the Content Admin can change metadata, move and delete boards inside a Space or section, and move boards to another Space or section. The same privileges apply to private boards, Spaces, and sections.

**More information:** See [Roles in Miro](../../getting-started/start-here/05-roles-in-miro.md).

:::note
Note that [space](../spaces/01-spaces.md) access and board access complement each other and work at the same time: for example, if a board is not [shared with the whole team](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md) but belongs to a team-view space, everyone in the team will be able to access the board.
:::
