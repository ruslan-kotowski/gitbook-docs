In some cases you may want to invite external users to your board without making them full members of your team - like if you’re working on a project with a consultant, client or colleague.

All users have the option to share boards with **Visitors** using a public board link. Paid plan users also have the option to share boards with **Guests**. The difference between Visitors and Guests is the amount of security you have over a board and the level of access you can provide to individual users.

Our recommendation is to invite:

- Visitors when you're working on one-time collaboration
- Guests when you're occasionally collaborating with clients or partners
- Full team members for frequent and ongoing collaboration

### External sharing options based on your plan

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| **Visitors**  **(public link)** | ✔  View only | ✔  View, comment, edit | ✔  View, comment, edit |
| **Guests** | ✘ | ✔  View, comment only | ✔  View, comment, edit |

## Collaborating with Visitors

To collaborate with Visitors you need to make your board public.

Visitors will be able to view, comment and edit depending on the access level you set and your plan type. Visitors don’t need to register with Miro and you can invite them to your board for free. Learn more about the [Visitor](08-collaboration-with-visitors.md) role.

:::warning
Miro strives to keep public boards from being indexed by search engines like Google or Bing. However, anyone with the link can access these boards, and the link might be shared beyond your intended audience. Depending on your Miro plan, you can enhance security by setting a [password for your public board](13-password-protection-for-public-boards.md).
:::

Free Starter, Business, Enterprise, Education

1. Go to your dashboard and hover over the board card
2. Click the three dots in the top right corner
3. Click **Share**
4. Set the type of access as **Anyone with the link**
5. Select **Can view** (editing and commenting are only available for Visitors on paid and Education plans)
6. Click **Copy board link** and share it directly with the user you'd like to invite

## Collaborating with Guests

Collaborating with Guests offers an additional level of security for your board. Your boards remain viewable only to those who are invited to them, either as Guests or team members.

Guests are required to sign in to Miro. They'll have access to many features of your paid Miro plan, as well as continued access to any shared boards in their dashboard. Adding Guests is free and there's no limit to the number of Guests you can add.

Remember to [enable Guests](07-collaboration-with-guests.md#enable-or-disable-guests) in your Team settings.

Learn more about [collaboration with Guests](07-collaboration-with-guests.md).

Business Enterprise Starter, Education

1. Go to your dashboard and hover over the board card
2. Click the three dots in the top right corner
3. Click **Share**
4. Enter their email address
5. Select **Can view, Can comment or Can edit** depending on the level of access you’d like to grant
6. Click **Send invitations**
7. If you choose **Can edit**, you’ll also see a pop-up with the option to add as a Team member or Guest. Choose **Guest**

Stop sharing a board externally

You can stop sharing a board with Guests and Visitors at any time.

### How to stop sharing with Visitors

To stop sharing your board with Visitors, you need to remove public access to the board:

1. Click **Share**in the top right of the board
2. Next to **Anyone with the link**select **No access** from the dropdown

![4 Share button (6).gif](images/20257399193618_4%20Share%20button%20(6)
*.gif)Removing access for Visitors*

### How to stop sharing with Guests

1. Click **Share**in the top right of the board
2. Go to **Sharing settings**
3. Click the dropdown menu next to guest
4. Select **Remove** to delete the user's access to the board

> **⚠️** When you change a user’s access level, there’s still a chance they may **retain their usage rights**if the board is shared with them publicly, or as part of a team, space, or company.
