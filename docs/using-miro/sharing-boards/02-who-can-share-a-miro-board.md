By default, when you invite **members of your team** to **edit** your boards, they can also [change how the board is shared](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md), invite new members and change access levels (view, comment, edit) of existing collaborators. On paid and Education plans, you can collaborate with additional confidence on Miro boards with advanced board sharing permissions.

### Advanced board sharing permissions

> **Available for:**Starter, Business, Education, and Enterprise plans
> **Set up by:** board owners, [co-owners](06-co-owners-of-boards-and-spaces.md)

Advanced board sharing permission settings allow board owners & co-owners to withdraw editors’ permissions:

- to change board sharing settings
- invite other users or change access of other users on the board

This way board owners and co-owners can proactively manage (and limit, if needed) who the board is shared with.

Open the board **Share** dialog, click on the **Permissions** tab in the **Sharing settings** window, and choose the right option:

- **Board owner and co-owners** if you want to reserve the permissions to share the board & invite collaborators to board owner and co-owners
- **Team members with editing rights** if you want to enable all board editors to change board sharing settings and invite new collaborators
