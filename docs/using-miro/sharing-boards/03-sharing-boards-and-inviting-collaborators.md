In Miro, you can share your boards with collaborators anywhere. There are several ways to invite users to your boards. Let's take a closer look at each of them.

## How to invite to a board

> **Who can do it: team members with editing and invite rights in [board sharing permissions](02-who-can-share-a-miro-board.md)**

By default, board editors who are members of the team can share the board with other users. Board owners and [co-owners](06-co-owners-of-boards-and-spaces.md) on paid plans can [restrict this option for board editors](02-who-can-share-a-miro-board.md).

There are two ways to share a board:

From within the board From the dashboard

Click **Share** in the top-right corner.

![share menu.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/16736550349842)
*Share option in the top right corner of the board*

From the **Home** dashboard, select the three dots (**...**) for the board you want to share. Then select **Share.**
![share from the dashboard.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/16736555729298)
*Share your board right from the dashboard*

## Ways to share Miro boards

Share Miro boards via Slack, email, private link, publicly, with the whole team, company, and via a project.

### Inviting from Slack

How to invite from Slack

1. Click the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the board.
2. Under the **Invite** tab, click **Slack**.
3. You will be redirected to the authorization page.

![2-1.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/20234332440082)
*Sharing a Miro board via Slack*
After successful authorization, you will see a list of your Slack contacts. Select a person (or several to invite in bulk) you'd like to share a board with, specify the [access rights](01-board-access-rights.md) level and click **Send invitations**.

![invite from Slack modal.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/16736556911506)
*Inviting a Slack contact to edit the board*

### Inviting via email

How to invite via email

To invite someone personally by email:

1. Click the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the board.
2. Under the **Invite** tab, enter the email addresses of your invitees, and set their [access rights](01-board-access-rights.md). Add spaces to separate emails.
3. Click **Send invitations.**

![2-2.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/20234291831058)
*Inviting collaborators from the board share dialog*

Your invitees will get a notification about the invite (registered users will get the invite if this is allowed in their [notification settings](../managing-your-profile/02-miro-notifications.md)). If an invitee is not yet registered they can follow the instructions in the email that will guide them through [the registration procedure](../../getting-started/start-here/02-how-to-register-with-miro.md) and then go to the board.

:::tip
To make the email invitation process easier, link your Gmail account to enable the auto-complete function. [Learn more about connecting Gmail to Miro](../../integrations-apps/google/04-google-contacts.md).
:::

**Inviting as a guest or as a member**

When you share a board over email or Slack with a user who is not a member of your team, you can either invite them to the team or make them [a board guest](07-collaboration-with-guests.md) depending on your plan and invitation settings. [Learn whether the option to invite guests is available for you](07-collaboration-with-guests.md).

### Sharing boards via a public link

How to share via a public link

You can share a board with anybody, including people who are not registered with Miro, by making the board accessible via a public link for [visitors](08-collaboration-with-visitors.md).

1. Click the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the board.
2. Under the **Invite** tab, go to **Anyone with the** **link**, and choose between view, comment, and edit access for your board visitors.
3. Click **Copy board link** and send it to your future collaborators.

:::note
Sharing boards with Visitors may not be possible if your Enterprise Admin has [restricted public sharing](../../enterprise-administration/canvas-25-admin-features/data-security/07-sharing-policy-on-enterprise-plan.md#restrict-sharing-via-public-link). Please contact your Admin if this sharing option is unavailable to you.
:::

:::note
Boards shared via a public link with the setting **all users can edit** are editable by users with a [Free Restricted](../../enterprise-administration/enterprise-subscription-management/enterprise-licensing/04-free-restricted-license.md) license.
:::

:::note
The option to share with commenter/editor rights via a public link is not available on the [Free plan](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md).
:::

![2-3.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/20234332458130)
*Enabling public access to the board*

People who follow your link won’t need to register with Miro to access the board. Learn more about [sharing Miro boards with visitors](08-collaboration-with-visitors.md).

:::warning
Miro strives to keep public boards from being indexed by search engines like Google or Bing. However, anyone with the link can access these boards, and the link might be shared beyond your intended audience. Depending on your Miro plan, you can enhance security by setting a [password for your public board](13-password-protection-for-public-boards.md).
:::

### Sharing boards and inviting to the team via a link

How to share and invite to the team via a link

> **Relevant for:** Free, Starter, and Education plans

To enable sharing via a link, the Admin needs to open **Team Settings > Permissions >** **Invitation settings** and toggle on **Enable team invite link**.

![team invite link.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/16753852607506)
*Admins can enable team invite link*

1. Click the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the board.
2. Under the **Invite** tab, go to **Invite to team and board**.
3. Set the access level.
4. Click **Copy team invite link** and send it to your team members. Once an invitee clicks the link, they will be asked to sign in to Miro. The user will become a member of your team and get instant access to the board.

![2-4.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/20234291847954)
*Sharing board and inviting to the team via a link*

As long as the team invite link is activated in the settings, users can join boards and the team via the link. Once this option is deactivated, all previously sent invite links will expire and new users won’t be able to join boards and the team with this link anymore.

If a Team Admin restricts non-admin team members from inviting new users but still allows the team invite link in [invitation settings](../../administration/user-management/02-invitation-settings.md), the option to copy the link will disappear from the Share dialog for  non-admin team members. However, users will still be able to join the team via the link if it had been shared with them previously (unless the Admin deactivates the link in Team settings).

### Sharing boards and inviting to the team via a Guest link

**How to share and invite to the team via a Guest link**

> **Available for:** Business Plan

Invite Guests to a board or to your team using a private link. This option only appears in your Share dialog if Guests are allowed in your [team invitation settings](../../administration/user-management/02-invitation-settings.md).

1. Click the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the board.
2. Under the **Invite** tab, next to **Invite guests** click the dropdown to grant **View**, **Comment**, **Edit** or **No access**
3. Click **Copy guest link**
4. Share the link directly with the user you wish to invite as a Guest

![2-5.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/20234291856914)
*Creating a Guest link*

:::note
For security purposes, guest links expire after 30 days. To ensure access after 30 days, you must generate a new link and re-send to guests.
:::

Sharing a board with your entire team

**How to share a board at the team level**

You can easily make any board available to the whole team.

1. Click the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the board.
2. Under the **Invite** tab, go to **Anyone at [team name]**, and choose **Can view/comment/edit** from the dropdown.
3. Your team members will immediately see the board on their dashboard.

The option to notify all team members is displayed for small teams with up to 31 members on Starter, Business, and Education plans. If you click **Notify members**, all team members will receive a notification to their email.

![2-6.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/20234291861906)
*Sharing a board with a team*

Boards on the [Free plan](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md) are available to all team members by default. On other plans, Team Admins can configure [default sharing settings](11-default-sharing-settings.md) for newly created boards and make all new boards visible for the whole team.

:::warning
The option is not available for users on Enterprise plan if the Admin [restricted it in settings](../../enterprise-administration/canvas-25-admin-features/data-security/07-sharing-policy-on-enterprise-plan.md).
:::

### Sharing a board with the entire company

**How to share a board at the company level**

> **Available for:** Enterprise Plan

You can make any board available to all users in an Enterprise organization.

1. Click the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the board.
2. Find **Anyone with the link at company** in the board Share dialog and select **Can view/comment/edit**.

Every member of your Enterprise plan will be able to find the board in the [search results](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/03-how-to-search-in-miro.md) and access it by a link (sign-in required). Members of the team where the board is stored will also see it on the team’s dashboard.

![2-7.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/20234291863442)
*Sharing a board with a company*

:::warning
The option to share a board with the entire company is not visible for users if the Admin has [restricted it in setting](../../enterprise-administration/canvas-25-admin-features/data-security/07-sharing-policy-on-enterprise-plan.md)s or enabled [Team privacy](../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/11-manage-team-privacy-and-discovery-on-enterprise-plan.md)*.*
:::

:::note
[Guests](07-collaboration-with-guests.md) cannot access company shared boards (unless the boards are shared with them via other means).
:::

### Inviting users to a board from your Microsoft directory

**How to invite from your Microsoft directory**

> **Available for:** Free, Starter, Education, and Business plans

Open the **Share** dialog in the top-right corner of your board. In the email invite line of the share dialog, click **invite from Microsoft****.** You'll be redirected to an authorization window.

Once authorized, you'll see a list of your Microsoft contacts. Select a person (or multiple people to invite in bulk), specify their access rights (can view, can comment, or can edit), then click **Send Invitation**.

## Start view

> **Who can do it:** board editors who [can share the board](02-who-can-share-a-miro-board.md)

Setting a start view is a great way to focus a collaborator's attention on a specific part of your board. When you share a board for the first time, you can set the board's **start view.** This is the place on the board that newly invited users are taken to when they access the board for the first time. If you don't set a start view, the entire board is shown.

![setting start view.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/16753946968338)
*Setting the start view*

### How to reset the start view

To reset the start view, in the Board bar select the vertical three dots. The **Main menu** opens. Select **Board** > **Start view**. You can now reset the start view.

## Stop sharing a board

You can stop sharing a board with your collaborators at any time. To change an individual user’s access:

1. Click the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the board (or use the three dots (**...**) icon from the dashboard and then click **Share**).
2. Click **Manage access** in the share dialog.
3. Choose the person whose rights you wish to modify, and set their new access level (edit, comment, view) or click **Remove**.

If the board is shared via other means (with the whole team, a project, company, or publicly), the user whose rights have been degraded may **retain the previous level of access** via team/project/company/public sharing. Learn how to see [who has access to your board](05-who-has-access-to-my-board.md).

![2 Remove contributor v2.gif](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/20257124251922)
*Removing a user's access from a board*

:::tip
If you are the board owner, you can also [reassign ownership](../managing-boards/05-how-to-transfer-board-ownership.md) to another user.
:::

## Publish your board

You can also share a board by saving it as a template for your team to use, or publish it to [Miroverse](../miroverse/01-what-is-miroverse.md).

1. Click the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the board.
2. Under the **Publish** tab, choose either **Save as template** or **Publish template**.

:::note
The option to **Save as template** is not available on Free Plan.
:::

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I share boards with people outside my team without adding them to the team?**

You can invite users as guests or as visitors by sharing the board publicly. Learn more in the article [Sharing with users outside your team](04-sharing-with-users-outside-your-team.md).

**How many collaborators can work simultaneously on the same board?**

The common scenario that we see almost every week is around 200 users (editors/commenters) working simultaneously on the same board. Learn more in the article [How many users can collaborate on a board](../troubleshooting-technical-questions/technical-guidelines/05-how-many-users-can-collaborate-on-a-board-simultaneously.md).

**What is the difference between Can view/comment/edit?**

Editors can change board content, commenters can leave comments, while viewers can only view Miro boards. Learn more about [board access rights](01-board-access-rights.md).

**I have shared a board with the team, but my teammates don't see it on their dashboards. Why?**

Your team members may see another team's dashboard. Please ask the users to [switch to the correct team](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/04-profile-and-teams.md) on the left sidebar of their dashboards.

**Can I share a board so that invitees can duplicate it and use it as a template?**

Yes, registered users can [duplicate a shared board](../managing-boards/03-how-to-duplicate-a-board.md) if this is allowed by the board owner in [board content settings](14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md). If you're the board owner, open the board **Share** dialog > **Sharing settings** > switch to **Permissions**. Choose who can copy your board content. Please note that **Anyone with the board access** option may be restricted by Team Admins.

**What is the best way to share a board with workshop participants?**

If you share your board for [public editing](08-collaboration-with-visitors.md), users will be able to immediately access it as visitors and start collaboration. Find more tips and tricks for [organizing a workshop with Miro](../../getting-started/feature-intros/03-miro-for-workshops-meetings.md).

**How can I share a board in the Mobile app?**

Open the board and tap the three dots in the upper-right corner of the screen to see board sharing options.

**Many sharing options are missing in my board Share dialog. How do I get them back?**

The issue may be caused by ad blocking extensions enabled in your browser. Try to disable them on Miro pages and see if the full Share dialog appears.
