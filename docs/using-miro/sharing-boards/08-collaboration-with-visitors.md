Invite Visitors to your boards for easy and on-the-spot collaboration. You can invite Visitors for free, and they don’t need to register with Miro.

## Collaborating with Visitors

To collaborate with Visitors you need to make your board public. Visitors will be able to view, comment and edit depending on the access level you set and your plan type.

:::warning
Miro strives to keep public boards from being indexed by search engines like Google or Bing. However, anyone with the link can access these boards, and the link might be shared beyond your intended audience. Depending on your Miro plan, you can enhance security by setting a [password for your public board](13-password-protection-for-public-boards.md).
:::

## How to enable access for Visitors

Visitors will have access to the board as long as it's public.

:::tip
To create the best experience for your Visitors, set a [start view](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#start-view) for your board. You can also [hide particular frames](../essential-tools/07-frames.md#hiding-and-revealing-frames) if there’s content on the board that’s not relevant for them.
:::

Starter, Business, Enterprise, and Education plans Free Plan

1. Go to your dashboard and hover over the board card
2. Click the three dots in the top right corner
3. Click **Share**
4. Next to **Anyone with the link**, select **Can view, Can comment** or **Can edit**. You can also [protect your board with a password](13-password-protection-for-public-boards.md)
5. Click **Copy board link** and share it directly with the user
   > **✏️** Sharing boards with Visitors may not be possible if your Enterprise Admin has [restricted public sharing](../../enterprise-administration/canvas-25-admin-features/data-security/07-sharing-policy-on-enterprise-plan.md#restrict-sharing-via-public-link). Please contact your Admin if this sharing option is unavailable to you.

### How public boards work for Visitors

#### What tools can Visitors use?

Depending on their level of board access ([view, comment](01-board-access-rights.md#board-access-rights), or [edit](#visitors-with-edit-access)), Visitors will see [shapes](../essential-tools/11-shapes.md), [lines](../essential-tools/05-connection-lines.md), [text](../essential-tools/16-text.md), [sticky notes](../essential-tools/14-sticky-notes.md), and the [pen tool](../essential-tools/10-pen.md) in their [creation toolbar](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/05-toolbars.md#creation-toolbar).

If a Visitor creates a Miro account, they'll get full access to the creation toolbar.

#### Password protected boards

Visitors may need to enter a password before opening the board if the board owner or co-owner has added [password protection](13-password-protection-for-public-boards.md).

**Public link expiration**
If you see a clock icon in the share window, [public link expiration](../../enterprise-administration/canvas-25-admin-features/data-security/07-sharing-policy-on-enterprise-plan.md#restrict-sharing-via-public-link) has been enabled by your Company Admin. Any public links shared with Visitors will automatically expire when a board hasn’t been opened for a set time. If you're no longer able to access a board, it's possible your link has expired. Try reaching out to the board owner and ask them to share the link again.

### Inviting Visitors to add their names

Registered users who sign in appear on the board with their real names while Visitors with edit access are automatically assigned a name by us (e.g. *Visiting Inventor*).

For a more collaborative way to engage with Visitors, some board owners and co-owners can [allow non-registered users to add their names](09-visitor-names-beta.md) when they join the board.

## Visitors with edit access

> **Available for:** Paid and Education plans

Invite Visitors with [edit access](01-board-access-rights.md#board-access-rights) when you need to work with partners or colleagues who either don't have a Miro profile or don’t have access to your Miro team.

### How public boards work for Visitors with edit access

Visitors have more limited functionality in Miro than Team Members or Guests. This is to ensure that as a board owner you maintain full control of your board.

From the [creation toolbar](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/05-toolbars.md#creation-toolbar), Visitors with edit access can add [shapes](../essential-tools/11-shapes.md), [lines](../essential-tools/05-connection-lines.md), [text](../essential-tools/16-text.md), [sticky notes](../essential-tools/14-sticky-notes.md), and use the [pen tool](../essential-tools/10-pen.md).

:::note
Non-registered visitors have more limited access to tools than registered visitors.
:::

#### Features and tools *not* available for Visitors with edit access

- Changing a board name
- Changing board sharing settings
- Access to the team dashboard
- Access to any other boards
- Mentioning others in comments
- Starting a video chat, voting session, or screen sharing
- Creating, editing, or adding Asana, Jira, Microsoft Azure, or Rally cards from the cards picker (however, they will be able to view them)
- Downloading board backups
- Locking and unlocking objects on the board, and locked objects are uneditable
- Receiving board notifications
- Accessing the Activity list and restoring board content
- Saving the board link to Google Drive
- Viewing object info
- Editing boards in a browser on mobile
- Copying board content if this is not allowed in board content settings
- Duplicating or exporting a board if they're not registered with Miro or if this is not allowed in the board content settings
- Bringing collaborators to their view
- Uploading content to a Miro board

:::note
Board owners on the Starter, Business, Enterprise, and Education plans can [configure board content settings](14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md) and define whether Visitors can [duplicate the board to their teams](../managing-boards/03-how-to-duplicate-a-board.md), download files/images/PDFs pasted on the board, copy widgets, [export the board as an image/PDF](../import-and-export/export/03-how-to-export-your-board.md).
:::

## Frequently asked questions

*Why don’t I have the 'Anyone with the link' option in my board’s Share menu?*

On [Enterprise plans](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/04-enterprise-plan.md), Company Admins can [restrict public sharing](../../enterprise-administration/canvas-25-admin-features/data-security/07-sharing-policy-on-enterprise-plan.md#restrict-sharing-via-public-link) due to security reasons.
Please note that sharing for public editing is not supported on boards stored in [free teams](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md).
The issue may also be caused by adblocking extensions enabled in your browser. Try to disable them and see if all sharing options appear.

*Why can’t my Visitors access the board with the link I shared?*

Please make sure that you chose **Can edit, Can comment or Can view** next to **Anyone with the link** option and sent the board link copied from the board Share menu or from the browser address bar, it should have the following format:

```
https://miro.com/app/board/o9L_kSnC_0=/
```

If the issue persists, ask your users to try opening the board in another browser or incognito mode.

*Will my Visitors receive an email invitation?*

No, Visitors are not invited to public boards explicitly via email. You simply need to share the board publicly, copy the board link, and send it to your Visitors. Anyone with the board link will be able to edit, comment or view its content unless you stop sharing the board publicly.

*Is a new board link generated when I share the board publicly?*

No, when you make a board public the link stays the same.

*How do I stop sharing my board with Visitors?*

Open the board Share menu, and choose **No access** next to **Anyone with the link** option.

*How many Visitors can access my board at the same time?*

You can invite an unlimited number of Visitors to your board for free.

We’re continually optimizing Miro so you can effectively collaborate with more and more users at the same time. Read more about [our largest session so far](../troubleshooting-technical-questions/technical-guidelines/05-how-many-users-can-collaborate-on-a-board-simultaneously.md).

*I’ve sent the link to a user so that they could join my board as a visitor with edit access, but they had to sign up and now they're on my team members list. Why?*

You may have sent the [Board and Team invite link](03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#sharing-boards-and-inviting-to-the-team-via-a-link) instead of enabling a public link for Visitors.

Read more on how to [Remove users and accidentally added seats (licenses)](../../administration/user-management/04-manage-extra-licenses.md).

*Why has the Visitor creation toolbar changed?*

Most Visitors use only a few tools, so we decided to make things simpler for them. They can access the full [creation toolbar](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/05-toolbars.md#creation-toolbar) by signing up for a Miro account.
