Go to Miro Profile settings to manage your name and photo and set up personal preferences. Follow [the link](https://miro.com/app/settings/user-profile) or access the settings from your [dashboard](https://miro.com/app/dashboard/): click your profile icon in the top-right corner and select **Settings**.

![profile_settings__Miro.jpg](images/21017528885394_profile%20settings,%20Miro.jpg)
*Getting to Profile settings from Miro dashboard*

From the Profile settings, you can:

- change your name, company name, industry, role, and photo
- manage cookies
- [change your email address](04-how-to-change-your-email.md)
- [change your password](05-how-to-change-your-password.md)
- [set up notification preferences](02-miro-notifications.md)
- manage [Miro connections to other services](../../integrations-apps)
- [create custom apps](https://developers.miro.com/docs/getting-started)
- [delete your profile](07-how-to-delete-your-profile.md)
