With the **reactions** in the moment you can *show it*, rather *than say it*.

You can select from six emojis to express how you feel in a given moment during meetings and workshops. Participants will also have the option to raise their hands and facilitators will get notified.

### How to use reactions

> **Set up by**: board editors

You can find the reactions feature in the top-right corner of the board, to the left of your avatar, under the **Facilitation tools and Reactions** icon.

At any given moment, you can use reactions to express how you feel by following these steps:

1. Click on the **Facilitation tools and Reactions** icon on the top right corner.
2. Click on one of the six emojis that best describes how you feel in that moment.
3. The emoji will then appear on the board together with your avatar.
