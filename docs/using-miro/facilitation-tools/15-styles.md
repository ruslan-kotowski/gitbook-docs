With styles, anyone can create visually engaging presentations and boards with a professional look and feel. Choose from 14 color palettes with multiple variations.

- Effortlessly style entire boards
- Create polished work for your audiences
- Reduce time spent on formatting

## Using styles

### How to apply styles to frames

1. Select a frame or multiple frames on the board.
2. The context menu will open.
3. Click the **styles** icon.
4. The styles panel will open.
5. Select one of the default styles, or a custom [brand style](../../administration/get-started-as-a-miro-admin/04-brand-center.md#how-to-upload-your-brand-colors)**.**
6. The style will be applied to the frame and any objects within the frame.

![Applying-styles-to-frames.gif](images/21016020643090_Applying-styles-to-frames.gif)
*Applying a style to selected frames*

### Apply different style variations

Explore multiple variations of a style. Open the **Styles** panel again, and next to **Recent**, hover over the style, then click to **Shuffle.** The style colors will appear in a new order.

![Trying-different-style-options-with-shuffle.gif](images/21016020640914_Trying-different-style-options-with-shuffle.gif)
*Applying different style variations*

:::note
To ensure our content is accessible and inclusive for everyone, some brand colors may be adjusted. These changes align with accessibility standards, as we prioritize usability to deliver an optimal experience for all users.
:::

## Resetting a style

Easily undo any unwanted changes and restore the original style of your board. Simply open the **Styles** panel, and next to **Applied style**, click **Remove style.**
