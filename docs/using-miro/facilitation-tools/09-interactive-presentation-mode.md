Interactive Presentation Mode allows collaborative teams to present their Miro boards with the highest level of interactivity and flexibility.

## How to present

Click on the **Present** button in the top-right corner of your board.

When you click **Present**, full-screen presentation mode will begin.

![toolbars-people.png](images/21999155124498_toolbars-people.png)

*The Present button is found in the People toolbar*

You can exit full-screen mode by pressing **Escape** on your keyboard. Clicking **Stop** in the toolbar at the bottom of your screen will exit presentation mode.

### Managing presentation frames

Miro [frames](../essential-tools/07-frames.md) are the slides of your presentation. You can manage your frames by clicking on **Frames and Layers** icon in the bottom right-hand corner of your Miro board and then clicking on **Frames**.

![frames-panel-entry-point.png](images/21537438755602_frames-panel-entry-point.png)

*The Frames and Layers icon opens a submenu*

This will open up the Frames panel. From there you can set the order of frames by simply clicking and dragging the frames on the sidebar.

You can rename a frame by clicking on the field below the frame. If you click on the three dots next to a frame's name, you’ll also have more options to hide the frame, export the frame, add another frame, copy the frame link, and delete the frame.

> **💡** Use [Magic organize](../essential-tools/07-frames.md#organize-frames) to rearrange frames automatically.

### Presenter notes

Add presenter notes to your frames for a more organized presentation. Presenter notes will appear in a separate window that's only visible to you. Utilize these private cues and reminders to deliver a smooth and confident presentation as you navigate through your slides.

There are two ways to access your presenter notes:

1. Access presenter notes from the board.

In the bottom right-hand corner of the board, click the **Frames and Layers** icon, then **Frames** to open the frames panel. Next to the **Present** button, click the three dots (**...**) and select **Presenter notes**.

2. Access presenter notes while in presentation mode.

In the presentation toolbar on the bottom of your screen, click the **Preferences** icon, and select **Presenter notes**.

![Open-presenter-notes-in-presentation-mode.png](images/21019604044690_Open-presenter-notes-in-presentation-mode.png)
*Opening presenter notes while in presentation mode*

### Activity apps

> **Relevant for:** Starter, Business, Enterprise, and Education plans

When you launch your interactive presentation, you can collaborate with your audience by starting a collaboration app. Simply click the **Activity apps** icon at the bottom of the screen and select [Breakout frames](05-breakout-frames-beta.md), [Timer](16-timer.md), or [Voting.](19-voting.md)

![Activity_apps.png](images/21019592999186_Activity%20apps.png)
*Launching a collaboration app*

From there, you can also manage participant tools during the presentation. Toggle on **Show creation tools** and select the tools your participants can use during the session.

:::note
The ability to restrict participant tools is not available on Starter Plan.
:::

## Presentation mode

As soon as you click **Present** either in the collaboration toolbar or in the frames panel, you'll begin presentation mode.

### Audience view

As soon as the presenter launches the presentation, the other participants on the board will see a modal with the option to join.

![Invitation_to_join_a_session.jpg](images/21019593005458_Invitation%20to%20join%20a%20session.jpg)
*The option to join a presentation*

When participants join, they’ll be guided by the presenter's view. They can move around the board and switch back to the presenter’s view by clicking **Return to presenter**.

The presentation experience remains interactive: participants can use reactions or raise their hand by selecting the corresponding options on the bottom toolbar.

On the left-hand creation toolbar they can use tools allowed by the presenter.

![User_interacting_with_the_board_during_a_presentation.gif](images/21019593008018_User%20interacting%20with%20the%20board%20during%20a%20presentation.gif)
*Users interacting with the board*

### Attention management

Keep your audience engaged and focused. You can seamlessly transition between a *slide-like* and *canvas-like* presentation of content in Miro.

**Lightbox effect:** Create a black border around frames so users can see exactly which content you're currently focused on. If you pan away or zoom out, the lightbox effect fades away.

Go to the presenter toolbar at the bottom of the screen, click **Preferences** > toggle on **Dark background**.

![Lightbox_effect.png](images/21019593019282_Lightbox%20effect.png)
*Toggling on Dark background for a lightbox effect*

**Click to zoom:** Spotlight content during your presentation. Simply click to zoom in on any frame.

![Lightbox_and_click_to_zoom_effect.gif](images/21019593022866_Lightbox%20and%20click%20to%20zoom%20effect.gif)*Click to zoom with lightbox effect enabled*

### Leaving and rejoining a presentation

To leave the presentation mode and return to the standard board view, click **Leave** on the bottom toolbar.

![Leaving_a_presentation.jpg](images/21019604029842_Leaving%20a%20presentation.jpg)*Leaving a meeting*

If you leave a presentation, you can rejoin it again by clicking **Join** in the top-right corner of your board.

![The_option_to_rejoin_a_presentation.jpg](images/21019604034834_The%20option%20to%20rejoin%20a%20presentation.jpg)*The option to rejoin a meeting*

### Hand off to a presenter

> **Relevant for**: Starter, Business, Enterprise, Education plans
> **Who can do it:** Board owners, co-owners, and editors

If it's a big meeting with multiple speakers, you can hand off the presentation to another speaker.

1. In the presenter toolbar at the bottom of your screen, open the list of people in the presentation.
2. Click **Handoff** and select a user to hand off to.

The original presenter will transition to the audience view and the new presenter will be able to control the presentation. The audience will be notified that there is a new presenter.

![Handing_off_to_another_presenter.png](images/21019593017490_Handing%20off%20to%20another%20presenter.png)*Handing off to another presenter*

### Take over from a presenter

> **Relevant for**: Starter, Business, Enterprise, Education plans
> **Who can do it:** Board owners, co-owners, and editors

Instead of waiting for a hand-off, you can take over a presentation at any time.

1. In the toolbar at the bottom of your screen, open the list of people in the presentation.
2. Click **Take over**and select a user to take over from.

The original presenter will transition to the audience view and the new presenter will be able to control the presentation. The audience will be notified that there is a new presenter.

![Take-over-from-presenter.png](images/21019604042386_Take-over-from-presenter.png)*Taking over from a presenter*

### Finishing the presentation

When the presenter clicks **Stop** on the bottom toolbar, all board collaborators are switched to the standard board view.

![Stopping_a_presentation.jpg](images/21019593013010_Stopping%20a%20presentation.jpg)
*Stopping the presentation*

## Frequently asked questions

**How is Interactive Presentation Mode different from other presentation tools?**

Interactive Presentation Mode supports a two-way interactive presentation experience. Not only can participants use reactions, post stickers or raise hand directly within the mode, they can also participate in activity apps like timer, voting and breakout frames. Another big highlight is that Interactive Presentation Mode allows you to easily switch from presenting in slides to showing people around the board in full-screen and back again. This means that you can present anything, with or without slides on Miro.

**How do I exclude a slide from my presentation in frames?**

To exclude a slide from the presentation, you can use the **Hide frame** icon in the context menu for the slide(s) you want to exclude. Alternatively, you can to delete the corresponding frame (you can do that without deleting the content inside it) or you can move the frame to the bottom of the Frames panel.

**Can I export my board as a PowerPoint presentation?**

No, but you can export each frame as an image and add it to your presentation. Learn [how to export your board](../import-and-export/export/03-how-to-export-your-board.md).

**Can I import a PowerPoint presentation to Miro?**

Yes, read how you can [add files to your boards](../working-on-the-board/07-ways-to-add-content.md).

**Can I create presentation notes hidden from other board participants?**

This is not currently possible but it is something that we hope to introduce in the near future.

**Who can start or join a presentation on the board?**

Editors who are team members or guests will have the Present button. Visitors will not. Anyone currently on the board when a Presentation is started can join.
