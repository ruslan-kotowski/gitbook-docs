Collaborate asynchronously in a more inclusive and efficient way than ever before with [Miro Talktrack](https://miro.com/talktrack/). Record an immersive audio or video walkthrough of a Miro board and leave it for other teammates to review.

Your collaborators will be able to interact with Talktrack board recordings by navigating through the board.

Whether or not you have a Miro plan and regardless of your plan type, you can watch any Talktracks shared with you.

## Best practices

Consider these tips and tricks before creating your Talktrack:

- **Refrain from clicking or interacting with board items while recording** (e.g. click to play video, click to open document)
  This isn't a screen recording but rather the recording of your movements across the board. Your interactions with the board will not be captured.
- **Be careful with significant board edits after recording**
  When the viewer plays the Talktrack, they will see the updated content on the board. However they will still be able to access the [original version of the recording](#playing-the-original-version-of-a-talktrack).
- **Consider locking your frames**
  Especially the ones you don't want to be altered. Or at least remind your viewers not to move things that shouldn't be moved.
- **Pro-tip: Reserve frames for input**
  Rather than asking viewers to add stuff on top of recorded screens, [make a frame](../../essential-tools/07-frames.md) reserved for their input. It will reduce confusion for other viewers
- **Be mindful of recording duration**
  Try to keep board recordings at 45 minutes or less for optimal viewing/ board loading performance.

:::note
When users [duplicate a board](../../managing-boards/03-how-to-duplicate-a-board.md), any Talktracks will also be duplicated to the new board.
:::

## Where can I use Talktrack?

Here are some common use cases where Talktrack is helpful:

- Meeting pre-reads and recaps
- Weekly team updates
- Stand-in recording for meetings you cannot attend
- Onboarding people to new processes or information
- Pre-workshop/class instructions
- Gathering feedback on your board content
- Sharing a project plan or roadmap

:::tip
Learn [how the Miro team use Talktrack board recordings](https://miro.com/blog/improve-collaboration-with-talktrack-videos/) to improve workflows.
:::

## How to create a Talktrack

> **Who can do it**: board owners, co-owners, editors and commenters

:::note
On Free Plans, users can each record up to 5 Talktracks.
:::

You can create a Talktrack from the collaboration toolbar in the top-right corner of the board.

1. Click **Video** icon and then **Record Talktrack** button.
   ![talktrack-entry-point.png](images/21999225231250_talktrack-entry-point.png)
   *The option to record a Talktrack is found in the collaboration toolbar*
2. A side panel with recording options will appear.
3. Your camera and microphone will be enabled by default. You can disable the camera or microphone or select another camera and microphone.
4. Click **Start recording**.

   ![talktrack-recording.gif](images/21999225233938_talktrack-recording.gif)
*Creating a Talktrack in Miro*
5. While recording, you'll see a toolbar in the top-right corner. You can delete, restart, or pause the Talktrack, or click **Stop** to finish recording.

   ![Talktrack-recording-toolbar.png](images/14509090106770_Talktrack-recording-toolbar.png)
*Talktrack recording toolbar*
6. After you finish recording, the Talktrack will appear in the panel on the right, and a shortcut will be automatically added to the board. Users can click the **Play** button on a Talktrack shortcut to start watching immediately. Reposition Talktrack shortcuts by clicking and dragging them.

   You can also manually add a Talktrack shortcut to the board later. Simply open the Talktrack panel, click the **three dot** (**...**) menu next to the Talktrack, and select **Add to board**.

   ![talktrack-add-to-board.png](images/21537438359570_talktrack-add-to-board.png)
*A recorded Talktrack in the side panel and its shortcut on the board*
7. You can rename the Talktrack by clicking the **three dots** (**...**) and selecting **Rename**.
8. **Copy the link** to the Talktrack and send it to your collaborators. As soon as they follow the link, the board will open, and the Talktrack will launch for them automatically.
9. Board owners can also drag and drop Talktracks to rearrange the order of the recordings in the panel.

   ![talktrack-copy-link.png](images/21999225235090_talktrack-copy-link.png)
*The options to copy the link, rename, and delete the Talktrack*

## Playing the Talktrack

> **Available on:** desktop browser, desktop app, mobile app

Anyone (including unregistered users) with access to the board can watch the board Talktracks.

There are two ways to play a Talktrack:

1. By following the direct link to a Talktrack.
2. By clicking the **Video** icon on the collaboration toolbar. Then click on the **Watch Recordings** button. If there are new recordings since you last visited the board, you will see a blue dot above the icon. Any board collaborator can copy links to Talktracks.

:::tip
You can watch Talktracks from your favorite creators in[Miroverse](../../miroverse/01-what-is-miroverse.md).
:::

### Playback controls

The playback experience is interactive: at playback you’re following the recorder's view, but you can also pan around the board and make edits.

To return to the recorder’s view, click **Return to [user’s first name]** at the bottom of the screen.

![Return-to-recorder-view.png](images/14509357917074_Return-to-recorder-view.png)

Viewers can skip forward and rewind 10 seconds when watching a Talktrack. And when multiple Talktracks are available, you can simply click **Next** from the player menu to watch the next Talktrack. To watch the Talktrack faster or slower, select different playback speeds in playback mode on the toolbar. Viewers can also turn on **Closed Captioning** from the playback settings.

![Talktrack_playback_controls.png](images/14509421558418_Talktrack_playback_controls.png)

#### Talktrack reactions

Viewers can instantly provide feedback on specific moments of a Talktrack by using emojis. In the playback toolbar, simply click on an emoji to react directly on the recording timeline. Talktrack reactions visually highlight important or notable moments within the Talktrack, for a collaborative viewing experience.

![Talktrak-reactions.png](images/17054285579026_Talktrak-reactions.png)

## Playing the original version of a Talktrack

:::warning
Any content that was on the board at the time of the TalkTrack recording will still be visible to viewers through this feature, even if you have since deleted it.
:::

Sometimes board content is moved, changed or deleted after a Talktrack recording is made.

To view a Talktrack recording with the original board content:

1. Start playing a Talktrack
2. In the playback controls panel, click the **Playback settings** icon
3. Click **Watch on original board**
4. A pop-up window will appear with the original version of the recording. Within the pop-up window, you can play the recording and move around the board, however you will not be able to edit the board content.

![Watch-original-talktrack.gif](images/13646189375890_Watch-original-talktrack.gif)
*Playing the original version of a Talktrack*

## Playing a Talktrack during a presentation

Play a previously recorded Talktrack in [Interactive Presentation Mode](../09-interactive-presentation-mode.md). Even when a presenter can't make it to the meeting, they can still present ideas async to the team.

1. While in presentation mode, go to the presenter toolbar at the bottom of your screen
2. Click **Timer, voting and tools**
3. Select **Talktrack**
4. The Talktrack panel will open

## Adding a Talktrack in Coda, Notion, and Productboard

Embed your Talktrack presentations directly within Coda, Notion, and Productboard for enhanced collaborative work and presentation flow. Viewers can directly watch and interact with the Talktrack without leaving the platform, streamlining the sharing and viewing experience.

**Copy the Talktrack link in Miro:**

1. Open the board where your Talktrack is located.
2. Click on the **Talktrack** button under **Present** in the [collaboration toolbar](../../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/05-toolbars.md#collaboration-tools).
3. The Talktrack panel will open on the right side of your board.
4. Within the panel, locate the Talktrack you wish to share.
5. Click on the **Share** option next to the Talktrack.
6. The Talktrack link will be copied to your clipboard.

**Paste the link into your desired platform:**

1. Open the Coda, Notion, or Productboard page where you want to add the Talktrack.
2. Find a text field where you wish to insert the Talktrack.
3. Paste the copied Talktrack link directly into the text field.
4. Depending on the platform (Coda, Notion, or Productboard), several options may appear after pasting the link. Select the option to **embed** the content.
5. Once embedded, the Talktrack will appear in playback view, allowing viewers to play it directly from the embedded Miro board on the page.

![Pasting-Talktrack-into-Coda.png](images/17549923667858_Pasting-Talktrack-into-Coda.png)*Talktrack embedded in Coda*

## Frequently asked questions

**Do Talktrack recordings expire?**

No, Talktrack recordings are available indefinitely unless they are deleted. Only Board Owners or the person who made the recording can delete them.

**Can you have more than one user's Talktrack on the same board?**

Yes, you can have multiple board editors add Talktracks to a Miro board.

**Who can create and/or view a Talktrack?**

Editors and commenters of a board can create a Talktrack and delete/rename their own Talktracks. Board owners and co-owners can not only create a Talktrack, they can also delete/rename Talktracks created by anyone on the board. Anyone (including unregistered users) with access to the board can watch the board Talktracks.

**Does Talktrack work with presentation mode?**

Yes it does. Once you start the recording, a presentation mode button will appear at the top so you can record yourself walking through your slides. Learn more about [Interactive Presentation Mode](../09-interactive-presentation-mode.md).

**Is Talktrack available on mobile devices?**

Mobile devices are currently not supported, but it could be prioritized if we see enough interest from our users.

**Is my entire screen being recorded?**

Only the viewport of the Miro board (canvas) is recorded, this does not include the side panels and tools. Talktrack recordings only capture the video and the movements of the recorder on the Miro canvas. It does not capture the recorder's interactions with the board (e.g. opening a document or adding a sticky note).
