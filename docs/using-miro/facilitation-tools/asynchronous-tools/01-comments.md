Boost team collaboration with comments. Share thoughts and ideas directly on your boards, engage in meaningful discussions, and make every project more interactive and productive.

- **Start conversations** right on the board with comments.
- **Jump into thread discussions** and improve teamwork with ease.
- **Use @mentions** to notify teammates.
  - If you're mentioned in a comment, it will feature an @ symbol.
  - New mentions are **blue**.
  - Read mentions are **white**.
- **Catch up quickly**:
  - New comments are **blue**.
  - Read comments are **white**.
  - Resolved comments have a **check**.

Quickly glimpse ongoing conversations or feedback directly on the board. Simply hover over any comment to see a preview.

## How to add comments

1. Click the **Comment** icon in the [Creation toolbar](../../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/05-toolbars.md#creation-toolbar) on the left, or press the **C** [hotkey](../../working-on-the-board/06-shortcuts-and-hotkeys.md).
2. To add a comment, click anywhere on the board or on an object. After clicking, start typing to [draft](#draft-a-comment) your comment.
3. Use [@mentions](../01-@mentioning-people-on-the-board.md) to tag someone. For [suggested mentions](../01-@mentioning-people-on-the-board.md#suggested-mentions-feature), simply start typing @. You can also tag yourself to assign tasks and set reminders in your [feed](../../managing-boards/10-feed.md) notifications.
4. To publish your comment, click the blue **Publish** button or press **Enter**.

> **✏️** Working directly with objects? Click on any object on the board to open its context menu and add a comment.

> **✏️** Use **Shift + Enter** to start a new line in your comment. Text formatting is not supported in comments.

![Comment-icon.png](images/21016043969042_Comment-icon.png)*Clicking the comment icon in the Creation toolbar*

### Draft a comment

If you navigate away from the comment without clicking the **Publish** button or pressing **Enter**, your comment will be saved as a draft.

To access your draft comment, click on the board to open the comment thread. You will see the draft text. If your draft is a response to an existing comment thread, simply click on the comment to open the thread and find your draft.

![Draft-saved-notification.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21016057341330)
*Draft saved notification*

### Edit a comment

To edit a comment, click on the body of text, update as needed, and then click **Save**.

![Editing-a-comment.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21016057341842)
*Editing a comment*

### Delete a comment

To delete a comment from the board, open the comment, click the three dots (**...**), and select **Delete.**

:::warning
Deleting multiple comments or all comments at once from the board is not possible. Additionally, removing a user from a team will not automatically remove their comments from team boards.
:::

![Deleting-a-comment.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21016057343250)
*Deleting a comment*

### Choose a comment color

Customize your comments with colors to signify importance or categorize messages for your team. For example, use blue for ideas and green for discussions. To change a comment's color, open the comment and select a color from the options at the top.

![Changing-the-color-of-a-comment.png](images/21016043972754_Changing-the-color-of-a-comment.png)
*Changing the color of a comment*

## Comments panel

The Comments panel brings all your mentions and comments together. To open the Comments panel, click the **Activity** icon in the top-right Collaboration toolbar. Selecting any comment in the panel instantly takes you to its location on the board.

![comments-entry-point.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21537454052114)

## Attaching comments to objects

Attach a comment directly to any object to make sure they move together around the board. To do this, simply position the comment over the desired object. If you need to move a comment to a different object, click and drag it to the new location.

![Comments-attached-to-objects.png](images/21016043986322_Comments-attached-to-objects.png)*Comments attached to a shape and a sticky note*

:::warning
Comments attached to objects cannot be copied, cut, or pasted along with the objects. Additionally, comments cannot be exported. However, if you [duplicate a board](../../managing-boards/03-how-to-duplicate-a-board.md), any comments will be copied along with it.
:::

## Managing notifications

Never miss an update in the conversation. Follow all threads or only specific ones of interest. Once you leave a comment, reply, or are [@mentioned](../01-@mentioning-people-on-the-board.md), you will get a notification. When you wish to stop receiving updates, simply turn off the follow thread feature.

:::tip
You can adjust your [notification preferences](../../managing-your-profile/02-miro-notifications.md) in your profile settings.
:::

### Follow all comment threads

To follow all threads on a board, go to the board header in the top-left corner, click the three dots (**...**) icon**,** and under **Preferences** toggle on **Follow all threads**.

![comments-follow.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21537437998866)
*The option to follow all threads*

### Follow a specific comment thread

To follow a specific thread, open the comment and click the **Follow thread** icon in the top-right corner.

![Turning-on-follow-thread.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21016057347346)
*The option to follow a specific thread*

### Pin a thread

Keep comment threads open on the board for discussion and brainstorming. To keep a thread open on the board, open the comment, click the three dots (**...**), and select **Pin**.

By pinning several comments you can keep multiple threads open simultaneously on your board.

![Pinning-a-comment-to-keep-it-open.png](images/21016043979794_Pinning-a-comment-to-keep-it-open.png)
*Pinning a comment*

## Managing comments

Mark comments as read or unread to better focus on important messages and tasks that require your attention.

### Mark a comment as unread

To mark a comment as unread, open the comment, click the three dots (**...**), and select **Mark as unread**.

![Marking-a-comment-as-unread.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21016057349906)
*Marking a comment thread as unread*

### Mark all comments as read

To mark all comments as read, open the [Comments panel](#comments-panel), click the the **Settings** icon, and select **Mark all as read**.

![comments-mark-as-read.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21537454069394)
*Marking all comments as read*

### Resolve a comment

Focus your attention where it’s most needed. You can mark any comment thread as resolvedto hide it from the board. Open the comment, and in the top-left corner toggle on **Resolve.**

![Resolving-a-comment-thread.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21016057353490)
*Resolving a comment thread*

### Show resolved comments

To view resolved comments, open the [Comments panel](#comments-panel), click the **Show** dropdown, and toggle on **Show resolved**.

![Show-resolved-comments.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21016057357842)
*Show resolved comments*

### React to comments with emojis

Click the emoji icon next to a comment to add a reaction.

![Reacting-to-a-comment-with-emojis.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21016057354386)
*Adding reactions to comments*

### Hiding comments on the board

To hide all comments on the board, open the [Comments panel](#comments-panel), click the **Settings** icon, and toggle off **Show comments on board**.

![comments-show-comments.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21537454079506)
*Hiding comments on the board*

## Comments in the mobile app

Engage effortlessly with discussions on our mobile app, where navigating through comments and threads offers a seamless experience similar to the desktop version.

**Viewing and replying to comments:**

- **Open threads**: Tap on a comment to view the entire thread.
- **Leave a reply**: Share your thoughts and contribute to the conversation.
- **Resolve the thread**: Mark discussions as completed.
- **Manage notifications**: Start or stop following the thread by pressing the bell icon.
- **Customize visibility**: Change the color of the comment for easier reference and organization.

**Adding a new comment:**

The process to add a comment on mobile differs slightly from the desktop experience:

1. Tap the plus icon (**+**) located in the bottom-right corner of your screen.
2. Choose **Comment** to share your new thought or idea.
