**Screen sharing** gives you an opportunity to take the stage and present the board to other participants. It allows to focus [workshop](../../getting-started/feature-intros/03-miro-for-workshops-meetings.md) or meeting participants’ attention on a particular spot on the board and follow your cursor.

:::warning
Starting May 15, 2023, Screen sharing will no longer be available. We're making this change as our [Follow](04-attention-management.md#follow-a-collaborator) and [Bring to Me](04-attention-management.md#bring-a-specific-collaborator-to-your-view) features are better suited for collaborative work on the board, and simplify the user experience.
:::

### How to start screen sharing

Screen sharing can be started via Miro's native [video chat](18-video-chat.md) app.

![video_chat.jpg](images/21016020014482_video%20chat.jpg)
*Video chat*

Open the video chat, ​and you will be able to start screen sharing.

![Screenshot_background_template__7__3_.jpg](images/21016033482386_Screenshot%20background%20template%20%207%20(3).jpg)
*Screen sharing*

Once you start screen sharing, your app screen gets outlined in blue, and other participants see a pop-up window indicating that the screen sharing session is on and showing the name of the user who initiated it.

Board members can **Join**your stream and see and follow your cursor highlighted. They will **not** be able to use any tools or take any actions on the board unless they leave the screen sharing session.

To leave/end screen sharing, click the corresponding option on the toolbar.

### Frequently asked questions

1. *Will the embedded video/uploaded presentation be played for users if the facilitator pushes the play button during the screen sharing session?*
   - No, participants should press the play button on their side.
2. *Can I share my device screen outside Miro?*
   - No, you only share your Miro board.
