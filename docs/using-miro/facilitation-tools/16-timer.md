The Miro **timer** helps to manage and track your time while running a whiteboarding session in real time. No matter if you are a team member, a group leader, or just want to manage your personal projects and meet the deadline — our timer will boost your productivity by taking care of your most precious resource.

### Using timer

The timer is located on the collaboration toolbar, under the **Facilitation tools and Reactions** icon in the top right corner of the board.

![timer-entry-point.png](images/21537453089298_timer-entry-point.png)
*Timer buttom from the collaboration toolbar*

Click the timer button to open the timer, set the time period by either typing in the time or using the **+** and **-** options, and click the blue play button to start.

While the timer is counting down, you have the ability to stop, pause, and add more time to the timer. Click the **+1m** or **+5m** buttons to add time to the countdown.

To pause the timer, click the corresponding button.

To stop the timer and reset the time if needed, click the stop button.

The options are available for the user who started the timer as well as for other board editors who were explicitly invited to the board [via email](../sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email) or have access to the board because they are part of a [space](../spaces/01-spaces.md) or [team](../sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md) in Miro.

The timer starts and ends for everyone on the board. You can minimize the timer and the toolbar icon will always show how much time is left.

When the time is up all the collaborators are notified visually and with a chime.

### Built in music player: play music directly from the timer

When you are setting up the timer you have the option to select from a list of royalty-free music to play during your countdown.

## Frequently asked questions

**The timer icon is not present on the collaboration toolbar. Why?**

Please note that timer *cannot* be set up by [viewers](../sharing-boards/01-board-access-rights.md), [commenters](../sharing-boards/01-board-access-rights.md), or [visitors](../sharing-boards/08-collaboration-with-visitors.md) and it is *not* available in [free teams](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md). You may also need to [install the timer](https://miro.com/marketplace/) for the team where the board you are working on is located or ask your Team Admin to install it. Note that timer [may be restricted](../../enterprise-administration/managing-apps-on-enterprise-plan/02-app-management.md) on Enterprise plan by your Company Admins.

**Can I see the timer while in [presentation mode](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/360017731073)?**

If you enter *full* presentation mode, the timer will pop up in the top left corner of the screen.

**Can I move the timer to a different spot on the board?**

No, this is not currently possible.
