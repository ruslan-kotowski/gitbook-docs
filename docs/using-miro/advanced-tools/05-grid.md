Miro Grid allows you to simply organize your board content quickly and efficiently.

- Create visual Grids using widgets and text to organize your board content.
- Design templates for repeatable processes that are professional, and easy to edit.

## How to access Grids

1. Go to the [Creation toolbar](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/05-toolbars.md#creation-toolbar) on the left side of your board.
2. Click the **Tools, Media and Integrations** icon (![icon-zoom-in.svg](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22913188998418)) and search for **Grid**.
3. Open the **Grid app**. You can also click and drag the Grid app to your Creation toolbar for quick access.

To add a Grid to your board, click on the Grid icon in the creation toolbar, then click the desired location on the board.

You can select the size of your Grid directly from the toolbar. The maximum number of cells per Grid is 2,500.

## Convert an Excel file to a Grid

1. Open a Miro board.
2. Click the **Tools, Media and Integrations** icon (**+**) on the creation toolbar.
   The Tools, Media and Integrations panel opens.
3. Search and select **Upload**.
   The Upload panel opens.
4. Select **My device**.
5. Navigate to and select the Excel file (.xlsx) you'd like to upload.
6. Alternatively, drag and drop an Excel file onto your board to upload.
7. A modal will open with options:
   1. Convert to table.
   2. Convert to grid.
   3. Add as file.
8. Select **Convert to grid**.
9. Click **Select**.
10. A Grid will be created on the board with the data from the Excel file.

:::warning
When uploading an Excel file, there is a limit of 500 rows and 30 columns.
:::

## Interacting with Grids

- Click once to select the entire Grid.
- Click a second time to select a single cell, or click and drag to select multiple cells.
- Click a third time to edit the text within a cell, or double-click a cell to edit it.

## Moving or resizing Grids

To move the Grid, click once to select it and then drag it around the board.

To resize the Grid, click to select it and then drag a corner.

## Add content

To add content like a sticky note, shape, card, or image to a Grid, drag it over the Grid. The cells will auto-resize to fit the content.

It's not possible to add other Grids inside a Grid.

![adding_content_greybg.gif](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21016940967698)*Adding content to the Grid*

The auto-resize feature is turned on by default. To turn it off click **Cell resize** > **Manual** from the context menu or press**Ctrl/Cmd** while dragging objects onto the Grid. To turn it on again, click **Cell resize** > **Auto**.

:::warning
Content within Grids cannot be [locked](../working-on-the-board/17-locking-content-on-the-board.md) or [grouped](../working-on-the-board/08-structuring-board-content.md#grouping). The [align objects button](../working-on-the-board/08-structuring-board-content.md#aligning) and the [auto-layout option](../working-on-the-board/08-structuring-board-content.md#auto-layout) are not available within Grids.
:::

## Columns and rows

:::note
If you're unable to select a column, row, or cell, turn on [select mode](../working-on-the-board/21-work-smarter-not-harder.md#switching-between-selectpan-modes) and try zooming in, as many small cells cannot be edited at a high zoom levels.
:::

To add columns or rows, you can:

- Click the **add column** or **add row** icon on the context menu.
- Use the **plus icons** (![icon-zoom-in.svg](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22913188998418)) on the right or bottom sides of the Grid.
- Click the plus icon next to the three dots that appear next to a column or row.

Drag and drop the three dots to reposition columns and rows in your Grid.

![moving_a_row_greybg.gif](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21016928023698)*Moving a row up*

## Customizing colors and sizes

To change the color of a column, row, or cell, click the three dots adjacent to that row or column and choose the color from the dropdown menu.

You can change the color of a cell, row, column, or the whole Grid.

To adjust the width of a column or row, click and drag its border. Make sure [select mode](../working-on-the-board/05-using-miro-with-a-mouse-trackpad-or-touchscreen.md#switching-between-the-pan-and-select-tool) is turned on.

![chaning_the_row_width_greybg.gif](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21016940981394)*Adjusting the size of a row*

To resize all rows or columns together, select the entire Grid and drag the borders.

To delete a column or a row, click the three dots adjacent to it, then click the trash icon on the context menu.

## Merging cells

To merge cells, select them and choose **Merge cells** on the context menu. Cells can be unmerged at any time.

![merge_and_unmerge_cells_greybg.gif](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21016928031762)*Merging and unmerging cells in a Grid*

## Working with text

To add text, double click in a cell and start typing. To change existing text, double-click the cell. You can adjust the size, color, and alignment of the text, and create bulleted lists.

To add vertical text to Grids, choose the vertical text position on the context menu.

![vertical alignment.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/21016941002386)*Vertical text alignment*

You can change the text formatting of the whole Grid, or a full row or column.

:::note
Every cell has a limit of 6,000 symbols. The limit includes spaces and all style changes in code, so text formatting may decrease the number of symbols you can input.
:::

:::note
To insert a link into the text, select the text and press **Ctrl** + **V** (*for Windows*) or **Cmd + V**(*for Mac*).
:::

:::tip
Speed up your work by using the [shortcuts](../working-on-the-board/06-shortcuts-and-hotkeys.md):
**- Ctrl** + **B** (*for Windows*) or **Cmd + B** (*for Mac*) to make text bold
**- Ctrl + I** (*for Windows*) or **Cmd + I** (*for Mac*) to make text italic
**- Ctrl + U** (*for Windows*) or **Cmd + U** (*for Mac*) to underline text
**- Arrows** to move between cells (*for Mac*)
:::

## Importing from Microsoft Excel or Google Spreadsheets

To import content from a spreadsheet to a Miro board:

1. Copy cells from the spreadsheet.
2. Open your board and press **Ctrl + V** *(for Windows)* or **Cmd + V** *(for Mac).*
3. You can paste the content as [sticky notes](../essential-tools/14-sticky-notes.md) or as a Grid. Select **Paste as Grid**. When copying tables with more than 2,500 cells, columns will be given priority over rows.

You can also copy cells in your spreadsheet, select a cell in your existing Miro Grid, and press **Ctrl + V** *(for Windows)* or **Cmd + V** *(for Mac).* The content from the spreadsheet will be copied to the Grid.

### Exporting your Grid

Export Grids as a CSV file via the context menu. You can also export Grids as images or PDFs. Learn more about our [export options](../import-and-export/export/03-how-to-export-your-board.md).
