:::tip
We've recently introduced a new way to build timelines. Learn more about our new beta [Timeline](../formats/15-timeline.md) feature.
:::

Quickly visualize a timeline and keep your work organized with the Timeline Builder. To remove the hassle of manually creating a timeline, play with a few settings, like timeframe and interval, and let the builder do the creation work.

### Install the Timeline Builder

To install the app for your team, go to [Miro Marketplace](https://miro.com/marketplace/timelinebuilder/?backUrl=%2Fmarketplace%2F) and click **Get app**. Then select a team for which you’d like to install the builder.

Or install the builder from within a Miro board. Click the arrows icon on the toolbar, type in the app name in the search bar, and click **Add**.

:::warning
Non-admin users can't install apps if it's not allowed by Admins in the**Apps & Integrations** settings.
:::

### Add a timeline

You will find the Timeline Builder among your apps. You can drag the app on the toolbar for your convenience.

![drag_to_the_toolbar.gif](images/21016941813522_drag%20to%20the%20toolbar.gif)
*Adding the Timeline Builder to the creation toolbar*

Having selected the app, you will be able to configure the timeline settings.

**Date settings**

- Whether you’re doing a weekly team planning, quarterly roadmaps, or yearly strategic planning, choose the **interval** that makes sense for the situation: hours, days of the week, days, weeks, months, quarters, years
- Set the **timeframe** (e.g. January - May if you’ve chosen months as an interval)

**Swimlanes**

- Add **labels** to swimlanes (e.g. Marketing, Product, Support if your timeline should include different streams). To add a swimlane, click the plus button

**Style**

- Choose the color for your timeline, the **palette** has 4 options: blue, green, red, pink
- Select vertical or horizontal **orientation**
- Enable or disable **alternate column or raw shading**

Click **Insert** to create the timeline.

![timeline_settings.gif](images/21016928851858_timeline%20settings.gif)
*Configuring the Timeline Builder settings*

### Add data to your timeline

As soon as the timeline is created, you can start adding information with [sticky notes](../essential-tools/14-sticky-notes.md), [cards](../essential-tools/02-cards.md) (including Jira Cards, Asana Cards, etc.), [shapes](../essential-tools/11-shapes.md), or by simply inserting text. Drop any object, including images, on the timeline to map out the events and tasks.
