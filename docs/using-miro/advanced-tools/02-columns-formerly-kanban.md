Visualize your workflow and get more done as a team with our Columns framework. Miro Columns can be used in a variety of use cases from agile development to personal task tracking.

## Adding Columns

To add Columns to your board, choose it in **Tools, Media and Integrations** (![icon-tools.svg](images/29029892260754_icon-tools.svg)), search for **Columns**, then click the board in the needed place.

You can change the size of your Columns widget by dragging its sides.

## Columns and swimlanes

Add and arrange columns and swimlanes to your Columns widget by:

1. Click on the Columns widget to reveal the context menu.
2. Click the **Add column** or **Add swimlane** icons.
   Columns will be added to the right-hand side while swimlanes will be added at the bottom.
3. Rearrange columns or swimlanes by clicking the **three dots** (**...**) icon and selecting the needed **Move** direction.
4. Double click the heading of a column to rename it.

Under the **three dots** (**...**) menu, you can add, move, rename, and delete columns and swimlanes or change the color of columns.

## Cards

:::tip
[Learn more](../essential-tools/02-cards.md) about Miro cards.
:::

To add a [card](../essential-tools/02-cards.md), click the plus icon inside a swimlane. Alternatively, drag an existing card to your Columns widget from the board.

Drag cards to move them to another column/swimlane.

:::warning
Cards within Columns can't be [locked](../working-on-the-board/17-locking-content-on-the-board.md) and [grouped](../working-on-the-board/08-structuring-board-content.md#grouping).
:::

Speed up your work by using the [shortcuts](../working-on-the-board/06-shortcuts-and-hotkeys.md):

- **Enter/Return** to create a new card
- **Tab** to switch to the next card in a column
- **Tab** + **Shift** to switch to the previous card in a column
- **←→****↑↓** to select a card
- **Ctrl** + **C** and **Cmd** + **V** (*for Windows*), **Cmd + C** and **Cmd + V** (*for Mac*) to duplicate a card
- **Ctrl + ↑↓** (*for Windows*), **Cmd + ↑↓** (*for Mac*) to add a new card below or above
- **Escape** to exit editing mode

## FAQ

**Is there an option to add images to Columns?**

No, you can only add [cards](../essential-tools/02-cards.md).

**Can I add [Jira Cards](../../integrations-apps/atlassian/03-jira-cards.md) to my Columns?**

Yes, you can add Jira Cards as any other cards ([Asana Cards](../../integrations-apps/asana/01-asana-cards-legacy.md), [Azure Cards](../../integrations-apps/microsoft/03-azure-cards.md)). To learn how to set up Jira Cards, take a look at [this page](../../integrations-apps/atlassian/12-deprecated-–-set-up-and-disable-jira-cards-using-oauth-1.0.md).

**Are there automatic reminders based on the card due date?**

No, this is not currently supported.
