Timeline is an interactive planning tool that strategically aligns stakeholders and teams. Use Timeline to roadmap your project, add visibility, and hit milestones more efficiently. Create, manage, and share your timeline directly from a Miro board.

Timeline visualizes your project as tasks, called records, and time bars. Each record represents a task, and the time bar vividly shows the time expected to complete that task. The Timeline widget also enables multiple users to interact with the timeline simultaneously.

Create groups to order records logically, and move records between groups. For example, you can order your project by milestone.

The Timeline widget also interacts with [Miro Cards](../essential-tools/02-cards.md).

:::note
Timeline is in beta. Some functionality described or shown is subject to change, or may be unavailable until the GA release later this year. Be sure to revisit this article for updates as Timeline continues to evolve.
:::

## Key features

- **Customize an interactive timeline** anywhere on your Miro board.
- **Add and delete records** inside the Timeline widget.
- **Drag and drop Miro Cards, Jira Cards, and Azure cards** into the Timeline widget.*
- **Create groups of records** to organize information logically.
- **Resize each time bar** to change dates for individual records.
- **Scale the Timeline view and time bars** automatically when you adjust dates.
- **Include multiple Timeline widgets** with different data sources on the same Miro board.
- **Share your timeline** with team members and stakeholders.

## Add Timeline to your board

1. Open a Miro board.
2. Select **More apps** (**+**) from the [creation toolbar](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/05-toolbars.md#creation-toolbar).
3. Search and select **Timeline**.
   Your cursor now shows as the Timeline icon.
4. Select anywhere on the Miro board to place your Timeline widget.

![](images/19967187350162_674eabbc-5629-426a-aa7d-c6199cc9687a.png)

*Placing the timeline widget*

*In beta, Timeline widget interaction with Jira Cards is limited. Interaction with Azure Cards is unavailable.

## Timeline interactions

The Timeline widget provides several controls that enable you to manage your planner.

### Add a record

Select the plus sign (**+**) above the records column. A new record appears on the timeline.

To reposition a record, drag and drop the record higher or lower on the planner. You can optionally drag and drop the corresponding time bar.

### Edit record text

Select the record or timebar, and then select the text. The blinking cursor indicates that the text is ready for editing.

### Delete a record

1. Select a record or time bar for editing.
2. Delete the text.
   A dialog appears below the timeline which warns you that deletion is permanent.
3. To delete the record, select **Delete anyway**.

:::warning
In beta, undo is unavailable.
:::

### Change start and end dates

Select the calendar button inside the Timeline widget, then choose your start and end dates.

The planner scales automatically to match your selection.

To change the start and end dates for a specific record, select and drag either end of the time bar.

![](images/19967187352978_0c37764f-62f0-4a06-b031-e0b6229807be.png)

*Changing start and end dates in the Timeline widget*

### Change record color

Select the color circle in the records column, then select a color.

The record and time bar color changes to match your selection.

### Reposition records

In the records column, drag and drop a record to reposition it on the planner. You can optionally drag and drop a time bar.

### Create a records group

A group enables you to organize your records logically, for example you want to order a roadmap by milestone.

To create a group, select the three dots (**...**) above the records column, then select **Add new group**.

![](images/19967187355794_4f687249-7573-4110-9c27-bb8dd45c8454.png)

*Adding a new record in the Timeline widget*

:::tip
You can drag and drop a record to another group.
:::

### Delete a records group

Select the three dots (**...**) above the records column, then select **Delete group**.

## Add cards to your timeline

The Timeline widget visualizes data from Miro Cards, Jira Cards, and Azure Cards.

On your Miro board, simply drag and drop any card to a Timeline widget.

:::warning

:::

## Share your timeline

On your Miro board, follow these steps:

1. Select the Timeline widget that you want to share.
   The context menu appears above the widget.
2. Select the **more** (**...**) menu.
   A drop-down menu opens.
3. Select **Copy link**.
4. Share the link with anyone who has permissions to view the board.
