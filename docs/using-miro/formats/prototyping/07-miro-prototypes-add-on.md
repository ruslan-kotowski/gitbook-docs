The Miro Prototypes add-on gives teams access to AI-powered prototyping features, interactive click-throughs and focus mode, and additional components for building advanced prototypes. The Miro Prototypes add-on may include additional AI credits, depending on [your plan](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/19756209116178).

To learn how to use these features, see [**Miro Prototypes**.](https://help.miro.com/hc/en-us/articles/26654269713682)

:::note
You must have an active paid Miro subscription to be eligible for a Miro Prototypes add-on trial.
:::

### **Starter and Business**

Starter and Business admins and members can start a [**7-day free trial**](https://miro.com/app/?createAddonTrial=addon-miro-prototyping) directly in product.

- Activation is instant and applies to the entire organization.
- The trial unlocks the full add-on feature set, and Prototypes actions **do not consume AI credits** during the trial.
- After the trial ends, access stops unless a Company Admins or Billing Admin chooses to purchase the add-on. You will **not** be automatically charged.
- Company Admins and Billing Admins can choose to purchase the add-on at any time.

**More information:** See [Purchase and manage add-ons](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/28897507989138).

### **Enterprise**

Enterprise organizations can start a [**14-day free trial**](https://miro.com/app/?createAddonTrial=addon-miro-prototyping) in one of two ways:

- **In product:**
  - Company Admins may see a *Start trial* option in Prototyping entry points. Activation is instant and applies to the entire organization.
  - During the trial, all **Company Admins** receive email and in-product notifications when the trial starts, is ending soon, and when it has ended. Users will see in-product notifications.

:::note
In product trials are gradually being rolled-out. As a Company Admin of an Enterprise account, if you currently can't start a trial, it may not have rolled out to your account yet or another Company Admin has already started a trial for your account.
:::

- **Through Sales:**
  - Company admins can request a trial directly through sales by completing [this form](https://miro.com/prototyping/contact/sales/), even if in-product activation is available.
  - When the trial starts, all Admins receive an email confirmation.
  - If your organization needs more time to trial Prototypes, contact sales by completing [this form](https://miro.com/prototyping/contact/sales/) to discuss your needs. They may able to extend your trial.

:::note
Enterprise Admins can optionally limit access to **AI features** through [Miro AI granular admin controls (Beta)](https://help.miro.com/hc/en-us/articles/27016283682578-Miro-AI-granular-admin-controls-Beta). Non-AI aspects of the add-on remain available to all users in the trialed org.
:::

During the trial, all add-on features are enabled and Prototypes actions **do not consume AI credits**.

## **User requests for access**

If a user without access selects a Prototyping feature, they may see a **Request access from your admin** prompt.

This sends a notification to **all Admins**, who can choose to start a trial or purchase the add-on depending on their plan.

Enterprise Admins receive a maximum of **one email per week** for access requests. Starter and Business Admins receive a maximum of **one email per day**.

## **Purchasing**

- **Starter and Business:** Billing Admins can purchase the add-on directly from the **Billing** page.
- **Enterprise:** Purchases are handled through Miro Sales.

**More information:** See [Purchase and manage add-ons](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/28897507989138).

## FAQ

**Why can’t I start a Miro Prototypes trial while my Business plan trial is active?**

Prototypes trials are currently not available during a Business plan trial. We know this is inconvenient, and we are working to make this more flexible in the future. Once your Business plan trial ends and you upgrade to Starter or Business Plan, you will be able to start a Prototypes trial.

**How do I cancel the trial?**

If you don't want to add Miro Prototypes to your subscription after the trial ends, no action is required. After the trial period, it will be automatically removed and you will not be charged.

**How do I keep Miro Prototypes after the trial?**

You can add Miro Prototypes to your Starter or Business plan subscription any time during the add-on trial. Admins can do this by navigating to the Billing settings page within [Admin Console](https://help.miro.com/hc/en-us/articles/22848173357970-Miro-s-new-Admin-Console) and click **Add to subscription** under **Add-ons**. You will only be charged after the Prototypes trial ends. Enterprise admins can reach out to Sales to purchase the Add-on. **More information:** See [Purchase and manage add-ons](https://help.miro.com/hc/articles/28897507989138).

**What happens to my Prototypes if I don't purchase the add-on after the trial ends?**

After the trial ends, your existing Prototypes will not be removed and you will still be able to view and manually edit the prototypes on the board. You will lose access to generate new Prototypes with AI, create connectors and preview. These will reactivate automatically if you decide to purchase the add-on.
