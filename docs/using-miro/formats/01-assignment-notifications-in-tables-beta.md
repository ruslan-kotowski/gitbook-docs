When you are assigned to an item using the Assignee field, you receive an email and in-product feed notification. To reduce inbox noise, email notifications are batched and sent every few minutes. If you are assigned to multiple items in a short period, you get one email listing all new assignments instead of a separate email for each.

Each email includes:

- The board and table name
- The item(s) you were assigned to
- A link to open the item or table

## Notification triggers

- You are assigned to an item using the Assignee field.
- You are assigned on a synced copy of a table; the notification links to the board where the assignment happened.

### What does not trigger an email

- You are removed from the assignee column.
- An item you are already assigned to is updated.

## Requirements

You must have access to the board where the assignment was made. You will not receive notifications on boards you do not have access to.

If a table has synced copies across multiple boards, the notification links to the board where the assignment happened, not the home board of the table.

## Manage notification preferences

1. Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Settings** > **Notifications**.
3. Toggle **Assignment notifications** on or off.

## FAQ

**I was assigned an item but didn't get a notification.**

Check that you have access to the board and that assignment notifications are enabled in your settings. Check the assignee column settings to ensure that the toggle for notifications is enabled.

**Why did I get one email with multiple assignments?**

Assignments are batched and sent every few minutes, grouped into a single email.

**Which board does the link go to for synced tables?**

The board where the assignment happened, not the home board.

**Can I get notifications in Slack?**

Assignment notifications are currently available via email and in-product feed. Slack support is planned for a future release.
