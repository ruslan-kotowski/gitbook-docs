Stickies capture allows you to convert real stickies to fully editable [Miro sticky notes](../../essential-tools/14-sticky-notes.md). Share them and collaborate in real-time, turn them into [Jira tasks](../../../integrations-apps/atlassian/03-jira-cards.md) or make a part of digital diagrams, [templates](../../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/04-templates.md), and more.

:::warning
Stickies capture requires Miro AI to be enabled.
:::

### How to convert stickies on your smartphone

1. Download [Miro Mobile app](../../../getting-started/apps-for-devices/08-mobile-app.md) ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/miro-formerly-realtimeboard/id1180074773), [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.realtimeboard)).
2. Open a board where you would like to place stickies.
3. Tap on the plus sign to open the menu and choose the **Stickies capture** icon.
   ![stickies_capture_on_mobile.jpg](images/21017004784146_stickies%20capture%20on%20mobile.jpg)
   *Stickies capture on the toolbar in Mobile app*
4. Choose an existing photo or take a new one. You can upload files with a maximum size of 15 MB.
   ![choose_a_photo.jpg](images/21016967314578_choose%20a%20photo.jpg)*The options to take a photo or choose from library to capture stickies*
5. If some stickies were not recognized correctly, tap on these stickies to add or edit contours. Then tap **Add to board**. Enable the toggle **Convert to text** so that your stickies are editable on the board. *![capture_stickies.jpg](images/21017004786578_capture%20stickies.jpg)**Capturing sticky notes*
   > 💡 Don't spend much time editing the selection of a sticky note, it will be converted into a Miro sticky note either way.
6. Your captured stickies will appear on the board. You can edit them: change text, colors, etc.
    ![digitized_stickies.jpg](images/21016967316498_digitized%20stickies.jpg)
   *Sticky notes digitized with Miro stickies capture*

:::warning
If the text on a sticky note was not recognized, then this note will appear on the board as an image.
:::

:::note
Stickies capture can digitize only *English* text. But you can still digitize stickies as images if you turn off **Convert to text** toggle in step 5.
:::

### How to convert stickies on the [Tablet App](../../../getting-started/apps-for-devices/11-tablet-app.md) or in web version

1. Open a board where you would like to add stickies.
2. Tap the **Stickies capture** icon on the toolbar.
   ![stickies_capture.jpg](images/21016967310738_stickies%20capture.jpg)
   *Stickies capture on the toolbar in Tablet app*
3. Choose the existing photo from your device.
4. If some stickies are not recognized correctly, click on these stickies to add or edit contours. Then tap **Add to board.**
5. Your captured stickies will appear on the board. You can edit them: change text, colors, add tags, etc.

:::tip
Digitized stickies look much better if you follow the simple rules:

- Write notes using a dark felt-tip pen which contrasts with the note color
- Don't overlap your sticky notes
- Stick notes separately on a monochrome surface
- Take a photo in a room with good lighting and avoid dark shadows
:::
