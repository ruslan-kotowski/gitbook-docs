Upload video files directly to your Miro Canvas for playback at any time by anyone on your board.

## Upload videos

Drag and drop supported video files directly onto the Canvas to upload. Alternatively:

1. Click on the **Tools, Media and Integrations** icon at the bottom of the Creation toolbar.
2. Search for and select **Video**.
3. Click on the Canvas where you'd like to place your uploaded video.
   An empty Video widget will appear.
4. In the created Video widget, click **Upload a video** to open the file browser.
5. Select the video file to upload and click **Open**.
6. Allow a few seconds for the video to upload, depending on file size.

:::tip
When creating a template, you can stop after step 3 above to create a Video widget placeholder for template users to upload their own videos later.
:::

You can also upload videos via the Upload option.

1. Click on the **Tools, Media and Integrations** icon at the bottom of the Creation toolbar.
2. Search for and select **Upload > My device**.
3. Find the file you'd like to upload and click **Open**.
4. Allow a few seconds for the video to upload, depending on file size.

:::note
Uploaded video files can be a maximum of 150MB.
:::

### Change the title of a video

The file name of your video will show up as the title on the preview thumbnail on the board. If you need to change this to something more descriptive:

1. Click on the video to show the context menu bar.
2. Click on the **title** in the context menu bar.
3. Type in your new title and press **enter**.

The updated title will appear on the video preview.

## Video formats supported

The following video formats are supported for upload:

- MP4
- MOV
- WebM

The following video codecs are supported:

- h264
- h265
- av1
- vp8
- vp9

## Video controls

Video controls include:

- Play/Pause
- Forward/Backward in 10 second intervals
- Audio on/off
- Full screen

When in Full screen mode, keyboard controls are also supported. Use the **space bar** to pause or play the video, the **arrow keys** to skip ahead or back in 10 second intervals, or **esc** to exit Full screen mode..

## FAQ

**Who can view my videos on the Canvas?**

Video files are only visible to other board collaborators once uploaded (they have the same permissions settings as the entire board).

**Can more than one board user watch the video at the same time?**

Yes, but each user will have to click **Play** on their own board. Board users can watch a video that is already being viewed by other board users, asynchronously.

**Can I leave comments on or annotate a specific part of the video?**

Not currently. However, you can pause the video, click the context menu, and select **Copy as image**. Then paste the image onto the board and leave comments or annotate the image.
