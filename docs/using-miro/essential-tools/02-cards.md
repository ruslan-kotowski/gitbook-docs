Miro cards provide you with a structured way to organize and assign work, provide details to align stakeholders, and keep everything on track. Use cards for [planning](../advanced-tools/02-columns-formerly-kanban.md) or [story mapping](../advanced-tools/07-user-story-mapping.md).

## Using cards

### How to add the card app to the toolbar

If you’re often working with cards on your board, add the card feature to your toolbar for faster card creation.

1. Go to the creation toolbar on the left side of your board
2. Click the **More apps** icon
3. Search for ‘card’
4. Click the **card** icon to add it to the toolbar

![Adding_the_card_app_to_the_toolbar.gif](images/21017682630802_Adding%20the%20card%20app%20to%20the%20toolbar.gif)*Adding the card app to the toolbar*

### How to add cards to the board

There are three ways to add a card to your board.

**Drag the card icon** from the creation toolbar and drop it anywhere on the board.

![Dragging_the_card_icon_to_the_board.gif](images/21017653007634_Dragging%20the%20card%20icon%20to%20the%20board.gif)*Dragging the card onto the board*

**Click the card icon** and then click anywhere on the board.

![Clicking_on_the_board_to_add_a_card.gif](images/21017653008914_Clicking%20on%20the%20board%20to%20add%20a%20card.gif)*Clicking on the card icon to add a card to the board*

**Use the D [hotkey](#card-hotkey-shortcuts)** and click anywhere on the board.

![Using_the_D_hotkey_to_add_a_card.gif](images/21017682640018_Using%20the%20D%20hotkey%20to%20add%20a%20card.gif)*Using the D hotkey to add a card to the board*

:::tip
[Enable dimensions](../formats/diagramming/01-miro-for-mapping-diagramming.md#use-object-dimensions-for-precision) to create the same size cards across your board.
:::

### How to delete cards from the board

1. Click on the card and press backspace on your keyboard
2. You can also click the three dots in the context menu (**...**) > **Delete**

*![deleting_a_card.jpg](images/21017682620178_deleting%20a%20card.jpg)**Deleting a card*

### How to convert other objects into cards

You can convert any existing [sticky note](14-sticky-notes.md), [text box](16-text.md), or [shape](11-shapes.md) into a card using the [Switch type](../working-on-the-board/20-switch-type.md) feature.

1. Click the object to open the context menu
2. Click the **Switch type** icon
3. Choose the card icon from the dropdown menu

![converting_a_sticky_into_a_card.gif](images/21017682622994_converting%20a%20sticky%20into%20a%20card.gif)*Converting a sticky note into a card*

**To bulk-convert multiple objects into cards**, click and drag to select the objects and click the Switch type icon in the context menu. Select the card icon.

*![Bulk_converting_to_cards.gif](images/21017682628370_Bulk%20converting%20to%20cards.gif)
Bulk converting multiple stickies into cards*

:::tip
You can [convert cards into Jira cards](../../integrations-apps/atlassian/03-jira-cards.md). Use bulk convert to create multiple Jira cards after a team brainstorming session or agile ceremony.
:::

### How to open the card panel

Open the card panel to see all the card details, make all your edits at once, and [add a description](#how-to-add-a-card-description).

To open the card panel, click the **Expand** icon either in the top right corner of the card or in the card’s context menu. You can also use the hotkey **Ctrl** + **Shift** + **Enter** (*for Windows*) **Cmd** + **Shift** + **Enter** (*for Mac)***.**

![Expanding_a_card.gif](images/21017682634514_Expanding%20a%20card.gif)*Opening the card panel*

## Editing and customizing cards

### How to edit a card

Click on the card to open the context menu, or open the card panel. Here you can change the color and size of cards, add tags, links and dates, and assign cards to users.

![customizing_a_card.gif](images/21017682624018_customizing%20a%20card.gif)*Editing a card*

### How to bulk-edit cards

1. Drag the selection field across the cards, or select cards one by one while holding down **Shift** or **Ctrl** *(for Windows),* or **Command** *(for Mac)*
2. The context menu will open
3. Choose any tool from the context menu to make edits

![Bulk-editing_cards.gif](images/21017682641298_Bulk-editing%20cards.gif)*Editing multiple cards at once*

### How to resize or rotate a card

Click on the card and drag the white dots. You can rotate the card by dragging the arrow icon.

![Changing_size_and_rotating_a_card.gif](images/21017682626834_Changing%20size%20and%20rotating%20a%20card.gif)*Changing the card size and rotating the card*

### How to assign a card to a user

Cards can be assigned to users who have access to the board via [email invitation](../sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md#inviting-via-email), [team share](../sharing-boards/03-sharing-boards-and-inviting-collaborators.md), or a [project](../sharing-boards/16-projects.md). Cards cannot be assigned to [visitors](../sharing-boards/08-collaboration-with-visitors.md).

1. Click on the card to open the context menu, or open the card panel
2. Click the **add assignee** icon
3. Select a user from the dropdown

![adding_an_assignee.jpg](images/21017682621970_adding%20an%20assignee.jpg)*Assigning a card to a user*

### How to add and remove tags from cards

1. Click on the card to open the context menu, or open the card panel
2. Click the **add tag** icon
3. Enter the text for your tag (and choose the tag color)
4. Click create or press enter on your keyboard
5. Click on a tag to remove it from the card

:::tip
Use tags to [search and filter](../working-on-the-board/19-searching-text-on-the-board.md) your cards.
:::

![managing_tags_on_cards.gif](images/21017682625426_managing%20tags%20on%20cards.gif)*Adding and removing tags from a card*

### How to update the status of a card

1. Click on the card to open the context menu, or open the card panel
2. Click the **add status** icon
3. Select **To do**, **In progress**, or **Done** from the dropdown.
4. To remove a status, click **Remove status**

![Updating_the_status_of_a_card.gif](images/21017653012626_Updating%20the%20status%20of%20a%20card.gif)*Updating the status of a card*

### How to add and remove dates on a card

1. Click on the card to open the context menu, or open the card panel
2. Click the **set date** icon
3. Enter the start date and/or due date.
4. To clear a date, click **x** next to the date field

![Adding_dates_to_a_card.gif](images/21017653013650_Adding%20dates%20to%20a%20card.gif)*Adding a start and due date to a card*

### Card titles and descriptions

You can add up to 20,000 text symbols in the card description and title. The title of a card is always visible. Users can expand the card to see the description.

#### **How to add a card title**

Click on the card and start typing. Changing the text font and size of a card title is not possible.

![Adding_a_title_to_a_card.gif](images/21017653015698_Adding%20a%20title%20to%20a%20card.gif)*Adding a card title*

#### **How to add a card description**

Click on the expand icon to open the card panel and start typing in the **Description** field. You can also paste copied text directly into the description field using **Ctrl + C**/**Ctrl + V** (*for Windows*) or **Cmd + C/Cmd + V** (*for Mac*).

![Adding_a_description_to_the_card.gif](images/21017682651410_Adding%20a%20description%20to%20the%20card.gif)*Adding a card description*

## How to export cards

1. Drag the selection field across the cards, or select cards one by one while holding down **Shift** or **Ctrl** *(for Windows)* or **Command** *(for Mac)*
2. Click the **Export** icon > **Export to** **Spreadsheet (CSV)**

![export_menu_cards_export_to_csv.gif](images/21017653019666_export_menu_cards_export_to_csv.gif)
*Exporting cards to a CSV*

## Card hotkey shortcuts

- **D** + click to add a new card to the board
- **Ctrl + Shift + Enter *(for Windows)*, Cmd + Shift + Enter *(For Mac)*** to open all cards
- **Tab** to create a new card and edit the card title
- **Ctrl + B** *(for Windows)*, **Cmd + B** *(For Mac)* - bold
- **Ctrl + I** *(for Windows)*, **Cmd + I** *(For Mac) -* italic
- **Ctrl + U** *(for Windows)*, **Cmd + U** *(For Mac) -* underline
- **“-“** + **space** at the beginning of a line for a bulleted list
- **“1.”** at the beginning of a line for a numbered list
- Select text and **Ctrl + V** *(for Windows) or* **Cmd + V** *(for Mac)* to create a hyperlink

## Frequently asked questions

**If I assign a card to a user, will they get an email notification?**

No, the user won't get an email notification.

**Can I configure email reminders based on the due date that I added to a card?**

This is not currently possible.
