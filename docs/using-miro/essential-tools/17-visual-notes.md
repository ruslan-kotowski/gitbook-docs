Miro visual notes is an advanced text editor embedded into each of your boards. Work with text like you would normally do in any text editor to make your notes easy to read.

### Collaborating in notes

Find the **Note** app under the **Tools, Media and Integrations** (**+**) menu in the Creation toolbar on the left-hand side of the board.

![visual-notes-entry-point.png](images/21537451739922_visual-notes-entry-point.png)
*Accessing notes*

You can easily resize the Notes panel by simply dragging the three dots of the section border.

![visual-notes-panel.png](images/21537435743634_visual-notes-panel.png)
*Resizing the notes*

There you are offered some pre-made templates such as *To-do list*, *Meeting agenda,* and others. Click on a template to add it to the note. The template will appear in the notes right away.

:::warning
You can add a template only while there is no content in the note section.
:::

![visual-notes-template.gif](images/21537451751442_visual-notes-template.gif)*Adding a template*

Work on notes together with your team and see who is working on what in real-time making collaboration convenient. All the changes are visible in real-time.

Click the **Show authorship** icon to see the initials of content creators to make it easier for other contributors to determine who is responsible for changes and additions to the note.

![visual-notes-show-authorship.png](images/21537435756434_visual-notes-show-authorship.png)
*Enable Show authorship mode to see the creator*

You can pin the notes by clicking the **Pin** icon to keep the section opened for all future visitors. Thus, you will let your collaborators know that you need their attention to the notes section. Any editor can unpin the notes by simply clicking the pin icon:

![visual-notes-pin.png](images/21537435760530_visual-notes-pin.png)
*If a note is pinned, the pinner's initials will appear on the icon*

You can easily discuss your ideas by leaving comments right in notes. Tag a teammate in the comments section using [@mentions](../facilitation-tools/01-@mentioning-people-on-the-board.md).

![visual-notes-comments.png](images/21537435765522_visual-notes-comments.png)
*Click the comment icon to leave/view comments*

### Formatting

In notes you can work with text like you would normally do in any text editor to make your notes easy to read. This enables the reader to easily extract the required information from the document just as it was meant to.

Highlight text to see the options for text formatting:

![visual-notes-formatting.png](images/21537451775762_visual-notes-formatting.png)
*Choosing text formatting*

:::warning
Note that there is **a limit of 200,000 symbols** in the notes section.
:::

:::tip
To link text in notes to an object on the board, [copy the link to the object](../working-on-the-board/16-internal-and-external-linking.md#links-to-objects-on-the-board), select the text in notes and link it.
:::

### Board content in notes

Embed objects from your board into notes to make it easier for other members of your board to navigate and contribute.

To add a board object to a note, select the object(s) and drag them to notes. This creates a copy of the content from the board in the note.

![visual-notes-drag-and-drop.gif](images/21537451782290_visual-notes-drag-and-drop.gif)
*Highlighting and dragging board objects into the notes*

You can easily navigate to the board content from the copy in the notes. To do that you need to select the content and click the arrow icon in the popup:

![visual-notes-board-object.png](images/21537451786514_visual-notes-board-object.png)
*Navigating the board right from the notes panel*

If the board content is updated, you can refresh the corresponding copy in the notes *manually*. For that you need to highlight the content in the notes and click the refresh icon in the settings popup.

![visual-notes-refresh-object.png](images/21537451792658_visual-notes-refresh-object.png)
*Updating the board content in the notes*

:::note
If you add another widget on an object that was embedded in notes and update the embedded object, the new widget will not appear in notes. Please remove the embedded object, select both widgets on your board, and add them to notes.
:::

:::warning
If the copied object in notes is deleted, the corresponding board object is not removed.
:::

### Export

You can export your visual notes in PDF format to make your content portable and make it possible to work on your text offline.

![visual-notes-export.png](images/21537451799314_visual-notes-export.png)
*You can export the notes as PDF*

### Notes on Mobile and Tablet apps

Visual notes are also available in the [Mobile app](../../getting-started/apps-for-devices/08-mobile-app.md) and [Tablet app](../../getting-started/apps-for-devices/11-tablet-app.md) for Android and iOS users. The Miro App version should be **3.3.1** or higher for the Android and iOS mobile and tablet apps.

The functionality is almost the same as in the desktop version except for some peculiarities.

:::warning
The notes take the whole screen, so you work either on the board or in visual notes.
⚠️ You cannot add board content to the notes. Although refreshing, navigating, deleting options are available.
⚠️ Export of visual notes is not possible in Mobile app.
:::

![notes_on_mobile.jpg](images/4815084087570_notes%20on%20mobile.jpg)
*Android mobile app version of visual notes*

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I delete notes from my board?**

You can delete content inside notes. If notes are opened every time you load the board, you can hide notes by unpinning them.

**Can I make multi-list notes/multiple notes sections?**

No, it is one page per board.

**Can I add the checklist template to my board?**

On your board, you can use checkboxes from the [Wireframe library](../miroverse/prototyping/02-prototyping-library.md).

**I accidentally deleted text in notes. What do I do?**

Use the Undo button on the top of notes - it will allow you to undo actions in the *current working session*. You can also use the shortcut **Ctrl + Z** (*for Windows*) or **Cmd + Z** (*for Mac*).
