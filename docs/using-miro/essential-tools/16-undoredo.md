Accidentally deleted or moved a widget on the board? Have no worries, as you can easily undo the action.

## Undo and redo options

**Undo** and **redo** buttons are available under the creation toolbar. You can undo and redo *up to 30 actions* in Miro during the current session. If you refresh the page or re-open your board, **undo** and **redo** commands *will be unavailable*.

:::tip
If you need to restore accidentally deleted content, [check this guide](../working-on-the-board/18-restoring-board-content.md).
:::

![undo-redo.png](images/21537436796434_undo-redo.png)
*Undo/Redo options on Miro board*

To **undo** your previous actions, press **Ctrl + Z** (*for Windows*)/**Cmd**+**Z** (*for Mac*) until you reach the action you want to undo. If you prefer mouse navigation to keyboard shortcuts, click the **undo** (![icon-undo.svg](images/27942193905298_icon-undo.svg)) button.

To **redo** an action you've undone, press **Ctrl + Shift + Z** (*for Windows*)/**Cmd**+ **Shift**+**Z** (*for Mac*) or click the **redo** (![icon-redo.svg](images/27942193906194_icon-redo.svg)) button.

Both the **redo** and **undo** buttons get activated only after at least one action has been taken on the board. You can undo/redo only your actions, not collaborators' actions.

## FAQ

**How can I undo the deletion of the whole board?**

Find the instructions here: [How to restore a deleted board](../managing-boards/08-how-to-restore-a-deleted-board.md).

**Can I undo the deletion of content on my board?**

Yes, [learn here how](../working-on-the-board/18-restoring-board-content.md).

**Can I undo changes made by other board editors?**

No, you can only undo your actions.

**The Undo/Redo buttons are not active. Why?**

Please note that the buttons only get activated once you make a change on the board in the current working session. They are not active when you start a new session (reload the page, for example). If the buttons do not get activated even after you change your content, please check our [troubleshooting guide](../tools/troubleshooting/07-how-to-troubleshoot-and-report-a-bug.md). Please note that toolbars may disappear on iPad - [learn more](../../getting-started/apps-for-devices/11-tablet-app.md#ipad-limitations).
