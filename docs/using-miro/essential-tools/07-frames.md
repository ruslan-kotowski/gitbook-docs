Add structure to the content on your board with frames. You can add, delete and customize frames, use them to easily navigate content on your board, present frames to an audience and export them as a PDF.

## Frame basics

### Add a frame

:::tip
When you open the frames tool, click **View templates** to access a variety of pre-set presentation and slide [templates](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/04-templates.md#from-template-panels).
:::

1. Click the **F****rame** icon in the [Creation](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/05-toolbars.md#creation-toolbar) toolbar on the left side of your board.
2. The frames tool will open.
3. Drag and drop frames right onto your board, or press the [F hotkey](../working-on-the-board/06-shortcuts-and-hotkeys.md) and click anywhere on the board to add a frame. You can also click and drag to create frames directly on the board.

![adding-a-frame-to-the-board.gif](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22431638268690)
*Creating frames on a board*

You can also add frames around existing objects.

Easily create frames around existing objects if you’ve already added content to your board. [Select the objects](../working-on-the-board/10-working-with-objects.md), click **three dots** (**...**) icon on the context menu, and choose **Create frame**.

![create-a-frame.gif](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22431638273170)
*Creating a frame around board objects*

:::tip
Enable [dimensions](../formats/diagramming/01-miro-for-mapping-diagramming.md#use-object-dimensions-for-precision) to create the same frames across your board with precision.
:::

### Delete a frame

1. Click the **three dots** (**...**) icon on the context menu.
2. Choose **Delete**.

![delete a frame.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22431681583378)
*Deleting a single frame*

You can also use hotkeys: click on the frame title and press **backspace** or **delete**.

:::tip
If you accidentally delete a frame you can always [restore from your board history.](../working-on-the-board/18-restoring-board-content.md#how-to-restore-content)
:::

To delete several frames:

1. Select the frames while holding down shift or draw the selection field. Learn more about [object selection](../working-on-the-board/10-working-with-objects.md).
2. In the context menu, use the filter to select **Frame**.
3. Click the **three dots** (**...**) icon.
4. Click **Delete**.

![delete-multiple-frames.gif](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22431638279186)
*Deleting several frames at once*

## **Customize frames**

**You can edit the placement, color, name, size, or ratio of a frame. And you can group, lock, and duplicate frames.**

### Move and edit frames

To move a frame:

1. Switch to the [select tool](../working-on-the-board/05-using-miro-with-a-mouse-trackpad-or-touchscreen.md#switching-between-the-pan-and-select-tool).
2. Click and drag the frame's border or title.

The default color of a frame is white. You can change the size and color of the frame in the context toolbar.

### Rename a frame

To rename a frame:

1. Double-click the frame heading.
2. Start typing a new name
3. Alternatively, click the **three dots** (**...**) icon in the context menu.
4. Click **Rename frame**.

### Resize a frame

Select the frame by clicking its border or title, then drag its borders to adjust the frame size. You can also change the frame's ratio in the context menu by clicking the **three dots** (**...**) icon.

:::tip
[Enable dimensions](../formats/diagramming/01-miro-for-mapping-diagramming.md#use-object-dimensions-for-precision) to create the same size frames across your board with precision.
:::

### Group frames

To group frames:

1. Select several frames while holding down Shift.
2. You can also use the filter option to select all frames.
3. Click the **Group** icon in the context menu.

### Lock a frame

Lock frames to prevent them from moving accidentally. If you want to restrict board editors from accidentally moving the content inside the frame, select all the objects and lock them.

The option to lock and unlock frames depends on your plan type and your access level. Learn more in [Locking content on the board](../working-on-the-board/17-locking-content-on-the-board.md#use-protected-lock).

### Duplicate a frame

To duplicate a frame use the [shortcut](../working-on-the-board/06-shortcuts-and-hotkeys.md) **Ctrl/Cmd + D**. If your frame's name contains a date, it will automatically be updated. Years advance to the next year, days advance to the next day.
![duplicating-a-frame.gif](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22431638284434)
*Duplicating a frame*

### Copying and pasting within frames

When you copy and paste content within frames, the content will appear in the same place within the frames it's pasted into as the original frame. To copy and paste between frames:

1. Select the content you want to copy. Hold down Ctrl/Cmd to select multiple objects.
2. Click on **Copy** under the **three dots** (**...**) icon in the context menu. Or use the keyboard shortcut: **Ctrl/Cmd-C**.
3. Select the destination frame(s). (You can group frames prior to doing this to make selection easier.)
4. Click on **Paste** under the **three dots** (**...**) icon in the context menu. Or use the keyboard shortcut: **Ctrl/Cmd-V**.

Note that if the destination frames are a different size than the original frame content was copied from, the content will be aligned based on the top left corner of the destination frame based on the original x-y coordinates. This means some content may appear outside of your frame or in a different position relative to the edges.

## Presenting with frames

### Presentation mode

Navigate smoothly between frames while presenting your board to an audience. To turn on presentation mode, click the Present button in the Collaboration toolbar, located in the top right of your board.

Your view will change to full screen and the frames will appear as slides, with board tools and frame titles hidden.

Use the arrows to navigate between your frames/slides.

### Hiding and revealing frames

> **Who can do it:** Board owners, board co-owners **Relevant for**: Education, Starter, Business, and Enterprise plans

Hiding frames lets you control what content is revealed to your collaborators and audience during workshops and meetings.

Once a frame is hidden, no one on the board can see its content. Hiding frames is helpful when it comes to designing meetings and organizing workshops step-by-step.

When a frame is hidden, your board participants will not be able to view its content. Instead, they’ll see a blank frame with a closed eye icon.

If users place an object on top of the hidden frame - it will go under the placeholder frame but is still editable.

:::tip
Split content into multiple frames to reveal them one by one in your meeting or workshop. You can create the agenda using visual notes and add hidden frames in notes.
:::

:::warning
Remember that hiding a frame does not conceal the frame’s comments and title.
:::

To hide a frame, click on the frame, then click the eye icon on the frame's object menu.

![hiding-multiple-frames.gif](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22431681592594)
*Hiding several frames*

You can also hide a frame from the frames panel, accessed from the navigation toolbar. Click on the **Frames** icon and then use the **three dots** (**...**) menu to access the Hide function.

To hide several frames at once, drag to [select the frames](../working-on-the-board/10-working-with-objects.md) and click the **eye** icon in the context menu.

To reveal a hidden frame, click the **eye** icon again in the context menu.

:::warning
When hidden frames are revealed on the board they won’t be revealed automatically in [notes](17-visual-notes.md). Please refresh notes to reveal the frames.
:::

:::warning
Board participants can’t copy or duplicate a hidden frame until the frame is revealed.
:::

## Quick presenting frame features

There are many features that can help you quickly organize frames for seamless boards, presentations, and workshops.

### Review and reorder frames

Quickly jump from one frame to another and reorder your frames in the Frames panel. To change the order of frames, drag and drop them into their new positions within the Frames panel.

![frames-button.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22431681594898)
*Accessing the Frames panel from the navigation toolbar*

:::warning
Re-ordering frames in the sidebar is not available on mobile and other touch devices for tablet app and browser.
:::

### Multi-select frames

Multi-select allows you to select multiple frames in the Frames panel to reorder or delete them in bulk. To select multiple frames, press and hold **ctrl** on Windows or **command** on Mac.

![frame-multi-select.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22431638291602)

*Selecting multiple frames to delete, hide, or move*

If you have several frames, it may help to organize them in list view. To view your frames in a list instead of thumbnails, click the **three dots** (**...**) icon and select **List View**.

![frames-list-view-2.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22431681597842)

*Listing frames in list view*

### Organize frames

Organize frames reads the order of frames in your board and sorts them in the sidebar automatically, allowing you to quickly prepare your presentation. The algorithm arranges frames according to the order in which they are placed on the board—from left to right and from up to down.

In case you used Organize frames and wish to go back to your previous frame order, click on the [Undo](16-undoredo.md) button under the creation toolbar on the left side of your board.

![frames-organize.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22431638296594)*Organize frames automatically from the three dots (...) menu*

### Share the link to a specific frame

When sharing your board, you may want someone to focus on a specific frame. You can copy the link to a specific frame within the object menu: click on the object > click the **three dots** (**...**) > **Copy link**.

![copy link to a frame.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22431681603346)
*Copying the link to a particular frame on the board*

## Exporting frames

You may want to export only one frame or a selection of frames.

To export a selection of frames:

1. Select the frames you want to export while holding down the shift key, or drag a selection box around the frames.
2. In the context menu, click the filter icon and select **Frames**.
3. Click the **three dots** (**...**) icon.
4. Choose an export option from the menu.

To export a single frame, select the frame, click the **three dots** (**...**) icon, and choose an export option.

![export a frame.png](https://help.miro.com/hc/article_attachments/22431638304914)
*Exporting a single frame*

### Exporting boards

You may want to share your presentation outside your team or with external collaborators and vendors. To do that, you can export the entire board. **More information:** [How to export your board](../import-and-export/export/03-how-to-export-your-board.md).

## Frequently asked questions

**Why aren’t objects sticking to my frame?**

Sometimes when adding a frame on top of objects they don’t attach. Select the objects and slightly move them - this should attach them to the frame.

**How do I copy my frame to another board?**

Use Ctrl+C/Ctrl+V (for Windows) or Cmd+C/Cmd+V (for Mac) to copy and paste a frame to another board. Please make sure that the board owner has permissions enabled for copying board content.
