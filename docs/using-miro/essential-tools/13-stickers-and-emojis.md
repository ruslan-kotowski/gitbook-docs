In Miro, you can place emojis, stickers, and GIFs on your boards and modify them. Share feedback, express your emotions, and visualize your thoughts and ideas.

## Stickers and emojis

Energize your Miro boards with stickers and emojis to add ownable moments with a playful touch to your content.

:::note
If you can't find the Stickers and Emojis app, download it from the [Miro Marketplace](https://miro.com/marketplace/emoji-app/?backUrl=%2Fmarketplace%2F). If you don't have app management permissions, please contact your Team Admin or Company Admin.
:::

### How to add stickers or emojis to a board

1. Navigate to the [creation toolbar](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/05-toolbars.md#creation-toolbar) on the left side of your board.
2. Click **Tools, Media and Integrations** (![icon-tools.svg](images/23227364594834_icon-tools.svg)) icon and search **Stickers and Emojis**.
3. Switch to the **Stickers** or **Emojis** tab at the top of the panel.
4. Click and drag stickers or emojis onto the board.

![stickers-and-emojis.gif](images/23227364597138_stickers-and-emojis.gif)*Adding an emoji and stickers to a Miro board*

You can add several emojis and stickers while keeping the library open.

Switch between different types of stickers on the horizontal menu.

![sticker-groups.gif](images/23227380926354_sticker-groups.gif)*Switching between sticker groups*

Use search to find an emoji or a sticker quickly.

After adding an emoji or sticker, you can click on it to change its size and rotate it.

Change the default skin tone for your emojis by clicking **Skin tone**. Note that some emojis' skin tones cannot be changed (for example, emoji smileys).

Recently used emojis and sticker will appear in the **Recent** section on their respective tabs.

:::warning
Due to technical limitations, flag emojis are displayed as letters on Windows OS. Besides, there may be issues with displaying emojis on operating systems produced before 2018.
:::

### Interactive Stickers

Some Stickers include interactive features, such as changes to their appearance when board users click on a Sticker to indicate their agreement. You can find these Interactive Stickers on the horizontal menu on the Stickers tab.

## GIFs

> **Available for:** Free, Starter, Business

Bring your Miro boards to life with GIFs for a fun, engaging, and visually dynamic collaboration experience.

### How to add a GIF to a board

1. Navigate to the [creation toolbar](../../getting-started/start-here/your-first-board/05-toolbars.md#creation-toolbar) on the left side of your board.
2. Click **Tools, Media and Integrations** (![icon-tools.svg](images/23227364594834_icon-tools.svg)) icon and search **Stickers and Emojis**.
3. Switch to the **GIFs** tab at the top of the panel.
4. Click and drag GIFs onto the board. You can also use search to find a GIF quickly.
