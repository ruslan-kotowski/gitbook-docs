You can duplicate a board from the dashboard or from the board info window.

### How to duplicate a Miro board

To duplicate a board from within the board itself:

1. Click on the **main menu** (![icon-main.svg](images/25472437261842_icon-main.svg)) icon in the top left toolbar.
2. Click on the **Board** submenu.
3. Click **Duplicate**.
   A dialog opens.
4. Enter the new board name, select whether you want to preserve sharing settings on the duplicated board, and then click **Copy**.

Alternatively, you can duplicate a board from the board details dialog:

1. Click on the **main menu** (![icon-main.svg](images/25472437261842_icon-main.svg)) icon in the top left toolbar.
2. Click on the **Board** submenu.
3. Click **Details**.
   A dialog opens.
4. Click the **Duplicate** button at the bottom.
   A dialog opens.
5. Enter the new board name, select whether you want to preserve sharing settings on the duplicated board, and then click **Copy**.

:::note
When users duplicate a board, any [Talktracks](../facilitation-tools/asynchronous-tools/02-talktrack-board-recordings.md) will also be duplicated to the new board.
:::

> **✏️** If you are a [guest](../sharing-boards/07-collaboration-with-guests.md) on the board and do not have membership in any other Miro teams or have a [Free Restricted license](../../enterprise-administration/enterprise-subscription-management/enterprise-licensing/04-free-restricted-license.md), you will be further suggested to create a new Miro team to save the board copy in.

To duplicate a board from the dashboard:

1. Hover over the board thumbnail.
2. Click on the **three dots** (**...**) menu icon.
3. Click on **Duplicate**.
   A dialog opens.
4. Enter the new board name, select whether you want to preserve sharing settings on the duplicated board, and then click **Copy**.

:::warning
Board names have a maximum character limit of 61. When duplicating boards, "Copy of..." will be added to the board name. If there are too many characters, the board will not copy. Shorten your board's name and duplicate the board again.
:::

## Frequently asked questions

1. *If I change the content on the board copy, will the original board be affected?*
   - No, the content on the copy has no connection with the content on the original board.
2. *How can I duplicate board content to another existing board?*
   - Please [select the needed content](../working-on-the-board/10-working-with-objects.md), copy, and paste it onto another board (you can use **Ctrl+C/Ctrl + V** *for Windows* or **Cmd + C/Cmd + V** *for Mac*).
3. *Can I duplicate several boards in bulk?*
   - No, this is not possible at the moment.
4. *Can I share my board with other users as a template so that they could duplicate it to their own teams?*
   - Yes, you need to make sure that this is allowed in your [board content settings](../sharing-boards/14-how-to-allow-or-restrict-copying-and-exporting-boards-and-content.md).
