### Two ways to delete a board

You can delete a board from the dashboard or from its info-card.

![Delete](images/21017348565650_Delete%20a%20board.png)*Deleting a board from the dashboard by clicking the three dots and choosing the option*

![Delete_a_board.gif](images/21017348564882_Delete%20a%20board.gif)
*Deleting a board from the info-card*

Once a board is deleted, it goes to Trash (on paid plans) from where [it can be restored](08-how-to-restore-a-deleted-board.md). On [Free plan](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md), a deleted board can be restored if the owner follows the board link. Boards can be restored via UI within 90 days after removal.

If you are not the owner of the board and not a Company Admin with [Content Admin permissions](../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/12-content-admin-permissions.md), you **cannot** delete it as shown above. Instead of the **Delete** button, certain users of paid teams who are not board owners see the option to **Leave board** (more in the article [How to leave a board](06-how-to-leave-a-board.md)). Note that free plan members cannot leave boards.

![Leave](images/21017348566290_Leave%20a%20board.png)
*The option to leave a board*

:::note
Admins can delete team members together with their boards.
:::

## Frequently asked questions

1. *I do not have the option to delete a board. Why?*
   - If you do not have the option, you are not the board owner. The option to delete a board is available for *board owners* and for *Enterprise plan [Content Admins](../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/12-content-admin-permissions.md)* only.
2. *I do not have the option to leave a board. Why?*- Please note that it's not possible to leave boards that are stored in [free teams](../../plans-billing/miro-plans/09-free-plan.md) or boards to which you have not been invited via email.
3. *I am an Admin. Can I delete my team members' boards?*
   - No, this option is only available for Company admins on Enterprise plan with enabled [Content Admin permissions](../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/12-content-admin-permissions.md). As an Admin on any other plan, you can [remove the board owner](../../administration/user-management/08-remove-users.md) and their boards or take ownership of their boards when removing.
4. *An employee left our company and we need to remove their boards from the team. How do we proceed?*
   - The Admin can [remove the user](../../administration/user-management/08-remove-users.md) together with their content. They can also assign their content to another Admin or themselves while deleting.
5. *Can I delete several boards in bulk?*
    - No, this is not currently possible.
