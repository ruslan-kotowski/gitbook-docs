Board owners in Miro can transfer ownership of their boards to other team members quickly and easily.

### How to reassign a board to another user

:::warning
When you confirm ownership transfer, you cannot undo this action.
:::

1. If the new owner isn't invited to the board, share the board with them via email/Slack. Open the board **Share** dialog, enter the user’s email address and click **Send**.
   ![share_via_email__1_.gif](images/21017043185682_share%20via%20email%20(1).gif)
   *Inviting a user to a Miro board*

2. Click **Sharing settings** on the board **Share** dialog.
3. Choose **Is owner** next to their name and click **Change ownership** to confirm.
   ![change_board_owner.gif](images/21017043185042_change%20board%20owner.gif)
   *Transferring board ownership to another team member*

:::warning
You can only reassign a board to a member of the team where the board belongs. If the user is not a member of the team, you’ll need to invite them or submit a request to invite them to your Miro Admin.
:::

### The board owner left the company

If a member of your Miro team is no longer available and you need to take ownership of their boards, reach out to your Team Admin.

The Admin can remove the user from the team and subscription and reassign the content to another member or themselves. See how Admins can [remove users on Enterprise plan](../../enterprise-administration/user-management/10-remove-users-on-enterprise-plan.md) or remove users [on all other Miro plans](../../administration/user-management/08-remove-users.md).

If you’re a Company Member on Enterprise plan with [Content Admin Role](../../enterprise-administration/managing-enterprise-teams-and-content/12-content-admin-permissions.md) enabled, you can follow the guide above to [reassign any board to another user](#how-to-reassign-a-board-to-another-user).
