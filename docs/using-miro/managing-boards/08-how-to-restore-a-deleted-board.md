**Recover missing or deleted Miro boards. You can restore a deleted board within 9****0 days****.**

:::tip
You can protect your boards by [saving board backups](../import-and-export/export/05-how-to-save-board-backup.md).
:::

## Restoring a board using the board link

> **Who can do it:** board owner
>
> **Relevant for:** all plans
>
> **Available on:**browser

### How to restore a board using the link

If you’ve accidentally deleted your board or your board has disappeared, you can restore it using the board link.

Example of a Miro board link:

```
https://miro.com/app/board/o9L_kSnC_0=/
```

You can try finding the board link by typing your board name:

- in your browser address bar
- in your browser history search
- in your email search

![searcing_for_a_Miro_board_in_your_browser.gif](images/21017515436050_searcing%20for%20a%20Miro%20board%20in%20your%20browser.gif)*Searching for a Miro board*

Once you’ve found the link and opened it in your browser, you’ll see a message that the board has been deleted.

![board_deleted_modal.jpg](images/21017515434258_board%20deleted%20modal.jpg)
*Board has been deleted message*

Click **restore the board** and you’ll be redirected to your Miro dashboard - there you’ll be able to open the recovered board.

![restoring_a_Miro_board_via_link.gif](images/21017528446354_restoring%20a%20Miro%20board%20via%20link.gif)*Restoring a Miro board using the board link*

## Restoring a board from Trash

> **Who can do it:** board owner
>
> **Relevant for:** Starter, Business, Enterprise, and Education plans
>
> **Available on:** **browser,** Desktop app, Tablet app

### Where to find the Trash feature

The [Trash bin feature](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md#accessing-deleted-boards-trash) is only available for paid and Education plans. If you’re on Free Plan you can [restore a board using the board link.](08-how-to-restore-a-deleted-board.md#restoring-a-board-using-the-board-link)

Your trash bin allows you to browse and restore all recently deleted boards. **Trash** can be found on your [dashboard](../../getting-started/start-here/miro-dashboard/01-what-is-on-your-dashboard.md) by click on your avatar in the upper right corner.

![trash.png](images/23122133552402_trash.png)
*The Trash bin icon*

### How to restore your board from Trash

1. Open **Trash** by clicking on your avatar in the upper right of the dashboard to see any deleted boards.
2. Click on the **three dots** (**...**) menu next to the relevant board to see the option to **Restore**.
3. Click **Restore**.
4. The board will be restored to the original owner and team.

![trash-restore.png](images/23122118937362_trash-restore.png)
*Restoring a board from Trash*

## Restoring a board from a past user

If a user was removed from the team and their boards were deleted, ask your Miro Admin to help you. They’ll be able to see the deleted boards in the trash and can **restore the board** **within 90 days of being deleted**. The admin will become the owner of the restored board.

:::note
Remember to [export your boards](../import-and-export/export/03-how-to-export-your-board.md) or [save board backups](../import-and-export/export/05-how-to-save-board-backup.md) before deleting your team.
:::
