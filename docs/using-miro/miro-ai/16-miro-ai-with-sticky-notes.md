Use Miro AI with Sticky notes to generate, cluster, summarize, or create Docs based on your selection on the board. You can also use Sticky Capture, which enables you to upload an image of stickies with content to generate fully editable Sticky notes on your board.

## Generate Sticky notes and Sticky Capture

You can prompt Miro AI to generate Sticky notes. You can also upload an image of stickies to produce editable versions as Sticky notes on your Miro board, or Sticky Capture.

Follow these steps:

1. Above the Creation bar, click **Create with AI**.
   The **Create with AI** panel opens**.**
2. Click **Formats** > **Sticky notes**.
   The prompting panel opens.
3. Under **Where would you like to start?**,select one of the following:
   - **A text prompt**
     Follow the on-screen instructions to compose your prompt.
   - (Sticky Capture) **An image of sticky notes**
     1. Click **choose file**.
     2. From your computer choose a PNG or JPG image of stickies.
4. In the input field, click the arrow or press **ENTER** on your keyboard.
   Your Sticky notes are added to the Miro board.

## Cluster Sticky notes

1. On your Miro board select a group of Sticky notes.
   A context menu appears above your selection.
2. Click **Miro AI** > **Cluster.**
3. Click one of the following:
   - **By keywords**
     Miro AI clusters your Sticky notes by keywords.
   - **By sentiment**
     Miro AI clusters your Sticky notes by sentiment.

:::tip
You can optionally rename each cluster.
:::

## Summarize a group of Sticky notes

1. On your Miro board, select a group of sticky notes.
   A context menu displays above your selection.
2. Click **Miro AI** > **Document** > **Summary**.
   Miro AI generates a summary based on your selected Sticky notes.

## Generate a Doc based on Sticky notes

1. Select a group of sticky notes.
   A context menu displays above your selection.
2. Click **Miro AI** > **Document**.
3. Select the type of document you want to generate.
   Miro AI generates your document.
