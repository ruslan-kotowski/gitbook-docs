Admin announcements are now generally available, giving admins a centralized way to communicate targeted, time-bound messages across their organization. With admin announcements, admins can share important updates, promote training, support feature rollouts, and drive adoption by displaying banners on boards and the dashboard. Announcements can be targeted to the entire organization or specific teams and scheduled to appear at the right time.

## What’s new

- **Target announcements to the entire organization or multiple teams**
  Reach the right audience by sharing announcements with everyone in the organization or selected teams.
- **Schedule announcements with start and end dates (up to 90 days)**
  Control when announcements appear and how long they remain visible.
- **Prioritize important messages for increased visibility**
  Ensure critical announcements are shown ahead of others.
- **Edit announcements at any time, including when live**
  Update content, audience, or schedule without recreating the announcement.
- **Create announcements quickly from** ***What’s new in Miro*** **with pre-populated content**
  Reuse existing product updates to generate announcements faster.
- **Track and manage announcements across their lifecycle**
  Monitor and manage announcements through draft, scheduled, live, ended, and canceled states.
- **Delegate access using custom admin roles**
  Assign announcement management capabilities without granting full Company Admin access.

To see admin announcements in action, watch the [Admin announcements video](https://academy.miro.com/admin-announcements/445331). For more information, see the [Admin announcements documentation](../../enterprise-administration/admin-announcements/01-admin-announcements-overview-(beta).md).
