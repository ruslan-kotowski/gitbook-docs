Miro AI Workflows delivers an enhanced AI-first workspace in Miro. Use Sidekicks, Flows, and an interface that weaves AI into every interaction, from prompts on new boards, to get from draft to done in minutes.

## AI Workflows features

- [**Sidekicks**](06-sidekicks-overview.md)
  Specialized AI agents that work alongside your team on the canvas, acting as virtual experts who can analyze content, provide guidance, and create outputs in real-time.
- [**Flows**](04-flows-overview.md)
  Visual AI workflows that transform your canvas content through connected, multi-step processes, that turn ideas into comprehensive deliverables like prototypes, documents, or diagrams.

## AI Workflows per plan

Business plans include the full scope of AI Workflows.

For Enterprise plans, AI Workflows is available as an add-on.

For Free, Starter and Education, plans, each team member can execute a maximum of 5 Flow runs, and use the [generalized Sidekick](06-sidekicks-overview.md#sidekicks-per-plan).
