Miro AI Translate enables you to translate text on your canvas to any of the following languages:

- Arabic
- Danish
- Dutch
- English
- Finnish
- French
- German
- Italian
- Japanese
- Korean
- Norwegian
- Polish
- Portuguese
- Russian
- Spanish
- Swedish
- Thai
- Turkish

:::note
To use Miro AI Translate, your Company admin must have Miro AI enabled for your organization.
:::

## Translate canvas text with Miro AI

1. Select one or more objects on your Miro canvas, like Sticky notes.
   A context menu appears.
2. Click **Miro AI**.
3. Hover over **Translate**, then select a language.
   Miro AI translates the text in your selected object(s).

   > 💡 The **Translate** drop-down menu shows the 8 most used languages. Click **Show more** to see the complete list of available languages. Your last used language is pinned to the top.

## Miro AI Translate credit consumption

Miro AI Translate consumes 1 Miro AI credit per translate action.

If you select an object on your canvas with 1 word, or 100 words, you consume exactly 1 Miro AI credit when you translate all text in one action.

If you have multiple objects selected and perform 1 translate action, you consume 1 Miro AI credit.
