Use Miro AI to generate Docs. For example, you can prompt Miro AI to generate a design brief, a proposal, research summary, or notes based on selected content.

You can also use Miro AI to modify and translate text.

## Create a Doc

1. Above the Creation bar, select **Create with AI**.
   The **Create with AI** panel opens.
2. Click **Formats** > **Doc**.
   The prompting panel opens.
3. Select one of the starter prompts to begin your description. In the prompting panel, describe the Doc you want to create. You can optionally select one of the starter prompts to begin your description.
4. Press **ENTER** on your keyboard, or click the response arrow.
   Miro AI generates a Doc based on your description.

:::tip
The prompting panel stays open, enabling you to refine your description and return the best output.
:::

## Modify and translate text with Miro AI

Use Miro AI to make the following changes to text on your board:

- Fix grammar and spelling
- Shorten text
- Rewrite for clarity
- Change tone to friendly, professional, business, or fun
- Translate text to 17 supported languages, including French, German, Japanese, Italian, and Turkish. To learn more, see [Miro AI Translate](12-miro-ai-translate.md).

Follow these steps:

1. On your Miro board, highlight any text.
   The context menu appears.
2. Click **Miro AI**.
   For text in a Doc, select how you want Miro AI to modify the text.
   For text in another Format, under **Adjust** select your modification.
