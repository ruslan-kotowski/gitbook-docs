> **Who can do it:** All users
> **Which plans:** Free, Starter, Business, Enterprise, Education
> **Which platforms:** Browser, Desktop, Mobile

AI Sidekicks analyze board content and give expert insights and instant feedback, acting as your knowledgeable colleagues. AI Sidekicks assist with tasks such as generating next steps, validating data, and refining content in real-time.

AI Sidekicks can assist you as a Product Leader, Agile Coach, or a Product Marketer, for example.

Miro collaborated exclusively with the Product Marketing Alliance (PMA) to create the PMA Sidekick, which gives you insights created by top marketing professionals. The PMA Sidekick is a Product Marketer that helps you iterate and validate marketing strategies on your Miro boards.

You can use AI Sidekicks on any Miro board. You can also choose from a group of [Intelligent templates with AI Sidekicks](#intelligent-templates-with-ai-sidekicks) that strategically feature AI Sidekicks.

AI Sidekicks on intelligent templates feature specialized [Action Shortcuts](../facilitation-tools/03-action-shortcuts-beta.md) that trigger the Sidekick to provide role-based insights. To learn more, see [AI Sidekicks with Action Shortcuts](#ai-sidekicks-with-action-shortcuts).

AI Sidekicks are powered by [Miro AI overview](01-miro-ai-overview.md).

## Access AI Sidekicks

1. Select content on your Miro board.
   The content menu appears.
2. Click **Miro AI**.
3. Select an AI Sidekick action. For example, **Get feedback & suggestions** to call on the Product Leader, or **Identify key themes** to call on an Agile Coach.
   The AI Sidekick reviews all content and generates relevant output.

### AI Sidekicks with Action Shortcuts.

[Action Shortcuts](../facilitation-tools/03-action-shortcuts-beta.md) trigger specific features on your Miro board. With an intelligent template that includes an AI Sidekick, specialized Action Shortcuts are already placed on the board.

For example, click an Action Shortcut to trigger your Sidekick to analyze board content and generate next steps as Sticky notes, or comment on your content directly, depending on the AI Sidekick role.
