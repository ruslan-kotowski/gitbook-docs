The Miro AI credits add-on is a paid subscription that increases your monthly AI credit limit.

Your plan allocates a set number of AI credits each month. For Starter, Business, and Enterprise plans, the Miro AI credits add-on enables you to scale your AI usage in Miro.

**More information:** See [Miro AI credits](../../plans-billing/billing-and-payments/03-miro-ai-credits.md) for general information about Miro AI credits.

:::note
The Miro AI credits add-on increases the AI credit limit for your team. The add-on does not provide features. To learn more about Miro add-ons, see [Purchase and manage add-ons](../../plans-billing/miro-add-ons/01-purchase-and-manage-add-ons.md).
:::

## Purchase and manage Miro AI credits add-on

### Free

Teams on a Free plan must upgrade to a paid plain to be eligible for the Miro AI credits add-on.

### Starter and Business plans

To learn about pricing and purchase the Miro AI credits add-on, or increase your credit limit, contact [Miro Support](../tools/troubleshooting/06-contacting-miro-support.md).

### Enterprise plans

Contact your account team to request the Miro AI credits add-on.
