Create with AI enables users to prompt, generate, and iterate on various types of content using Miro AI and Formats. Use Create with AI to prompt Miro AI with existing content on your Miro board, or write your own prompts to generate new content.

This article describes Create with AI capabilities. For general information about Miro AI, see [Miro AI overview](01-miro-ai-overview.md). To learn about AI credits consumption, see [Miro AI credits and AI add-on](../../plans-billing/billing-and-payments/03-miro-ai-credits.md) or (Enterprise) [Miro AI credits for Enterprise plans](../../enterprise-administration/enterprise-subscription-management/enterprise-billing/03-miro-ai-credits-for-enterprise-plans.md).

Create with AI supports the following Formats:

- [**Diagrams or mindmap**](13-miro-ai-with-diagrams-and-mindmaps.md)
  Generate and visualize ideas, systems, processes, or connections.
- [**Docs**](14-miro-ai-with-docs-and-text.md)
  Generate documents like design briefs, summaries, or outlines. For example, interview outlines.
- [**Images**](15-miro-ai-with-images.md)
  Create, iterate, and add images based on prompts and existing board content.
- [**Kanban**](../formats/05-kanban.md)
  Organize tasks in columns to track progress, and seamlessly switch between Kanban, Timeline, and Table views to plan, execute, and adjust in one place.
- [**Miro Prototypes**](../miroverse/prototyping/03-miro-prototypes.md)
  Create interfaces for apps, websites, or landing pages.
- [**Sticky Notes**](16-miro-ai-with-sticky-notes.md)
  Generate ideas for brainstorms, insights, workshops, or roadmaps.
- [**Tables**](../formats/14-tables.md)
  Create tables for planning, tracking, research, backlogs, or analysis.
- [**Timeline**](../formats/15-timeline.md)
  Plan or roadmap your project with a customizable and interactive timeline that aligns stakeholders and teams.

:::note
In Miro, if [Create with AI](#access-create-with-ai) is unavailable above the [Creation bar](../working-on-the-board/02-miro's-new-simplified-user-interface.md), then contact your Company admin. Company admins must enable Miro AI for your organization.
:::

## Access Create with AI

On any Miro board click **Create with AI** above the [Creation bar](../working-on-the-board/02-miro's-new-simplified-user-interface.md#create-with-ai-creation-bar). The Create with AI panel opens.

From the panel, select one of the following options:

- **Sidekicks**
  Create a custom AI partner that assists you with building your ideas, solutions, and next steps.
- **Formats**
  Use Miro AI to generate Docs, diagrams, tables, and more.

Next, the prompting panels opens. To start creating, follow the panel instructions.

## Use Create with AI

### Create from the prompting panel

The Create with AI prompting panel enables you to prompt Miro AI to generate content. You can optionally select a prompt suggestion, shown as tags in the response box.

![](images/28836961018130_mceclip0.png)*In the prompting panel, enter text in the response box inside. You can optionally choose a prompt suggestion.*

For some Formats, you can click **Advanced processing** to reveal the **Basic processing** option. Use basic for quick results, or use advanced for more relevant results which take more time to create.

Advanced processing is required for images.

:::tip
Write your prompts with as much detail as possible. You can also select content on your board for additional context and more relevant results.
:::

### Refine your prompts

After your content is generated, the prompting panel stays open. To ensure that you achieve the most relevant results, you can continue to prompt Miro AI.

Prompt refinement enables you to try different prompting strategies to achieve your result.

### Prompt Miro AI with board content

Create with AI uses visual context processing to analyze visual elements on your board.

You can select any content not hidden on your Miro board as context for your prompt. For example, select Diagrams, images, or Sticky notes.

![](images/28836961020178_mceclip1.png)*Prompt Miro AI with selected context from your board.*

### Create from the Miro AI context menu

On your Miro board, you can access Create with AI options from the **Miro AI** context menu.

For example, select a group of Sticky notes. A toolbar appears above your selection. Click **Miro AI**, and the context menu opens.

![](images/28836961021330_mceclip2.png)*Access Create with AI options from the Miro AI context menu.*

In this example, you can convert Sticky notes into a Doc, for example a meeting summary, or Prototype. You can also cluster your notes by sentiment or keyword.

:::tip
Select **More Objects** to access the **Create with AI** panel.
:::
