Conversation Summaries generate a summary of lengthy comment threads on your Miro board. For boards with a lot of activity, use Conversation Summarties to quickly review and prioritize specific insights.

You can use Conversation Summaries for any comment that is longer than 200 characters, and has more than two replies.

Conversation Summaries are powered by [Miro AI](01-miro-ai-overview.md).

## Access Conversation summaries

1. Hover over the comment.
   The **Summarize** button appears.
   ![Miro AI Conversation Summary button.](images/21496703781394_Snap-to-grid-canvas%20(1).png) *The Miro AI Summarize button.*
2. Select **Summarize**.
   Miro AI generates a Conversation summary.

:::note
The **Summarize** button does not appear if you are the only commenter in the thread.
:::

## FAQ

**Does Conversation summaries use Miro AI credits?**

Yes, Conversation summaries consumes Miro AI credits. To learn more, see [Miro AI Credits and AI Add-On](../../plans-billing/billing-and-payments/03-miro-ai-credits.md).

**How does Miro AI and Conversation summaries use my data?**

Conversation summaries relies on Miro AI, which generates a response from user-submitted input. Data submitted, including content and usage data, are not used for model training. To learn more, see [Miro’s AI Privacy Policy](https://miro.com/legal/privacy-policy/#miro-ai).

**As an Enterprise or Education plan admin, how can I disable Miro AI?**

Admins can disable all Miro AI features in their profile settings. To learn how, see [Miro AI Admin security](../../enterprise-administration/canvas-25-admin-features/data-security/04-miro-ai-admin-security.md).

**What AI model is used for Conversation summaries?**

Conversation summaries uses GPT 4.0. To learn more, see [Miro AI reference](18-miro-ai-reference.md).
